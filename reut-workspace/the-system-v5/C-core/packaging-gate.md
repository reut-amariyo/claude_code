# Packaging Gate - applies to EVERY creative, every platform

From the Ralston Packaging SOP. Caleb Ralston gave it to Reut and Lior on 2026-11-17.
Full source, kept out of git: `C-core/ralston-packaging-sop-source.md`. Standing order from Reut, 2026-11-18:
learn it deeply and apply it to every creative Claude proposes, on any platform.

"Packaging" = the words and picture that make someone stop or click.
- **IG / TikTok reel:** verbal hook (what he says) + written headline (text on screen) + visual (what they see).
- **LinkedIn / X post:** the first line, plus the image.
- **Carousel:** slide 1.
- **YouTube:** title + thumbnail.
- **Story:** frame 1.
- **Email:** subject line.

---

## 0. Before any packaging: the gift
Idea first, packaging second. Never the other way around.
**Gift = one ideal-customer problem + Lior's own solution to it.** Packaging is the wrapping paper.
If I can't write the gift in one line, there is nothing to package yet.
Never start from "this title format went viral, let's find an idea for it". That makes content for competitors, not customers.

## 1. The 4 curiosity conditions. Check every hook against all 4
Only three things can happen when someone sees it: **curiosity, confusion or indifference.**
Trying to be clever or mysterious almost always produces the last two.

| Condition | Question | Usual fix |
|---|---|---|
| Relevance | Does the ideal customer care? Either learned (their work, their goals) or biological (fear, loss) | call-out, Identity, Fear |
| Gap | Is something they want to know left open? Don't close it in line 2 | the promise |
| Belief | Do they believe THIS person can close the gap? The one most people miss | Credibility line, proof in the frame |
| Novelty | Is it different from baseline? | Contrarian, Shock, the visual |

All 4 present and still too complex = fail. **It must be understood in under 1 second.**

## 2. The 10 levers. Every hook names at least one
| # | Lever | Lior-safe example angle |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Fear** - consequence of not acting. Aim it at the problem, never the viewer | "This hire kills startups at 20 people" |
| 2 | **Big Outcome** - specific, picturable result | "First profitable month, zero funding" |
| 3 | **Deepest Darkest Desire** - the lazy guilty want | "Run a company without being needed" |
| 4 | **Ease** - easy to consume, do, or fast | "17 years of hiring in 90 seconds" |
| 5 | **Contrarian** - a true point they don't expect. The strongest lever for Lior | "Stop managing your team" (macro-management) |
| 6 | **Credibility** - proof tied to THIS promise. Failure counts | "Turned down $38M. Sold for $92M." |
| 7 | **Action** - a direct command | "Delete your business plan" |
| 8 | **Identity** - who they are or want to be. Repels on purpose | "For founders who hate fundraising" |
| 9 | **Shock** - a jarring real number or claim | "200 people, zero quarterly plans" |
| 10 | **Mystery** - open loop. Only with obvious stakes. Last resort | "This is why your team stalls" |

Stacking: pick the second lever by asking which of the 4 conditions is weakest.
Lior's contrarian swords: macro-management / marketing beats product / no plans / profitable from day 1.
They are the default Contrarian material. Use them only once Lior confirms he'd fall on that sword.

## 3. Mechanics, non-negotiable
- **Short-form headline:** 5-8 words, 2 lines max. Max 3 pull points: headline, face, captions.
  Check it on a phone with the UI on top. Nothing near the edges or the like/comment column.
- **Thumbnail / cover / carousel slide 1:** 3-5 words, 3 lines max. Check it at thumbnail size, the shrink test.
- **LinkedIn first line:** under 10 words with a concrete anchor. That's the house rule, and it fits the SOP.
- Simple words, no double negatives, nothing that sounds smart. Clean sans serif. Contrast over looks.
  No small text anywhere.
- **Face 95% of the time.** Chest-up, face big in the frame. Lior's real face beats a cartoon of him. Caleb: a cartoon builds a mascot.
- **Same promise, different job:** the headline never repeats the verbal hook word for word.
  Title and thumbnail never repeat each other, and never contradict each other.
- **Every claim is traceable** to a moment in the content. No promise the content doesn't pay off.
  Match the strength of the claim to the strength of the point.

## 4. How I deliver every creative proposal, on any platform
For each option:
1. **Gift** - problem + Lior's solution, one line.
2. **Lever(s)** - named, e.g. "Contrarian + Credibility".
3. **Hook package** - verbal + headline + visual for video. First line + image for posts.
4. **4-condition check** - R / G / B / N, each ✅ or 🟡 with what would make it stronger.
5. **Mechanics** - word count, line count, 1-second read.

When I give several options, each one pulls a DIFFERENT lever.
Start wide and go narrow. Never 3 versions of the same angle.

**Headline run**, when Reut sends a reel transcript or a script:
write 10 options for each of the 10 levers, 100 in all, to `O-output/headline-runs/YYYY-MM-DD-<slug>.md`.
In chat, show only the 7 best, already filtered against the house anti-AI rules.
Caleb: "92 of them will suck. Pick the one that doesn't sound like AI."

## 5. Where the SOP meets house rules. House rules win
- **Credibility "How I ___":** a LinkedIn hook never opens with "I" unless the verb is a confession.
  So: "Built and sold for $92M", or "I turned down $38M", where the confession verb is allowed.
  On-screen IG headlines may use "How I ___".
- **Big Outcome / Desire on BYS:** no income promises. "$X a day" style lines fail the BYS compliance gate in `/vsl`.
- **Contrarian / Fear / Shock** must pass the CEO reputation lens. Never imply Lior cuts without thinking.
  No "it's not X, it's Y" phrasing, even as a contrarian line.
- **Mystery:** almost never for Lior. The fake "LEAKED" hooks on X flopped twice.
- **Odd numbers in lists** still apply.
- **IG Yes/No gate** from Emily Shwed still runs. Its "one credibility line" fix IS the Credibility lever.

## 6. Pre-ship checklist. Short version, run silently on every creative
- [ ] Gift written: problem + Lior's solution
- [ ] At least one named lever
- [ ] All 4 conditions present, with weak ones filled by the headline or the visual
- [ ] Read in under 1 second
- [ ] Word and line limits for the format
- [ ] Every claim true and paid off in the content
- [ ] Headline ≠ verbal hook, title ≠ thumbnail
- [ ] Face present, or a stated reason why not
- [ ] Options pull different levers

## 7. After results
- **Don't assume packaging made a winner or a flop.** Diagnose: was it the topic, the intro or the packaging?
- **Proof:** has it happened before? If not, repeat it once or twice before building on it.
- **Filter:** do we want Lior known for this?
- Packaging formats wear out faster than content formats. Track which headline formats win, not just which topics.
  Instagram is the place to test text hooks in volume.
- During the transition, judge new-style posts on leads and DMs from the ideal customer, not on views.
