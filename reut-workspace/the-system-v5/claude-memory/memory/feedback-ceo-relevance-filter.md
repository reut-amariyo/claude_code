Before drafting a post for Lior built around a chart, trend, or news item, first ask: is this something a hi-tech SaaS CEO would credibly speak on?

A hi-tech CEO's lane: company strategy, scaling, hiring, OKRs, leadership decisions, product bets, capital allocation, competition, macro trends that affect his industry's economics, founder journey lessons.

NOT his lane: consumer behavior trends, marketing tactics, content creator economics, platform usage stats, CMO-level observations. These belong to marketers and creators, not CEOs.

If the chart/topic is interesting for a CMO or content creator but not for a CEO, Lior writing about it will feel like he's stretching to stay relevant - which kills credibility worse than not posting.

**Why:** Reut flagged that I drafted a post around a chart showing social media usage declining. That's a CMO/creator topic, not a CEO topic. A hi-tech CEO commenting on TikTok attention trends sounds off-lane and performative.

**How to apply:** Before saying "yes there's an angle here" on any trend/chart/news, run the filter: "Would a hi-tech SaaS CEO credibly speak on this?" If the answer is "only if we stretch," tell Reut the topic isn't his lane instead of drafting. Save her the cycle of pushing back on weak angles.
