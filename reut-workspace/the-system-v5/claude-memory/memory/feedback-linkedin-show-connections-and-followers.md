Reut, 2026-10-01, September review: "I want all the connections and the followers here." She
did not recognize "net followers" as a useful number on its own.

**Why:** Lior thinks in network size. A bare "+718 net" reads as a jargon delta, not a picture of the account.

**How to apply:** in every monthly LinkedIn block show a small table: Followers (total) and
Connections (total), both read live from Lior's profile — connections from
linkedin.com/mynetwork/invite-connect/connections/, followers from the profile or
mynetwork/network-manager/people-follow/followers/. State that connections auto-follow, so the
follower count already contains them; never add the two. Then show the monthly change with
**new followers joined (gross) as the HEADLINE number** and the change in the total as a note.
Reut 2026-10-01: "put the cleanup as a note; I care how many joined this month, regardless of
the accounts LinkedIn deletes". Never lead with net on LinkedIn.

LinkedIn periodically removes inactive accounts from follower lists (banner on the followers
page, seen 2026-10-01). That explained ~492 of the Sep 2026 gross-vs-net gap. Gross new
followers is the fair content measure.

Sep 30 2026 baseline: 13,698 followers / 11,824 connections.
