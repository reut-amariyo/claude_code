URL: https://en.globes.co.il/en/article-tech-layoffs-are-not-all-because-of-ai-1001544648 (published 2026-06-01, byline "CEO of SaaS company autoDS").

Lior's on-record positions:
- Two kinds of tech layoffs: downsizing dressed up as "AI streamlining" because it's the easiest story for the board, and companies whose product AI is actually breaking.
- The math of "replace a person with a cheap machine" rarely works; in most cases AI opens growth, not cuts.
- Companies should give employees training and support to adapt; employees' job security = moving fast, even taking a new title.
- Startups: go niche, close to one customer's pain; giants will crush "does everything" products.
