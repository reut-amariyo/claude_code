When Reut approves or praises a hook during an iteration cycle, that hook is LOCKED. Later revisions (structure changes, style transplants, new beats) must keep the agreed hook verbatim unless Reut explicitly asks to change it.

**Why:** On 2026-07-09, after Reut approved "This month is insane for programming." she asked to restyle the post on the Base44-killer template. The restyle replaced the hook, and she pushed back: "don't change the hook once we agree on that."

**How to apply:** Treat an approved hook like an approved fact. When applying a new structure or template to a draft, adapt the template around the locked hook, even if the template implies its own hook style.
