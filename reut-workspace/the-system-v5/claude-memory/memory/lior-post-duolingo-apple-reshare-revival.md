Post: "How to steal Apple's hype without buying a single ad? Duolingo just gave a guerrilla marketing masterclass" + Duolingo owl at the Apple launch image. Published ~2026-11-12.

- At 14h: 1,968 impressions, 30% in-network / 70% out, 1,441 reached, 2 profile viewers, 0 followers, 52 engagements.
- Then Reut's team reshared it. At 2d: 90,711 impressions, 1% in-network / 99% out, 66,178 reached, 124 profile viewers, 19 followers, 804 engagements. That is about 46x the 14h number.
- My 14h read called it a structural newsjack flop: Duolingo is the hero, so nobody clicks Lior. The reshare proved distribution was the bottleneck, which is what Reut said. Lesson for me: a newsjack with a decent 14h engagement rate, 2.6% here, is a reshare candidate, not a post-mortem.
- Plus ~40 connection requests from the post, per Reut 2026-11-14, so about 59 new people in total. I graded it on the 19 followers alone and Reut had to remind me, which breaks [[feedback-post-followers-undercount-connection-requests]]. Always ask for or add connection requests before grading followers.
- Even counting those, Reut agrees it is reach content more than follower content: Duolingo stays the hero. Next to [[lior-post-built-to-sell-38m-record]] the follower conversion is lower. Use this format when the goal is reach, not followers. See [[feedback-newsjack-human-not-news-anchor]], [[feedback-linkedin-kpi-followers-not-impressions]].

**Why:** Oct-Nov 2026 reach is held back early ([[linkedin-reach-drop-oct-2026-first-48h]]), and one reshare can unlock out-of-network distribution.
