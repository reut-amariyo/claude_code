Lior tasked Reut (weekly of 2026-08-19) with a plan to reach **500K Instagram followers
and 100K LinkedIn followers**. He asked specifically for a 3-month Excel per channel:
posts published, avg views/post, total views, profile visits, followers from that,
in both numbers and percentages.

Deliverables built 2026-08-19, in `the-system-v5/O-output/growth-plan-500k-100k/`:
`lior-growth-data-3months.xlsx` (formula-driven, yellow cells = still to pull) and
`weekly-brief-500k-100k.md`.

**The finding that should drive the LinkedIn plan:** followers do NOT scale with reach.
Apr 49K impressions -> +922 followers. May 292,705 -> +1,100. Jun 51,839 -> +1,009.
Jul 71,776 -> +821. Impressions moved 6x, new followers stayed in a 922-1,100 band every
month. May's two viral posts bought 240K extra impressions and 91 extra followers.
So "more reach" cannot deliver 100K; the bottleneck is the conversion step after the
impression. At the current ~963/month pace, 100K is ~92 months away.

**Instagram by contrast** is a run-rate extension: +24,354 in July vs +4,740 in June,
16.4 months to 500K if July repeats. But 57% of July's growth came from ONE asset
(the AGE 14 childhood photo post), so the whole forecast rests on whether the
personal-artifact post is a repeatable series. Reels volume doubled (48 -> 97) while
per-reel views halved (40,945 -> 20,619): volume buys total reach, not better reach.

**Never pulled, and the highest-value missing metric:** LinkedIn monthly profile visits.
Without it we cannot locate where the LinkedIn funnel leaks.
