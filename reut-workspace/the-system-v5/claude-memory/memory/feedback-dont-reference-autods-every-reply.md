Don't bring AutoDS into every reply or comment. Naming the company every time reads as repetitive product/brand marketing.

**Why:** Reut flagged it directly (2026-06-25) on a LinkedIn reply that opened "People think AutoDS had some breakout moment." She wants varied proof and a lighter touch, not the same company name in every draft.

**How to apply:**
- Lead with the generic insight pattern when it fits: "people think building a startup looks like X, but in reality it's Y."
- Weave in **the exit** elegantly as proof of credibility instead of naming AutoDS or describing the product. e.g. "the exit didn't come from the flashy weeks, it came from the boring ones."
- A real Lior story is still required per the reply rules — just source proof from his journey (eBay ban, 100 accounts, the exit, specific incidents) without defaulting to the AutoDS name every time.
