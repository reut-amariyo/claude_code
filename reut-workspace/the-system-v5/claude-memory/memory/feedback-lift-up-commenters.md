When drafting Lior's replies to comments on his posts (LinkedIn, X, Bluesky), open by giving the commenter credit for their observation. Acknowledge them by name when natural, validate that their insight is sharp or ahead of the curve, then add Lior's story or angle.

**Why:** Reut's direction (2026-07-07, Fable 5 LinkedIn post replies): "תרים לאנשים שמגיבים לנו" — lift up the people who comment on us. Commenters who feel seen come back, engage more, and become advocates. A reply that only continues Lior's own story reads as self-centered.

**How to apply:** First line credits the commenter ("Sharp observation Kody", "You named the shift before most people see it"), then the Lior story with a specific number. Keep the credit genuine and specific to what they said, never generic flattery. Still respects [[feedback-linkedin-comments-keep-short]] (2-4 lines) and [[feedback-reply-voice]].
