When Reut replies with just "close" (or similar terse end-signals), she means **end the task / stop iterating**. Do not interpret it as "the draft is close to done, keep refining."

**Why:** Misinterpreted "close" as "almost there" and produced another iteration when Reut wanted the conversation closed. Wasted her time.

**How to apply:** On a one-word "close" reply, acknowledge briefly and stop. Do not deliver another draft, do not ask follow-up questions about the work itself, do not propose variants. The task is over.
