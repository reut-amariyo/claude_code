All social media post drafts for Lior (LinkedIn, X, Instagram, etc.) must be written in English.

**Why:** Lior's audience is global/English-speaking, and his published posts are in English. Even when Reut briefs a task in Hebrew, the output post itself is always English. She had to correct me on this after I drafted a LinkedIn post in Hebrew.

**How to apply:** When drafting any post for Lior's social channels, write the post in English by default. Questions, commentary, and back-and-forth with Reut can be in the language she uses, but the post copy itself is always English. Don't ask which language — just write English.
