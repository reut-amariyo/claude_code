**Learnings:**
1. Animated story ads win outright. The two cartoons = ~49% of spend but ~66% of free
   trials and ~71% of paid trials, and are the only rows above 100% ROI. This is the paid
   proof for the in-house animated reels engine, see [[project-ai-animated-reels-the-promise]].
2. Emotional origin beats product. Dad / first sale stories convert; the product demo
   "I built the BYS flow" is the worst row on CTR, trial rate and $514 per paid trial.
3. Cheap CPM lies. Lior5 had the lowest CPM yet ~20% ROI because few free trials go paid.
   Judge on cost per PAID trial and ROI, never CPM or CTR.
4. Generic CEO content pulls tire-kickers: high CTR and free-trial rate, worst paid
   conversion at 6%. Flex/luxury and B-roll hooks show 20%+ paid conversion, so the
   audience qualifies, but the click-to-trial step leaks.
5. Rows under ~$2K spend have under 10 paid trials each. Treat them as directional only.

**Open questions for Reut:** how ROI is computed given 0 Online Sales; what the
"Free Trials/" column denominator is, likely clicks; time window of the report.

**How to apply:** when briefing new BYS ads or deciding organic content to boost,
default to an animated Lior life-story episode with a money/first-win beat; make the next
cartoon episodes before more talking-head or product-demo cuts. Report BYS trials per
[[feedback-lior-monthly-metrics-manychat-bys]]. Related: [[project-vsl-agent-build-your-store]].

## Competitor creatives, Meta Ad Library scan 2026-11-12

Source: Reut's "Competitors" Google Sheet, 7 rows. Active ads checked sorted by impressions.
Long-running ads = proven winners, since nobody pays months for a loser.

- **Ramin Popal / aistorebuilder.com**, ~75 ads, one copy everywhere: "STOP wasting money on
  courses! Free store in under 10 min, 20 trending products, US suppliers". Creative = luxury
  backdrops, private jet, Dubai, penthouse, with a white-box TEXT STACK over them: "Handing out
  50 free AI stores this week", "Free store + free $3,000 course, you own 100%, no cut", 3
  steps, "Claim one before spots fill". #1 by impressions is the jet static. Also an IG
  "Ask me a question" sticker answering "what's the catch? why is this free?" and talking
  head with one-word captions "paying", "FREE".
- **Nathan N / aidropshippingbuilder.com**, ~110 ads, OLDEST winners in the set, running since
  Apr-May 2026. Copy = third-person customer story: "Last year, Tyler was stuck in a 9-5..."
  then 30 days later first $3,000. Creatives = objection skits: "Dude I'm not buying your
  $3000 course" while eating on a private jet, "He's just another scammer" in a Lambo,
  Netflix cost vs My course cost $0 meme, "F**k it, I'm giving everything away for FREE".
- **Storebuild.ai**, ~330 results: $5 "AI e-com agents starter kit, make $4K in 30 days or
  I'll refund your $5" box statics; an older finance-guru talking head saying "Five dollars"
  with 12-13 variants; halal-earning angle; Hindi-language UGC. Heavy niche/local targeting.
- **Dropmagic**, ~24: UGC creators "AI Drop Shipping, Beginner Edition" in car/kitchen,
  "Wait how is this even legal?!", podcast-set clips, screen demo, full French versions.
  Copy story: "3 months ago, Max was still thinking about starting his store".
- **Adrian Morrison / esamastery**, ~820: FOUNDER is the face, closest analog to Lior.
  Studio set with Shopify box props + captions, selfie in car, on yacht, on a plane with his
  daughter. Running since May-June. Copy: "This shouldn't be free. We didn't build it to be..."
- Atlas AI Store Builder: 0 active ads. Amer Barham / ecomwebsites: not found by name.

**Patterns across competitors:**
1. Everyone sells the same offer, free store + 20 products + minutes. The offer is a
   commodity, so the creative and the face are the only edge.
2. The "why is this free / what's the catch" objection is answered IN the ad by 3 of 5.
3. Wealth proof is the default backdrop, jet/Lambo/yacht. BYS PrivatePlane_Luxury got 75% ROI.
4. Customer story in the copy, "Tyler", "Max", name + before + 30-day result.
5. Many cheap variants of one winner: same copy, 10-25 visual cuts.
6. Nobody runs animated story ads. BYS's cartoon winners are a format no competitor uses.
7. None has a real $92M-exit founder. Adrian Morrison is the only founder-face rival.
