**What happened:** The 2026-08-12 Anthropic-watermark post went through ~10 iterations. It started as a news-explainer (section headers: The news / Why it matters / What's next, C2PA jargon, arms-race analysis). Lior rejected that register twice via voice notes: "פוסט מאוד אנושי, my media ולא news media". The published version was a human reaction: proven hook shape → "Everyone is panicking over it. / But does it even matter?" → eye-level 2-layer explainer → personal take → "The thinking has no watermark" → "Teach me in the comments." Result: exceptional comment volume, Lior explicitly happy and named the humanity as the reason. Impressions: 3K → 7K within day 0, still climbing (7d final pending) — early reach lagged the comment wave, so treat "conversation lever vs reach lever" as provisional until the final number lands.

**Why:** Lior's page wins as an operator reacting to news like a person, never as an anchor desk. The audience comes for his read, not the recap. The curiosity close (CEO asks the audience to teach him) inverts guru posture and is a stronger comment engine than any expertise close tested so far.

**How to apply:**
- Newsjack/release-decode drafts: human-reaction spine first (panic/noise → "does it even matter?" deflation → what actually happened, in mass-clarity language → my take → genuine open question). News-explainer sections are the failure mode.
- Cut technical completeness from the post body; keep it as ammunition for Lior's comment replies (that's where GPTZero/KGW/SynthID material performed).
- In comment replies, never recycle an image across threads — each reply gets a fresh metaphor.
- Extends the hook register rule in [[favorite-content-hooks]] (conversational reacting-now > data-headline) from line 1 to the whole post. Related: [[Lior LinkedIn — Top 13 / 10K+ Impressions Playbook (LEADING RULES)]] archetype 4.
- **The register is a conversation lever, not a distribution lever.** When the goal is reach, pair it with a proven reach lever: tag the giant company, sharpen the threat frame, or ship on day 1 of the wave. Do not expect the human register alone to clear 10K. Full analysis + hypotheses in the-system-v5/M-memory/learning-log.md (2026-08-12 entry).
