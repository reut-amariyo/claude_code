Never write content that claims Lior reads books, finishes books, or is "always reading." He is dyslexic.

He DOES consume book content, just not by reading:
- Podcast interviews with authors
- Audiobooks
- Summaries his team prepares for him
- Skimming / chapter sampling
- Conversations about books

He posts book recommendations on Instagram. Those are real recommendations based on the methods above, not because he read the book cover to cover. Don't undercut that by writing "I just finished reading..." in another channel.

**Why:** Reut flagged this on the CandleKeep LinkedIn post draft (2026-05-03). Saying "10 years of my reading" would have been a lie that contradicted his IG book recs and ignored a real, vulnerable, on-brand part of his story.

**How to apply:**
- Never use phrases like "I just read", "I finished the book", "in my reading", "books I've read", "I'm a heavy reader"
- When books are relevant to a post, frame consumption honestly: "podcast with the author", "audiobook on a flight", "the chapter my team flagged for me", "I bought it but never opened it"
- The dyslexia angle itself is fair game and actually a strong hook — vulnerable, specific, founder-credible. Use when the post is genuinely about that, not as decoration.
- Cross-platform consistency: assume any reader who follows him on IG also sees his LinkedIn/X. Stories must hold up.
