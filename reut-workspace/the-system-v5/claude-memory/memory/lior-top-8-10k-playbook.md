## The 5 Universal Rules (apply to every post)

### Rule A — Hook under 10 words, concrete anchor in line 1
Every winning hook contained ONE of these in line 1:
- Named famous entity (Shopify, Nike, Amazon, Anthropic, Israel, Lex Fridman)
- Specific number (10,000 reviews, $47.8B, 4.90x)
- Year (2019)
- Provocative confession ("I set up AI agents to replace my entire team")

**Banned in line 1:** "Let me tell you...", "You know that feeling...", "Recently I...", "In this post I'll...", "Reflecting on...", "I'm excited to share..."

### Rule B — Visual is never Lior-marketing-team-designed
4 valid visual families (see Section "Visual Families" below). Zero Canva quote cards. Zero branded promotional graphics. Either authentic, viral, illustrated-series, or third-party-authoritative.

### Rule C — Hand-drawn red circle when post hinges on a detail
When the visual has a small detail the reader would miss, mark it with a hand-drawn red circle (Markup / Preview, not Figma). Looks like "I noticed this myself" — operator energy. Confirmed by 2 of 8 top posts (Nike dates, Alcaraz wristband).

### Rule D — There's always a "vs." structure
Every winning post has a foil — external or internal. Before drafting, articulate the "vs." in one sentence. If you can't, the post isn't ready.

### Rule E — "I" provocative-confessional hook IS allowed (refines anti-AI Rule 8)
"I" opener WORKS when the verb is provocative-confessional (set up / fired / killed / gave up / made the mistake of) AND the body is build-in-public truth including what failed. Still banned: vanity "I" hooks ("I built X and scaled to Y," "I learned that...").

## The 10 Confirmed Archetypes

Each archetype = a repeatable template proven to clear 10K+ impressions. When drafting a Lior LinkedIn post, identify which archetype the topic fits, then follow that archetype's pattern.

### 1. Crisis Response + Mindset Reframe
**Top-8 example:** Shopify reviews removed (~620K imp — highest of set)
**Full template:** [lior-post-shopify-reviews-620k.md](lior-post-shopify-reviews-620k.md)
**Hook pattern:** "[Platform] just [past-tense verb] [specific number] of our [thing]."
**Visual:** Personal artifact — the actual notification email/dashboard alert that delivered the bad news
**Topic source:** AutoDS daily ops, SASI by Mantle alerts, AppStore reviews, Stripe disputes, partner platform policy changes
**Use when:** A real external platform/market shock hit AutoDS (algorithm change, regulation, competitor move). Must have a real plan to share in the body.

### 2. Big-Brand Reversal Case Study
**Top-8 example:** Nike returns to Amazon (~50K imp, 577 reactions)
**Full template:** [lior-post-nike-amazon-case-study.md](lior-post-nike-amazon-case-study.md)
**Hook pattern:** "In [year], [Famous Brand] [past-tense verb] [Famous Platform]."
**Visual:** Receipt Stack — two real news article screenshots from different dates, dates circled in red marker
**Topic source:** The Verge, WSJ, FT, Stratechery. Search triggers: "[company] returns to", "[company] reverses", "[company] pivots back"
**Use when:** A famous company in commerce/SaaS reverses a famous decision. Lior must have operator standing in the category.
**Length exception:** This archetype earns ~1,500-1,600 chars (above the 750-1,200 sweet spot) — 3 numbered stat-beats need breathing room.

### 3. Spotted Strategy / Guerrilla Decode
**Top-8 example:** Alcaraz Nike-on-wristband / Polo sponsor (~20K+ imp, 201 reactions)
**Hook pattern:** "How to [provocative verb e.g. steal] [quantified outcome e.g. millions in exposure] without [impossible cost e.g. paying for it]"
**Visual:** Side-by-side press photos with hand-drawn red circle on the spotted detail
**Topic source:** Marketing Brew, The Hustle, Tom Goodwin's LinkedIn, r/marketing. Search triggers: "guerrilla marketing", "ambush marketing", "without paying"
**Use when:** Lior spots a clever marketing/brand move in the wild that has a transferable strategic lesson.

### 4. Industry Release Decode
**Top-8 example:** Anthropic Skills (.md files) — Wojak grandpa meme (~15K+ imp, 55 reactions, 23 comments — high quality engagement from AI builder crowd)
**2026-05 record example:** "Google just dropped a Base44 killer" (142,818 imp — highest of ANY post to date) — decoded Google AI Studio for Android as a giant-ships-your-roadmap threat, then gave the operator defense ("narrow the niche, own one vertical"), closed "Agree?". Tagged Google + Base44 = borrowed reach. ⚠️ But 0.16% eng rate — reach play, not resonance (see May 2026 caveat above).
**~93K tutorial example:** "Anthropic's team just dropped the workflow trick they use internally" (MD→HTML, ~93K imp — #2 all-time) — a *step-by-step how-to* that won big by being framed as insider intel + operator confession, not a tool tutorial. See "The tutorial exception" note in the additions section for why this is allowed and where the line is.
**Hook pattern:** "[Famous Company]'s team just dropped [thing]." or "[Company] just shipped [thing]." or "[Giant] just dropped a [Smaller-co] killer." or "[Authority]'s team just dropped the [trick] they use internally."
**Threat-decode sub-pattern (confirmed via Google post):** When a giant ships a feature that threatens a category, frame = (1) name the threat, (2) name the founder fear it triggers, (3) give the one defense that actually works. CEO voice, not doom.
**Insider-trick sub-pattern (confirmed via 93K MD→HTML post):** A how-to IS allowed here — but ONLY when (1) framed as insider intel from an authority Lior stress-tested himself ("the trick they use internally… tested it last week"), (2) opened with an operator confession ("if you're in the 'approve all without reading' gang"), and (3) closed on a strategic/human insight, not a step. Plain "here's how to use [tool]" with none of these three is still banned.
**Visual:** Cultural meme template (Wojak, distracted boyfriend, etc.) — never a tool screenshot
**Topic source:** Anthropic blog + Twitter, OpenAI announcements, Hacker News front page (when release stays #1 for 6+ hours), AI Twitter (swyx, Simon Willison, Karpathy)
**Speed:** Post within 24-48h of the release. After that the wave passes.
**Critical guardrail:** This must be a STRATEGIC SIGNIFICANCE decode (CEO voice: "this matters because…"), NOT a tutorial (creator lane: "here's how to use it…"). See [feedback-no-productivity-tool-tips.md](feedback-no-productivity-tool-tips.md) for the line.

### 5. Dialogue Critique of a Common Practice
**Top-8 example:** Walking Red Flag / Slack-as-task-manager (~12K imp, 20 reactions — recurring branded cartoon series, strong save signal for managers)
**Hook pattern:** "He said: '[quoted wrong-take]'"
**Visual:** Original branded illustration with Lior signature — character with on-the-nose label ("WALKING RED FLAG" t-shirt) doing the wrong thing
**Topic source:** Real conversations at conferences, YPO/EO peer groups, founder dinners. Anything Lior actually heard another manager say.
**Use when:** A common-but-fixable management practice (Slack-as-task-manager, hiring-from-LinkedIn-only, etc.). The practice must be (a) common, (b) clearly labeled as wrong via visual or text, (c) followed by Lior's better way.
**Refines:** [feedback-dialogue-posts-fire-people.md](feedback-dialogue-posts-fire-people.md) — dialogue posts work in TWO modes now: firing-the-employee scene OR "He said:" + bad-practice quote. Both valid.

### 6. Build-in-Public AI Experiment Report
**Top-8 examples:** AI agents replacing team (~30K imp — Paperclip-tagged meme) + No team, no code (~12K imp — Lex Fridman frame)
**Hook patterns:**
- "I [risky verb e.g. set up] [provocative outcome e.g. AI agents to replace my entire team]."
- "No [X]. No [Y]. Just [Z]." (triple-contrast / negate-negate-reveal)
**Visual:** Cultural meme (self-contained, like Brad Pitt church) OR podcast frame (borrowed authority validation, like Lex Fridman)
**Topic source:** Lior's actual experiments with AI/automation. Things he tried at AutoDS that didn't quite work — the failure is the post.
**Tool-icon-as-meme-caption tactic (confirmed via Paperclip post 6):** Embed your product logo INTO the meme as part of the joke (Paperclip icon overlaid on each panel). Subtle brand placement without pitch posture. Lior-made meme = allowed; Lior pitching the product in the text = banned.
**Cadence:** 1-2 per month max. Each needs a real experiment.

### 7. Authority Ranking — Tribal Pride
**Top-8 example:** Anthropic ranks Israel #1 in Claude usage (~15K imp)
**Hook pattern:** "[Famous Authority] ranked [Tribe] #[position] in [category]."
**Visual:** Third-party authoritative data graphic (Anthropic infographic, McKinsey chart, government data viz) — polish is OK here because credibility belongs to the source
**Topic source:** Anthropic Economic Index, Statista, Crunchbase rankings, Start-Up Nation Central, Israel Innovation Authority, McKinsey/BCG/Bain annual reports
**Use when:** A credible authority ranks Lior's tribe (Israeli tech / SaaS founders / e-commerce) at the top of anything. Tribal-pride activation, not self-promotion.

### 8. Milestone Reflection Listicle (NEW — confirmed 2026-05-24)
**Confirmed example:** "Sold AutoDS to Fiverr at 28. Turning 31 today got me reflecting…" → 10 numbered lessons (17,970 imp, 198 likes)
**Hook pattern:** "[Exit/credibility fact with number]. [Milestone moment] got me reflecting on [what]:" — line 1 carries a concrete proof number (Fiverr, 28) AND a personal milestone (birthday).
**Body:** A clean numbered list (8–10 items) of hard-won operator lessons. Each item = one punchy imperative + one-line why ("Kill the side projects. Focus is the only real edge."). Lessons must hit the brand pillars (focus, automate/be lazy, ship before ready, be home for dinner = "live on 200" balance).
**Visual:** Personal artifact / authentic photo (birthday, a real moment) — not a designed quote card.
**Close:** Warm, low-ego ("Took me years to learn. Hope it saves you some (:")
**Use when:** A real Lior milestone (birthday, exit anniversary, funding date, headcount round number) gives natural license to reflect. Needs a genuine credibility anchor in line 1 — without the "sold at 28" the listicle is generic.
**Cadence:** Rare — tied to actual milestones. Don't manufacture.

### 9. Market-Empathy Newsjack + Open Arms (NEW — confirmed 2026-05-28)
**Confirmed example:** "Tough days for Israeli tech. Wix. Meta. Oracle. Cisco." → layoff-wave empathy + "we're hiring, DMs open" (13,256 imp, 232 likes = highest-liked of the May set, 1.95% eng — best resonance of the three)
**Hook pattern:** "[Emotionally honest read on a current market event]. [List of named companies hit]." — newsjacks a real, painful industry moment.
**Body:** (1) Name the moment honestly, (2) reframe with dignity for those affected ("you didn't fail, the market shifted"), (3) THEN a genuine generosity CTA — hiring, DMs open, "drop it in the comments." The generosity must be real and specific (named open departments, "I'll route it personally").
**Visual:** Authentic / restrained — this is not a meme moment.
**Use when:** A real market event is causing widespread pain in Lior's tribe (layoffs, downturn, closures) AND AutoDS can genuinely help. Empathy first, ask second.
**⚠️ Why this is allowed despite the old hiring-CTA rule:** This is market-empathy + generosity, NOT a personal vulnerability story with a bolted-on hiring ad. The hiring CTA here is the *generosity*, not a hijack. See loosened [feedback-no-hiring-ads-as-story-posts.md](feedback-no-hiring-ads-as-story-posts.md).
**Best for engagement, not just reach** — model this when the goal is comments/saves.

### 10. Cultural Mega-Moment Decode (NEW — confirmed 2026-08-04)
**Confirmed example:** "Messi was barely running" — World Cup final 2026 → Messi walks 83% of the match → "the lazy method" → "Busy players run more. Great players run right." (10K+ imp confirmed; exact figure not yet logged)
**Hook pattern — two-beat anomaly:** Line 1 sets the shared-context scene ("Watched the World Cup final live yesterday, and one thing kept me busy:"). Line 2 lands the anomaly in under 6 words with a world-famous name ("Messi was barely running."). ⚠️ Line 1 runs over the 10-word limit — allowed ONLY in this two-beat shape, where line 2 is the real hook and carries the anchor.
**Body beats (7, in order):**
1. Anomaly spotted live at a mass cultural event.
2. Humility disclaimer — "I'm not a football expert" — kills the punditry objection and keeps him in the operator lane.
3. Operator curiosity — "bothered me enough to go digging" — the text version of the hand-drawn red circle (Rule C energy: "I noticed this myself").
4. ONE undecorated stat as the payoff (83%). No source citation, no decoration — matches his spoken voice DNA.
5. Explicit one-line bridge: "It clicked instantly. Business feels exactly like this." Then the personal founder arc: past failure mode (sprints, no sleep, chasing everything) → current method (pick where to attack, delegate, leave ego out).
6. Quotable parallel principle with a one-word swap: "Busy players run more. Great players run right."
7. Question close that turns the metaphor on the reader: "Which balls are you still chasing?" — textbook engagement gap.
**"vs.":** running more vs. running right / sprint-everything founder vs. focused delegator.
**Pillars hit:** focus (pick where to attack) + "I live on 200" delegation joy (the lazy method). Two of three pillars in one post.
**Visual:** not captured for this post — presumably a broadcast/press frame of Messi walking (borrowed third-party authority family). Log it when known.
**Topic source:** World Cup, Olympics, Super Bowl, Champions League final, major award shows — any event most of the feed watched last night. The detail must be genuinely counterintuitive AND provable with a single stat.
**Speed:** within ~24h of the event. Shared-context decay is faster than tech-release decay (Archetype 4 gets 48h; this gets 24).
**Use when:** a mass cultural moment contains one counterintuitive performance detail that maps cleanly onto an existing Lior pillar. If the bridge needs more than one line to click, the metaphor is forced — kill it.
**Distinct from Archetype 3 (Spotted Strategy):** #3 decodes a marketing tactic with a quantified business outcome; #10 decodes a performance detail from a mass-audience moment into an operating principle, with a personal arc in the middle and a question close. #3 is a curiosity play; #10 is built for resonance — universal shared context + reader-facing question.
**Rule bends this win confirms (narrow, don't generalize):**
- Two-beat anomaly hook may exceed 10 words in line 1 (see hook pattern above).
- Self-deprecating method-naming is allowed: "the lazy method" survives the no-concept-naming rule because it's anti-guru, mildly self-mocking, and earned by the failure arc right before it. Grandiose renaming ("the art of X") stays banned.
- First-person count runs ~5, above the ~3 target — acceptable in personal-arc archetypes ONLY when the close hands the post fully to the reader.

## The 9 Confirmed Hook Archetypes

All contain a concrete anchor and skip "you know what" preambles. All keep line 1 under 10 words EXCEPT #9, the two-beat anomaly, where line 2 is the real hook.

1. **Concrete-event past-tense (platform/number)** — "Shopify just removed over 10,000 of our reviews."
2. **Year + Brand + Verb** — "In 2019, Nike walked away from Amazon"
3. **Curiosity-gap "How to" + provocative verb** — "How to steal millions in exposure without paying for it"
4. **Source + recency release** — "Anthropic's team just dropped [thing]..."
5. **Reported dialogue** — "He said: 'I just Slack my team to get stuff done.'"
6. **First-person provocative confession** — "I set up AI agents to replace my entire team. Including me."
7. **Source + ranking + outcome** — "Anthropic ranked Israel #1 in global AI usage..."
8. **Triple-contrast / Negate-Negate-Reveal** — "No team. No code. Just a conversation with [X]."
9. **Two-beat anomaly (mega-event)** — "Watched the World Cup final live yesterday, and one thing kept me busy: / Messi was barely running." Line 2 under 6 words, famous name + counterintuitive behavior.

(Note: 9 hook variants across 10 archetypes — Build-in-Public AI Experiment has two valid hook openers, #6 and #8.)
