# Topic gate: "only Lior could write this" (run before choosing a topic, before drafting)

Reut's decision, 2026-09-22, after the 09-21 Creator Analytics lock (63 posts with
profile viewers + followers gained per post).

**Why:** followers come from exactly three shapes in Lior's own data: a first-party
artifact (exit deck 1,179 profile views / 94 followers), an operator-seat rules post
(janitor 115 / 12, 1.9 per 1K), a named-person personal post (Galit 44 / 14, 1.70 per 1K).
Every tool how-to (5 posts), every scoreboard post, every generic curation post and every repeat
topic converted 0. A follow is a subscription decision: the reader bets there will be
more of something only Lior can supply.

**Curation carve-out (Reut, 2026-10-01, from the September review):** the 2026-09-23
"This is how I scaled my business to $50M ARR" post, a list of the 50 people who taught
Lior the most, hosted on follow.liorpozin.com, was September's best follower post:
24,023 imp / **21 followers / 78 saves / 83 link visits / 303 engagements**. Curation passes
the gate when it is HIS receipt: "the people who taught ME", picked by him, tied to his
result, on his own domain. Generic curation ("9 repos you should try") still converts 0.
Test: would the list exist without Lior's story? If yes, it fails.

**How to apply:** answer all 7 before a topic is approved. Questions 1-3 are blockers.

1. **Could only Lior have written this?** Delete his name; if another CEO, an AI
   newsletter or a LinkedIn coach could post it unchanged, it fails.
2. **What does the reader take home, and who owns it?** A document Lior actually used,
   a rule he actually runs by, a real person from his life. "Someone else's tool" or
   "a generic list of other people's things" = 0 followers even with saves (9 repos: 8 saves, 0 fol).
   A list of the people who shaped HIM, owned on his domain, passes (50 mentors: 21 fol).
3. **Why would a stranger press Follow?** Good: "wants the next document / rule / story".
   Bad: "learned something useful" or "impressed". The second is a reach play, 10% budget only.
4. **Which seat is he speaking from?** 200 people, 4 acquisitions, the exit, 10 years.
   If someone who never managed anyone could say it, it is not his seat.
5. **Is there a named person or a number that is HIS?** Galit, Michael, Mom, 23 days,
   5 years without salary. "Successful people" / "every founder" did 687 and 1,084 imp, 0 fol.
   Generic lesson lists die every time: "9 lessons I've learned" 779 (Sep), Jim Rohn 695 (Aug),
   SaaS steps 1,659 (Aug).
6. **Lesson or scoreboard?** 100K IG: 45 views / 0 fol. "At 28, 7 things": 166 / 5.
   Same credential, one week apart. A milestone travels only through what it teaches.
7. **Topic already in the performance log? What is new?** News trigger or a new artifact,
   or it halves (founder salary 4,614 May -> 2,294 Sept, 0 fol).

Bonus, timing: a 20K+ post in the last 48h? That slot gets the strongest first-party
artifact in the queue. Wasted 3 times (08-27, 08-31, 09-10).

Cadence: never leave more than 2 days without a post. Sep 18-22 had a 4-day gap and the
account fell to a 433-812 impressions/day floor with half the follower rate. Lior has no
passive reach; non-post days run ~600 impressions.
