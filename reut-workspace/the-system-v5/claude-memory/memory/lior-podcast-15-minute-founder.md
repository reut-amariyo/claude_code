- First purchase after the exit: McDonald's, a deadpan joke.
- NYC rooftop line: "still not all the buildings are mine."
- He automated his OKR dashboard, then removed it, because without manually checking managers' progress "I don't really know what's going on in the business."
- His advisor reframed "we had no strategy, just did what they do but better" as the all-in-one strategy.
- He took no money home for 5 years and calls that a mistake: "why not take 5%, 10%?" He traveled to 30+ countries during the build.
- First hire = ROI math: a $10K/mo feature vs a $5K/mo developer.
- Zero-to-$1M order: problem → market/competitor reviews → solution, with the MVP on Lovable.
- Coffee went from 6 to 3 a day. Book: The Power of Habit. Billboard: "Just jump to the water."
