Every hook, headline, first line, cover, thumbnail or subject line I propose for Lior, or for any client, goes through `the-system-v5/C-core/packaging-gate.md`.
That file is distilled from the Ralston Packaging SOP that Caleb Ralston gave Reut and Lior on 2026-11-17. The full source is gitignored at `C-core/ralston-packaging-sop-source.md` because it was given privately.

**Why:** Reut said "learn it really well and apply it to every creative you suggest, forever, no matter the platform". Caleb built Hormozi's brand and says packaging is one of the top 2 things to get right.

**How to apply:**
- Each option shows: gift (problem + Lior's solution) → named lever(s) → hook package → R/G/B/N check → word count. Options pull different levers.
- Headline limits: 5-8 words / 2 lines on reels, 3-5 words on covers and thumbnails. Readable in under 1 second.
- Headline run: Reut sends a transcript → 100 options (10 levers × 10) go to a file, I show the top 7 in chat.
- House rules win any conflict, e.g. no "I" opener on LinkedIn unless it's a confession, no income promises for BYS. Mystery is a last resort.
