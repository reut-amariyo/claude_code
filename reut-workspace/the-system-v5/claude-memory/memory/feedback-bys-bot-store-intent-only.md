In the Lior Store Builder AI bot, every suggested reply, home prompt and Lior question must speak as a beginner who wants to open an online store: how to start, which niche, cost, time, experience, next steps, doubts. Lior steers to the 5 BYS niche cards, never asks "what do you love buying".

**Why:** Reut 2026-10-29: "I love my dog" chips made it look like a hobby chat, not a bot that sells Build Your Store.
**How to apply:** any new line or suggestion in [[project-lior-store-builder-ai]] passes the test "would a person about to open a store say this?"
