Every X week for Lior should hit a deliberate mix across 7 content styles. Each style accomplishes something different — the ratio builds authority, not just engagement.

**The 7 styles (Wiz of Ecom system, 2026-05-01):**
1. **Conviction posts** — what Lior strongly believes about SaaS/founder life.
2. **Story posts** — specific moments from running AutoDS (the dollar bug, firings, hires, decisions).
3. **Midas posts** — results, case studies, milestones with real numbers.
4. **Doc posts** — frameworks, systems, breakdowns.
5. **Hot takes** — contrarian opinions on industry topics.
6. **Transformation posts** — before/after (company state, team state, personal state).
7. **Vulnerability posts** — admitting failure, doubt, hard moments.

**Lior's tuned weekly mix (5 posts/week from scout-day):**
- 2 × Story posts (operator BTS — strongest fit)
- 1 × Vulnerability post (failure / doubt / firing regret)
- 1 × Midas post (specific number, ARR/team/ops milestone)
- 1 × Conviction OR Hot take (rotate weekly)
- 0 × Doc/framework posts (not his lane — see [x-archetype-operator.md](x-archetype-operator.md))
- Transformation: weave into story posts when natural, not as standalone

**Why:** The Wiz of Ecom thread emphasizes the mix is intentional — wrong ratio kills authority. For Lior specifically, framework/doc posts conflict with the Operator archetype and read as generic. Story + vulnerability + midas is where his unique proof lives (250-person SaaS, 8+ years, real incidents like the dollar bug).

**How to apply:**
- scout-day output should label each of the 5 posts with its style.
- If the week's drafts are 4 story + 1 conviction with no vulnerability or midas, force a swap.
- x-analyst-weekly should track which styles are winning bookmarks (not just likes) and shift the mix accordingly.
