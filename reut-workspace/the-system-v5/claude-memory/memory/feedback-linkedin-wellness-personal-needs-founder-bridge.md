When Lior wants to post a personal experience on LinkedIn (wellness retreat, Wim Hof, travel, family moment, hobby), the post will hit a hard ceiling (~300-1K impressions) UNLESS it does all of the following:

1. **Bridge to a founder/SaaS lesson explicitly.** The personal scene is the hook; the founder reframe is what makes ICP stop scrolling. Without the bridge, senior SaaS founders read it as "vacation post" and scroll. Example: Wim Hof retreat → "the same thing happens when I sign a check that scares me at AutoDS."

2. **Add friction / one contrarian line.** Every line being universally agreeable ("first step is hardest", "just go") = zero comments = algorithm death. Need at least one line where someone could disagree.

3. **Open-gap ending, not a closed conclusion.** Don't end with "Just go." or "Don't think about it." — that hands the reader the full answer. End with a question Lior is genuinely wrestling with: "What's the last decision that scared you enough you almost didn't take it?"

4. **Avoid numbered listicle (1. / 2. / 3.) on personal stories.** Numbering pushes intimate stories into template territory. Personal/emotional posts work as flowing scene — see winning Post 18 pattern.

5. **Solo hero photo, not blurry group shots.** Personal posts need ONE clear emotional anchor in the image — Lior in the lake, alone on the mountain, eyes closed mid-breath. Blurry/crowded group photos hurt performance.

**Reference case (failure):** 2026-04-28 Wim Hof post. Strong hook ("hiked a mountain in shorts at -15°C, sat in frozen lake for 7 minutes") + real personal stake, but: no founder bridge, no friction (every point universally agreeable), closed ending ("Just go."), numbered listicle structure, blurry group photo. Result: under 1,000 impressions.

**Why:** Lior's audience is senior SaaS founders. Off-ICP topics hit a ~300-2K ceiling regardless of writing quality. The bridge to founder life is what makes ICP feel "this is for me." Without it, even great personal content underperforms.

**How to apply:** Before drafting any Lior LinkedIn post on a personal/wellness/non-business experience, force the bridge. If you can't find a real SaaS/founder lesson the experience taught Lior, the post shouldn't go on LinkedIn — save it for IG/X.
