When using the sandcastles MCP to find video ideas for Lior, do NOT just relay the watchlist. Reut's watchlist is heavy on Hormozi/GaryVee, so a raw pull returns generic guru motivation she could find herself by manual search. She was disappointed by exactly that.

**Why:** the value is surfacing non-obvious, high-performing, Lior-executable ideas — not echoing famous accounts.

**How to apply (default filters for any Lior video-idea pull):**
1. **Outlier ≥ 3x.** A big account doing big numbers (e.g. Hormozi at 1.2x) teaches nothing. High outlier (8x–500x) = the idea overperformed, which is the gold. Kill low-outlier even if view count is huge.
2. **Replicability — solo only.** Cut anything needing a live audience/stage, a second person/interview, or production crew. Keep: solo-to-camera, voiceover+B-roll, simple talking-head.
3. **Source diversity.** Max one video per creator. Go beyond watchlist — use `search_all_videos` and `discover_channels` to find fresh voices (e.g. ericcrackschina sourcing, starterstory, bootstrapped-SaaS founders).
4. **Lior-fit = real asset, not generic wisdom.** Must map to his actual edge: ecommerce/dropshipping/sourcing mechanics, M&A (he bought DSMTool/Salefreaks/Yaballe and was in deal rooms), 0→1 build, focus, "I live on 200". See [[lior-pozin-os]], [[lior-brand-pillars-2026]].
5. **Kill banality.** Any motivational line Reut could write herself = out.

Be transparent about proof level: group ideas by outlier strength and state what you killed and why, so she can trust the filter. The strongest ideas are ones only Lior can make (his sourcing expertise, his M&A both-sides-of-the-table story) — lead with those. Stage/audience formats are always out (she called this out explicitly).

**Two failure modes to avoid (both happened, both rejected):**
- **Too generic:** echoing watchlist guru advice (Hormozi/GaryVee focus/risk/charge-more). She can find this herself.
- **Too gimmicky:** comedy skits, AI image trend-jacks ("Humans vs AI", Messi-mode prompts), boss-employee memes. These go viral but are OFF-BRAND for Lior — he's a serious B2B SaaS CEO building a focused-operator personal brand, not a comedy/corporate-humor account. Don't suggest skit/meme/trend-jack formats for him.

**The real fix = calibrate on her taste with examples, don't keep guessing filters.** When a Lior video-idea request feels open-ended, ASK her for 3-5 reference videos she'd want Lior to make (or 2-3 target creators), then reverse-engineer the pattern. Abstract filters alone can't capture her quality bar. Also ask the 4-line brief: goal (reach vs authority), tone (funny allowed or serious only), pillar, and hard-no's.
