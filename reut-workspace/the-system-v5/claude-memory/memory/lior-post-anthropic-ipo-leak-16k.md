Published 2026-09-29 (day 1 of the leak wave). ~16K impressions by 2026-09-30, Reut: "עבד מעולה".

**Shape that worked:**
1. Hook with the news verb in caps: "Anthropic $2T IPO just LEAKED:" + line-2 parenthetical "(The numbers are wild)". Reut insisted on "leaked"; X post with LEAKED had 590K views.
2. Stacked data block, 4 ➤ lines with before→after comparisons, emphasis marks (!) (!!) on the 2 shocking ones (Reut's choice).
3. Punch pivot: "2 customers." / "And investors are still lining up."
4. "3 lessons for every founder:" each = short title + 1-2 lines, lesson 3 carries the quotable: "The more a customer pays you, the more it pays them to replace you."
5. Open question + "P.S. Who do you think the two customers are?" as a guessing-game comment engine.
6. Visual: dense Claude-palette infographic (Astra) signed "Lior Pozin", plus Telegraph headline screenshot.

**What Reut rejected on the way:** news-anchor register; Lior's own billing/cost switching story ("not interesting for Lior"); the accounting-charge explainer as a lesson; depressing CTA questions.

**Final read for the September review (2026-10-01):** 17,338 impressions in day 1, 110
engagements, 33 comments, **8 followers**, 4 saves.

**LEAKED pattern, corrected 2026-10-01 after the X data:** on LinkedIn the hook works when the
leak is REAL news (Anthropic IPO, 17K day 1, 8 followers). On X, the Reddit-"LEAKED" framing hit
once (2026-08-19, 219,123 imp but only 51 followers) and then flopped twice (09-05 1,196; 09-12
2,176). So: a real leak = reach play from the 10% budget; a manufactured "Reddit leaked" hook is
not a repeatable format. Either way it buys reach, not followers; follow it with an artifact slot.

**Why it matters:** a reach-budget newsjack that cleared 10K where the watermark newsjack did ~7K; data + founder lesson beats pure human reaction for reach. Verify every figure (the $8.31B came from Wall St Engine after 3 sources lacked it).
