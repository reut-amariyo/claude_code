When Reut asks for hooks or opening lines for posts, suggest from this list:

1. **"Don't use Claude until you read this"** — strong curiosity + authority hook

2. **"The only instruction I gave Fiverr Labs, our innovation team, was simple: don't ask me for permission."** — delegation/trust + insider scene. Pillar 1 + Pillar 3 fuel.

3. **"There's this thing that happens when you build a company."** — open-loop curiosity, conversational entry into a build-in-public lesson.

4. **"The best thing you can do for your work today is cancel your next meeting."** — contrarian productivity-by-subtraction. Pillar 3 (focus).

5. **"I just lived MY DREAM TRIP"** — personal/big-moment hook. Reut flagged this as a strong fit for a Reel/short-form video (reel hook), not necessarily LinkedIn text.

6. **Expectation vs. reality (CEO morning routine):** "CEO morning routine, expectations: [polished 5am-run-green-smoothie version]. CEO morning routine, reality: [six hours sleep, two cigarettes + a double espresso that spilled on me]." — full expectation/reality contrast structure. Reut called the executed version "a perfect post." Self-deprecating, anti-LinkedIn-guru, very on-voice. Reusable template for any "expectations vs reality" founder topic.

7. **"This month is insane for programming:"** — in-the-moment emotional claim + concrete domain. Reut flagged as a good hook 2026-07-09.

8. **"grok 4.5 made me give grok build a serious run today / here's my honest first impression"** — personal action just taken + promise of an honest verdict. Reut flagged as a good hook 2026-07-09.

9. **"I can finally share that [company name]..."** — suppressed-secret announcement hook, proven by Maor Shlomo (Base44 CEO, sold to Wix) on his LinkedIn. Reut flagged 2026-07-16 for the AutoDS-in-Claude-connectors post. The "finally" implies a held secret, so use only when the news genuinely took time behind the scenes; on Lior's page fold a concrete anchor into the same line (e.g. "I can finally share this: AutoDS is inside Claude.").

10. **"I just did a 1-hour masterclass on national TV! / But you don't have 1 hour so... 😊 / My LinkedIn friend, I've summarized the value."** — appearance-flex + time-respect + summarized-value promise. Reut flagged 2026-08-04 with a SPECIFIC TRIGGER: proactively suggest this hook (adapted) next time Lior is interviewed on a podcast we want to publish/promote. Adapt the frame to the actual appearance (podcast episode, not national TV) and platform (drop "My LinkedIn friend" off-LinkedIn). The mechanic: big-appearance proof in line 1, acknowledge the reader's time, promise the condensed value.

11. **"You are going to die."** — mortality shock hook, proven by Matt Gray (296 likes, 102 comments). Reut flagged 2026-08-09 and said the hook can be COPIED VERBATIM next time she asks for a post. Second person, 5 words, provocative universal truth. The mechanic has two mandatory beats: the shock line, then immediately acknowledging the shock ("I know that's a hell of a way to start a post. But sit with it"). Full post structure logged in the-system-v5/M-memory/learning-log.md (2026-08-09 entry). Caveat for Lior: the source post is Motivator-archetype; on Lior's page the body must bridge to a founder/operator story fast, never stay in generic-inspiration land.

12. **"Most leadership books tell you to wake up at 5:00 AM. / That's actually when I go to sleep."** — cliché-inversion two-beat hook, flagged by Reut 2026-08-09 from a 352-like CEO sleep-schedule post. Beat 1 quotes the guru advice with a concrete number, beat 2 flips it personally while REUSING THE SAME ANCHOR (5 AM serves both beats). That reuse is the mechanic to copy. Template: "Most [authority genre] tell you to [common advice with number]. That's actually when/what I [do the opposite]." Same anti-guru family as hook #6 (CEO morning routine expectation vs reality). Built-in "vs." satisfied in line 1. The body must then justify the inversion with operator payoff, never quirk-flexing. Full teardown in the-system-v5/M-memory/learning-log.md (2026-08-09).

13. **"If I had to rebuild my _____... / I wouldn't change much. (just a few things)"** — fill-in-blank rebuild hook, proposed by Reut 2026-08-10. Two-beat: hypothetical restart, then the humble twist that promises a short list. Best fit: milestone / lessons-listicle posts (Archetype 8 energy) where the body delivers the "few things". NOT for single-principle posts — the frame implies things he'd change, so it fights keep-one-habit content (rejected for the Jim Rohn "just ask" post for that reason).

14. **"My husband said five words to me that made my worldview flip on never missing a chance to see a loved one: “Measure in times, not years.”"** — the **intimate-source quote hook**. Reut flagged 2026-08-23 and asked that it be offered whenever she requests an alternative hook. For Lior the source becomes **his wife or his co-founder**.

**Template:** `[Named intimate person] said [odd number] words to me that [changed my view] on [specific belief]: "[the quotable]"`

**Why it works, five mechanics:**
- Borrowed authority from a *relationship*, not a guru. Nobody argues with someone's wife or co-founder.
- An odd, specific word count ("five words") — precision manufactures credibility and curiosity at once. Matches the odd-number rule.
- A stated transformation, so the reader knows the stakes before the payload.
- The colon delivers the quotable **immediately**. No withholding, no bait.
- It opens on the OTHER person, not on "I", which satisfies the I-hook rule elegantly. Rare and worth reusing for that alone.

**⚠️ One guardrail on the payload.** In the source example the quotable itself is "Measure in times, not years", which is an X-not-Y negation pair and therefore sits inside the banned contrast-template family. The hook SHAPE is clean. When building Lior's version, write the quoted line WITHOUT the not-Y construction. A quoted line from another person is softer than Lior's own prose, so this is a flag rather than a kill, but default to rebuilding it.

**Before using the co-founder variant:** verify who Lior's co-founder actually is against [[lior-pozin-os]]. Do not invent one.

**Register rule (2026-07-09):** Reut rejected a stat-headline hook ("4 frontier AI models shipped in 30 days.") in favor of #7/#8. For newsjack posts, prefer conversational, in-the-moment, reacting-right-now hooks over data-headline hooks. The stat goes in line 2-3, not line 1.

**Why:** Hooks Reut explicitly flagged as ones she loved (hooks #2-6 added 2026-06-03 from a batch she sent; #7-8 added 2026-07-09 from the Grok 4.5 post iteration). These are general favorites, NOT tied to one specific post.

Match the hook to the right format (e.g. #5 reads as a Reel hook; #6 is a full-post expectation/reality template).
