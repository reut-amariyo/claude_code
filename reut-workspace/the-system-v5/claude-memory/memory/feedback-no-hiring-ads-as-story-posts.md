# Never blend a founder vulnerability story with a hiring CTA

**Rule:** A post that opens with personal vulnerability or a founder-journey story must NOT pivot into a "we're hiring [Role X], link in comments" CTA. If a hire needs to be promoted, run it as a separate, expectations-managed hiring post — never as the back half of a story post.

**⚠️ LOOSENED 2026-05-31 — scope this to VULNERABILITY stories only.** The 2026-05-28 "Tough days for Israeli tech" post blended a market-empathy newsjack (the layoff wave) with an explicit "we're hiring, DMs open, I'll route your CV personally" CTA — and it was Lior's highest-*engagement* post of that week (13,256 imp, 232 likes, 1.95% eng rate). The difference: there the hiring CTA reads as **generosity** (offering help to people who were just laid off), not as bait after an emotional personal reveal. So:
- **Still banned:** hiring CTA after a *personal vulnerability* hook ("I got rejected", "I almost gave up", "it took me years") — the bait-and-switch logic below still holds.
- **Now allowed:** hiring/generosity CTA inside a *market-empathy newsjack* — see Archetype 9 in [lior-top-8-10k-playbook.md](lior-top-8-10k-playbook.md). Lead with empathy + dignity for those affected, THEN offer help.
- **Litmus test:** Is the CTA *taking* (recruiting off your emotional story) or *giving* (offering a lifeline to a hurting audience)? Giving is fine.

**Why:**
- **Bait & switch breaks trust.** The hook ("I got rejected. Over and over.") promises a personal/emotional reveal. Pivoting to a job listing makes the reader feel manipulated — they came for the story and got a recruiting ad. The 2026-04-28 "Director of Partnerships" post (~860 impressions / 35 likes / 1 comment at 18h) is the proof.
- **Violates THE REAL PERSON PRINCIPLE.** Lior's lane is founder journey, not AutoDS marketing. Hiring is recruiting marketing — the moment the post lands on a job CTA, it stops being a "real person on a real path" post and becomes channel content.
- **Closes the engagement gap.** Per `feedback-linkedin-engagement-gap.md`, a closed CTA ("If you've spent your career running partnerships... that's the seat. Link is in the comments.") leaves no room for the 99% of readers who aren't candidates to engage. They lurk, scroll past, no comments, algorithm flatlines.
- **Algorithm reads it as an ad.** "Link in the comments" + role title + company tag is a classic recruiting-post signature. LinkedIn suppresses these vs. organic content.
- **Shareability collapses.** Pure founder-journey posts get reshared because the lesson is universal. Hiring posts get reshared only by people in the company or in the candidate's exact niche — a much smaller pool.

**How to apply:**
- If a draft starts with "I got rejected" / "I almost gave up" / "It took me years to..." (any vulnerability hook), the close MUST be an open question or reflection — never a job listing.
- If a hire needs promotion, write a SEPARATE post that's openly framed as a hiring post. It will reach less but that's fine — it's a different job.
- The story half of the failed post is salvageable: the rejection → 200 creators arc is gold. Repackage it as a standalone founder post with an open ending like "What's the 'no' that built your business?" Then the hiring post can ride the same week's attention without contaminating the story.
- Red flag phrases that mean a story post is about to turn into a hiring post: "Which is why we're hiring..." / "...the person who'll shape what's next" / "Link is in the comments." If you see these in a draft after a vulnerability hook, split the post.

**Confirmed failure data point:** 2026-04-28 "I got rejected" / Director of Partnerships post — see `lior-performance-log.md` entry for that date. ~860 impressions at 18h, projecting well below the 2,500 🔴 floor at 7d.
