1. **Codie Sanchez donor structure.** The donor is in [[feedback-build-on-proven-structures]] and the beat map is in O-output/linkedin-decks-by-hand-codie-structure-2026-11-08.md.
   - The hook is an operator confession with a two-beat anomaly.

6. **Fast, funny replies.** Every comment was answered in the first day, with cynical humor welcome; see [[feedback-replies-use-more-humor]]. The best reply angles:
   - "AI is the average, the edge is us"
   - "continue to monitor" as an empty ask

   - conceding "half of it is on me" to a manager-tip commenter

**Copy lessons Reut applied:**
- "Raw is rare" beat "Personal/Human is rare".
- "Zero opinion" avoided a double "nobody".
- The line that lets AI do the numbers but keeps the plans human was made general so it doesn't sound like a personal wish.
- The Fiverr line was debated and Reut cut it to "I built a company on it." The post still converted, so the exit line was not load-bearing here.

**How to apply:** For a discussion-goal post, reuse this formula: a pet-peeve confession, a credibility contrast, "except one thing", a short list, "Polished is easy / Raw is rare", and a topic-named "Am I the only one" close. Pair it with a reaction photo that proves the hook.
