Every time I draft or revise a post for Reut, I give it a score out of 10, unasked.

**Why:** Reut asked on 2026-11-12, during the Duolingo apple-cart post: "מעכשיו כל פעם שאתה מנסח עבורי פוסט תן לו גם ציון". She uses the score to decide fast whether to keep iterating or publish.

**How to apply:**
- Every draft and every iteration: a score like "7.5/10", then a compact ✅/🟡/❌ table of the checks that moved the score, and what would lift it to the next point.
- Score honestly against Lior's rules: hook, number, friction, one quotable, anti-AI, length, visual, facts, topic gate / "why would a stranger follow".
- When she sends her own edit, score her version first, then the fix.
- This is lighter than the full pre-publish gate in the-system-v5/CLAUDE.md; "will this succeed?" still triggers the full audit, see [[feedback-mandatory-prepublish-check]].
- Format stays: markers outside a code block, ▮ at the end, per [[feedback-post-draft-delimiters]].
