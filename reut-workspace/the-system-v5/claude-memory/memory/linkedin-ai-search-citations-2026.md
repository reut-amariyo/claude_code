## The one number that matters for us

**75% of all LinkedIn citations in AI answers come from individual member profiles, not company
pages** (Meltwater, 2026). LinkedIn is the most-cited domain globally for professional queries
(Profound, 2026). Demonstrated expertise beats follower count in what gets cited.

This is the strongest external argument yet for the Lior personal brand over an AutoDS company page,
and it gives LinkedIn work a KPI that is not followers or impressions. See
[[feedback-lior-personal-brand-not-autods]] and [[feedback-linkedin-kpi-followers-not-impressions]].

## Articles are the missing format

- **LinkedIn articles carry ~60% of LinkedIn's AI citations; posts carry ~40%.** Lior publishes
  posts only. The article layer does not exist yet, and it is where the citations live.
- Article spec: 800-1,200 words, executive summary at the top, clear subheadings phrased as the
  questions the audience actually asks. This is the "depth layer".
- Post spec: 200-300 words, primary keyword in the opening line, simple Q&A structure extracts
  best. This is the "distribution layer".
- Working unit: **one anchor article broken into 3-5 supporting posts**, each carrying one idea or
  data point from the article.
- Own **3-4 core topics** and hit them from many angles in plain language. Consistency across
  angles is what builds topical authority.
- Beyond structure, citable content needs a perspective only that author could have written.

## Measurement

Three layers: leading indicators (impressions, reactions, comments, shares), outcome metrics
(citation count, share of voice, citation rank, sentiment), diagnostic patterns (which format,
which author, which query type is underperforming).

**Timing: median 6.8 days for ChatGPT/Claude to cite a new page, 37 days at the 90th percentile.
Judge nothing before 30 days.**

## Market context (their cited figures, not ours)

94% of B2B decision-makers already use AI search (6sense). Gartner projects traditional search
volume down 50% by 2028. LinkedIn's own marketing sites saw up to 60% traffic loss on non-branded
keywords. 90% of C-suite prioritize AI search visibility but only 34% feel ready to execute,
51% blaming knowledge gaps (Forrester).
