When describing what AutoDS IS, the category word is **e-commerce** (Hebrew: איקומרס). Never "dropshipping platform", never "פלטפורמת דרופשיפינג", and no substitute category word either. Applies to podcast intros, bios, press, decks, posts, replies.

**Why:** Reut, 2026-08-25, while editing the "99 זה לא 100" podcast intro. Dropshipping carries a stigma in Israeli media and boxes AutoDS into a niche below what it actually is.

**How to apply:** "AutoDS, פלטפורמת אוטומציה לאיקומרס" / "AutoDS, the e-commerce automation platform". Users of the platform are "סוחרים" / "sellers", not "דרופשיפרים". The word dropshipping may still appear when quoting Lior's own origin story or when a host asks about it directly, but never as the label for the company. Note the canonical bio doc [[lior-pozin-os]] and autods.com still use "dropshippers" in their stats lines - reword those before use.
