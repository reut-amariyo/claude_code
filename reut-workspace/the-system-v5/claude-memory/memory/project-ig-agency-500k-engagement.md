An external agency runs Lior's **Instagram** account against a 500,000-follower target.
Confirmed at the weekly of **2026-09-02** (Plaud, 2h44m). Extends
[[project-agency-produces-content-i-learn]] from X to IG.

**The math they are working to:**
- Baseline at engagement start: **100,601**. On 2026-09-02: **~105,000**. Target **500,000**.
- Actual pace, 3-month average: **~10,000/month**. Required: **25,000/month**, a 2.5x.
- Monthly quota: **20 carousels, 31 videos, 9 collabs**, plus ~10 original automations (ebooks).
- Daily: 2 original pieces + **10 trial reels/day**, deliberately maxing the trial-reels ceiling.
- One post out-earned the other 113 combined. Both sides accept the plan rides on gold posts,
  which matches the July finding that the AGE 14 carousel drove 57% of the month's growth.

**Facebook broke this month:** reach down ~97%, ~100K views gone, most likely because the FB page
was disconnected from Instagram so a professional ad account could run Build Your Store. Lior says
the personal page cannot be monetised or run ManyChat so it is worth little; Reut says it
compounded reach while connected. Unresolved. Ideas on the table: reconnect via the new
"Lior Pozin Stores Builder" page, or open a deliberately scrappy second page that reposts viral
founder content.

**Four things nobody decided, so they are mine to raise:**
1. Reels will now carry the $92M number, which until now lived only in carousels. Allowed by
   [[lior-exit-amount-92m-vs-sec-filing]] as a reported figure, but at 31 videos/month it becomes
   the account's headline claim; the wording deserves one deliberate decision.
2. Fabrication was proposed three times on tape and never refused: inventing e-commerce proof,
   "fabricating a bit" on the 0-to-exit money graph, and a made-up Forbes story. Separately Lior
   offered, as content, asking friends to leave reviews for a product they never used.
3. The prison/detention story: he wants PR first, the agency wants it from him personally.
   Reut's point that an article burns the card was the strongest one and nobody answered it.
4. Headcount: Lior said the **200** figure "may have dropped a lot" since support moved to agents
   and Intercom. Do not use 200 or any replacement number publicly until someone confirms it.
   See [[feedback-stop-overusing-250-employees]].

Also stated and unverified: influencer/partnership spend "over $10M a year", acquisitions in the
multi-millions. Do not repeat publicly without a source.

Dubai shoot lands 27th of the month, Lior there 24-28 with Galit. Drone needs a UAE permit check.
