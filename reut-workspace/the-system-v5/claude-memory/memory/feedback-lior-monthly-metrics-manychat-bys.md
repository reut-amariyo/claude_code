Reut, 2026-08-23: two business metrics matter to Lior and must appear in **every**
monthly summary and in **every** data pack prepared for him, alongside the reach and
follower numbers:

1. **New people who joined the ManyChat flow that month** (the comment-gated DM
   automation, e.g. the "Comment CEO" post mechanic).
2. **Build Your Store trials that month** (@buildyourstoreai).

where audience turns into business, so they are what makes the brand work legible to
Lior. He asked for them specifically.

**How to apply:** treat them as standing columns, not an extra. Add them to the monthly
review deck, to any spreadsheet built for Lior, and to any ad-hoc "give me the numbers"
request. If a month's figure is unavailable, show the row with the gap named rather than
dropping the row.
