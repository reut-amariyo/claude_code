Standing rule from Reut (2026-09-06), applies EVERY time she asks for a comment on a post
on Lior's behalf, even when she doesn't repeat it:

1. She attaches a reference comment that already performed well on that post or a similar one.
   Use its INTENT, STRUCTURE and WAY OF LOOKING at the content. Never copy its wording,
   its content, or its examples. She says this explicitly: "לא להעתיק (בכלל!)".
2. Draft only. Never post. She comments manually. See [[feedback-linkedin-comments-manual-posting]].
3. Keep it tight, 2 lines max, the quotable IS the comment. See [[feedback-linkedin-comments-keep-short]].
4. Plain copy-paste text, no blockquotes. See [[feedback-reply-formatting]].
5. Deliver 2-3 variants so she can pick, not one.

What "spirit of the reference" has meant so far:
- Dan Martell AI-maturity post → calm principle-level extension of the author's framework,
  forward-looking, generous, no war story.
- Alex Hormozi $100K/day post → sharp reframe of the premise + one concrete closing image.
- Tony Robbins post → reflective, second-person-inclusive, open question inside the comment.

**Why:** she is choosing the register of the comment through the reference, not the topic.
Reading the reference as a content template produces a copy; reading it as a posture produces
what she actually asked for.

**How to apply:** before drafting, name to yourself what the reference is DOING (reframe /
extend / question / image) and reproduce that move with Lior's own material and voice rules.
Anti-AI rules still bind, including the ban on "not X, it's Y" even when the reference uses it.
See [[feedback-anti-ai-writing-rules]] and [[feedback-replies-engage-post-first]].
