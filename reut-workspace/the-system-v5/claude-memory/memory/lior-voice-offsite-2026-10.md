Third unscripted Lior source, the first one internal: Lior speaking to about 20 AutoDS managers at the
2026-10-29 offsite. Transcript quality is low, so the lines are paraphrase. Detail is in
`the-system-v5/B-brain/meetings/2026-10-28-29-management-offsite.md`.

**Signatures, consistent with [[lior-spoken-voice-dna-e97]] and [[lior-spoken-voice-dna-hebrew-99-lo-100]]:**
1. **Flat verdict, then the reason.** "I don't agree. We never checked." "I think it's bad, it's really bad." No softeners.
2. **Concedes fast and in public.** When his head of product pushed back: "I get it. I agree. I agree with you." No defensiveness. That is the CEO-reputation gold.
3. **Says "I don't know" without drama.** "I don't know what will happen in 28." "Guys, I don't know the answer."
4. **Risk as balance plus a number, never fear.** "They will not decrease us to zero." Even at a lower payout, more links keep the funnel whole.
5. **Preference stated with its cost in the same breath.** "Even if we were losing money, some bugs, it was a mess... I prefer this way than holding another month."
6. **Gives power away explicitly.** "I do have ideas, many of them, but I intentionally keep it for you." "I want to hear ideas from you, and from your teams."
7. **Plain metaphor, then plain restatement.** "The captain is here. He just wants other chefs to also cook."
8. **Contrast images from bootstrap life.** "Two guys sitting in their parents' apartment" vs a company of hundreds.

**Why:** this is the register for any employee-facing Lior text and for leadership posts. It is more
direct and more conceding than his podcast voice.

**How to apply:** for internal Slack updates or leadership posts, use #1, #2 and #6. A Lior line that
argues back or defends himself is off-voice. A line where he concedes and then holds the direction is on-voice.
