# Podcast Vetting Checklist

Before adding any podcast to Lior's "To Pitch" list, verify ALL THREE filters below. Reut has caught all three failure modes multiple times.

---

## Filter 1 — Published in 2026

**Rule:** Every podcast on the list must have published at least one episode in 2026.

**Why:** Reut caught TechTO Together, Float or Founder, The Business Gay, and The Business Leadership Podcast as dead. Dead podcasts waste pitch time and look bad to hosts.

**How to apply:**
- WebFetch the Apple Podcasts or Spotify page directly. Do NOT rely on WebSearch result snippets — they've returned wrong dates (showing "2026" when actual was 2025).
- If most recent episode is pre-2026, mark ⚫ "Not active" and exclude.
- For borderline cases (Dec 2025 + silent since), flag to Reut and ask.

---

## Filter 2 — Non-Canadian / international guests

**Rule:** Lior is Israeli. AutoDS was built in Israel. Verify the show features non-Canadian (or explicitly international) guests before adding.

**Why:** Reut caught Canada's Entrepreneur ("Canada's #1 Podcast for Entrepreneurs by Entrepreneurs" — all Canadian) and Yes, We Are Open (Canadian small businesses only). Pitching an Israeli founder to a Canada-only show wastes time and signals weak research.

**How to apply:**
- Check recent guest list for non-Canadian/international founders.
- Read the about page / tagline. If it says "Canadian entrepreneurs" or "Canadian small businesses" → hard no.
- For Canadian-focused shows that occasionally host internationals → flag to Reut, don't auto-include.
- Strong fits: business/leadership/tech podcasts with international lineups, shows that welcome "world's top business leaders".
- Borderline: Canadian tech podcasts (BetaKit) — require a Canadian angle (customers, investors, partnerships) to pitch.

**Examples:**
- ❌ Canada's Entrepreneur, Yes We Are Open
- ⚠️ BetaKit (needs Canadian angle)
- ✅ Startup Talk, The Business Leadership Podcast, Taking Stock with Amanda Lang

---

## Filter 3 — Actually about founder/entrepreneurship, not generic "business"

**Rule:** Verify the show is about **entrepreneurship / founder journeys / scaling a company** — not just "business" (which can mean stocks, macro, politics, pop culture).

**Why:** Reut caught two mistakes:
1. The Ben Mulroney Show — "business" in description, actually politics/pop culture.
2. Taking Stock with Amanda Lang — "business news", actually markets/stocks/macro. Lior is a bootstrapped founder, not a public-market CEO. Wrong audience.

**How to apply:**
- Read 2-3 recent episode titles before adding.
- Ask: does this show interview founders about *building* a company? Or does it cover markets/politics/commentary?
- Good fit: "How I built X", "Scaling from 0 to Y", founder origin stories, bootstrap lessons, exit stories.
- Bad fit: stock analysis, economic commentary, political takes, pop culture, news recap, generic "leadership" with no founder focus.
- When in doubt: send Reut 3 most recent episode titles and let her decide.

**Examples:**
- ❌ The Ben Mulroney Show (politics/pop culture)
- ❌ Taking Stock with Amanda Lang (markets/macro)
- ✅ Startup Talk (Craig Major) — founder stories
- ✅ BetaKit Podcast — Canadian tech founder interviews

---

## Order of operations

When researching a new podcast, run filters in order — fail-fast saves time:
1. Filter 1 (active in 2026?) — kill quickly with one WebFetch
2. Filter 3 (founder/entrepreneurship?) — read 2-3 episode titles
3. Filter 2 (international guests?) — check guest list

Only podcasts passing all three go on the "To Pitch" list.
