Verified 2026-08-26 against Fiverr's Form 20-F for FY2024 (Note 3, SEC EDGAR):

- Total consideration transferred for AutoDS at acquisition: **$55.658M**
  (cash $39.339M + Earn-out fair value $14.116M + accrued payment $1.556M + unvested options $0.647M)
- Earn-out: **up to $36M** in cash and shares over 3 years, subject to financial targets. Fair value at closing $14.116M.
- Separately, **$12.168M** hold-back to certain AutoDS employees, released over 3 years subject to continued employment. Booked as compensation expense, NOT purchase price.
- Fiverr's own press release did not disclose a price. Hebrew press (Calcalist, Geektime) did not either. Forbes Israel says "hundreds of millions of shekels".
- The **$92M** figure comes from US media (Entrepreneur, 2025-11-13). It most likely equals 55.658 + 36 = 91.658, which double-counts the earn-out, since the earn-out is already inside the $55.658M at fair value.

**Why:** $92M is fine as a media-sourced claim in Lior's own brand content, and [[project-lior-1billion-summit-pitch]] already treats it as publicly usable. It is NOT safe wherever someone will check the filing: Wikipedia, journalists, investors, due diligence, analyst-facing decks.

**2026-09-02 update:** the IG agency found that almost every top-5 follower post carries the 92 number, and plans to push it into reels for the first time, at 31 videos/month. That is inside the rule below, but it turns the figure into the account's headline claim, so decide the reel wording once rather than by drift. See [[project-ig-agency-500k-engagement]].

**How to apply:** In brand/social content, keep using $92M as reported. In anything fact-checkable, write it as attributed ("reported at $92M") or use the filing numbers. Never state $92M as the audited price. Related: [[lior-pozin-os]], [[project-lior-wikipedia-entry]].
