From the IG weekly of **2026-09-02**, where Lior pulled the top posts by follower gain live.

**Format roles, now settled:**
- **Reels = reach.** Views, not followers.
- **Carousels and posts = followers and engagement.** This is the growth engine.
- **Stories = a direct sales tool**, not a diary. 2-3 frames maximum, minimal text on a black
  background, always a CTA plus a ManyChat automation plus a DM. Nobody had run stories until
  the day before this meeting, so there is no performance history yet.
- Every carousel from now on carries an automation.

**The three proven follower engines. Lior wants ten.**
1. **AGE 14** — childhood photos, 0-to-100 arc. Number one by followers.
2. **The Fiverr pitch deck** — the exit-deck artifact.
3. **The startup-list carousel** — shared vocabulary the audience wants to take away.

What one and two share is a **first-party artifact plus an ungated takeaway**. That is the same
mechanism as [[lior-post-exit-deck-lead-magnet]] on LinkedIn. The pattern is now proven on both
platforms, so treat it as the cross-platform rule rather than a LinkedIn rule.

**The $92M number appears in almost every top-5 follower post** and has barely been used in reels,
because for a long stretch there were restrictions on stating it. That is the biggest untapped
lever on the account. See [[lior-exit-amount-92m-vs-sec-filing]] for where it is safe.

**Talking-head video:**
- Black background, minimal captions, direct to camera performed best of all his talking video.
- **Cap at ~40 seconds.** He speaks slowly; 90-second and 2-minute cuts kill watch-through.
  More short messages beat stretching one.
- Editing note: he draws out the connector before a sentence ("And I think that you..."). Silence
  between sentences is easy to cut, the stretched connector is not. Shortening that one word is
  what makes him editable.
- ⚠️ Unproven: the agency's editor claims "I" in a hook suppresses reach and prefers "how you can".
  Reut pushed back and the room agreed only to test it. Do not adopt as a rule.

**Reut's formula, her words:** what always works in social is a big promise with a small, simple
action. Where it fails is when the small action gets complicated. Matches
[[reference-tom-orbach-humor-playbook]].

**Collabs framework:** one influencer per month with three pieces built around them, starting with
AC; three collabs a month with AutoDS and Build Your Store so the two audiences finally connect;
content produced for Galit's own page specifically to reach the female audience the account lacks.
Evidence it works: a story Mark reshared reached 27-28K views.
