Reut's proven closing question: "Would you bootstrap today, or raise?" (bootstrap sorry/not-sorry post, 18K).
On 2026-10-27, for the IKEA/AI-layoffs post, she rejected open, two-part and "share your experience" questions, plus a hypothetical "what role would you want next", and pointed to that one as the model.

**Why:** a binary choice is answerable in one word, both sides are defensible so it starts a fight, and people naturally add "X, because..." Two-part or experience questions are too much work.

**How to apply:** under 8 words, "Would you..." or "X or Y?", personal and present tense, both options legit, about the post's core tension. Only add a question when she asks for one, see [[feedback-no-closing-question-default]]. Lists of options don't need a separate experience prompt.

Update same day: after seeing binary options for the IKEA post she asked for the opposite - "have you experienced an AI layoff? what do you think, who's responsible, employer or employee?" So on societal-debate topics, use an easy yes/no experience opener plus one opinion prompt, max 2 short sentences, and always name AI. Binary stays the default for founder-choice topics.
