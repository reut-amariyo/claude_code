From "99 Is Not 100" (Hebrew podcast about ex-USSR immigrants, host Alex Rif; episode "כן אמא, אני עדיין יכול להיות מתכנת", https://open.spotify.com/episode/5rFy8iBmw6P3N6N28IhlvW; Lior is its first Israel-born guest, recorded after June 2026, analyzed 2026-09-15). Everything below is translated from Hebrew: close paraphrase, never quote as verbatim English. Full analysis: `the-system-v5/M-memory/lior-spoken-voice-99-lo-100-podcast-analysis-2026-09-15.md`. Transcripts (he + en, agency-ready): `the-system-v5/O-output/podcast-transcripts/99-lo-100-lior-pozin-2026-09-transcript-*.md`.

**New public facts, from his mouth:** dream = billionaire by 45 (impact + the person he must become; "a million lives may not be enough, I need 100 million"). 10 hours a week on personal development, before and after the exit (E97 said 3+, newer wins). "Nobody is born a CEO." "I really believe in no: our lives are made of many no's, and it's our choice whether I take the no or make it a yes." He listens to books, cannot sit and read; last one The Science of Scaling (unreasonable size goal + unreasonable deadline). Family is Ukrainian. First eBay profit $30 on a Chinese phone at 14 (OS said $40; prefer $30). First shekel at 12 fixing neighbours' PCs, ~100 shekels.

**Story bank (each with a number):**
1. Dad's bribe (one more year of Russian evening school = own computer) → Dad's password as punishment → he learned to hack it → Dad removed the power cable → he kept a spare. "Part of my game." Origin of hacking/security.
2. Judo third place; parents praised the kid in first. Basketball, same. Engine for life: "if he can, so can I", later aimed at older, funded AutoDS competitors.
3. Contrarian kid at the religious "Russians' school" in Beer Sheva: kippah, seat, music lessons, all refused. "Don't have it? I'll have it. He's better? No, I'm better."
4. Mom, last Saturday: with thousands of customers she asked if he could still be a programmer; this week she asked if his co-founder, the CTO, "can still find a job as a programmer".
5. Expelled from school (public copy says AT 14, Reut 2026-09-20; do not correct "14" to "10th grade"), managed at the second school; school forum, freelance sites, e-cigarette stock at home; parents "gave up".
6. Army: landed in an IT technician team, refused to work until moved, self-taught the course, demanded a test, became team leader. "Nobody came to me and said prove yourself. I said it." Unit name in transcript is unverified, never publish.
7. Everyone in his unit went to cyber; he built a system to run 100 eBay stores for himself; manual sellers asked for it; AutoDS.
8. First hires: a developer in Armenia because cheap; then a high-schooler working 24/7.
9. $1 bug, fullest version: see [[lior-story-dollar-bug]] (updated with the 3 AM "congratulations" beat, hundreds of products at $40-50 avg, $200→$400 salary plan, Head of Support with ~100 people, competitor's 20% poach offer, nobody moved).
10. Openness as management: "most open in the world, for better and worse"; expectations set in the first interview; "I give you the space: run, fall, get up, fail. But I always tell the truth."
11. Mindset yes, skillset no: told his coach the day before that he is "already there in mindset, not yet in skillset", drowning in projects taken on to reach the goal. Only vulnerability register he uses: named skill gap + plan.
12. Advisors from day one, a high share of expenses, rotated as he outgrew them, domain-specific down to "paid ads in one channel"; many are coffee, not retainers; "in Israel people say yes".
13. Bootstrapped by ignorance (did not know raising existed), later by choice ("not my style, no time for decks"). AI era: less money to build, more creativity to win; deep tech/medical/hardware need capital, SaaS does not. Solve your own pain or a daily-contact's pain.
14. Three languages, fighting his English accent because it mixes all three; gave a talk to new immigrants translating himself three ways.
15. Freedom is the value; money was for a skateboard and trips, never the goal.
16. Sister, an immigrant, sent him to the podcast.
17. Kids: no direction pushed ("obviously entrepreneurship", kidding), but be the best at whatever you choose.

**Deltas vs canon:** aliyah 1990 on tape vs 1989 in OS (born 1995, "five years after"): do not publish a year. Host says 250 employees and $92M / 23 days; those are her lines, public canon stays 200. Guardrail: he says "no options, no percentage" about the first hire; the no-equity rule for drafts still stands.
