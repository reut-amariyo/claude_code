Conference/event recap posts for Lior must include a concrete AutoDS-Operator moment, not just observations from the stage. Pure recap content flatlines on LinkedIn (real case: LangTalks AI Engineering recap got 400 impressions).

**Why:** Lior's archetype is The Operator (70%). When he posts pure curator/educator content (recapping someone else's talks, praising someone else's tool without testing it), the audience that follows him for AutoDS-scale operator lessons disengages. The Real Person Principle gets broken — he didn't *do* anything in the post, he just *attended*. No personal stake, no number he tracked, no team moment, no production decision.

**How to apply** before drafting any conference/event/wellness/external-event post:

1. **Operator-stake test:** Does the post include a concrete AutoDS moment Lior experienced, not just stage observations? If no, kill or rewrite.
2. **Brand pillar check:** Does it hit at least one of the 3 pillars (200% intensity / 0-to-1 / focus)? Pure recap usually hits zero. If zero, kill.
3. **Friction requirement:** Wellness/retreat/conference posts need a founder bridge with friction (per existing rule). Praise-only posts ceiling at ~1K, often lower.
4. **Anti-AI scan:** Conference recap posts are extra prone to the "it's not X, it's Y" / "stopped being X. Became Y." watermark. Scan twice.
5. **Third-party product mentions:** Never promote another person's tool/demo without an Operator verdict ("we tested it at AutoDS and here's what broke / worked"). Pure praise reads like a sponsored shoutout.
6. **CTA test:** Don't ask "which shift hits you hardest?" style questions that force re-reading. Use a one-breath answerable open question.

**Pattern that worked vs. didn't:**
- ❌ "I spent yesterday at [Conference]. Here are the shifts I walked away with: [3 observations]" → 400 impressions
- ✅ Recap frame + ONE shift tied to a specific AutoDS production moment, with a number or named incident → earns engagement
