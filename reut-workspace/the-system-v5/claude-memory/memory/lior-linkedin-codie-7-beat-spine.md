Reut, 2026-09-22, after I scanned Codie Sanchez's profile (43 posts, ~6 weeks, 607,916 followers,
median 1,807 reactions / 377 comments): "תלמד מזה כשאתה כותב פוסטים ללינקדין."
Full teardown: the-system-v5/M-memory/linkedin-codie-sanchez-teardown-2026-09-22.md

**Why:** she runs one skeleton at ~7 posts/week and it holds across every topic. It is the most
repeatable donor structure in the bank, and three of its rules contradict assumptions I was drafting
on (that short wins, that a link is cheap, that the quotable is a definition).

**How to apply — the default spine for a Lior teaching post:**

1. Hook under 12 words. An inversion, a status loss, or a hard number.
2. A 3-4 item **negative** bulleted list immediately after. No transition sentence. Teach the right
   thing by listing the wrong thing. Odd or even follows the real content, 4 is fine here.
3. An outside example the reader already knows, BEFORE any Lior receipt. Codie uses Google ping-pong
   videos, Jordan, Goggins, a viral small-town barber. The Lior equivalent is a named company or a
   public moment, never AutoDS first.
4. The turn on its own line. One word ("How?") or "Here's the kicker".
5. Lior's operator receipt, with one number. One only, per [[feedback-stop-overusing-social-proof]].
6. **One quotable that reverses the reader's excuse.** This is the gate. If the line defines a concept
   instead of turning the reader's own justification against them, the draft is not ready.
   Her shape: "being indispensable is just a nicer way to say you're trapped."
7. `↓↓↓` as a visible fence, then the artifact link. The fence is what lets a post carry a CTA without
   reading as an ad.

**Rules the data forced, which override older assumptions:**
- **Length is not the constraint.** Her 2,000+ char posts (median 1,854) beat her sub-400 posts
  (median 1,254). Hook shape drives the number, not word count. Stop trimming a good post to hit a
  character target; see [[linkedin-posting-rules]] 750-1,200, which stays a narrative-post rule only.
- **The link tax is ~3x.** Her 4 link-free posts median 4,945 reactions vs 1,607 for the 39 with a
  link. Only pay it when there is a real first-party artifact on the other end, per
  [[lior-post-exit-deck-lead-magnet]]. Never a link for a link's sake.
- **Re-run proven posts verbatim at 2-4 weeks.** She reposts unchanged and gets 38-51% of the original
  (1,836→929, 1,814→681). At Lior's follower count almost nobody saw round one.
- **Visual stays one artifact, one number, or a face.** Her only three families: black quote-card with
  avatar in the corner, a candid portrait unrelated to the topic, a 9:16 video with a burned-in
  question caption. Zero diagrams. Confirms
  [[feedback-linkedin-visual-one-artifact-not-diagram]].
- **Open with "I" only on a loss.** She never opens with "I" as a brag. Matches the existing hook rule.

**Hook families to pull from (with her results):** diagnosis by inversion "Here's how to lose good
employees:" 2,624 / direct address to a group "Dear chronic people pleasers," 4,158 / absurd
cause-and-effect "Doing your own laundry might be why your business isn't growing…" 3,704 / scary stat
2,548 / taxonomy with a question "There are 3 types of winners. Which one are you?" 2,535 /
status-loss confession "My coworker got the promotion over me." 1,836 / inherited story "Story from my
dad today." 2,728.

**Never copy from her:** profanity, a link in every post, Main Street populism and Google-as-villain
framing, constant scale-flexing ("9 figures", "22 businesses") which breaks
[[feedback-lior-never-arrogant]], her 40-hashtag posts, and the 20-character identity post that only
works on a 600K parasocial base.
