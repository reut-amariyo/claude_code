**Why:** Caleb's rule is that you only talk publicly about what you did privately, and you pick a narrow "pond" first.

**Reut's audience decision, 2026-11-18, used in the 2027 IG strategy deck:**
- LinkedIn (the higher level), nested ladder: business people → founders, CEOs, senior managers → SaaS founders and CEOs → PUDDLE = the bootstrapped B2C SaaS founder (Reut, 2026-11-18).
- IG + TikTok (one level below, lighter content), nested ladder: people who want to make money → people who want to build a business ONLINE → first-timers + small business owners going online → PUDDLE = the first-timer building a first real online business, up to the first paying customers. Never define the viewer by "$10K a month", it pulls the make-money-online crowd. Bridge: "Instagram sees how he started, LinkedIn sees how he finished."
- The common thread is "people who build". Always describe a person, never a topic like "business".
- Lior hasn't confirmed it yet.

**How to apply:** Lior's biggest open item is the pond.
- Under the money test, Lior said "B2C SaaS founders".
- Reut leans toward entrepreneurs who dream of an exit, because that pond matches who buys BYS and the AI app.
