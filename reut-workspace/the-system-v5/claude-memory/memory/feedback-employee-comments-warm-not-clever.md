When an AutoDS employee reposts or praises Lior, his reply is WARM and human: name them, mark the years, thank them, elevate their journey. Not a clever reframe, not a quotable line, not a lesson pulled back to his own rules.

Reut, 2026-09-09, on Alae Ben Azzouz's 7.5-year repost of the "janitor" post: my first drafts ("rule 3 is easy to write on LinkedIn, you spent 7.5 years proving it works") were called "ממש גרועות" because they made a colleague's tribute into a stage for Lior's content. She pointed at Ofir Bokobza's comment as the correct posture: disbelief at how fast the time went, thanks, "your journey is an inspiration".

**Why:** the one-cutting-line rule in [[feedback-linkedin-comments-keep-short.md]] is for public/stranger threads where the comment competes for attention. Inside the company the comment is a relationship, and cleverness reads as cold.

**How to apply:** 2-3 warm lines. Anchor on the person's tenure or specific role growth, thank them plainly, and if a brand point comes in, it comes in as credit to them, never as a restated rule. Follow [[feedback-comment-reference-spirit-not-copy.md]] against the partner's comment already on the thread so Lior does not repeat its beats. Anti-AI rules still hold. Note the spelling on LinkedIn is Ofir Bokobza.
