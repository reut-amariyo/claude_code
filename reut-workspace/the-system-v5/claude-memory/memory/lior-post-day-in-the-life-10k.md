Lior LinkedIn post, reported by Reut 2026-09-29 as a hit: ~10K impressions.

Full text:

Day in the life of a 31-year-old exited founder running 200 people:
(Spoiler: no 5 a.m. alarm.)
8:30 - eyes open. Slack, then, unfortunately, Instagram.
8:45 - matcha and cuddles with Ray 🐈.
9:00 - gym. First thing I protect in my schedule.
10:15 - 12-minute walk to the office, sending voice notes to my AI agents.
10:45 - committing to 2-3 big things for the day. / Otherwise, the big things lose to the urgent ones. / My agent checks in on them 3 times a day.
11:00 to 18:00 - meetings. Every one recorded. / They all live in one AI brain that knows how I talk and think (using Plaud)
18:30 - home for dinner. Everything else can move.
19:30 - coach.
20:30 - laptop opens again.
23:00 - breathing exercises before sleep.
P.S. About 10 hours of my week go into working on myself. / Therapy, mentors, gym, learning.
It is the first thing most founders cut when the calendar fills up, and the last thing I will ever cut.
Your company can only grow as fast as you do. ▮

**Why it worked (beat map, reuse the skeleton, never the content):**
1. Hook = familiar format "Day in the life of" + identity stack of 3 concrete anchors: age 31, exited, 200 people.
2. Line-2 parenthetical counter-hook under 6 words, the ONE allowed parenthesis, kills the 5 a.m. cliché.
3. Timestamped rows: skimmable, dwell time, voyeur pull. Small human confessions ("unfortunately, Instagram", Ray the cat) make the status feel earned, not flexed.
4. The AI system is shown as life, never taught: voice notes to agents, the 2-3 big things agent, the Plaud brain. Builder proof without being a tool tip.
5. P.S. pivots the whole schedule into ONE idea: 10 h/week on himself, the thing founders cut first.
6. Quotable last line that reverses the reader's excuse: "Your company can only grow as fast as you do."
