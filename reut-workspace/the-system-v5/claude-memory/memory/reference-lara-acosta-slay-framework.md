Reut, 2026-10-14: "learn everything from this link for our LinkedIn posts" - the Literally Academy
Notion dashboard by Lara Acosta. Full teardown with the 8 annotated post breakdowns, the template
fit table and the conflicts: the-system-v5/M-memory/linkedin-lara-acosta-literally-academy-teardown-2026-10-14.md

**Why:** her course is built for beginners, but its mechanics hold at any size and they sharpen rules
we already run. SLAY is the first framework we have that explains WHY a story comes before advice:
ChatGPT can write the advice, it cannot write Lior's story.

**How to apply when drafting a Lior LinkedIn post:**
- **SLAY as a check, not a new skeleton.** Run it inside [[lior-linkedin-codie-7-beat-spine]]:
  Story = 2 lines max, bullet extra story beats instead of narrating them. Lesson = 1 line.
  Actionable = ONE sub-topic in depth, never the full list, so the reader gets something they can apply
  rather than something they already knew. You = close on the reader with a line that echoes the hook.
- **3-keyword hook test:** a teaching signal + the ICP named + a tension word. Example shape: "The #1
  reason founders stall at $1M ARR."
- **Name the rehook flavor on line 2:** contradict, which buys curiosity, or state the obvious, which buys
  agreement. Same as Rule F in [[lior-top-8-10k-playbook]].
- **One trade-secret line** a non-insider wouldn't know. It carries the authority.
- **Pick the story first, then the topic:** what do I want to teach, and which recent Lior receipt proves
  it? A recent win lends credibility to the advice that follows.
- **Line-level:** every line is a hook, a quick win per line, no "very", no over-explaining, solutions
  only, usable today.
- **Image = first hook.** A photo of the actual moment beats design. Consistent with
  [[feedback-linkedin-visual-one-artifact-not-diagram]].
- **P.S. question allowed** if it's easy and on topic. Never "Repost ♻️", never "Agree?".
- **Templates:** only the receipt-driven ones pass [[feedback-topic-gate-only-lior-can-write]]:
  #52 #1-reason-X-fail, #57 60-second masterclass, #53 start-from-0, #58 overnight-success timeline,
  #16 no-experience-then-today, #24 milestone shoutout + lead magnet.
  BANNED for red-rule reasons: #2, #10, #14, #15, #19, #28, plus her "Most people / 99% of people" openers.
- **Her contrast quotables** ("not X, but Y") get rewritten as one declarative line, per
  [[feedback-anti-ai-writing-rules]].
- **Driving-story candidate:** Lior's dyslexia is literally her example of a driving story. It's fair game per
  [[feedback-lior-dyslexia-no-reading-claims]], but Reut approves the angle first.
- **Distribution ideas for [[project-foundera-linkedin-agency]]:** a 10x10x10 list of 10 leaders, 10 peers
  and 10 prospects. Growth needs more TOFU stories, conversion needs more BOFU how-tos.
