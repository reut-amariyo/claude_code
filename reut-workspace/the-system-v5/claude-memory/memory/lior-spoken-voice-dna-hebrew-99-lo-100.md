Second unscripted long-form source, first in Hebrew (analyzed 2026-09-15). Full analysis: `the-system-v5/M-memory/lior-spoken-voice-99-lo-100-podcast-analysis-2026-09-15.md`.

**Why:** the Hebrew is closer to the raw machine than his English. Every E97 signature reappears here (question-and-flat-answer chains, threes, flat punchline, undecorated wins, "not really" self-deflection, "I really believe in..."), so they are HIS, not the language's.

**How to apply, the 8 mechanics the Hebrew adds:**
1. After every abstract line, a plainer restatement with a number or object in it (his "זאת אומרת" engine, ~30 uses). Never leave an abstraction alone.
2. One line of reported speech per story, opened with "Listen," (his שומע / תקשיב). He plays people, he does not narrate them. One line, not two.
3. "In the end," once per post, on the quotable line (his "בסוף", ~15 uses, marks the sentence he wants remembered).
4. The biggest number goes in the aside, never the hook ("billionaire. By 45, by the way."). Line-2 parenthetical or a trailing "by the way".
5. One self-correction per post, stated flat, not apologised ("not necessarily the best book, but it is the last one"; "a salary I would not offer anyone today").
6. Humor only self-directed and cultural, dry ("like a good Soviet", "obviously entrepreneurship, kidding"). Never at the audience.
7. A story beat without a number is not finished: 12, 100 shekels, $30, 100 stores, $40-50 to $1, 3 AM, $200 to $400, 100 people, 20%, 10 hours, 45, 100M lives.
8. Vulnerability = a named skill gap plus a plan ("mindset yes, skillset not yet"). Never fear, never disbelief.

Strip when converting speech to text: כאילו / אה / נגיד / וזה / כזה. Keep: זאת אומרת (as restatement), בסוף, אגב, שומע.
