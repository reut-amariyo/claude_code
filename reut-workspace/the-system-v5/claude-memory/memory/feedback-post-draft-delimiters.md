When delivering any post, reply, or comment draft (LinkedIn, X, Bluesky, IG caption, anywhere), wrap the copyable text in explicit delimiter lines:

--- POST STARTS ---
(the draft, plain text)
--- POST ENDS ---

For replies/comments use --- REPLY STARTS --- / --- REPLY ENDS ---.

**Why:** Reut said (2026-07-08) she can't tell where a drafted post starts and ends when it's mixed into my prose. Reconfirmed 2026-08-03 for LinkedIn replies ("תמיד תעשה לי מאיפה התגובה מתחילה ואיפה נגמרת"). Reconfirmed AGAIN 2026-08-06 after several chat iterations were delivered bare ("תחזור לכתוב לי תחילת פוסט וסוף פוסט תמיד") — this applies to EVERY draft type, EVERY iteration in chat, always, without being asked. Do not drop the markers mid-conversation even when iterating rapidly on small edits.

**How to apply:** Markers on their own lines, nothing else on them. Still plain text inside, never blockquotes per [[feedback-reply-formatting]]. If delivering multiple drafts, wrap each one separately. Optional add-ons go inside the markers only if they're part of the recommended final text; otherwise present them as a separate marked block.
