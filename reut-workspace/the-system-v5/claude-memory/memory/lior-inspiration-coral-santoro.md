# Coral Santoro — Lior's inspiration figure (added 2026-06-10)

## Her core principles that overlap with Lior's lived experience
- **"Failure does not exist. It's just data."** → maps to Lior's eBay-ban-became-AutoDS origin and the $1 bug.
- **Discipline over motivation; "keep going even when it stops being exciting"; "be repetitive about the boring stuff."** → maps to 5 years no salary, 100+ YouTube videos himself.
- **"I don't believe in five-year plans"; vision stubborn, path flexible.** → maps directly to Lior's never-plan-more-than-a-year rule (course Module 2).
- **"Once you believe you are better than anyone else, you're done"; CEO speaks LAST in Monday meetings; no yes-people (HiPPO).** → maps to Lior's retention calls (manager listens, doesn't respond) and 30+ advisors / "it's not a shame to say you're struggling."
- **Hiring: takes candidates on a "smoothie ride," watches how they treat the waiter; "kind heart and values first, the resume will follow."** → maps to "motivation beats skills 100% of the cases," "never hired off a certificate," right-person-not-best-person.
- **"Find the three-star reviews — that's your gap."** → near-identical to Lior's competitor-review obsession (Trustpilot, rival user groups → acquired them).
- **"Overnight success exists after you work 10 years with nobody watching."** → Lior started at 14, decade to exit at 28.
- **"Success has no finish line, so you can't compare timelines."** / "Real success is who you become in the process."
- **"Intuition is just data you've lived."** → pairs with Lior's "without data you're just another person with an opinion."
- **"I love being underestimated — you get to build quieter."**
- **"Market saturated was invented by someone with low creativity."**
- **ABC — Always Be Communicating; say the uncomfortable things.** → maps to dollar-bug Facebook live, share-everything-bad-and-good management.
