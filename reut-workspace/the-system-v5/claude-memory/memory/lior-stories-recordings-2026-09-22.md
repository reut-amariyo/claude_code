Two Hebrew voice recordings, 2026-09-22, Lior thinking out loud with Reut about post ideas.
Transcript: `the-system-v5/O-output/agency-briefs/lior-recordings-2026-09-22-transcript-he.md`.
Agency brief: `agency-material-lior-recordings-2026-09-22.md` in the same folder.

**Story 1, the AI brain behind the personal brand.** His own hook framing: "I run a
company of 200 people and I still reach [X] impressions a month." His argument: with all
the AI, we are losing some of our humanity, so the cheapest channel left is a personal
brand, organic content, people connect to people. The mechanism he volunteered: every
meeting recorded, every thought sent as a WhatsApp voice note, a year of it now in one
brain that holds everything he thinks and does plus his coaching and psychologist
sessions, "so that Claude knows me as well as anything can." His advice to someone with
no team: the leverage is the AI, not the headcount. "Just record yourself, without
stopping." He asked for a saveable asset of the loop: meeting, record, Claude, learn.

**Story 2, two or three big things.** After a mentor session he named it precisely: a huge
amount was happening and it was not a feeling, things really were getting done, but the
big items that would move the business kept getting pushed aside because they were big
and needed more thinking. Fix: an agent on Claude set up as an assistant, asks every
morning what two or three things he commits to, follows up three times a day, reports on
Friday and at month end on committed versus completed. He still works the twelve hours
because he likes it, but "what I measure is only the two or three big things." Verdict on
the alternative: "far more measurable than a shopping list of small tasks that do not
really help." He said yes when Reut asked whether the prompt itself could be given away,
so a lead-magnet version exists.

**The compound-lift metaphor, his best original image from either tape.** In the gym there
are compound exercises, not isolation. A deadlift or a bench press works around 70 percent
of you, and one exercise gives the effect of three or four. Same with work: one big task
can be worth many small ones even when it takes less time. Reut said "big", he corrected
her: "Big in substance."

**Why:** these are the first two tapes where he describes his own operating systems rather
than his history, and the personal-brand one is rare first-party material about how the
content itself gets made. Both are follower-shaped, not impressions-shaped.

**How to apply:**
- Every impressions, followers and hours-per-week figure in recording 1 is a PLACEHOLDER.
  He said "X X" out loud. Never publish one of these numbers without pulling the real data.
- "200 people" is his own line and is safe. The twelve hours is hedged, the 70 percent is a
  verbal estimate, keep it as "around".
- The coaching and psychologist detail is his and volunteered, but it is proof of how
  complete the brain is, never the subject of a post.
- Do not flatten story 1 into generic "use AI for content" advice. The brain is the
  differentiator and the only reason the post clears the topic gate.
