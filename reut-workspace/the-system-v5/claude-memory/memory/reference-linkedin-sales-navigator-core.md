LinkedIn Sales Navigator Core, the plan Reut buys for adding LinkedIn connections. Checked 2026-10-25.

- Buy / free trial, official: https://business.linkedin.com/sell/sales-navigator/compare-plans → pick "Core"
- Price: US$119.99/month, or US$1,079.88/year (~25% cheaper annually)
- Free trial: only if the account has no paid LinkedIn plan now, Premium included, and no LinkedIn trial in the last 365 days. Card required; cancel before trial end. LinkedIn emails a reminder 7 days before.
- Buy it on the account that will do the connecting. A Premium subscription already on that account blocks the trial, and the two overlap.
- Avoid third-party "discount" links. Buy only from linkedin.com.

Reut wants this kept for her future business too. Related: [[project-lior-us-senior-connect-batch]], [[project-foundera-linkedin-agency]].

**Upgrade gotcha, hit 2026-10-25:** upgrading from an existing Premium plan failed with "Your subscription was purchased in a different currency. Please contact customer support". So if the current plan was bought in a different currency, the self-serve upgrade is blocked. Options: ask support to switch the plan, or cancel Premium, let it expire, then buy Core fresh. An existing paid plan also removes the free trial.
- Reut wanted to keep Premium AND add Sales Navigator, 2026-10-25. As far as I know, LinkedIn allows only one paid subscription per account, and Sales Navigator already includes Premium's main features, so having both normally means a switch, not an add. Confirm with support before assuming.

**LinkedIn support answer, 2026-10-25, on Lior's account:** Lior has Premium Business. Support confirmed it can't run alongside Sales Navigator because the features overlap. The path: cancel Premium Business, keep it until the billing cycle ends, then buy Sales Navigator Core. Free trial not guaranteed, since it depends on past Premium trials. To avoid purchase errors, the profile location, card billing address, and the location you buy from must match. No VPN when buying.
