Post: "1 hour a week. 200 employees. One habit." Lior records every meeting, coaching and
psychologist session, a year of it in one Claude brain, 100+ pieces of content a month.
Visual: candid Lior at laptop + a 1,126,813-impressions screenshot with a red arrow.
Result at ~2d: 1,893 imp, 55% out-of-network, 48 react, 10 comm, 16 profile viewers,
**0 followers**. Bottom-half 🔴. Story source: [[lior-stories-recordings-2026-09-22]].

Why it flopped:
1. Hook promised a number the body never paid: "1 hour a week" is never explained, and
   recording every meeting is not one hour. Reader feels baited by line 2.
2. The only Lior-only detail, "even my psychologist sessions", sat as list item 4 instead
   of the hook. The strongest confession was buried.
3. Second half went generic: "In a world being reshaped by AI, being visible online is no
   longer optional / people connect to people". Any coach writes that. Fails
   [[feedback-topic-gate-only-lior-can-write]].
4. Self-undermining: "100+ pieces a month come from Claude" tells the reader this post is
   machine output. Comments came back AI-generated, the audience mirrored it.
5. Visual = a vanity-metric screenshot whose number never appears in the text. Reads as
   a creator flex, not an operator receipt. See [[feedback-linkedin-visual-one-artifact-not-diagram]].
6. Lior asked for a SAVEABLE asset of the loop: meeting, record, Claude, learn. Not shipped.
   No artifact = no follow reason. See [[feedback-linkedin-kpi-followers-not-impressions]].
7. "Personal brand advice" is a creator/CMO topic, [[feedback-ceo-relevance-filter]].

**How to apply** next time this story or any "how I run my brand/AI system" post ships:
- Hook = the confession: the psychologist tapes, or "a year of my meetings lives in Claude".
- Every number in the hook must be paid in the body with the mechanism.
- Frame it as how a 200-person CEO thinks and decides, never as content-creation advice.
- Never say the content is AI-made; say what the brain KNOWS, not what it WRITES.
- Ship the loop as a first-party artifact: document carousel or the actual setup prompt.
- Visual = the artifact, or the brain itself; never an impressions screenshot.
