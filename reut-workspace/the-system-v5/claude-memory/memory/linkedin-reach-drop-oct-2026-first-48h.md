Source: Taplio newsletter Reut pasted 2026-10-18, citing Lara Acosta's newsletter + Jasmin Alić's post. Third-party observation, NOT a LinkedIn statement - LinkedIn has said nothing.

**What's happening, Oct 2026:**
- Impressions at an all-time low across creators. Jasmin Alić, 381K followers: impressions -14% in 30 days, a post "locked" after 10 minutes.
- Pattern on Taplio's accounts: fewer views per post, HIGHER engagement rate per post. Their writer's last 4 posts went 11.5K -> 7.3K -> 4.7K -> 1.1K views while ER rose 1.3% -> 3.1%.
- First 1-2 days a post lives ~80% inside the poster's own network, then settles near 50/50.
- LinkedIn said in May 2026 that generic posts are held inside the 1st-degree network. That is the "48-hour wall"; one number or story only the author could tell gets past it. Same logic as [[feedback-topic-gate-only-lior-can-write]].
- LinkedIn engineering ranks on how long people pause on a post, since likes are too rare. With own followers that pause is decided by the first 2 lines -> lead with the number or the contradiction. Matches [[linkedin-interest-graph-2026]].

**How to apply:**
1. Reading Lior's numbers: a falling impressions line in Oct 2026 is partly industry-wide. Don't call a post a flop on impressions alone; check ER, comments, followers gained, per [[feedback-linkedin-kpi-followers-not-impressions]]. Compare against his own recent median, not the summer.
2. Judge every post on its first 48h, not a week later. In post-mortems and the performance log, note the 48h numbers.
3. Comment coverage is the cheapest lever: be on LinkedIn ~15 min before and after posting, comment on others, answer every comment. Taplio's writer had a 4x-median breakout with comments still unanswered 27 days later. Reinforces the first-hour rule in [[linkedin-posting-rules]]; ask who covers Lior's comments in the 48h window - Lior, Reut, or [[project-foundera-linkedin-agency]].
4. Hook = number or contradiction in line 1-2. Already the house rule; this is why.

**Post mechanics from the 3 trending posts that week:**
- Uncomfortable stat from a big account + "what you control" + P.S. question asking "is yours lower too?" -> ~1,400 comments. P.S. question did most of the comment work, cf. SLAY "You + P.S." in [[reference-lara-acosta-slay-framework]].
- Answer the question everyone asks privately, with info people don't normally get - benchmarks by follower count.
- Small founder admits a tiny number - 28 impressions - then asks a real question they want answered; bigger accounts filled the comments. For Lior: the "admit the number" move only if the number is a real, earned confession, never a vanity stat, see [[feedback-ai-brain-post-flop-2026-10]].
