## 💡 3 Action Items for Lior's Next Posts

### 1. Run setup-twist as a third A/B before declaring it a template
The hook structure ("[positive claim]. [Pause/turn word]. [Inverse problem]") has worked twice in a row. Run it a third time on a different topic (suggested: pricing, hiring miss, or product decision) to confirm it's not just topic-bound. Draft idea: "We had 250 employees. Then I learned the math doesn't work that way." or "We were profitable from year one. Which almost killed us."

### 2. NEVER post a conference recap without a production stake
Locked rule from 4th confirmed flop. If Lior attends a conference and wants to post, the structure must be: **a specific AutoDS production decision that changed BECAUSE of what he heard.** Not "3 shifts I walked away with." Instead: "Yesterday at LangTalks, [speaker name] said [one specific thing]. Today, we're rebuilding [exact AutoDS system] because of it." If there's no production change, skip the post.

### 3. Pre-flight test: would the post survive without the CTA?
Three of this scan's 🔴 posts had soft CTAs that didn't convert (find-skills "link in comments", LangTalks "Which shift hits you hardest?", "I got rejected" "Link in the comments"). The pattern: CTAs work only when the post earned them. **Rule going forward:** Draft the post WITHOUT the CTA first. If the body holds attention on its own and ends on a quotable line, the CTA can stay. If you're adding the CTA because the post feels incomplete, the post itself is the problem — don't band-aid with a CTA.
