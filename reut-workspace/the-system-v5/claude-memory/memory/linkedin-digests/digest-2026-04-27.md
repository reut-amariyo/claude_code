## 🧩 Patterns That Worked This Week

Based on available data (limited week) + patterns from prior week's library entries:

1. **Build-in-public with the ugly middle:** Tyler Denk's Q1 win post works because he included the near-failure context (fired team, cut spend, almost sold). The milestone headline without backstory is noise; the milestone + "we almost didn't make it" is a story. **Pattern: earn your win before announcing it.**

2. **AI as the foil, not the hero:** Lior's two likely posts this week (Jensen Huang takeaways + 10-80-10 rule) frame AI as a multiplier for human judgment/vision — not a replacement. This is a consistent contrarian position in the AI flood on LinkedIn right now. Readers who are tired of "AI will replace everyone" content are engaging with nuanced "AI does 80% but you still steer" takes.

3. **Short text + bold number in the hook:** Gary V's "social organic" post (855 likes) and Tyler Denk's Q1 post both lead with a specific data point or claim. The feed is skimmable — posts that front-load a number or a bold claim above the fold are getting more taps to "see more."
