## 🧩 Patterns That Worked This Week

1. **Conviction/belief statements dominated the mid-tier.** Justin Welsh placed 4 posts in the top 10 with zero stories — just short, second-person belief statements against grind culture, each pulling 475-919 comments. The format *is* the engine: state a belief most people quietly hold, phrase it as a line they can quote, leave room to agree loudly.
2. **Anaphora/repetition is a save-and-share multiplier.** Hormozi's 8× "I've built wealth without ___" (5,821) and Simon Beard's "You pay the… You pay the…" (1,469) both used rhythmic repetition. Lists and repeated stems out-travel paragraphs — they're screenshotable.
3. **Hiring posts CAN go mega-viral — but only as broad open invitations, never bait-and-switch.** Bartlett's "I'M HIRING ANYONE with a world-class attitude" hit 21,355 because it's an *identity trigger for the reader* ("you, reading this, could do your best work here"), with no role gate and no "link in comments." This is the exact inverse of Lior's failed "I got rejected → Director of Partnerships" bait-and-switch. The lesson isn't "don't post hiring" — it's "make the reader the hero, not the job spec."
4. **Lists/milestone-reflection is a proven organic lane — and Lior just proved it works for him too.** Lior's birthday "10 lessons" (8,858 @ 16h) mirrors the Hormozi numbered-truth structure. Credential-anchored milestone + scannable one-liners = reach without a trending-tool dependency.
5. **Even strong creators had quiet weeks — volume ≠ reach.** 6 of 11 creators posted zero 1000+ posts. Gary V (4 posts/16h, top 514) and Matt Gray (daily, top 620 but 76% comment-ratios suggesting pod activity) show that cadence and comment-engineering don't substitute for a resonant single idea.
