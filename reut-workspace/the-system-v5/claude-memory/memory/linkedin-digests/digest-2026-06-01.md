## 🧩 Patterns That Worked This Week

1. **Contrarian command/aphorism = the dialogue engine.** Justin Welsh's "Stop bookmarking content" (45% comment-to-react ratio) and "Building on your own terms makes you unemployable" (607 comments) prove that a 1-2 line take attacking a behavior the reader is *doing right now* out-converts long posts on comments. The entire structure is the hook.

2. **Childhood/origin story was the week's #1 post.** Bartlett's bunkbed-fake-business story (11,960 / 799 reposts) beat every business-tactic post. Origin narratives travel because readers reshare them as "this was me." Lior has an untapped origin arc.

3. **One-word "Agree?" CTA converges across the top tier.** Hormozi (#6, 1,962) and Lior (Google, 144K) both closed on a bare "Agree?" on a universal stake. On a claim everyone has a side on, the minimal yes/no CTA out-performs an open-ended question for reach.

4. **Failure/struggle cadence beats success-flex.** Hormozi's "1st failed, 2nd failed, 3rd failed…" (6,093) and Tyler Denk's "overnight success that took 4 years" ($30M ARR) both led with the struggle, not the win. The ugly middle is what readers finish and reshare.

5. **Hiring-as-gift works; hiring-as-role-gate fails.** Bartlett's "I'M HIRING ANYONE" (last week, 21K) and Lior's "Tough days / my DMs are open" (13,400, 15 reposts) both make the reader the hero. The role-spec/"link in comments" version (Lior's "I got rejected", 2,066 🔴) is the failure mode.
