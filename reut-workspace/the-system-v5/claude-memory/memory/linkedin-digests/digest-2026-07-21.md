### The World Cup post — what actually happened

Full text on file. The structure:
1. **Newsjack with a real-time anchor** — "Watched the World Cup final live yesterday." Biggest global event of the month, posted the day after.
2. **A specific, surprising, checkable number** — "Messi walks 83% of the match."
3. **Deliberate non-expertise** — "I'm not a football expert, but it bothered me enough to go digging." Removes any authority-flex risk.
4. **Founder reframe** — early years = sprints, no sleep. Then "the lazy method: pick where to attack, delegate, leave ego out of it."
5. **The quotable** — "Busy players run more. Great players run right."
6. **Open question CTA** — "Which balls are you still chasing?"
7. **Visual = personal artifact** — his own photo at the World Cup, alt "Lior at the World Cup". Not a marketing graphic.

That is the **cultural-moment + operator-reframe** archetype executed cleanly, and it produced ~3.4x the July baseline in 24 hours.

## 🧩 Patterns That Worked This Week

1. **The cultural-moment newsjack out-performed everything Lior has run in two months.** World Cup 10,163 vs a 3.0K baseline. The mechanism is a shared, time-bound event that the whole feed already has in its head, plus one number nobody knew. Cheap attention, then the operator lesson does the work.
2. **Welsh's conviction-aphorism format is now the most consistent creator pattern in the log — 5 weeks running.** All 4 of his qualifiers (3,548 / 3,489 / 2,554 / 2,503) are 40–60-word second-person belief statements with 843–1,296 comments each. The one that added a *number* (the $200K / $600K salary math) took second place. **Still untested on Lior with a real contrarian edge.**
3. **Named humans are a distribution engine.** Will Ahmed's #1 (8,258) is a monthly ritual of naming new hires. Bartlett's #1 in window (2,762) names Hormozi's mother. Lior's Paul post (5,564 ✅) names Paul. Every named person is a reshare node. Compare: Lior's DotDev post named a *brand activation* (20,000 cups) and got 1,707.
4. **Hormozi took 2 of the top 10 with posts that have no text at all.** Image-only quote cards, 4,023 and 1,896, with the week's highest repost count (205). The asset is the shareable unit. This is a real format, not an accident — and it is the exact opposite of Lior's text-first practice.
5. **Announcement posts split hard by whether a person is at the center.** Bartlett's Shark Tank *personal* milestone: 18,833. Will Ahmed's *executive hire*: 2,625 and 965. Tyler Denk's *product launch* on his biggest news day of the quarter: 866. Lior's *conference presence*: 1,707 🔴. Product and logistics announcements do not travel; personal milestones do.
