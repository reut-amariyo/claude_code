## 🧩 Patterns That Worked This Week

### 1. Contrarian-on-AI is the dominant winner pattern across creators
- Bartlett's "Companies should ban AI writing" hit **11,589 reactions / 951 reposts** — biggest non-mega-post of the week.
- Bartlett's "AI gives answers, not understanding" hit **6,094 reactions / 612 reposts** — same theme, compressed.
- This is the same pattern that drove Lior's Paperclip post to **30K impressions**. **The pattern is reproducible.**

### 2. The closing-question lottery: specific > rhetorical
- Justin Welsh's posts won the comment volume war this week (1,025 / 1,185 / 1,397 comments) by closing with **specific, answerable questions** ("What's the ONE thing you've been waiting to start?" / "What 'unreasonable' decision has been your best?").
- Lior's "I got rejected" post FAILED on this exact axis — closed with a job listing instead of a question. The 1-comment final number proves the diagnosis.
- **Specific question close ≠ rhetorical-question close.** "Is this not the greatest time in history to be building?" (Paperclip) is rhetorical and stops dialogue. "What's the one thing you've been waiting to start?" is specific and starts it.

### 3. "Today vs. then" stat contrast hooks dominate founder-journey posts
- Hormozi's $3M → $17M wife post (5,093 reactions)
- Hormozi's Prius → $250M revenue post (2,400 reactions)
- **Pattern:** specific dollar amount BEFORE → specific dollar amount AFTER → mechanism in middle.
- Lior has the raw material (bootstrapped → 250 employees → Fiverr exit) but rarely uses the explicit before/after numbers in the hook.

### 4. Numbered-takeaway sequels close the dialogue the setup post opened
- Lior's Wim Hof sequel ("5 days taught me… 1. … 2. … 3. …") got **0 comments at 7d** vs. cold feet's **12 comments**.
- The setup post asked "wish me luck" (open). The sequel delivered closed takeaways.
- **Pattern violated:** when a setup post earns deep engagement via vulnerability + open close, the sequel must keep the same dialogue structure, not collapse to a takeaway list.

### 5. Bait-and-switch (vulnerability hook → CTA pivot) confirmed killer at scale
- Hormozi's Prius post is the cleanest counterexample: same vulnerability arc as Lior's "I got rejected," but Hormozi closes with a principle ("never quit"), not a job listing. **2,400 reactions vs. Lior's 55 likes.**
- Same template, different close → **70x reach delta.**
