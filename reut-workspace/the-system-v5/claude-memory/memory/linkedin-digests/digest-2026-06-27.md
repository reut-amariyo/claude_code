## 🧩 Patterns That Worked This Week

1. **Conviction/aphorism hooks own the creator mid-tier — again.** Welsh placed all 3 of his qualifiers (7,854 / 6,264 / 4,255) with bare second-person belief statements, no story, no stat, 1,000+ comments each. This is now the most consistent cross-week creator pattern in the log. **Still untested on Lior** (his closest attempt this week, "Team game," was an aphorism but a *flat* one — no contrarian edge, no agree-gap).
2. **Broad-invitation hiring beats everything on raw reach (Bartlett 15,868).** The reader-as-hero, no-role-gate hiring hook is his evergreen #1. This is the same lane as Lior's ✅⭐ Tough-days (value-first + hiring-as-gift) — confirmed traveling. The inverse (role-spec + "link in comments") still dies.
3. **Named-guest / named-brand authority carries reach.** Bartlett's Jeremy Grantham AI-investing interview (3,189) and Will Ahmed's FDA-milestone (2,295) both leaned on a concrete named entity + stakes. Lior's World Cup/**Levi's** case-study (4,099 🟡) is the same mechanism — a named brand's story is more shareable than a personal opinion.
4. **AI-name WITHOUT a contrarian thesis is now a confirmed Lior dead-end (n=4).** Anthropic-Slack (1,798 🔴) joins AutoDS-Claude, MS+Anthropic, and Anthropic-repricing. The AI lane only breaks out when the name anchors an *argument* readers project onto — never a how-to.
5. **Open-question CTA = comment engine, not reach engine.** Lior's SaaS "What would you choose?" earned a 40% comment ratio on 865 imp. Welsh's contrarian questions do the same at scale. Useful when the goal is *discussion/saves*, but it won't lift impressions on its own.
