## 🛑 HARD CAVEAT ADDED 2026-08-02, CONFIRMED AT 7d LOCK 2026-08-06 — reuse the spine, never the text

The agency published the 2-year version on **2026-07-30** as a **near-verbatim republish** of this post: only "Last year" → "2 year ago", "7 years ago" → "8 years ago", and the closing directive triple was dropped. **7d FINAL: 806 impressions** against the original's 200K+, with copy, structure, author and account held constant — a **~250x collapse**, and the 4th-worst post in a 40-post log.

Diagnostic, from [[lior-performance-log]]: its engagement rate was **7.4% at lock, the highest in the entire log by a wide margin**, on the log's **4th-lowest reach**, with **0 followers gained**. Record warmth plus record-low reach is the fingerprint of **duplicate-content suppression**, not of a weak post — the people served it loved it, LinkedIn just refused to distribute it. Two controls rule out an account-level throttle: a fresh origin post the next day ran **2.0x** its reach at matched age, and a fresh credential post four days later did **5,412 impressions in 17 hours** on the same account.

**Rule: a proven post is a proven TEMPLATE, never a proven TEXT.** For any anniversary or milestone reuse, keep the spine below and rewrite every sentence, with this year's numbers and at least one story the original did not contain. Also restore a give-back close — the republish ended fully resolved with no engagement gap, which violates [[feedback-linkedin-engagement-gap]].

**Archetype:** "Anniversary reflection + 3 keys" — milestone hook ("Last year, I sold that business to Fiverr"), origin reminder (started living with parents, 7 years ago, just an idea + himself + a laptop), then 3 numbered drivers, each with a short story, closing with a punchy directive list + one quotable line.

**The 3 choices (structure to preserve):**
1. **Advisors** — hired A LOT of them, swapped them as needs changed; they own their domain, give objective business-focused help, kept him from mistakes, accelerated growth, kept the company lean.
2. **Networking** — went to A LOT of events, talked to A LOT of people; the one trick was "be transparent" — told everyone everything (challenges, partnerships, offices, tech, employees). People reciprocated, offered help/insight, became partners and customers.
3. **Partners** — chose 2 people to run the company with him, both very different from him. He "runs frantically forward, causing a mess"; partner 1 = organized responsible adult; partner 2 = in the middle, keeps everyone in sync + ROI in mind. Hard for the first year or 2, then you realize it's gold — partners keep you safe and focused.

**Close:** "Building a business is such a huge roller coaster." → Advisors, Networking, Partners — those will make or break your business. Any business. → Hire the right advisors / Network relentlessly / Find the right partners. → "These are the real keys to success."

**Why the ORIGINAL worked:** vulnerable origin ("living with my parents"), self-deprecating honesty ("causing a mess" — fits [[feedback-lior-never-arrogant]]), concrete and specific, ONE clear takeaway triple, no jargon. Full original text is in the 2026-06-28 conversation.
