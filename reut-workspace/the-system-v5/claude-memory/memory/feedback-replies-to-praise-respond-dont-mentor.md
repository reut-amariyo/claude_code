When someone praises or agrees with Lior's post, the reply responds to their words with warmth and gratitude. No mentoring, no "try it for a week", no new principle stacked on top, no topping their point.

**Why:** Reut, 2026-10-11: "מפרגנים לך, אתה לא צריך להרים עוד את האף" — replies that teach back to a complimenting commenter read arrogant and ignore what the person actually said.

**How to apply:** Before drafting, ask "what did this person actually say?" and reply to that. Default shape: thank them + pick up their specific point + at most one short personal note. Cut any instruction, advice or aphorism aimed at the commenter.
