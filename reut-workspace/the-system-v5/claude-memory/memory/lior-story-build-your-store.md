From Lior's Hebrew voice recording, 2026-08-11 (transcript translated in O-output/agency-briefs/agency-material-lior-recordings-2026-08-11.md).

The story, in his numbers:
- 2023: AI wave starts; team asks how to integrate AI across AutoDS.
- At Shopify's 2023 conference, Shopify shared that ~30% of new customers never finish setting up their store (his recollection, "כנראה 30 אחוז" — verify before an exact-number hook). Same pattern inside AutoDS.
- Solution: AI removes the store-setup barrier → Build Your Store, launched as a separate brand for complete beginners, one of the first steps beyond dropshipping.
- Product: pick a niche → fully designed store with 10+ trending products, from AutoDS data on 400M+ products.
- Outcome: AutoDS became Shopify's biggest partner a year later, recognized for global expansion in 2024 and 2025 (exact award name unverified). 1M+ people created stores.
- Free for the user; Shopify pays AutoDS for customers brought. Includes a beginner e-commerce course.
