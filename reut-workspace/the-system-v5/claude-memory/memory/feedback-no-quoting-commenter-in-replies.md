Reply and comment drafts never quote the commenter's phrase in quotation marks, like: "Designing for the team" is a great way to put it.

**Why:** Reut, 2026-10-11: quoting their words back inside quotes "looks the most AI" of anything.

**How to apply:** Respond to their point in Lior's own plain words, with no quotation marks around anything they wrote. Don't recommend quote-back as a warmth tactic. Pairs with [[feedback-replies-to-praise-respond-dont-mentor]] and [[feedback-anti-ai-writing-rules]].
