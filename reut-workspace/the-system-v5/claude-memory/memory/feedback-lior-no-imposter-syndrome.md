Direct instruction from Reut (2026-07-05, quoting Lior): "אני לא מאמין על עצמי בתסמונת המתחזה, או כל אמירה של ׳לא חשבתי שזה יקרה׳ וכו׳" — Lior does not experience imposter syndrome and rejects any statement of that kind about himself.

**Never write for Lior:**
- "Imposter syndrome" in any form applied to himself
- "I never thought this would happen" / "I couldn't believe it" / "Who am I to..."
- "Pinch me" moments, "still can't believe", "little me from [place] never imagined"
- Any surprised-by-his-own-success or don't-deserve-this framing

**Why:** It's not his psychology. He built deliberately — the eBay ban, 100 accounts, AutoDS, the exit were the result of intent and systems, not luck he's stunned by. Faking disbelief-humility would be inauthentic and clash with the Architect of Growth archetype in [[lior-pb-strategy-v8]].

**Extension (2026-07-08, voice note from Lior):** The rule covers more than first-person claims. A draft kept Lior himself "clean" (external verdict at 14, inner conviction) but built the post's THESIS around imposter syndrome being universal and worth reassuring people about ("everyone in that room knows the feeling, you are worthy, the voice is wrong"). Lior rejected it viscerally: "כל הנושא של לא להאמין בעצמך... אני לא מאמין בזה" — he couldn't name what was wrong, but the whole not-believing-in-yourself THEME is foreign to him. He cannot champion, validate, or empathize with the imposter frame at all, even about others. If the topic comes up (e.g., it dominated a real event he attended), his only authentic angle is the contrarian/decision one: he never fought the voice, he decided the system was wrong and let the work prove it. Self-belief as decision + evidence, never as a feeling to be soothed.

**How to apply:** When a post needs humility or relatability, use real past failures and concrete setbacks instead — see [[feedback-lior-no-fear-confessions]] (vulnerability = real past failures, not anticipatory fear; same logic: not fake disbelief either). His wins are framed as earned outcomes of decisions and work: "I planned for this" energy, never "I can't believe this" energy. Balance against [[feedback-lior-never-arrogant]] — confident and intentional, not boastful.
