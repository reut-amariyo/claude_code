Asked for the monthly pacing to 500K on Instagram, I published a full page: three
pacing models, a projection chart, a conversion-math section, a review gate. She sent it
back with "זה צריך להיות מסמך הרבה יותר פשוט - כמה פולוארז אנחנו צריכים עד סוף כל חודש".

**Why:** the document gets shared with Lior and the agency. Its job is to settle one number
per month so nobody argues about it. Every extra section is another thing that can be
questioned in the room, and it buries the number she actually needs them to agree on.
The reasoning is what she asks ME for in chat; the artifact carries the output.

**How to apply:** for any shareable deliverable, write down the one question it answers in a
single sentence first, then put only that on the page. Analysis, models, alternatives and
caveats stay in the chat reply where she can read them and decide what to raise herself.
If a caveat truly has to travel with the document, it goes in a one-line footer, not a section.
