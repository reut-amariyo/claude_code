Never draft productivity hacks, AI workflow tips, "how to use Claude/ChatGPT/[tool]" posts, or any tool-recommendation content for Lior. This includes Anthropic/OpenAI feature explainers, prompt-engineering tricks, and "the X trick I use daily" formats.

**Why:** Confirmed by a 400-impression flop (2026-05-12) — a post explaining the "MD files → HTML" Claude review workflow. Reasons it failed:
- Justin Welsh, Greg Isenberg, and every AI-tools creator own this lane — Lior has no credibility advantage as a Bootstrapped→Fiverr founder with 200 employees
- Hits zero of the Three Pillars (200% / 0→1 / focus) — could've been written by any consultant
- No story, no scene, no stakes — violates Real Person Principle
- No quotable founder principle = no saves (saves = highest intent signal)
- Reads as Anthropic marketing, not Lior's founder journey (extends [feedback-lior-personal-brand-not-autods.md](feedback-lior-personal-brand-not-autods.md) — personal brand isn't tool-vendor marketing either)

**How to apply:**
- scout-day, copywriter-scout, and all LinkedIn/X drafting skills must skip this archetype entirely
- If a tool/workflow IS central to a story, frame it as a CEO scene (what was at stake, who pushed back, what broke, what principle emerged) — not as a how-to
- Pre-publish filter: if removing the tool name still leaves a strong post, fine. If the post collapses without the tool, kill it.

## Confirmed AI-topic exceptions (refined 2026-05-16 from top-8 audit)

Two AI-topic archetypes ARE allowed (both confirmed 10K+ on Lior's profile). They are NOT tutorials — they're CEO-voice operator commentary on the AI ecosystem.

### Exception 1 — Industry Release Decode
**Allowed:** When a major AI/SaaS company (Anthropic, OpenAI, etc.) ships something category-shaping, Lior can post the STRATEGIC SIGNIFICANCE — what changes for builders, what the meta-shift means, what the implication is for SaaS founders. Confirmed by post 4 (Anthropic Skills, ~15K imp).

**Required framing:** "this matters because…" / "this shifts the industry because…" / "what most builders missed…"

**Banned framing:** "here's how to use it…" / "the prompts I use…" / "5 steps to set this up…"

### Exception 2 — Build-in-Public AI Experiment Report
**Allowed:** When Lior actually ran an experiment (AI agents, automation, replacing manual workflow) — post the honest result, including what failed. Confirmed by post 6 (AI agents replacing team, ~30K imp) and post 8 (No team, no code, ~12K imp).

**Required:** A real experiment Lior actually ran. The post is the REPORT (what happened, what didn't work, what he learned), not the tutorial.

**Banned:** Fabricated "thought experiments" with no real test, "here's my workflow" tip-stacks, prompt-engineering tricks.

### Litmus test for both exceptions
**Would a Fortune-500 SaaS CEO credibly post this?**
- "Anthropic just shipped X, here's what it means for AI apps" → YES, CEO commentary
- "I tried replacing my team with AI agents, here's what actually happened" → YES, founder experiment
- "Here's the prompt I use with Claude every morning" → NO, creator lane
- "5 ways AutoDS uses Claude in production" → NO, vendor content
- "I just discovered this Claude feature and it's a game-changer" → NO, influencer voice

### Subtle product placement (allowed via meme caption)
Confirmed via post 6 (Paperclip icon overlaid as meme caption on Brad Pitt church meme). Embedding a Lior product logo INTO a cultural meme as part of the joke = allowed brand placement. Pitching the product in the post text = banned. The meme must be Lior-made (custom), not generic stock.

- Original rule still holds for: tutorials, "how I use X," tool-stack lists, prompt tips, feature explainers
- The exception is narrow: strategic-significance commentary OR honest experiment report — never user instruction
