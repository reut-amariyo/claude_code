scout-day (and any X scout work) must always run the data-gathering step (Grok x_search + RSS feeds + Hacker News top stories) before drafting. Pure founder-memoir posts with no current-events anchor are NOT acceptable scout output.

**Why:** On 2026-05-09 Reut explicitly called this out. She had said "the role of the scout is to find what's happening NOW and react. Pure story posts aren't 'the scout' — they're memoir." The first attempt that day had 5 founder-stories (dollar bug, 5 years no salary, first conference, churn replies, money is a game) with zero topical anchor. Even with strong storytelling, they read like LinkedIn evergreens, not daily X scout posts.

**How to misread the hypothesis (the trap):** The May-2026 hypothesis says "0 × news-reaction" and "Avoid: any post structured as 'Company X just did Y, here's what I learned at AutoDS.'" That phrasing makes it tempting to skip news entirely. That's wrong. The hypothesis is killing the dead STRUCTURE (company-name hook → AutoDS frame → punchline), not killing the topical anchor.

**How to apply correctly:**
1. Always run the data gathering: Grok responses API broad query + TechCrunch AI / The Verge AI / OpenAI / Hugging Face RSS feeds + Hacker News top stories with >50 comments.
2. Pick 4 of 5 posts to anchor on a topical event from today's gather. Hold 1 of 5 for a pure founder story (story-mix slot from the hypothesis).
3. For each topical post: Lior-first hook (a scene, a number, a pattern Lior observed) → current event woven in as beat 2 or 3 → personal proof / AutoDS receipt → land on insight. Never open with the company name.
4. Examples that worked on 2026-05-09 rewrite: AWS-outage post led with "Half of Twitter spent yesterday refreshing dashboards while AWS was down" + 3 AM alarm story. Cloudflare-layoffs post led with "Watched plenty of teams quietly stop shipping their best work the week after layoffs" + Cloudflare news as beat 2. Oracle-severance post led with "Fired plenty of people in 9 years at AutoDS" + Oracle news as beat 3.
