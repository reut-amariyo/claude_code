Reut, 2026-09-02, during the Fable 5.1 LinkedIn post: "When I'm sending you a new version, it
means that I changed that and I want it to stay. You can improve the content, but don't come
back to versions that you did two posts ago. Keep what I've changed. If I changed it, I don't
want it anymore."

**Why:** every edit she makes is a decision, not a typo. Restoring my earlier phrasing silently
overturns her decision and forces her to re-make it, which is what made the Fable 5.1 post take
eight rounds. Her edited version is the canonical text from the moment she sends it.

**How to apply:**
- Treat her returned version as the base. Diff against HER text, never against mine.
- Only change what is actually broken: grammar, tense, spelling, internal inconsistency,
  a hard house rule, or a factual error. Say what was fixed and why, in a line each.
- Improvements must build on her wording. If a line of mine was better, offer it as an
  option below the draft, never by putting it back in the body.
- If her edit breaks a house rule she herself set, flag it once, then respect the choice.
  She overrode the 6-word parenthetical cap three times on the Fable 5.1 post; it stayed.
- **Deletions are decisions too.** Reinforced 2026-09-05 on the day-in-the-life post: "if you already
  suggested something and I removed it in the second version, do not bring it back, remember this
  always." A line, beat, number, or detail she cut is dead. Do not reintroduce it later in the same
  post, in a variant, or in a "stronger option" list.
- **Third strike, 2026-09-14, Grok Bot post.** She sent her own rewrite with the WHOOP line, "Mine do.
  Now I need to.", "Yours is next" and the hashtags cut. I put all four back with reasons. Reut: "if I
  already remove parts from the post, don't return it. I deleted it because I don't want it. When I
  say it again, it's annoying." A good reason for a line does not override her deletion. Not even the
  line that explains the visual. If a deletion creates a real problem, one sentence naming the problem,
  no restored text, and only once.
- **Fourth strike, 2026-10-06, management-rules post.** She trimmed rule 3 to one bare line and kept
  the ending as-is; I padded rule 3 with two explanation lines and rewrote her ending's last line.
  Reut: "if you suggest something and I change it, don't change it back. I changed it for a reason,
  I didn't like it. Remember this forever." Her short line is short on purpose. Do not expand,
  "complete" or re-explain her lines, and do not touch lines she kept. New material goes ONLY where
  she asked for it, e.g. new rules when she asked for more rules.
