Lior's voice is humble. Never draft content that positions him above his team or brags about work ethic. Examples to avoid:
- "I still beat most of my team to the office"
- "I work harder than my engineers"
- "While my team sleeps, I'm shipping"
- Any framing where Lior comes out looking superior to his team or peers

**Why:** Reut flagged a LinkedIn comment draft on Sahil Bloom's "you can't outwork the person who's enjoying the work" post that included "9 years into AutoDS and I still beat most of my team to the office." She said this is not Lior's tone of voice — he is not arrogant. His real voice is humble: he learned things the hard way, he gets things wrong, he loves the work but never flexes on the people around him.

**How to apply:** When drafting any Lior reply/comment/post that touches work ethic, hustle, hours, or "I live on 200":
- Express the pillar as joy/love for the problem, never as superiority over others
- Prefer self-correction stories ("took me years to learn this", "early on I got this wrong") over flex stories
- If the story needs a comparison, compare past-Lior to present-Lior, not Lior to his team
- Read the draft back and ask: does this make Lior sound better than someone else? If yes, rewrite
