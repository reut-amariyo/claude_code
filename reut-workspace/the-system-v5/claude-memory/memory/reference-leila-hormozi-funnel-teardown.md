**The machine, 3 layers, no arrow skips a layer**
1. **Attention = rented.** 6 platforms, one idea recorded once and cut per platform. "Volume is a distribution decision, not a content decision." Two audiences = same person at two stages: aspiring founders (confidence, discipline, money; bigger, emotional) grow the account, operators (hiring, leadership, scaling) buy. Zero pitch, price or calendar link on this layer: an early offer converts nobody and costs reach.
2. **Trust = owned.** Podcast, "Dear Leila" (audience submits problems, she answers on camera = pre-validated topics + viewers become participants), YouTube long form. All three drain into ONE weekly email she owns. Reel buys 20 min of long form, long form buys an email address.
3. **Money = gated ladder.** Free courses as a filter (topic only appeals to someone with a business) → "Requested by 250,000 business owners" proof ON the button → capture email AND phone → "qualifying call" (a door with a lock is worth more) → a "no" loops back into free content, never deleted → $5K workshop is the audition → equity is the real business.

**Test line:** "They find me on X, stay for my long-form Y, join my list, raise a hand by the free thing, buy Z after the call." Every blank you can't fill = a layer people fall through.
