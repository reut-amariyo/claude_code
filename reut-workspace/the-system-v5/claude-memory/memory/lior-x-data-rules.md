## Iron-clad rules (confirmed by data)

### Rule 1 — Replies CAN be the reach engine, but only if they land in live threads — the target pool is currently dead (UPDATED 2026-06-27)
**Why:** Replies have historically run 2–8× originals (838/525/504 in the Jun 2–9 week). But **this week the reply engine fully collapsed**: 9 replies shipped, avg 15.4 imp, 5 of 9 at ≤1 impression. Reply *content* was fine (operator reframes per Rule 3); the *targets* were dead. This is the [[project-x-reply-pipeline-whitelist-collapse]] failure realized — the 403 auto-blocker has cannibalized the whitelist down to low/no-traffic accounts, so even hand-posted replies reach nobody. A reply's reach is set by the THREAD's traffic, not the copy.
**How to apply:** Replies are still worth shipping, but ONLY into genuinely live threads — surface open-reply, non-verified, **high-traffic** targets (active thread, recent OP, 200+ likes / 10K+ views, multiple fresh non-verified replies). Do NOT reply into stale/low-traffic posts — they return ≤1 imp. Post by hand (don't rely on `reply_x.py` — it 403s). **#1 unblock: rebuild the reply target pool** — the whitelist collapse is the binding constraint on the reply channel.

### Rule 2 — The breakout ORIGINAL form is the age-anchored transformation / origin-story listicle (NEW 2026-06-27, REPLACES "originals are capped")
**Why:** The 6-week "originals are reach-capped at 10–60 imp" rule was **decisively broken this week**: 4 of 4 originals cleared 200 imp (avg 991), led by an "At 21 → Me at 31" transformation listicle at **2,376 imp** (first viral original since the Apr backfill) and an "At 14 I was smoking an e-cigarette…" origin-story listicle at 523. This is now the account's highest-reach original form, full stop.
**How to apply:** (a) **Lead with "At [age]:" + a bulleted ledger of CONCRETE biographical facts** — public-speaking fear, no salary 5 yrs, parents' apartment, 100 eBay accounts → sold to Fiverr, 200 employees, 1.8M dropshippers, 4 acquisitions, married Cat. Specific numbers and real nouns, not feelings. (b) Two shapes both work: the before/after ledger ("At 21 / Me at 31") and the full chronological arc ("At 14 → … → sold at 28"). (c) Keep them as deliberate companion pieces covering DIFFERENT life chapters — don't paraphrase the same chapter twice (Rule 5). (d) Number + lived scene + one-line lesson is still the best structure for NON-listicle originals (see the 398-imp management take).
