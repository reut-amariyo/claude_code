Two years before the Fiverr exit, Lior negotiated for 6 months with a private equity firm. Full due diligence, everyone ready to sign. A day before closing, he walked away.

The reason, per Reut on 2026-09-11: he realized the PE CEO's approach was purely to cut staff and turn AutoDS into a cash cow, culturally and morally misaligned with him; many employees had been with him from day one and he would not hurt them. NOT "I had a bigger number in my head." Separately on record: the Fiverr price was ~4x the PE offer, and the Fiverr deal closed in 23 days vs the 6-month PE grind.

**Why:** first used in the X agency's Miro/Bending Spoons brief (2026-09-11). Pairs with the no-investors / no-preference-stack story: bootstrapping meant the people who built it got paid on the real price.

**How to apply:** any post on valuation, equity, M&A, PE, or employees underwater on options. Frame the no as protecting the team, never as holding out for money. Keep the exit amount out or use the SEC figure, see [[lior-exit-amount-92m-vs-sec-filing]].
