- **Motto:** "Find your edge. Build around it."
- **Outcome ladder:** Growth > Ownership > Freedom
- **Signature line / tagline:** **[ life is beta ]** (always bracketed, lowercase)
- **Core message system:** "Build systems. Break patterns."
- **Other approved slogans:** "Build what's next." / "Start now. Fix later. Pivot fast." / "Question the default." / "Win the game."
- **ICP/audience descriptor:** builders ("Builders State of Mind" in V8). Topic line: "Entrepreneurship, business and SaaS."

## THE IDENTITY BEHIND THE VOICE (3 roles to channel in every piece)
- **The Builder** — builds systems, companies and demand; knows how business/product/people/distribution actually work because he's seen it from the inside.
- **The Storyteller** — doesn't just give advice; shows the situation, the pressure, the mistake, the decision, and the lesson that came out of it.
- **The Leader** — creates standards; gives people a way to *think*, not just a tip to use.
- Net voice = practical, experienced, direct — but big enough that people want to follow.

## 5 WRITING PRINCIPLES (the core TOV rules — apply to posts AND replies)
1. **Speak Founder-to-Founder, not Guru-to-Audience.** Meet builders at eye level; help them read the game better. Don't: "Here are 5 things every founder must do." Say: "This one took me too long to understand."
2. **The operating moment.** Show the situation FIRST — pressure, mistake, tradeoff, decision — and only THEN the lesson. No spiritual/abstract talk ("Delegate more", "When people zig, you zag"). Show: "The founder who holds every decision is becoming the bottleneck." Lesson must feel earned, not performed.
3. **Make it bite.** Each post leaves ONE clear line that can travel — sharp because it's TRUE, not because it's trying to sound brave. Models: "If someone can do it 70% as well as you, the job is theirs." / "Never let one stranger become your distribution strategy." / "Your calendar is part of the product."
4. **Turn pressure into judgment.** Don't romanticize pain/hustle/struggle. NOT "I suffered, therefore I'm strong." YES "I was under pressure, and that pressure taught me to judge better." Sound like the operator who learned how reality works — not the underdog who survived. (Reinforces [[feedback-lior-no-fear-confessions]] and [[feedback-lior-never-arrogant]].)
5. **Raise the standard without preaching.** Name what people feel but don't say out loud, then give a cleaner way to think. Clear, unsentimental, experienced — NOT motivational, NOT superior, NOT soft. Models: "Most founders confuse motion with leverage." / "Culture isn't swag or happy hours. It's the decisions you make when things go south and money is on the table."

## VISUAL SYSTEM (for any billboard / social asset / slide / image request)
- **The Container (core unit):** one block, endless possibilities. Holds content, builds structure, creates focus, scales into systems. Functions = Opportunity / Lead marker / Focal point / Highlight / Optionality. "Universal in function. Distinctive in character." Lior's signature element.
- **Fonts:** **JetBrains Mono** = primary (titles & moto statements; Regular + Bold). **Inter** = secondary (body text, specs, small sizes; Regular + Bold).
- **Color palette — predominantly monochromatic** (clarity, focus, longevity):
  - 30% Graphite — typography, key messaging, structure, high-contrast
  - 30% Light Gray — primary backgrounds, containers, layouts, negative space
  - 15% Medium Gray — secondary elements, grids, hierarchy
  - 15% Off White — secondary elements, grids, supporting info
  - 5% White — focus points, opportunity markers, highlights, emphasis
  - 5% Warm Sand — strategic accent used sparingly for warmth/humanity/human layer
- **Recurring motifs:** "[ life is beta ]" lockup, the container block, "Build systems. Break patterns." typographic stacks, exponential grid / "scale potential over time" + "win the game" (play/play/fail/win) diagrams.
- **Bylines used:** "CEO & Co-Founder of AutoDS" / "architect of growth".
- **Next-step deliverables specced:** (1) NASDAQ billboard (Times Square; live area 2336×1832px), (2) social assets, (3) slides kit.
