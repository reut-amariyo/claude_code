AutoDS employees did not hold equity or options. Reut, 2026-09-11, on the Miro/Bending Spoons X brief: do not mention this, but also never write anything that implies they had it.

**Why:** the first draft said "pay people in cash first, equity second" and "a number your team will have to grow into to be even", both of which read as if AutoDS staff held options. A public claim like that is checkable and false.

**How to apply:** in any post on equity, options, valuation, M&A or exits, Lior's standing is the no-investors / no-preference-stack story and protecting jobs, see [[lior-story-pe-offer-refused-day-before]]. Say "the people who built the company", "my team", "jobs"; never "their options", "their equity", "their shares". Audit every equity-topic draft for the implication, not only the word.
