Lior's own cold-DM structure, confirmed by what Reut actually sent to videographer Jasim Abudan on 2026-08-27 (IG) and by his Noelle sponsorship DM:

1. `Hey bro, I'm Lior, CEO of AutoDS (acquired by Fiverr), the #1 Shopify AI store builder.`
2. One line of genuine praise for their work, then the ask: what he's building and what role he needs.
3. The concept in one sentence + the money signal: `I like long-term partnerships, and have the budget for it.`
4. Close: `Would you be interested?` and nothing after it.

Rules that differ from post drafts:
- **Parentheses are allowed in DMs.** `(acquired by Fiverr)` is his signature credential format. The vault no-parentheses rule is a POST rule, not a DM rule. See [[feedback-no-parentheses]].
- Salutation is `Hey bro`, not the person's first name.
- First person singular: "I like long-term partnerships", not "we".
- No call-to-action stacking. Do NOT append "happy to jump on a call" - the question IS the close.
- Keep the exit number out; "acquired by Fiverr" only. See [[lior-exit-amount-92m-vs-sec-filing]].
- Short bubbles with blank lines between them, phone-readable.

Context of the Jasim DM: Lior is launching a YouTube channel with Curtis Tilbury, vlogs from global events with the influencers he works with, and needs a high-end shooter/editor. See [[project-lior-channels]].

Note: in the sent version the "#1 Shopify AI store builder" descriptor landed on AutoDS instead of BuildYourStore. If reusing the line, keep BuildYourStore named when the descriptor is used.
