Reut, 2026-08-24: "אתה כותב מלא ואני לא מבינה איפה מסתתרת התשובה". Answer in 1-3 lines. Put the
actual answer in the first line, before any reasoning.

**Why:** she works fast between drafts, platforms and approvals. A paragraph of rationale around
the answer costs her time to parse and she stops finding the deliverable in it.

**How to apply:** lead with the deliverable (the line, the number, the decision). Add reasoning
only if she asks, or compress it to one short sentence after the answer. Options get 1 line each,
never a paragraph each. Long-form belongs in the vault file, not in chat. Related:
[[feedback-close-means-end-task]], [[feedback-linkedin-comments-keep-short]].
