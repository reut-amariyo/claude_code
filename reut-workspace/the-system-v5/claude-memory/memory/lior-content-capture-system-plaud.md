Lior records every meeting with a Plaud AI recorder (Reut, 2026-09-02). Meetings, arguments with the team, podcasts and interviews are the raw-material layer for his content; he does not start from a blank page.

**Why:** it answers the standing CEO objection "I don't have time for a personal brand". The bottleneck is raw material, not writing, and recording removes it. It also fits [[feedback-lior-dyslexia-no-reading-claims]] - voice in, never typing.

**How to apply:** when drafting content-system or "how do you find the time" posts for Lior, the capture stack is recorded meetings + voice notes + mined podcast recordings, and his own time cost is framed as a small weekly approval slot, not creation time. Name Plaud at most once as an aside so it stays an operator confession and not a tool tip ([[feedback-no-productivity-tool-tips]]).
