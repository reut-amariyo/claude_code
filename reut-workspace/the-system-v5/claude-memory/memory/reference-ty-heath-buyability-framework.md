Source: Devon Reed podcast with Ty Heath, global director of thought leadership go-to-market at LinkedIn, co-founder of the B2B Institute. Transcript shared by Reut 2026-08-10; full analysis in the-system-v5/M-memory/learning-log.md (2026-08-10 entry).

Core data (citable, high-authority):
- 40% of B2B buying decisions end in NO decision — the group never felt confident enough to say yes. B2B Institute's multi-year research focus.
- Buying groups grew from ~10 to up to 22 people.
- Hidden buyers (legal, finance, procurement) hold ~50% of decision power and never meet sales.

Buyability = 3 signals: Risk (make yes feel safe, arm the internal champion), Visibility (be known to hidden buyers too), Proof ("someone like me chose this and was OK").

Key quotes/concepts: "LinkedIn is the conversation before the conversation" (profile checked before any meeting); exec reframe = count what you lose by NOT posting; 10-5-5 cadence (10 min notice / 5 min post / 5 min engage — ops use only, never a Lior post topic per [[feedback-no-productivity-tool-tips]]); AI to sharpen not to think; useful beats entertaining but conviction makes it travel; upcoming study "The Credibility Code" + creator stack (brand + employees + customers + creators).
