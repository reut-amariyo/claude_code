# Diary of a CEO: the founder-story door is closed, enter through AI

Built 2026-09-23 after Reut's 17 Sep call with FlightStory. Full research, running order and
copy: `the-system-v5/O-output/doac-pitch/`. Pitch page: https://claude.ai/artifact/4fVHFovDYBB1euHmtxhc95

**The finding that decides everything:** DOAC does not book founders to tell business stories
anymore. Per the audit at doac.audits.com, founders and execs fell from 28-33% of guests to 13%
by 2024, and business as a topic collapsed from a 42% peak in mid-2022 to **1% by 2026**.
Scientists and academics are 31%, health 30%, money 13%, AI 13%, geopolitics 7%. Era 3, from
Apr 2025, also invented multi-guest debate panels.

**Why:** the 17 Sep brief pitched "bootstrapped founder, 200 employees, sold to a public
company." That is an Era 1 pitch aimed at the 1%. It gets a polite no, not because Lior is wrong
but because there is no slot shaped like it.

**How to apply:**
- Enter through **AI** with the Avatar Factory reveal, or through **money and class** as the live
  counter-example to their own "the ladder is gone" thesis. Never through the company journey.
- **Ask for a debate seat first.** It costs them one chair and it is the cheapest yes available.
- Their title grammar, measured over 199 episodes from 2025-26: 93% colon, 81% exclamation, 50%
  a credential word, 43% "you", and only 50% name the guest. **They sell the claim, not the
  person.** Offer titles, not a bio.
- They publish a **Most Replayed Moment** every Friday. Hand them the chapter that will be it.
- Closing tradition: the guest leaves a handwritten question for the next one. Lior's is written:
  "What was the last no you accepted without checking whether it was true?" It comes straight from
  [[lior-podcast-99-lo-100-canonical-stories]] and should stay his.
- Exit figure in anything their research team will check is **"a reported $92 million"**, per
  [[lior-exit-amount-92m-vs-sec-filing]]. Their researchers will find the 20-F.
- Two consent items before the link goes out: the arrest and house-arrest story needs Lior's
  wording and age confirmed, and naming **Avatar Factory** is a deliberate exception to
  [[project-lior-avatar-factory-side-project]] and needs his explicit yes.

**The reusable lesson beyond this pitch:** measure a media property's last 6-12 months of actual
output before pitching it. A show's name and reputation can describe a format it has already
abandoned.
