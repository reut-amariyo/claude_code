# LinkedIn Q1 2026 Trends — Hypotheses for Lior

**Source:** External creator analysis shared 2026-05-04. Industry-wide LinkedIn benchmarks, NOT Lior's own data.

**Why:** Per [lior-linkedin-data-rules.md](lior-linkedin-data-rules.md), external patterns are hypotheses; Lior's analyst data overrides. But these are concrete Q1 2026 numbers worth testing — and several reverse advice from a year ago.

**How to apply:** When drafting a Lior LinkedIn post, use these as a tiebreaker when Lior's own data is silent on the question. If they conflict with Lior's analyst log, follow Lior's data.

---

## The 8 Trends

### 1. Follower count matters less in Q1 2026
- Q4 2025: accounts with 100k+ followers grew 17.6x faster
- Q1 2026: 10-25k creators are catching up
- **Apply:** Lior's ~30k follower band is now competitive. Don't assume he needs to "catch up" to mega-accounts — quality content compounds at his size.

### 2. Niche beats broad
- Specific, interest-led infographics pulling 100k+ views
- Relatable/wellness/remote-work topics losing reach
- **Apply:** Already aligns with CEO Relevance Filter. Lean harder into SaaS scaling, pricing, founder ops, AI-tools-tested-in-production. Cut "wellness" / "leadership in general" angles unless bridged to a founder lesson.

### 3. Posting frequency compounds
- 6x/week creators get 3.1x more views per post (vs 2-3x/week)
- **Apply:** Hypothesis only — Lior's bandwidth is the constraint. Don't push frequency for its own sake. But if Lior wants reach growth, the lever is cadence, not better single posts.

### 4. Claude freebies > GPT freebies as lead magnets
- "Claude freebies are outperforming everything else across every niche"
- Some posts hitting 4,000-5,500+ comments
- **Apply:** When Lior wants to do a "give a thing" post, the asset should be a Claude prompt/skill/template, not a ChatGPT one. AutoDS-relevant Claude workflows = strong angle.

### 5. Infographics are the dominant format
- Q1 2026: infographics = 29% of all top 1% posts
- Carousels falling behind
- **Apply:** When briefing visuals, default to single-image infographic over carousel. Conflicts somewhat with [linkedin-visuals-library.md](linkedin-visuals-library.md) — flag this when next briefing visuals.
- **CAVEAT:** Lior's data ([lior-linkedin-data-rules.md](lior-linkedin-data-rules.md) Rule 4) says graph/chart images HURT his performance. So "infographic" for Lior ≠ chart-of-numbers. It means a designed single-image with a clear visual point — not a Bloomberg screenshot.

### 6. Voice-note style, 1,250-3,000 characters wins
- Posts at 1,250-3,000 chars perform 228% better than short formulaic posts
- Tone: conversational, slightly imperfect, like a "well-structured voice note"
- **Apply:** Already aligns with the "Dear son / Love, Dad" rule. Length target = 1,250-3,000 chars (NOT super-short power posts). Slightly imperfect > polished.

### 7. Links in the post (NOT in comments) — REVERSAL
- Old rule: "links in comments" to protect reach
- Q1 2026: 3+ external links IN the post = 441% higher reach
- **Apply:** When a Lior post needs a citation/source/podcast link, put it in the post body. Stop asking him to drop links in the first comment. **Test this on the next 3 link-bearing posts and let Lior's analyst confirm.**

### 8. Native video building its own ecosystem
- LinkedIn native video creators forming a distinct culture (vs. just trust-reinforcement)
- **Apply:** If Lior wants to expand to video on LinkedIn, the platform now rewards it natively. Different angle than "repurpose IG Reels to LinkedIn" — needs LinkedIn-native talking-head or studio content.
