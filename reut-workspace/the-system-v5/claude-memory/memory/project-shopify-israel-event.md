The non-obvious parts worth keeping:

- **The winning ask is not "let's do an event."** Shopify's Partner Town Hall runs IRL in Toronto + NYC and live-streams everywhere else. The ask is "Tel Aviv should be the next IRL room, AutoDS funds it, Shopify sends the stream and one speaker." Near-zero cost for Shopify, precedented, and Atlee Clark (VP Partnerships) publicly said she wants more of these.
- **Liam Griffin is the right first contact** (Reut named him, and it checks out): Developer Advocate / community, Galway Ireland, posts the Partner Town Hall announcements himself as "Liam-Shopify", organised DotDev 2026, amplifies community-run meetups. EMEA time zone. Engage his posts before DMing; he is a community person.
- **Emilie Benoit-Vernay** = Director of Partnerships EMEA, the regional "yes". Israel sits in EMEA. Backups: Apsara Chidambaram (Head of Alliances EMEA), Aoife Dowling (Head of Global Agency Group Partnerships), Deann Evans (MD EMEA).
- **Route 0 beats every cold DM**: AutoDS is Shopify's #1 partner 2024+2025, so someone at Shopify already owns the relationship. Ask them for the intro first.
- **No publicly named Shopify Israel country lead exists.** Don't invent one. Shopify's Israel footprint is R&D (Donde Search, 2021), not partnerships.
- An Israeli Shopify summit already exists: Shoptimize Israel, Eretz Israel Museum. Someone already stands in that space.
- DM deviation from [[lior-dm-outreach-template]]: first name instead of "Hey bro" (corporate contacts, not creators), and no budget line — "I have the budget" reads as buying access when the counterparty is the platform.

Copy discipline per [[feedback-shopify-affiliate-and-partner]]: "Shopify's #1 partner in 2024 and 2025", never affiliate. "Acquired by Fiverr", no exit figure ([[lior-exit-amount-92m-vs-sec-filing]]).
