Minimize the word "just" in Lior drafts. Lior himself flagged it (via Reut, 2026-08-06, Shopify earnings post): "יש יותר מדי just". Hooks like "X just dropped/launched/shipped" pile up across drafts and inside single posts.

**Why:** It's a filler intensifier that reads as AI-ish/newsjack-template when repeated. Lior noticed it as a pattern across the copy, not one instance.

**How to apply:** Audit every draft for "just" before delivery — allow at most one, and only when recency is the point. Swap alternatives: "yesterday", "this week", drop the word entirely, or restructure the verb ("Meta dropped", "the report landed"). Related: [[feedback-anti-ai-writing-rules]].
