Lior declined an interview on Popcorn (פופקורן, Lior Frenkel's podcast) on 2026-08-09, even though it passed the standard vetting checklist (active, entrepreneurship in scope, strong story fit). Reason: the show is too "עממי" — broad mainstream career/self-development audience — and not the people he wants to know him.

**Why:** Lior optimizes podcast appearances for audience precision, not reach. The target listener is a founder, operator, or tech person — the Builders State of Mind ICP — not the general public building a career.

**How to apply:** Add a fourth filter to [[Podcast vetting checklist before recommending]]: after active/founder-topic/geography checks, ask "who actually listens?" A show can cover entrepreneurship and still fail if its core audience is general-public career builders. Prefer niche founder/tech/SaaS shows over big mainstream ones. When a show is borderline (big reach, mixed audience), flag the audience profile to Reut instead of recommending.
