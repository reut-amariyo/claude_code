# LinkedIn KPI changed: followers per month, not impressions

Reut's decision, 2026-08-23, after the Yonatan Raveh call. Impressions are demoted to a
diagnostic input. The win condition per post is no longer "10,000+ impressions".

**Why:** three findings, all from our own data.
1. Apr-Jul 2026 impressions moved 6x while net new followers never left a 922-1,100 band.
   May's two viral posts bought 240K extra impressions and ~91 extra followers.
2. The two best-converting months were the two LOWEST-reach months. April 18.7 and June 19.5
   followers per 1K impressions; May, the record-reach month, 3.8.
3. LinkedIn is 70K of the 7M/month company reach OKR, about 1%. IG and FB carry that number,
   so LinkedIn was never the place to buy reach.

**How to apply:**
- Monthly post mix is a budget: 60% conversion posts (personal artifact, real past failure,
  operator confession, market empathy), 30% niche authority, 10% reach plays (~1 Archetype 4
  newsjack, capped not defaulted).
- [[lior-top-8-10k-playbook]] archetype ranking inverts: 8 and 9 promoted, 4 demoted. That
  re-ranking uses engagement rate as a PROXY for follower rate; it is a hypothesis until one
  month of daily follower-delta attribution confirms it. If the data contradicts it, data wins.
- Pre-publish gate has a new mandatory row: "why would a stranger who has never heard of Lior
  press Follow after reading this?" No good answer = it is a reach play, ships only in the 10%.
- ⚠️ CORRECTED 2026-08-23 by Reut, same day: do NOT reason only from Lior's own data. It is
  4 months and ~50 posts and can only show variance inside what we already do. Look at the
  accounts that HAVE the followers (Chris Donnelly 20K->1M+ in ~2yrs, Rowan Cheung, Zain Kahn,
  Allie K. Miller, Justin Welsh) and take what they share.
- What they share: a follow is a SUBSCRIPTION decision. Every one of them runs a signature
  series on a fixed rhythm, states the promise in the profile headline, posts 3-5x/week,
  sells renewable utility ("stay updated") over one-off narrative, and ships saveable artifacts.
- So the primary follower engine is a SIGNATURE SERIES, not personal posts. Personal/artifact
  posts are the affinity layer around it.
- "Volume is not a lever" (my earlier claim from July's 21 posts -> 821 followers) is WITHDRAWN.
  July had the frequency and neither prerequisite: no repeating format, no topic consistency.
  Avg imp/post fell 7,406 -> 3,418, which is a confused classifier. Ramp 3/week Sept, 5/week Q4.
- The guides objection is narrowed: a single tutorial's 0.09% ER says nothing about a RECURRING
  format, because recurrence is the variable. What survives: an unnamed stream of generic AI
  guides is the Educator lane and we lose to the AI-newsletter accounts on speed and staffing.
- Take the MECHANISM from the big accounts, never the voice. Donnelly's voice is motivational,
  which is the Motivator archetype and banned for Lior. They are media businesses; he is a CEO.
  His unfair advantage is ACCESS, not curation: recommended spine is "the operator's read on
  AI commerce", weekly fixed day, verdict from inside a 250-person commerce company.
- Steerable model: followers = impressions x (profile visits / impressions) x (follows / visits).
  Monthly profile visits have NEVER been pulled and are now blocking, per [[project-500k-ig-100k-linkedin-plan]].
- Baseline 963/month. Q3 KR unchanged (13.5K by Sep 30, on track). Q4 ladder proposed to Lior:
  1,400 / 1,800 / 2,200. Organic 100K lands 2029; paid or the IG-to-LinkedIn port is the only
  way to pull it earlier.

- **MAIN AXIS = SAVE-FIRST (Reut, 2026-08-23). Mix 3 save-first : 1 personal.** A save is a bet
  on future utility, not enjoyment. Every save-first post carries a concrete enumerable artifact.
  Verified by reading Chris Donnelly (1.25M) and Jasmin Alić (374K) live: Donnelly's save engine
  is a lead-magnet giveaway (prompt pack, comment-to-receive) which is BANNED for Lior; Jasmin's
  engine is actually COMMENTS not saves (comment/reaction ratio 0.53-0.66, extraordinary), but his
  LINE CRAFT is the thing to steal (798-1,093 chars, one idea per line, 5-item lists).
  Take the trigger, never the lead-magnet layer.
  Spec: `the-system-v5/O-output/linkedin-save-first-content-spec-2026-08-23.md`

- 🔑 **BIGGEST FINDING (2026-08-23, measured live): the mega-accounts IMPORTED their followers
  from another channel.** Zain Kahn has 1,001,483 followers and his last 5 posts drew 38-121
  reactions, ~6 per 100K, the weakest in the sample. His posts are newsletter AD CASE STUDIES,
  not audience content. His headline is a follow CTA ("Follow me to learn how you can leverage
  AI..."), his banner is SUPERHUMAN, and Hamna Aslam Kahn (280K) runs the SAME headline formula
  pointing at the same 1M-reader newsletter. It is a multi-account network feeding one list.
  Allie K. Miller (2M, 17 per 100K) looks identical: her headline stacks a 400K-student course
  plus IG/X/TikTok. **Conclusion: the newsletter built the LinkedIn following, not the posts.**
  We own the same kind of asset: 100K+ Instagram vs 11.6K LinkedIn. The IG-to-LinkedIn port plus
  a follow-CTA headline is now the observed mechanism, not a hypothesis.
  🛑 Corollary: you cannot copy Zain Kahn's growth by copying his posts. That copies the dead half.
