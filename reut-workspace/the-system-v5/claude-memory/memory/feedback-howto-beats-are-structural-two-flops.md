Grok Bot migration post, published ~2026-09-14: 1,173 impressions, 35 reactions, 12 profile views,
**0 followers gained**. Teardown: the-system-v5/M-memory/linkedin-grok-bot-migration-teardown-2026-09-15.md.
Five days earlier the Astra co-founder post flopped the same way, and the rule had already been
written in [[feedback-linkedin-visual-one-artifact-not-diagram]] on 2026-09-13. I did not apply it.

The three posts share one skeleton, hook + 3 steps + close. Only the Codex post (25K) kept the beats
around the steps: trigger, permission line, operator confession, named cost of staying, a visual
with numbers the text did not have. Both flops shipped after those beats were trimmed. Two data
points, identical pattern: **a how-to without the Lior beats is a tool tip, and tool tips die on
his account.** This is a law now.

**Hit rate, July-September 2026 (September review, 2026-10-01): 1 in 5.** Codex migration 26,087
imp / 6 followers / 42 saves. The other four (Fable 5.1 2,428, 9 repos 2,203, Astra 1,966,
Grok Bot 3,499) totalled ~10K impressions and 1 follower. Even the hit converts poorly on
followers (0.23 per 1K). Treat AI how-tos as a reach play from the 10% budget, max ~1 per
2 weeks, and only with a real "why now" plus all four Lior beats.

**Why it kept happening:** the trims came from Reut, and [[feedback-never-revert-to-my-earlier-draft]]
says her deletions stay. Correct. But that rule also allows one sentence naming a real problem, and
the sentence "this cut removes the confession, which is one of the three things that make a how-to
legal on this account" was never said. Silence read as agreement. Then the gate ran once, on v24,
and nine later versions shipped ungated, which the Astra memory had already forbidden.

**How to apply:**
- The beat table is a structural check, run on the FINAL text, every time, before the word "ready".
  A version that lost a beat gets the table again, not "gate is clean".
- When an edit deletes the trigger, confession, permission or cost beat, say so once, in one
  sentence, at that moment: what was removed and what it does to the post's category. Then respect
  the decision. Never restore the text.
- A how-to needs a "why now". No launch, no number, no news = no out-of-network distribution.
- "Why would a stranger follow" is not answered by a procedure. It is answered by a first-party
  artifact only: his real bots, his real screenshot, his real number.
- The visual carries information the copy does not, or it is cut. A meme that restates the joke is
  the Astra flowchart again.
