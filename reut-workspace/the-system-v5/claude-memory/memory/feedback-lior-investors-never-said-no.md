Reut, 2026-09-16, correcting a TEDx pitch line "Investors said no, so I bootstrapped": this is FALSE. Fix it and remember it forever.

The true bootstrap story: AutoDS was profitable from the start, and Lior did not know raising money from investors was even an option. He came from no background and had zero connections in the startup world. No investor ever rejected him. Later, staying bootstrapped became a choice ("not my style, no time for decks", per [[lior-podcast-99-lo-100-canonical-stories]]).

**Why:** the "rejected by VCs" arc is a common founder trope that AI reaches for, and it rewrites Lior's actual story. His no-investors fact is about profitability + ignorance of the option, which is a stronger and more honest positioning than a rejection story.

**How to apply:** in any draft, bio, pitch, or talk that touches funding, never phrase bootstrapping as a no he received from investors. Use: "profitable from day one, never raised a dollar, didn't know raising was a thing." Related: [[project-lior-book-bestseller]], [[lior-pozin-os]], [[feedback-autods-employees-no-equity]].
