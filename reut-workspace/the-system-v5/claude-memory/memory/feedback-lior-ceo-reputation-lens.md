On 2026-10-27, in the IKEA / AutoDS AI-layoffs post, I offered "did we do enough?" and then "Next time, before we cut, I'll look for the role that could help us grow." Reut rejected both: "People will read it as he didn't think enough before he cut people. It can give him a bad reputation. Think before you offer me stuff."

**Why:** Lior is a CEO. A confession can show feeling, like "we sadly let people go", but it can't suggest he was careless, didn't consider alternatives, or failed his people. Readers fill in the worst version, and former employees can confirm it in the comments.

**How to apply:** test every vulnerable or reflective line before offering it. Ask: "Could a reader conclude he decided carelessly or didn't do his job?" If yes, cut it. Safe moves are acknowledging the cost with feeling, crediting another company's model, or stating a principle. Avoid regret questions, "next time I'll...", and "I missed...". Don't put claims about his decision process in his mouth without confirming them. Related: [[feedback-lior-no-imposter-syndrome]], [[feedback-lior-never-arrogant]].
