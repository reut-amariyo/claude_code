Reut's own receipts under her עוסק פטור business ("רעות דולה") are issued through **SUMIT** (sumit.co.il).
Confirmed from the PDF footer of receipts #80003 / #80004 (Aug 2026, in ~/Downloads):
"מסמך ממוחשב זה הופק באמצעות SUMIT".

Do not confuse with iCount, which appears in AutoDS corporate decks as the *company's* invoicing tool.

Practical note: an issued Israeli חשבון/קבלה cannot have its date edited after the fact — it has to be
cancelled (מסמך ביטול) and reissued with the correct date.
