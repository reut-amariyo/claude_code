# Viral post inspiration must be RECENT

**Rule:** When Reut asks for viral LinkedIn posts / AI posts / inspiration / trending content — the default time window is **the last 12-24 hours**, not "any time." Older posts are only useful when explicitly asked about historical patterns.

**Why:** Reut runs a live content operation. She uses viral inspiration to draft posts TODAY. A year-old post has:
- Stale news/tools embedded in it
- Different LinkedIn algo behavior
- Lower shareability if referenced (audience will notice)
- No current-moment relevance

Presenting a year-old post as "here's a viral post you could adapt" reads as tone-deaf and wastes her time.

**How to apply:**
- Every time she says "viral post" / "trending" / "פוסט ויראלי" / "אקטואלי" / "trendy" — filter to LAST 12-24 HOURS by default
- Use LinkedIn's `datePosted="past-24h"` filter or scan recent activity of top creators
- If the freshest post I can find in a category is > 48h old, SAY SO explicitly — don't paper over it with older examples
- If nothing viral exists in the last 24h in a category, better to say "nothing hit in this format today" than surface older content
- Historical references are only appropriate when: (a) Reut explicitly asks about a past post, (b) I'm citing a proven-formula example alongside fresh ones (like Lior's own 30K post as a template), or (c) she asks about "patterns over time"

**Context:** Reut flagged this after I served a Fredrik Hjelm post from 1 year ago as a "viral AI example" and other year-old posts. She said: "הבאת לי פוסט מלפני שנה — זו אף פעם לא הכוונה שלי כשאני נותנת לך משימה כזאת. אני רוצה פוסטים מה-12 שעות האחרונות שיהיה אקטואלי."

**Exception — Lior's own reference winners:**
Lior's own historical winners (e.g., his 30K Paperclip post from April) ARE legitimate to cite as *proven formulas for him* — they're his data, not just any viral post. Frame explicitly as "your proven archetype" not as "here's a viral post."
