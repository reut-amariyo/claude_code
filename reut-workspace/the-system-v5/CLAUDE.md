## LinkedIn — leading reference

### KPI (changed 2026-08-23 by Reut): net new FOLLOWERS per month

LinkedIn is no longer judged on impressions. Impressions are a diagnostic input.
Reason: across Apr-Jul 2026 impressions moved 6x while net new followers stayed inside a
922-1,100 band, and the two best-converting months were the two LOWEST-reach months.
LinkedIn is also only 1% of the 7M/month company reach OKR, so it was never the place to
buy reach. Baseline 963 followers/month. Q4 ladder proposed: 1,400 / 1,800 / 2,200.
Full strategy: O-output/linkedin-follower-kpi-strategy-2026-08-23.md

Consequences for drafting:
- Monthly post mix is a budget: 60% conversion posts (personal artifact, real past failure,
  operator confession, market empathy), 30% niche authority posts, 10% reach plays
  (~1 Archetype 4 newsjack/month, capped not defaulted).
- Archetype ranking inverts: Archetypes 8 and 9 are promoted, Archetype 4 is a budgeted
  reach play. This is inferred from engagement rate as a proxy; it is a hypothesis until
  one month of daily follower-delta attribution confirms it.
- Frequency IS a lever, but only behind a repeating format and a consistent topic.
  Corrected 2026-08-23 by Reut: July's 21 posts underperformed because they were 21 scattered
  one-offs, not because volume is bad. Outside benchmarks: 3-5 posts/week is the band, 5x/week
  reportedly grows ~12x faster than 1-2x. Ramp: 3/week in Sept, 5/week in Q4.
- The primary follower engine is a SIGNATURE SERIES on a fixed rhythm, not personal posts.
  A follow is a subscription decision: people follow when they expect more and fear missing it.
  Personal/artifact posts are the affinity layer around that engine, not the engine.
- Recommended spine: "the operator's read on AI commerce", weekly fixed day. Others report the
  news, Lior delivers the verdict from inside a 200-person commerce company. Sells ACCESS, not
  curated information, which is the only version of this that clears the CEO relevance filter.
- Profile headline must state the promise of following, like a subscription pitch.
- **MAIN AXIS = SAVE-FIRST CONTENT (Reut, 2026-08-23). Mix is 3 save-first : 1 personal.**
  A save is a bet on FUTURE UTILITY, never a reaction to enjoyment. Every save-first post
  carries a concrete, enumerable, specific artifact the reader expects to need again.
  Five legal formats: decision checklist / threshold numbers he manages by / teardown
  scorecard where the criteria are the artifact / briefing with a "so what" list /
  anti-checklist of things he stopped doing. All operator-seat, zero tool tips.
  Line craft from Jasmin Alić: one idea per line, blank line between nearly every line,
  odd-numbered lists, every item useful alone. Save-first posts may run to 1,500 chars.
  Visual stays in the 4 families: the REAL artifact photographed or screenshotted, never a
  designed checklist card. Full spec: O-output/linkedin-save-first-content-spec-2026-08-23.md
- 🛑 Never copy the lead-magnet layer from Chris Donnelly or Jasmin Alić: no comment-to-receive,
  no ♻️ repost CTA, no ✅/👇 emoji manifests, no prompt packs. Verified 2026-08-23 by reading
  their live posts: that layer is creator/marketer content and it fails the CEO relevance filter.
- No stream of unnamed generic AI guides (Educator lane, we lose to the AI newsletter accounts).
  A NAMED recurring operator-judgment series is the opposite and is the recommendation.
- Win condition per post: 150+ net followers in the 48h after publish, provisional until
  recalibrated. It replaces "10,000+ impressions".

The Top 13 / 10K+ playbook wins over all older rules: 10 confirmed archetypes,
hooks under 10 words with a concrete anchor (exception: the two-beat anomaly
hook, where line 2 lands the anchor in under 6 words), always a "vs." in one sentence,
visuals only from the 4 families (third-party authority / personal artifact /
cultural meme / signed illustration) — never marketing-team graphics.
Mechanics: 750–1,200 chars, no arrow lists (→ killed three posts at ~800 imp),
no "Read that again" template phrases, leave an engagement gap open at the end.

### Platform mechanics added 2026-08-24 (source: Tom Orbach newsletter, his numbers)

- **AI-slop penalty is real.** LinkedIn's "seems like AI slop" report (3-dot menu) has 1M+ presses;
  slop-classified posts get ~40% fewer views, and "it's not X, it's Y" is reportedly auto-suppressed.
  The anti-AI rules in the vault-root CLAUDE.md are now a distribution rule, not a taste rule.
  After publishing, check post analytics for the AI-report warning; a flagged post explains a
  sudden reach drop that is neither throttling nor topic mismatch.
- **Hashtags control the post URL.** LinkedIn builds the permanent slug from the post's first line
  unless hashtags are present, then it uses the hashtags. Add exactly 2 on-pillar topical hashtags
  at the end of every post (#ecommerceautomation #aiagents, never #mondaymotivation). LinkedIn posts
  rank in Google and AI answers read Google, so the slug is a free machine signal. Pairs with ALT text.

### Pre-publish gate (mandatory before any go/no-go)

April 2026 shipped 10 failures out of 12 by skipping this. Audit every final
draft against: the non-negotiables above, the anti-AI rules in the vault-root
CLAUDE.md, archetype match, hook discipline, visual family, and the "vs." test.
Output a visible ✅/❌ table with a 🟢/🟡/🔴 verdict. Any hard-rule ❌ = do not
publish, no overrides.

**Added 2026-08-23, mandatory row in the table:** "Why would a stranger who has never heard
of Lior press Follow after reading this?" If the honest answer is "they would not, but it
will travel", the post is a reach play and only ships inside the 10% monthly reach budget.
