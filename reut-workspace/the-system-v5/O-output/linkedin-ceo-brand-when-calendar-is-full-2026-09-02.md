## DRAFT

--- POST STARTS ---

Apple's new CEO found time to post on his first day.

Running a 200 person company, my calendar says that should be impossible.

It is not. Writing was never the bottleneck. Raw material is.

Five things that keep it running through a full week:

1. Record every meeting.
A Plaud sits on the table. The line that traveled furthest last month came out of an argument with my team, never from a blank page.

2. Never start from a blank page.
Only write from a question someone actually asked you that week. Team, user, DM. If nobody asked, you have nothing to say yet.

3. Voice, not keyboard.
Three minutes talking in the car beats an hour staring at a doc. Say it once, shape it later.

4. One recording, five outputs.
Every podcast and interview gets mined for the lines you already said well. You said it once. Do not write it again.

5. Thirty minutes a week, on the calendar.
Not for creating. For approving. Everything else runs without you.

Most CEOs treat a personal brand as a project. It only works as a routine that survives a bad week.

What is the smallest slot you could protect for this?
