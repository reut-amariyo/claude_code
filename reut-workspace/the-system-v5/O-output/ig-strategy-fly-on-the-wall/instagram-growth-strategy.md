## Part 3: The Raw Moment Capture System

**The core problem:** Reut creates the content but doesn't have access to Lior's real unscripted moments. The agency creates polished graphics because raw material doesn't flow in consistently. This system creates that flow.

### 3A. Lior's Daily "Voice Drop" — 2 Minutes, Every Day

**What:** Lior records a 60-120 second voice note or selfie video at the end of his workday.

**When:** Fixed time — right after his last meeting or during the drive home. Same time every day so it becomes habit.

**Where:** A dedicated WhatsApp chat or Telegram channel (just Lior + Reut). NOT in a group. NOT via email. Minimal friction.

**The "5 Prompts" — Lior picks 1-2 daily:**

1. **"What decision did I make today that I'm not 100% sure about?"**
   → Uncertainty from a CEO = magnetic content. The "truth" Reel (top follower-driver) worked because it showed depth, not certainty.

2. **"What surprised me today?"**
   → Unexpected things are inherently interesting. A CEO being surprised = access.

3. **"What did I say to my team today that I actually needed to hear myself?"**
   → Self-awareness content. This is what the "business builds YOU" Reel tapped into.

4. **"What number did I look at today and what did it make me feel?"**
   → The "$30,000" Reel worked because it had a specific number. Revenue, churn, tokens, headcount — numbers + emotion = engagement.

5. **"What's one thing that happened today that nobody outside my office would ever see?"**
   → This IS the fly-on-the-wall moment. The "2016" origin story worked because it showed something private.

**Rules for Lior:**
- Don't try to be insightful. Just talk.
- Messy is good. Reut will shape it.
- If nothing happened, say "nothing happened today" — that's also content.
- Do NOT filter for what's "Instagram-worthy." That's Reut's job.
- Video > voice note. Even a shaky selfie video is usable footage.

### 3B. Reut's "Shadow Hours" — 2 Hours/Week

**What:** Reut spends 2 hours/week physically near Lior with phone camera ready.

**When:** During high-energy moments:
- Team standups / all-hands
- When Lior is working through a problem at his desk (this is what "2016" captured — and it got 33K views)
- Walking between meetings
- Lunch with co-founders or VPs
- Right after a big call (the exhale moment)

**What to capture:**
- B-roll: Lior's hands on keyboard, whiteboard scribbles, office chaos, walking with phone
- Micro-moments: Lior laughing, frustrated face, thinking pause
- Audio snippets: Lior explaining something to a team member
- Environment: the office, screens, the car, the view from his window

**This is NOT a photoshoot.** The "2016" Reel is the #1 most-viewed post because it looks like someone casually filmed Lior at his desk. That's the energy.

### 3C. The "Screenshot Drop"

**What:** Lior screenshots interesting things from his day and drops them in the shared chat.

**Examples:**
- A Slack message where he gave tough feedback
- A dashboard showing a metric that moved
- A calendar showing back-to-back meetings
- An AI output that was surprisingly good or bad
- A hiring rejection email he agonized over

**Why:** Screenshots = proof. The content that drives follows is content that proves Lior lives what he talks about.

## Part 4: The Content Pillars (Instagram-Specific)

### Pillar 1: "Behind the Decision" (40% of content)
The moment BEFORE and AFTER a business decision. Not the polished takeaway — the messy middle.

**Why it works for followers:** This is content only a real CEO can make. Nobody can fake this.

**Format:** Reel (30-60 sec), Lior talking to camera or walking
**Source:** Voice Drops prompt #1 and #4
**Example hooks:**

- "Today I had to choose between hiring 3 senior devs or 6 juniors for the same budget."
- "I just looked at our churn numbers. We lost 200 customers this month. And honestly? I think that's a good thing."
- "My VP told me I'm wrong. In front of the whole team. Here's what I did."
- "We're burning $X on a feature that 4% of users use. I'm killing it tomorrow."

### Pillar 2: "CEO Reality Check" (25% of content)
The unglamorous truth of running a company. What the day actually looks like.

**Why it works for followers:** This fulfills the "fly on the wall" promise. It's why people followed after the "2016" Reel — they wanted more access.

**Format:** Story series (raw footage) or Reel
**Source:** Shadow Hours + Screenshot Drops
**Examples:**

- Lior's actual calendar for the day — "11 meetings. 0 deep work blocks."
- Timelapse of Lior's desk from morning to evening
- The walk from the parking lot to his office — what's he thinking about?
- Screenshot of his phone screen time + "This is what CEO life does to your screen time"

### Pillar 3: "What I'm Testing Right Now" (20% of content)
Real-time experiments with AI, tools, processes — shown as they happen, not after they succeed.

**Why it works for followers:** The "AI Levels" Reel (4 follows) and AutoDS content (4 follows) worked because they showed Lior's actual expertise in action.

**Format:** Reel (screen recording + voiceover) or Story series
**Source:** Screenshot Drops + Voice Drops prompt #2
**Example:**

- "I gave my team an AI challenge this week. Here's what happened."
- Screen recording of Lior using a tool + honest reaction
- "We switched from [tool X] to [tool Y]. Day 3 results."

### Pillar 4: "Unfiltered Take" (15% of content)
Lior's honest reaction to industry news — but through HIS lens, not as generic commentary.

**Why it works for followers:** The "truth" Reel (20 follows — best conversion) was philosophical, deep, and had a strong POV. Not a trend. An opinion.

**Format:** Reel (talking head, raw, minimal editing)
**Source:** Voice Drops prompt #2 and #3
**Rules:** MUST include personal experience. MUST have friction (not everyone agrees). MUST be something only Lior can credibly say.

- "Everyone's celebrating AI replacing jobs. I have 100 engineers. I'm not replacing any of them. But I AM changing what I expect them to deliver."
- "You don't need a co-founder. I've built two companies without one. Here's what nobody tells you about doing it solo."

## Part 5: Reel Structure — The "Lior Format"

Based on what converted to followers, here's the structure every Reel should follow:

### The 4-Beat Reel (30-60 seconds)

**Beat 1 — The Interrupt (0-2 sec)**
Visual: Lior mid-action (at desk, walking, in car). NOT standing still talking.
Audio: A line that creates tension or curiosity.
Examples:
- "I made a $40K mistake this week."
- "My VP told me I was wrong in front of 20 people."
- "I've been lying to my team about something."

**Beat 2 — The Context (3-15 sec)**
What happened. Quick, specific, with numbers.
- "We were choosing between two strategies for Q2. I picked the risky one. Here's why."

**Beat 3 — The Insight (15-40 sec)**
The real lesson — but delivered as a STORY, not a lecture.
- Not: "The lesson here is that leaders need to take risks."
- Yes: "Three of my VPs disagreed. I went ahead anyway. The first week, the numbers tanked. I almost reversed the decision. Week two, something shifted..."

**Beat 4 — The Open Loop (last 3-5 sec)**
End WITHOUT full resolution. This is what makes people follow — they want the next episode.
- "I'll tell you if it worked in a week."
- "Still don't know if I made the right call. What would you do?"
- "Part 2 coming when the numbers come in."

### Why Open Loops Drive Follows
The current content gives complete takeaways. People consume them and leave satisfied — no reason to follow. An open loop creates a reason to come back. "I need to follow to see what happened next."

## Part 5B: The Stories System — The Daily Relationship Engine

Reels bring new people in. **Stories keep them.** Stories are the #1 tool for building the "I feel like I know this person" relationship that prevents unfollows and drives DM conversations (which the algorithm rewards heavily).

### Why Stories Matter More Than You Think

- Instagram ranks accounts by "relationship score." Stories interactions (replies, poll votes, reactions) are the strongest signal.
- A follower who watches your Stories daily is 5-8x more likely to see your Reels in their feed.
- Stories are where the "fly on the wall" concept lives most naturally — they're raw, temporary, and feel private.
- DM conversations triggered by Stories are the #1 algorithm signal for relationship strength. More DMs = more reach on everything.

### Stories Volume: 5-10 frames/day, every day

Not 2-3. Not "when we have something." Every single day, including weekends. Stories are how you stay at the top of the feed and top of mind.

### The 5 Story Types (Rotate Daily)

#### Type 1: "The Fly on the Wall" (Use 3-4x/week)
Raw, unscripted moments from Lior's day. No text overlay needed. No editing. Just post.

**Examples:**
- 10-second clip of Lior walking into the office, no talking
- Lior's hand scrolling through Slack on his phone
- The view from his desk at 7 AM before anyone arrives
- Lior on a call (muted audio, text overlay: "Hour 3 of back-to-back calls")
- His coffee cup + laptop + mess on the desk — no caption, just the reality
- Lior laughing at something off-camera
- The office whiteboard after a strategy session (zoomed in so it's slightly readable)

**Source:** Shadow Hours footage + Lior's own phone camera (takes 5 seconds to capture)

**Why it works:** These feel like the viewer is literally there. No performance, no message, just *access*. This is what builds the parasocial relationship that creates loyal followers.

#### Type 2: "The Screenshot Story" (Use 2-3x/week)
A screenshot from Lior's real work, with 1-2 lines of context.

**Examples:**
- Screenshot of his calendar: "This is what Tuesday looks like. 9 meetings. Send help."
- Screenshot of a Slack message he sent to his team (blurred names if needed): "I sent this to my team at 11 PM. Too much?"
- Screenshot of an AI output: "Asked Claude to write our investor update. Here's what it came up with. Would you send this?"
- Screenshot of his phone screen time report: "13 hours. And I'm supposed to be the guy who has it all figured out."
- Screenshot of a hiring pipeline: "47 applicants. We'll interview 5. The hardest part of being a CEO isn't making decisions — it's saying no 42 times."

**Source:** Screenshot Drops from the WhatsApp chat

**Why it works:** Screenshots are the most believable format on Instagram. They can't be faked. They feel like a friend showing you their phone.

#### Type 3: "The Engagement Driver" (Use 1-2x/day — EVERY day)
Stories with interactive stickers: polls, sliders, Q&A boxes, quizzes. These are not optional — they're the algorithm fuel.

**Polls (this or that):**
- "I have to fire someone today. Should I do it in person or over Zoom?"
- "New feature launch: spend $20K on ads or give it to 100 influencers for free?"
- "My VP says we should hire 10 juniors. I say 3 seniors. Who's right?"
- "Morning person or night owl? (I'm answering calls at 6 AM whether I like it or not)"
- "Should a CEO respond to every customer complaint? Yes / That's what managers are for"

**Sliders:**
- "How burned out are you this week? 🔥" (put Lior's answer as the story after)
- "Rate my desk setup" (show his actual messy desk)
- "How much would you pay for this AI tool?" (show a screenshot of something he's testing)

**Q&A Box (use 1-2x/week):**
- "I'm running a 250-person company. Ask me anything."
- "What's the hardest part of being a founder? Ask and I'll answer honestly."
- "I'm testing 3 AI tools this week. Which one should I review?"

**Quiz:**
- "How much did my first company make in year 1?" (give 4 options — the real answer surprises people)
- "How many meetings did I have yesterday?" (show the answer in next Story)

**Why it works:** Every poll vote, slider move, and Q&A reply is an algorithm signal. It tells Instagram "this person cares about Lior's content" → show them more Reels. This is the growth flywheel.

#### Type 4: "The Reaction" (Use 2-3x/week)
Lior reacts to something in real-time. A 10-15 second selfie video, raw, unedited.

**Examples:**
- Just walked out of a tough meeting: "That was... intense. Can't talk about it yet but wow."
- Saw a competitor's announcement: "Just saw [competitor] launched X. My honest reaction..." (10 seconds, raw)
- A metric just changed: "Churn just dropped 2%. Doesn't sound like much but that's $X/month. I might cry."
- Reading a bad review: "Someone left this review about AutoDS. And honestly... they're right."
- An AI surprise: "I just asked Claude to do X and it did something I didn't expect. Look at this." (show screen)

**Source:** Voice Drops (the short ones that aren't full Reel material) + spontaneous moments

**Why it works:** This is the CEO equivalent of a friend texting you "you won't believe what just happened." People come back to Stories to see what Lior is reacting to today.

#### Type 5: "The Bridge" (Use 1x/day on Reel days)
Stories that push people to the latest Reel or create anticipation for the next one.

**Before a Reel drops:**
- "Posting something later today that I almost didn't share. Stay tuned."
- "I had a conversation with my VP yesterday that changed how I think about [X]. Reel coming today."
- Behind-the-scenes of filming: "My content person is making me talk about [X]. Here goes." (humanizes the process)

**After a Reel drops:**
- Share the Reel to Stories with a poll: "Would you have made the same decision?" or "Agree or disagree?"
- Add context that didn't make the Reel: "What I didn't say in that Reel is..."
- Show a reaction to comments: screenshot a good comment + "This person gets it" or "Interesting take, I disagree because..."

**Why it works:** This creates a Story-to-Reel loop. Story viewers go watch the Reel. Reel engagement goes up. Algorithm pushes the Reel to more people. More people discover the profile. More follows.

## Part 6: The Lior On-Camera System

Lior needs direction on camera. Here's the system to make it easy:

### Before Filming
- Reut gives Lior a 1-sentence brief: "Tell me about the hiring decision from yesterday. Start with what happened, not what you learned."
- NO script. Bullet points maximum (3 bullets on a sticky note).
- Lior should NOT look at the camera for the first 2 seconds. Start mid-action (typing, walking, looking at phone) then look up.

### During Filming
- Film 2-3 takes of each Reel. First take is warmup. Second is usually the best.
- If Lior's energy drops, Reut asks a follow-up question off-camera: "Wait, what did you actually SAY to them?" — this reignites natural energy.
- Let Lior talk past the "end." The best content often comes AFTER he thinks he's done.

### Settings
- Always vertical (9:16)
- Natural lighting only. Sit near a window or use office lights.
- Phone at eye level (stack books if needed)
- Background: real environment (office, car, desk with screens). NEVER a blank wall.

### On-Camera Don'ts
- Never start with "Hey guys" or "So today I want to talk about..."
- Never look directly at camera and deliver a "lesson" — that's guru mode
- Never film in a studio setup unless it's for a specific series

- Subtitles on every Reel (large, bold, high contrast — white text with black outline)
- Natural lighting. Office light > ring light.
- Show Lior's real environment — this IS the content
- Freeze-frame thumbnails with 3-5 word curiosity text
- Quick cuts only when energy drops. Let strong moments breathe.
- First frame = Lior mid-action (not standing still staring at camera)

### DON'T:
- No agency-designed graphic templates (proven zero followers)
- No stock music. Trending audio OR Lior's voice only.
- No heavy color grading. Real skin tones.
- No corporate visuals (graphs, charts, infographics)
- No "LinkedIn carousel repackaged for Instagram" designs
- No posed thumbnails. Mid-sentence freeze > posed photo.
- No animated/illustrated content unless for a specific storytelling purpose

## Part 10: Growth Mechanics

### Engagement (10 min/day by Reut)
- Reply to EVERY comment within 2 hours (first-hour replies boost algorithm)
- Comment on 10 accounts in the founder/CEO/tech space daily
- Reply to DMs in Stories (screenshot + answer) — this drives more DMs
- Use Story polls, sliders, and Q&A boxes daily — these signal engagement to algorithm

### Reels Distribution
- Post Reel → immediately share to Story with a poll ("Would you have made the same call?")
- Post between 9-11 AM EST or 6-8 PM EST (international audience, English-speaking)
- First 30 minutes of engagement determine reach — have 3-5 people from the team comment immediately

### Hashtag Strategy
- 5-7 hashtags in first comment (not in caption)
- Mix: 2 broad (#entrepreneur #ceo), 3 mid (#saasfounder #techstartup #aitools), 2 niche (#founderstory #ceolife)

### Collaboration (1x/month minimum)
- Go Live with another founder for 15-20 min
- Guest appearance on other accounts' Stories/Reels
- Tag people genuinely (not spam) — especially in Elon/industry content

### The "Series" Strategy
Create recurring series that give people a reason to follow for the NEXT episode:
- **"What I'd Do Differently"** — weekly Reel where Lior revisits a past decision
- **"$X Decisions"** — Reels that start with a specific dollar amount
- **"Ask the CEO"** — weekly Q&A from DMs/comments (builds relationship)

## The Bottom Line

**The problem is not views. Lior gets 337K views/month.** The problem is that 337K people watch, and almost none of them follow. Because the content doesn't give them a reason to follow *Lior specifically.*

Agency-designed trending graphics → views, zero followers.
Real Lior footage and stories → fewer views, but actual followers.

The shift:

| | Old Approach | New Approach |
|---|---|---|
| Content source | Agency designs trending templates | Lior's real Voice Drops + Shadow Hours footage |
| Content identity | Generic entrepreneurship wisdom | "What actually happens when you run a 250-person company" |
| Bio promise | Resume (who he is) | Access promise (what you'll see) |
| Viewer experience | "That's a nice quote" → scroll | "I need to see what happens next" → follow |
| Lior's time | Reviews finished content | 2 min/day Voice Drop + 40 min/week filming |
| Goal per Reel | Maximize views | Maximize follow rate |

People don't follow for information. They follow for **access to a life they can't live.** Lior runs a 250-person company acquired by Fiverr. He sat across from Elon Musk. He makes million-dollar decisions.

Stop giving advice. Start opening the door.
