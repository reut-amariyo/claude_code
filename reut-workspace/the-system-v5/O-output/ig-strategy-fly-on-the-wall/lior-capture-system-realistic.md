# Lior Capture System — The Realistic Version
## Built for a CEO who won't remember to send you content 3x/day

---

## The Problem With "Send Me 3 Things a Day"

It won't work. Here's why:
- Lior is in meetings, calls, and decisions all day
- He'll do it for 3-4 days, then forget, then feel guilty, then stop entirely
- "Send me content" feels like a task, and CEOs deprioritize tasks that aren't urgent
- If Reut has to chase him 3x/day, she becomes a nag, not a content partner

**The fix: Design the system so Lior does ONE thing, once a day, and Reut extracts everything else herself.**

---

## The Actual System: "One Moment + Shadow"

### Lior's Only Job: The Evening Voice Drop (1x/day, 90 seconds)

**When:** Same time every day. Pick ONE:
- Right when he gets in the car to go home
- Right after his last meeting
- While walking to his car

**How to trigger it (pick one — test for 1 week, then adjust):**

| Option | How It Works | Best For |
|---|---|---|
| **A. Reut sends 1 message at 17:00** | One WhatsApp message: just the prompt question, nothing else. "מה ההחלטה שלא היית בטוח בה היום?" He replies with a voice note or video. | If Lior responds well to direct questions |
| **B. Automated daily reminder** | Set a recurring reminder on Lior's phone at 17:00: "Record 90 sec for Reut." No message from Reut needed. | If Lior doesn't want to feel "chased" |
| **C. Attach it to an existing habit** | Lior already does something at end of day — gets in car, starts driving, drinks water. The rule: "When I turn on the car engine, I record." Physical trigger > phone reminder. | If Lior forgets scheduled reminders |

**The message Reut sends (if using Option A):**

Don't send "Hey, don't forget to send me content!" That feels like a task.

Instead, send ONE specific question. Rotate daily:

| Day | The Question |
|---|---|
| Sunday | "מה ההחלטה הכי גדולה שקיבלת היום?" |
| Monday | "מה הפתיע אותך היום?" |
| Tuesday | "איזה מספר הסתכלת עליו היום ומה הרגשת?" |
| Wednesday | "מה אמרת לצוות היום שבעצם היית צריך לשמוע בעצמך?" |
| Thursday | "מה קרה היום שאף אחד מחוץ למשרד לא היה רואה?" |
| Friday | "מה הדבר אחד מהשבוע שהכי נשאר לך?" |
| Saturday | אין. שבת. |

**Why one specific question works better than "send me something":**
- It removes the decision of WHAT to record
- It frames it as answering a question, not "creating content"
- Lior talks naturally when asked a question. He doesn't talk naturally when told "make content."
- If he has nothing for that prompt, he says "nothing today" in 10 seconds and that's fine

**The rule for Reut:** If Lior doesn't respond within 2 hours, don't send a follow-up. Don't chase. Just note that day as a gap. If he misses 3 days in a row, bring it up in the weekly meeting — not via WhatsApp.

### Screenshots: Make It Passive, Not Active

**Don't ask Lior to screenshot things.** He won't.

Instead:

**Option 1: Reut asks ONE follow-up question after the Voice Drop**

Lior sends his evening voice note. Reut listens, then asks one follow-up:
- "Wait, you mentioned a dashboard. Can you screenshot that tomorrow morning?"
- "That Slack message you sent — can you forward it to me?"
- "What did the calendar actually look like today? Screenshot it when you get a chance."

This works because:
- It's a RESPONSE to something he already said (not a random request)
- He can do it in the morning when he's fresh
- It's one thing, not a list

**Option 2: Reut captures screenshots during Shadow Hours**

When Reut is on-site (2 hrs/week), SHE screenshots things:
- "Can I take a photo of your screen right now?"
- "Can I film your calendar for 3 seconds?"
- "Can I screenshot that Slack thread?"

This way Lior doesn't have to remember anything. Reut is there and captures it live.

**Option 3: The "Interesting Things" Album**

Set up a shared iPhone album called "IG Raw." The only instruction to Lior:
- "Whenever you see something interesting on your screen or in the office, just take a photo and drop it in the album. No pressure, no timeline."
- Some weeks he'll add 5 things. Some weeks 0. That's OK.
- This removes the daily obligation and turns it into an anytime-you-feel-like-it thing.

### Morning Fly-on-the-Wall Clips: Reut Does This, Not Lior

**Don't rely on Lior to film himself in the morning.** He's not thinking about content at 8 AM.

**Solution: Reut captures morning content during Shadow Hours.**

During the 2 hours/week Reut is on-site:
- Film Lior arriving at the office (from a distance, natural)
- Film his desk before he sits down
- Film the office environment (people working, screens, whiteboards)
- Film Lior on a call (from the side, muted)
- Film the view from his window

**This gives Reut a week's worth of Fly-on-the-Wall Story content from just 2 hours.**

Film 20-30 short clips (5-15 seconds each) during Shadow Hours. That's enough for:
- 5-6 "Fly on the Wall" Stories spread across the week
- B-roll for Reels
- Backgrounds for Screenshot Stories
- Lifestyle content for Saturday/Sunday

**Lior's involvement in morning content: zero.**

| When | What | Time | Frequency |
|---|---|---|---|
| Evening (~17:00) | Answer Reut's daily question with a voice note or selfie video | 90 sec | Every workday |

**That's it. Everything else is Reut's job.**

### Reut's Workflow: Where Stories Content Actually Comes From

| Story Type | Source | Lior's Involvement |
|---|---|---|
| Fly on the Wall | Shadow Hours footage (Reut films) | Zero — he doesn't even know she's filming |
| Screenshot Story | Follow-up screenshots from Voice Drop OR Shadow Hours captures | Minimal — one screenshot when asked |
| Engagement (polls/sliders) | Reut writes them from Voice Drop material | Zero |
| Reaction | Best part: Voice Drops that are too short for Reels | Zero — his voice note IS the content |
| Bridge | Reut creates to push Reel traffic | Zero |

**Key insight: 4 out of 5 Story types require NOTHING from Lior beyond what he's already doing (the evening Voice Drop + Shadow Hours).**

## The Accountability System (Without Being Annoying)

### Week 1-2: Build the Habit

- Reut sends the daily question at 17:00 sharp. Same time, every day.
- If Lior responds: 👍 react. That's it. Don't over-thank, don't make it a big deal.
- If Lior doesn't respond: silence. No follow-up.
- At end of Week 1: "You sent 4 out of 5 this week. That's great. Here's what I made from them." → Show him the content. This is the reward loop.

### Week 3-4: Prove the ROI

- Show Lior: "This Reel came from your Tuesday voice note. It got X views and Y new followers."
- Show him: "This Story poll got 300 votes. People are engaged."
- When Lior sees his 90-second voice note turned into actual results, the habit sticks.

### Ongoing: The Weekly "Content Receipt"

Every Sunday, Reut sends Lior a 30-second voice note or short text:
- "This week you sent me 5 voice drops. I made 4 Reels, 25 Stories, and we gained X followers. The $30K decision Reel is at X views. Thanks."

This does three things:
1. Shows Lior his effort → results (motivation)
2. Keeps him informed without a meeting
3. Makes him feel like a partner, not a content subject

## What If Lior Stops Sending Voice Drops?

It will happen. He'll have a crazy week, travel, or just forget.

**Don't panic. Here's the backup plan:**

### Backup Content Sources (When Lior Goes Quiet)

1. **Shadow Hours footage bank** — If you filmed 30 clips during Shadow Hours, you have enough Fly-on-the-Wall Stories for 5-6 days even without new material.

2. **Old Voice Drops** — Voice Drops from previous weeks that weren't used for Reels can become Story content. "Last week Lior told me something I couldn't stop thinking about..."

3. **Screenshot Story from public data** — Lior's companies are public enough. Revenue milestones, team photos from company events, product updates — these don't need Lior's daily input.

4. **Engagement-only days** — When there's no raw material, post polls, sliders, and Q&A boxes. These don't require Lior content. They keep the Story streak alive and the algorithm fed.

5. **"Throwback" content** — "3 years ago, Lior was sitting in a room with 2 people. Today it's 250." Use old photos + context.

**Rule: Never let Stories go dark for more than 1 day.** Even if Lior sends nothing, there's always something to post from the bank.

| What | Who | Frequency |

**If this floor is maintained, the system works.** Everything above this is optimization.
