Cost: ~3 hours in Tel Aviv on a Sunday morning, 6.5 months out. Reach: ~0 (60 people, no stage video). Decision rule: if Helena gives 3 of the 5 asks below, accept. If it stays "panelist #4 of 5", decline warmly and ask to be first call for the 3rd edition.

## What it is really worth

1. **Network, not audience.** 60 handshakes with founders in his city, plus KPMG partners in the room. KPMG Israel's tech practice is a real relationship for a CEO who buys companies (4 acquisitions so far).
2. **Photos.** The photo-card generator only works with candid Lior photos and we are short on them. A pro shoot on a stage with a KPMG backdrop is a content asset for months.
3. **Credit line.** "Spoke at the KPMG CEO event alongside Bright Data / Verbit / Workiz CEOs" goes in every future speaker pitch, including 1 Billion Summit and podcast pitches.
4. **Book material.** Record the panel and breakout on Plaud. The US-GTM-from-Israel story is not yet in the source bank.
5. **One LinkedIn post, maybe two.** Photo + one principle from the panel. Expect middling reach unless the visual is strong. Do not count it as a growth lever.

## What is wrong or unclear

- **Two dates.** April 4 vs April 5 2027. Clarify before anything.
- **"Sponsors & their guests" dinner.** The PDF never says speaking is free. Past CEO panelists were CEOs, not sponsors, so probably free, but ask in writing: "Is there any fee or sponsorship expectation attached to the speaking slot?"
- **Panel math.** 45 minutes, 5 panelists + moderator last time = about 7 minutes of Lior talking. That is the weakest possible use of his morning.
- **Topic fit needs one fact check.** Does AutoDS have any US entity or US-based staff today? If yes, the "no US office" line changes to "R&D stayed 100% in Israel". Verify with Lior before he takes the stage angle.
- **Seed / round A framing.** The room raised money; he did not. Good for contrast, but he should not lecture them on not raising. Investors never said no to him, he didn't know raising was an option. Keep it as "here is what a bootstrapped path to US customers looks like".

## The 5 asks to send Helena (in order of importance)

1. A **solo opening slot** of 10-12 minutes before the panel, or a **fireside** format with 2 speakers max instead of a 5-person panel.
2. **Recording rights**: full video or at minimum audio of his segment, ours to clip.
3. **The attendee list 2 weeks before**, so we pick 5-7 people to meet in the breakouts.
4. **Photos delivered within 7 days**, raw + edited, usable on all his channels.
5. Written confirmation of **no fee / no sponsorship expectation** and the **final date**.

If she gives 1 + 2 + any other, accept. If she gives only 3-5, decline for this edition.
