### What the winners have in common, and what it means for our post

1. Both big posts gate a **deck**, the same asset class we are giving away. This is the proven
   category for the mechanic, not a generic ebook.
2. Both state the **connection requirement inside the post**. LinkedIn DMs only reach 1st-degree
   connections, so the ask is comment + connect, not comment + follow. Our CTA currently says
   Follow, which does not open a DM channel.
3. Both name the **borrowed proof in line 1** (Airbnb/Snapchat/Dropbox, $380M+ raised). Ours is
   $92M and Fiverr, which is the same move with a first-party number instead of a borrowed one.

## Reply bank for the comment section (2026-08-24)

Rotate these. Hundreds of identical replies read as automation and invite the AI-slop report.
Every variant either carries the connection ask or invites a response, because a commenter who
is not a 1st-degree connection cannot be DMed at all.

**Praise with no ask ("Nice!", "Love this"):**
- Thanks. Want the 47 slides? Add me and they're yours.
- Appreciate it. Say the word and the template is on its way.
- Thanks. The template is open to anyone here, just add me and I'll send it.

**Requests ("Please share", "Amazing! Please share :)"):**
- On its way. Add me as a connection so the DM can land.
- Sending it over. LinkedIn only lets me DM connections, so add me and it's yours.
- Sent. Tell me which section you get stuck on and I'll help.
- Done. Which one are you doing, raising or selling?

The last two are question-replies. Use on roughly one in five. They pull the commenter back for
a second comment, which is another reach signal on the post.

## DM fulfilment (2026-08-24)

**Do not DM everyone.** The DM has zero effect on the post's reach, which was banked when the
person commented. Sending the file to a friend who was only showing support buys nothing, and
dozens of near-identical DMs in a short window is what LinkedIn's spam detection looks for.

Split:
- Asked for it ("please share", "interested", "EXIT") → DM. When in doubt, send. An unanswered
  public request under the post is worse than an unwanted file.
- Bare praise, no ask → reply in the comments offering it. If they want it they will say so,
  and that is a second comment on the post.

Mechanics: spread sends over a few hours, not 50 in ten minutes. Use one view-only link rather
than a file attachment, so the deck can be updated without re-sending.

**Main DM (shortened at Reut's request, 2026-08-24):**
Hi [name], here's the full template, all 47 slides: [link]

Same structure that sold AutoDS, with placeholders where our numbers were.

If you're building a deck now, which section is giving you trouble?

Three blocks: the file, why it is worth opening, one question. The question is the only part that
starts a conversation, so it survives any further trimming.

**Even shorter:**
Here you go [name], all 47 slides: [link]

Same structure that sold AutoDS. Which section are you stuck on?

**Short version, only when volume gets heavy:**
Here you go [name], the full 47-slide template: [link]

Financials sit last on purpose. The first 60 pages earn the right to show them.

**Friends and supporters, if sending anything at all:**
Thanks for the support. If you ever want the deck itself, it lives here: [link]

The closing question in the main DM is the point of the whole post. It converts a file drop into
a conversation with a founder mid-sale.
