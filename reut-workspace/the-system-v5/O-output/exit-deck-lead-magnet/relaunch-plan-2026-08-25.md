# Exit deck post — relaunch plan for 2026-08-25

## What happened on 2026-08-24

Published ~16:00 with a comment-gated CTA ("Leave any comment & like the post / Add me in
connections / I will send you the link in the DM"). At 1h: **55 impressions, 15 members reached,
78% in-network, 22% out-of-network, 17 comments**. All 17 comments came from people Reut asked
directly via a link to the post. So the organic reach really was ~55, not a lagging counter.

Diagnosis, in order of confidence:
1. **Engagement-bait CTA.** "Leave any comment & like the post" is the phrasing LinkedIn demotes
   by policy. It sat at the bottom of the post where the classifier reads it.
2. **Off-feed comment burst.** LinkedIn's early distribution test measures engagement from people
   it showed the post to in the feed. Comments arriving from a direct link do not feed that test,
   and a synchronised burst from off-feed traffic looks like pod behaviour.

Post deleted the same evening. **This is the data that confirms the standing rule in
the-system-v5/CLAUDE.md against copying the comment-to-receive lead-magnet layer.** It was tested
once, on a first-party artifact with a $92M number attached, and it still got suppressed.
