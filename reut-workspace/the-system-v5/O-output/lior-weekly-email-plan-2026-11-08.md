# Lior weekly email: one-page plan

Drafted 2026-11-08 from the Leila Hormozi funnel teardown. Status: proposal for Reut, nothing built.

## Why now

Lior has the top of the funnel (6 platforms, 7M+ views/mo target) and the bottom ($1 Build Your Store via ManyChat). Nothing in the middle is his. Followers are rented: one algorithm change and they're gone. The proof the gap is real: ManyChat V4.4 collected **46 emails and sent zero**.

The email turns attention into an audience he can reach on purpose, every week, without asking a platform.

## The product

- **Name:** "The $1 Club", Reut's idea, built on his $1 paid-trial belief. Risk to check: it must not read as a BYS promo. Fallback: a plain "Lior's Friday note".
- **Promise, one line:** what a founder who built and sold a company is deciding this week, and why.
- **Format:** one idea, about 300 words, one real artifact or number of his. No link lists, no tool tips, no news roundup. Same CEO relevance filter and topic gate as LinkedIn.
- **Cadence:** weekly, fixed day. Proposed: Thursday, so the LinkedIn post of the week can point to it.
- **Voice:** written from his recordings, never from scratch. Lior is dyslexic, so he approves from a voice summary or 2-line brief, never a full read.

## Where each issue comes from

1. **Ask Lior box** in IG stories once a week. He answers one question on camera, and that answer becomes the issue. The topic is pre-validated by the person who asked, like "Dear Leila".
2. **Plaud recordings** in B-brain/meetings: one story or rule per week.
3. **The week's best post, deeper:** the part that didn't fit in 1,200 characters.

## Where people sign up

| Spot | How | Note |
|---|---|---|
| follow.liorpozin.com pages | Subscribe box under each giveaway: /skills, /html-plan, 50 mentors | Giveaway stays ungated. The box is optional, never a wall. |
| Exit deck download page | Same optional box | The ungated version got 66,682 impressions; the gated one died at 55. |
| ManyChat flow | Add a newsletter opt-in step | The 46 existing emails were given for BYS, so they need a fresh opt-in before any send. |
| LinkedIn / IG bio + featured | One link | Profile headline already sells a "follow"; add the email as the deeper step. |
| Proof on the button | His real number at the moment of signup, e.g. "built and sold a $92M company" | Use the cleared figure only, see the 20-F note. |

No pitch in posts. The email is the first and only ask on the content layer.

## The ladder after the email

Email → 1 issue in 4 carries a P.S. to the $1 Build Your Store trial → trial. Anyone who doesn't buy stays on the list. Nobody gets deleted.

## Numbers to track

Subscribers, open rate, replies per issue, and unsubscribes after each BYS P.S. These go next to the 2 standing monthly columns for Lior: ManyChat joins and BYS trials. Targets get set after issue 4, once there's a baseline.

## Decisions for Reut / Lior

1. Name: The $1 Club or a plain one?
2. Platform + sender domain: suggest beehiiv or Kit on @liorpozin.com, owned by Lior's account, not the agency's.
3. Who writes: Reut from recordings, or added to the agency's scope?
4. Does Lior sign off on a weekly commitment? An email that stops after 3 issues does more harm than none.

## First 4 issues, candidate topics

1. The deck he sold the company with, and the one slide he'd cut today.
2. Why every SaaS should run a $1 trial instead of a free one, the belief behind the name.
3. The first Ask Lior answer.
4. One of his 5 acquisition buy rules, with the deal that taught it.
