Anything your company owns: keep it out. That discount is the most expensive thing you can buy.

And the bigger move: keep your prompts, docs and context in files that live outside any tool. That's what let me test a new agent in one evening and be back at work the next morning, no migration, no drama.

Own your files. Rent your agent.

A new agent every month is a distraction dressed as progress.
Focus compounds. Tool-hopping doesn't.
