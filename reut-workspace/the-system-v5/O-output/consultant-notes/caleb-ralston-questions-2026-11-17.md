# Caleb Ralston one-time consult - what to ask

2026-11-17. One expensive session, so every minute goes to what only he can answer.
Hebrew version: `caleb-ralston-questions-2026-11-17-he.md`.

## Before the meeting

- Send him sections 1-6 of the brief today, without section 7.
- Ask if we can record.

## Opening, 2 minutes

"We have 275K followers, 13M views a month, 3 products, and no system that turns the audience into revenue. We want to leave with one path to build first and a list of what to stop."

## 7 questions, in priority order

1. Based on the numbers we sent, which ONE revenue path would you build first: a subscription app, Lior's own course or community, or affiliate products like Build Your Store? And in what order would you put the three products in front of people?
2. When you started monetizing Gary's brand, what came first, and what would you do differently today?
3. Our audience is split between beginners who want to make money online and founders and CEOs. Can one brand serve both, or do we choose?
4. Does selling beginner products hurt Lior's position as a serious founder? What should he refuse to sell?
5. We have an email list we've never sent to. Value only for six months, or sell from the first email?
6. Our growth comes from one-off hits. How do we turn Lior's story into a repeatable format people recognize instantly, the way you did with Gary?
7. What should we stop doing tomorrow?

## If there's time

- How many new creatives a month does a brand this size need, and who should make them?
- What audience-to-paying-customer conversion should we plan on in year one?

## Closing, 5 minutes

- "If you were us, what would you do in the next 30 days?"
- Ask him to email his top 3 recommendations.
