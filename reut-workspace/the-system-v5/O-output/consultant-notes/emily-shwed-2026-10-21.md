## 1. Yes/No gate for every IG post, carousel, reel and story

A post is YES only if all four pass:

| # | Check | Fail example |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Only Lior can say it. Delete his name and the post breaks | generic "10 AI tools" |
| 2 | His face is in it | pure cartoon, text-only card |
| 3 | A first-time viewer knows it's him: shirt, format, signature | stock look, anonymous voice |
| 4 | It sounds like him, per voice-dna + anti-AI rules | polished agency copy |

Fixing a NO is usually small. Add one credibility line, put his face on the cover, or put the beta shirt in the frame.

- The handle on the shirt or in the bio: "the guy in the beta shirt"
- Buy the matching domain, e.g. theguyinthebetashirt.com or similar. Check if it's available. Redirect it to liorpozin.com / follow.liorpozin.com
- Tie it to the tagline [ life is beta ], always be testing
- Wear it in every face-to-camera piece. Consistency matters more than variety. Once it's locked in, never change it. Precedents: Hormozi's Acquisition.com chest, Jobs' glasses, Emily's own hair.

Decision for Reut and Lior: wording on the shirt + domain. Proposed: keep the [ life is beta ] wordmark, add the domain small on the sleeve or back.
