## 4. What we know grows the account

1. **Followers come from Lior's own story and his own artifacts, on every platform.** X: three "I'm Lior" origin threads = 96% of September's new follows. LinkedIn: the ungated exit pitch deck = best follower post ever, ~590 extra followers in 3 days. IG: the AGE 14 childhood-photo carousel = 57% of July's growth.
2. **Reach is hit-driven.** One post makes the month. Facebook: one reposted reel = 78% of the month. X: 3 threads = 94%.
3. **Reels = reach, carousels = followers, stories = sales.**
4. **"Theory" story reels** are a new engine: Train Station Theory 536K, Black Coffee Theory 498K on IG, Flight Home Theory 351K on TikTok.
5. **The $92M credential travels**, 475K on IG, 308K on FB.
6. **More volume did not buy better reach.** IG reels doubled, per-reel views halved.

## 6. Our own diagnosis - to test against his

1. **The middle layer is missing.** We have attention on top and a $1 offer at the bottom, but nothing Lior owns in between: no newsletter, no community, no long-form home.
2. **Three products, no ladder.** BYS, Avatar Factory and the Lior AI app each want the same audience with no clear order.
3. **Audience quality is drifting from the buyer.** FB grows in Lagos and Cape Town, IG has a large India share, LinkedIn reach skews to big-tech engineers. The buyer for BYS is US/UK.
4. **Creatives are the bottleneck.** Only 1-2 ads work at any time. We are deciding between hiring one creative person or using an agency.
5. **The brand is still "followers", not a thing people pay for.** Lior wants to be number one, not another trends guy.

10. What should we stop doing now?
