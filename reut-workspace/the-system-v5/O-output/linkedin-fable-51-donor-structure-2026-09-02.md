--- POST STARTS ---

Fable 5.1 finally killed the AI website look.

Rebuilt my own pages with it this week. They come out premium now, and one new ability is doing most of the work. It can look at what it built.

The process came down to seven things:

1. Start with brand guidelines so the colors, type and visual language stay consistent.

2. Define the pain, the person and the promise so the design fits the business and the people it is for.

3. Give the visitor feedback while they scroll, even if that is only text fading in or elements moving at different speeds.

4. Pull specific inspiration from sites like 21st.dev, Godly, Awwwards and Motion Sites.

5. Reuse components that already exist instead of asking the model to invent every button, card and animation from scratch.

6. Check the phone, because desktop animation does not translate to a small screen on its own.

7. Run screenshot verification across the full scroll so the agent can catch broken layers, awkward cuts and elements sitting outside the frame.

Then I ran the same prompts twice, once with step seven and once without it.

The version without it went out with a layer hanging off the edge on mobile. Nobody would have caught that before a visitor did.

Seven is the step everyone skips. It is also the one doing the work.

Which of the seven is missing from your process?
