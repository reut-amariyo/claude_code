## Draft

START
```
As a founder, when you run out of good ideas, where do you go?

ChatGPT. Reddit. A podcast with someone who seems to have it all figured out.

They help sometimes. The best answer at AutoDS was already on payroll.

The support team.

In the early days, support was me. Customer chats at 3 AM, between writing code and calls with the accountant. One of those nights a customer told me her store had sold out at $1. My bug.

Soon after, she joined us for $200 a month. She went on to build and lead a support team of about 100 people.

Today her team is closer to our customers than I am. And I used to answer every single chat.

Her team hears:
- the question that comes back every week
- the features people keep mixing up
- the complaint that is about to turn into a feature request

Half of the "new ideas" in our strategy meetings, they heard weeks earlier.

Next time you're stuck, close the chat window. Walk over to support and ask what customers asked for this week.▮
```
END

## v2 (2026-11-11): Reut's hook + extra material from a donor-post comment

Added: the 3 AM "congratulations" misread scene, dashboards vs support line, mix-ups = naming problem, a concrete one-question CTA.

START
```
As a founder, when you run out of good ideas, where do you go?

ChatGPT. Reddit. A podcast with someone who seems to have it all figured out.

They help sometimes. My best ideas came from the support team.

Early on, support was me. 3 AM, a customer messaged that her store was flooded with sales. I wrote back "congratulations."

Every product in her store was priced at $1. My bug.

Soon after, she joined us for $200 a month. She went on to build and lead a support team of about 100 people.

Today they're closer to our customers than I am.

Every dashboard I own counts the customers who came back. Support talks to the ones who almost didn't.

They hear:
- the question that comes back every week
- the 2 features people keep mixing up, a naming problem no dashboard will flag
- the complaint that is about to become a feature request

Half of the "new ideas" in our strategy meetings, they heard weeks earlier.

So next time you're stuck, skip the chat window. Ask support one question: what did customers ask for 3 times this week?▮
```
END
