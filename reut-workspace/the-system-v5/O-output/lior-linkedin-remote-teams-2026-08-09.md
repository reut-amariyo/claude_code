--- POST STARTS ---

We went remote 4 years before COVID.

2016. My co-founder was in the US, I was in Israel, and there was no playbook to copy. So we wrote our own.

Ten years later, AutoDS runs with 250 employees across multiple countries on 3 rules:

1. Connection has a budget.
Teams at every site abroad get a recurring budget to meet face to face. It sits in the plan like servers do.

2. All hands means the whole business.
Everyone sees the numbers, the wins, the misses. A developer in one country understands why support in another country had a brutal week.

3. Home comes first. Always.
When the war reached our team in Ukraine, that was the only message that mattered. Family, safety, then everything else. They kept shipping anyway. Nobody asked them to.

Most companies adopted remote work as a cost saver. We built it as a trust system, and trust is the more expensive of the two.

What do you budget for your remote team beyond salaries?
