## Draft v4

--- POST STARTS ---
If you struggle with imposter syndrome, read this:

I don't have it, and I'm going to be harsh about why...

Reframe it.
Sit with it.
Remind yourself you've earned your seat.

You've read that already. Several times, probably.

But a syndrome is something you have. Nobody diagnosed you with this. There is no treatment, because there is nothing to treat. You're doing something you haven't done before, and your body filed an accurate report about the odds.

The feeling is real. The syndrome is invented.

So treat it like a game. Open the next map. Find the boss. Beat it. Open the one after that.

You don't need a plan for map nine while you're standing on map two, and you couldn't build one that survived contact anyway.

Every time something hard shows up, I open one note on my phone. I wrote it to myself: it's all nonsense, everything passes, enjoy the road.

A year from now you'll have built the thing, and the nerves will have done their job the whole way.

Boring and true beats clever. Enjoy the road.

PS: "Not ready yet" is the best excuse there is. You can name yours in 10 seconds or less. What is it?

## Gate notes

- Posture: Lior never claims the feeling as his. The only first-person lines are "I don't have it" and the harsh
  warning, both from the article. Nerves are described in second person as an accurate report, the article's exact frame.
- Left out on purpose: the article's "I got nervous this week / at 14 / same nerves both times" and "I used to think
  the feeling would go once I hit something big enough." Both true to the article, both read as inside-the-feeling
  on LinkedIn without the 1,500 words around them. Add back only if Reut wants one personal nerves line.
- Anti-AI: no parentheses, no dashes, no "not X, it's Y", no red-list words, zero "just". Triple of 3, burst of 5.
- I/me/my: 2.
- Facts: none needed. No ages, no numbers, no AutoDS, no exit figure.
- Why follow: a founder telling the feed the diagnosis is fake, against the donor and every "outwork it" post.
- Comment engine: the PS asks for the excuse, not the feeling.
