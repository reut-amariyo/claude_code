The mechanism, in one line: a number the viewer wants, shown on a real-looking screen, with a human face reacting to it, then a 5-to-7 step ladder that ends on a cash-out screen and BYS as step 1.

Four components every winner has. A hook that drops one of them loses:
1. A specific dollar number with a time compression next to it. 5,700 orders in 26 minutes. $80,000 in a single day. Never a round "make money online".
2. The number is ON A SCREEN. Phone, watch, laptop. Never a caption alone.
3. A person reacts to the screen. Gasp, hands to face, a friend behind the shoulder. This is the retention beat the flat creatives are missing.
4. The action is tiny. "Search buildyourstore.ai" and "pick a niche". Reut's rule: big promise, small action.

## Two things to settle before shooting

1. Proof ownership. Every reference uses borrowed UGC dashboards. On Lior's verified page, in a paid ad, a fabricated earnings screen is a Meta income-claim violation and a brand risk. Every hook below has a "proof needed" line. The fix is cheap: pull 5 to 7 real BYS user stores that agreed to be shown, or a real AutoDS user, and film their dashboards. The agency already flagged that Lior has no operator proof of his own, so the proof has to be a user's.
2. The keyword. Scripts say "Comment STORE" and "DM me STORE". Memory has BUILD as an unconfirmed keyword. Swap to whatever is live in ManyChat before export.

## Timing template for a 20-28 second ad

- 0-3s hook: overlay + spoken line + the screen
- 3-6s reaction beat
- 6-8s rehook: "want the same store?" or "here is how"
- 8-20s body: one of the 3 bodies below
- 20-25s cash-out screen
- 25-28s CTA into ManyChat

## CTA lines for the ManyChat handoff, pick one per ad

For a comment-triggered flow:
Comment STORE and I will send you the link.

For a click-to-message ad:
Tap the button, type STORE, your store link comes back in 10 seconds.

For a story or feed ad with a link sticker:
Your store is one tap away. Free.

Never in the CTA: "hit your first 10k", "make money", "passive income". Those are the lines Meta's income-claim review and the AI-slop report both catch.
