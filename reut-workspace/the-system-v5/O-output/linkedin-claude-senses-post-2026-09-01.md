--- POST STARTS ---

Claude can read and write. That is the whole product.

Everything past that is something you bolt on yourself.

I tested 14 of those bolt-ons this year. 9 came off within a week. Most of them were not bad, they solved a problem nobody on my team actually had.

5 survived. All 5 are still running.

Each one has a small setting that decides whether it is worth anything. That part never makes it into the docs.

1. Ears, mlx-whisper, free
Turns any recording into searchable text. Run it locally instead of shipping files to an API and customer calls never leave the machine. That one choice is why legal signed off.

2. Eyes, Puppeteer, free
Screenshots any live page. Everyone runs the default viewport and gets the top of the page. Switch it to full page and you can hand it a competitor checkout every Monday.

3. A browser, Playwright MCP, free
Most people paste a screenshot and ask what is broken. Give it the URL instead. It opens the page, clicks through, and tells you where the flow dies.

4. An inbox, Gmail MCP, free
It writes the email and stops. Keep it in draft only. Send permission is the line where an assistant turns into a risk.

5. The numbers, Apify, 29 dollars a month
Pulls what actually happened on every post. Set the window to 30 days, not 7. Weekly data makes noise look like a trend.

11 of the 14 were free. The three I pay for come to 68 dollars a month, less than one software seat.

The tool is never the expensive part. The setting is.

Save this. You will not remember these names tomorrow.

Which sense would you give it next?
