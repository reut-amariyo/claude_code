# 1 Billion Followers Summit — How to actually reach the organizers
Built 2026-08-26. Target: Lior Pozin speaking slot, 5th edition, Dubai, 8-10 Jan 2027.

---

## 0. The thing that changes the pitch

The summit is **not a commercial conference**. It is organized by the **UAE Government Media Office**, under the patronage of Sheikh Mohammed bin Rashid Al Maktoum, and delivered through **New Media Academy** and **Creators HQ**. Its 2026 theme was "Content for Good".

That explains the silence on the money offer. A government summit does not sell stage time the way a trade show does. Sponsorship buys booths, branding and transport partnerships. Stage slots are **curated by the programming team against a national agenda**: prove the UAE creator economy produces real income for real creators.

So the ask has to be reframed. Not "we will pay to speak". Instead:

> AutoDS deploys $10M+ a year to 200+ creators. We will commit a defined slice of that budget to UAE and MENA creators, sourced through Creators HQ, and announce it from your stage.

That turns a sponsorship request into a contribution to their KPI. It is the only version of this pitch that a government media office has a reason to say yes to fast.

**Correction to carry into every asset:** the public figure is **$10M+ per year across 200+ creators**, per Lior's own approval on 2026-07-02. Not $1M/month, not $1B/year. The current pitch draft has this right, keep it.

**Second reframe worth testing in the title.** The talk is currently creator-facing advice. The scarcer asset in that room is that Lior is the **buyer**. Fifteen thousand creators are there hunting brand deals. Almost no one on stage actually writes the checks. Lead with that.

## 3. Every other route, in priority order

### A. Platform partners. Free, fastest, highest leverage.
Meta, TikTok, YouTube, Snap and X are all summit partners, and Creators HQ has a formal Meta partnership. AutoDS is a real ad spender on at least some of these.

**Action:** ask your Meta and TikTok account managers for a warm intro to whoever owns their 1 Billion Followers Summit programme. Their creator-partnerships leads sit in the same Dubai rooms as the summit team and hand over speaker suggestions. This costs nothing and skips the queue. Do this on Monday.

### B. The ambassadors. Approach them as a buyer, not as a petitioner.
2026 ambassadors: **Simon Squibb, Alexandra Mary Hirschi (Supercar Blondie), Khalid Al Ameri, Omar Farooq, Ammar Kandil (Yes Theory).** Ambassadors advise on programming and are re-appointed year over year.

**Action:** open a genuine brand-deal conversation with one or two of them through their business inbox or management. Supercar Blondie and Khalid Al Ameri are Dubai-based and inside the ecosystem. A creator who is negotiating a real deal with AutoDS has every reason to say "you should have this guy on stage". Budget is not the constraint here, so use it where it buys access.

### C. Creators HQ, in person. This is the underrated one.
Creators HQ is the year-round entity, at **Jumeirah Emirates Towers**, which is also the summit's main venue. It runs roughly 300 events and workshops a year and is staffed daily.
Phone: **+971 4 702 7718**. Contact page: https://creatorshq.com/contact-us/

**Action:** call, then fly. Ask to host a brand session at Creators HQ in Sept, Oct or Nov 2026: "How brands choose creators", free to members. You deliver value to their members, you meet the summit's own team on their floor, and by January you are a known quantity rather than an inbox. A booked session at Creators HQ is the strongest possible warm-up for a summit slot.

### D. Both official forms, filed separately and properly.
- Speaker application: https://1bfs.typeform.com/to/qetiNCfj
- Sponsorship application: https://1bfs.typeform.com/to/S9033Apd
- Email: hello@1billionsummit.com

Submit **both**. They almost certainly route to different teams. In the sponsorship form, ask specifically about a **Strategic Partner** tier, which is what AGMC held, and about a Brands or Economy track presence, since the summit runs three tracks: Economy, Content, Technology. Lior belongs on Economy.
On email, do not resend a pitch into the same silence. Send a new subject line naming the budget commitment, and CC nothing.

### E. Existing sponsors as brokers.
Known partners: DIFC (Gold), The Sustainable City (Platinum), Pepperstone (Gold), AGMC (transport), plus Fujifilm, AnyMind, OKX, Wio Bank, Red Bull, Teachable, Nomada.
**Action:** if anyone in AutoDS's network has a contact at any of these, ask for the name of the summit contact who sold them their package. Sponsors always have a direct human. That human is the one you have been missing.

**DIFC in particular** runs its own ecosystem and events team and is a Gold Partner. A DIFC intro carries weight because they are a Dubai government entity talking to a Dubai government entity.

### F. Press first, stage second.
Arabian Business, Gulf News, Khaleej Times, Entrepreneur Middle East, Fast Company ME and Tubefilter all cover this summit closely and are read by its organizers.
**Action:** pitch a regional business outlet an exclusive: "The brand behind a $10M creator budget explains how creators get picked." It is a genuinely strong story, it costs a PR retainer not a sponsorship, and a published piece is the single best credential to attach to the speaker form. Organizers of government summits book people who are already visible in their own press.

### G. Hire a Dubai government-relations or PR firm.
Since budget is not the constraint, this is the cleanest use of it. A Dubai GR firm with UAE Government Media Office relationships can get a name and a meeting in days. That is literally what they sell. Budget roughly $10K to $30K for a short engagement, far less than a sponsorship and far more likely to work.

### H. Israel-UAE business channels.
The UAE-Israel Business Council, Dubai Chamber of Commerce and the Israel Export Institute all broker introductions into Emirati government entities and regularly run Dubai delegations. An Israeli founder with a $92M exit and a $10M creator budget is exactly the profile they like to place. Ask for an introduction to the UAE Government Media Office or Creators HQ.

### I. Lior's friend at the summit.
Already noted in the file. Use him now, not later, and give him the one-paragraph forwardable version plus a specific ask: "who owns speaker programming for the Economy track?" A vague forward gets a vague answer.

### J. Buy your way onto the floor if the stage is blocked.
Corporate tickets, the Creators Market and exhibitor space all exist. Worst case, AutoDS shows up in January 2027 as an exhibiting brand actively signing creators on site. You will meet the entire programming team in person, and the 2028 slot becomes easy. Treat this as the floor, not the goal.
