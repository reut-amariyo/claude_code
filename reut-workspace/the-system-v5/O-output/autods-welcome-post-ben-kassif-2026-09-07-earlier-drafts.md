## The donor structure

I could not verify a single named welcome post with public engagement numbers, so I am not going to invent one. What IS verified for 2026:

- Native documents and multi-image posts are the top-performing formats on LinkedIn right now, ~6.6-7.0% engagement rate, ahead of single image and text
- Welcome posts that frame the hire around a philosophy or a decision outperform posts that list credentials
- Candid photo of the person on site beats a posed headshot
- Ben's own new-role posts prove the audience exists: 148 reactions on a 1.4K-follower account

The structure all the high performers share, and what the three drafts below run on:

1. Line 1 is a surprising fact about the person, never "please join me in welcoming"
2. Two or three lines of proof, named companies and real years
3. One line naming the mandate, so the post is about company direction and not HR admin
4. An open door to the outside world, which is what makes it shareable
5. Short welcome close
