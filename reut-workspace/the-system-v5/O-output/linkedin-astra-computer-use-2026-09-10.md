GPT-6 now clicks on my Mac. 3 steps to set up.

(No code. No terminal.)

Last week's post helped the people who live in a terminal. Which at most companies is almost nobody.

The rest live in Chrome, Sheets and Slack.

This week OpenAI put Astra in the ChatGPT desktop app with a plugin called Computer Use. It opens your apps and clicks through them the way a person would.

Every employee already had the model. What they were missing was the green light.

Here's how to turn it on, Plus plan or higher:

1. Update the ChatGPT desktop app. Switch the toggle at the top from Chat to Work.

2. Open Plugins, pick Computer Use, install and enable it. Turn both toggles on. On a Mac, allow Screen Recording and Accessibility when it asks.

3. Type @Computer or @ plus the app name, then the task.
Start with something boring: "Open @Chrome and check that the checkout page still works."

It asks for permission before every app it touches. That prompt is the whole job now.

Give it the access you would give a new hire on day one. Day-100 access it earns.

## Caveats kept out of the post, keep for replies

- Windows: it runs in the foreground, you cannot use that desktop while it works.
- It cannot drive terminal apps or ChatGPT itself, cannot approve system permission dialogs.
- Enterprise workspaces: admins can restrict which apps it may open. Astra off by default in enterprise at launch.
- OpenAI's own safety advice: keep sensitive apps closed, be careful with signed-in browser sessions. This is the "day-one access" close.
