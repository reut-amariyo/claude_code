## 1. The single most important reframe

Do not open with "let's host a Shopify event in Israel." That is a budget request and it dies in someone's inbox.
Open with the version Shopify already does and already has precedent for:

> **Shopify's Partner Town Hall ran IRL in Toronto and NYC and live-streamed everywhere else. Tel Aviv should be the next IRL room, and AutoDS will run it.**

Atlee Clark, Shopify's VP of Partnerships, posted after that town hall: "Can't wait to do more stuff like this with the best ecosystem in tech." That sentence is the opening Lior is walking through. You are not asking Shopify to create an event. You are handing them a city they do not cover, with a host who is already their #1 partner.

### Ask ladder — pitch A first, keep B and C in your pocket

| | Ask | Friction for Shopify | Who signs off |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A** | Tel Aviv = an IRL location for the next Partner Town Hall. AutoDS covers venue, food, AV, audience. Shopify sends the stream + one person on stage. | Lowest. Precedented. Near-zero budget. | Liam Griffin operationally, EMEA partnerships to bless |
| **B** | A Shopify Dev / Merchant meetup in Tel Aviv, community-organised, AutoDS hosts, Shopify sends a speaker and the brand. | Low-medium. Shopify already reposts community-run meetups. | Liam Griffin + EMEA partnerships |
| **C** | A full Shopify-branded Israel summit, Shopify co-marketing. | High. Real budget, legal, brand review, months of lead time. | Emilie Benoit-Vernay / Deann Evans level |

Land A, and C becomes a conversation instead of a cold pitch. Note that an Israeli Shopify-focused summit already exists (Shoptimize Israel, at the Eretz Israel Museum), so someone else is already standing in the "Israel Shopify conference" space. A is how you get there without competing.

## 4. The messages

Voice notes, so the drafts stay in Lior's mouth: short bubbles, first person singular, no call-stacking, the question is the close. Two deliberate deviations from the standard DM template, both on purpose — the salutation is the person's first name instead of "Hey bro", because these are corporate contacts and not creators, and there is no budget line, because "I have the budget" reads as buying access when the other side is a platform. Copy discipline: **partner**, never affiliate. "#1 partner in 2024 and 2025", both years. "Acquired by Fiverr", no exit figure.

## 5. Sequence

**Days 1-2.** Route 0: ask AutoDS's existing Shopify contact who owns EMEA community events, and ask for the intro to Liam. In parallel, Lior starts commenting on Liam's and Atlee's posts. Real comments, one line each.
**Day 3.** Build the one-pager (section 6). Nothing goes out before it exists, because the first reply will be "send me details."
**Day 4.** DM Liam (4a). If a warm intro landed, the intro replaces the DM.
**Day 6.** DM Emilie (4b) with the market framing. Different angle, so this is not a duplicate pitch, but say the words "I've also reached out to Liam on the community side" — Shopify people talk, and hiding it costs more than saying it.
**Day 10.** No reply: Apsara or Aoife, same email, one line added about having contacted the community side.
**Day 14.** Still nothing: stop the ladder. Run the event as an AutoDS Israeli e-commerce night with Shopify partners on stage, publish it, then invite Shopify to the second one. A photographed room full of merchants is a better pitch than any DM.

## 6. The one-pager Shopify needs before anyone can say yes

Keep it to one page. Every line answers a question that would otherwise become an email round-trip.

- Date window, two options, at least 8 weeks out
- City and venue, with capacity
- Expected attendance and **who** they are: merchants, agencies, developers, with a rough split
- How AutoDS fills the room: list, community, Lior's own audience
- What Shopify provides: the livestream, one speaker, brand approval. Nothing else
- What AutoDS covers: everything else, named line by line
- Content plan for the local portion, 2-3 sessions
- Who runs the day, with a named point of contact
- Post-event: recap content, footage, attendee list handling
