## DRAFT

--- POST STARTS ---

Apple's new CEO posted one word yesterday. 2.6 million views.

The word was "hello".

First post of his life, on his first day in the job. The account had been sitting private since June, waiting for the title.

The internet read it as a cute Apple moment. The number underneath it is the real story.

Tim Cook spent 15 years building 19.8 million followers.
John Ternus took the chair and started at 34 thousand.

He inherited Apple. He did not inherit the audience.

That is what most CEOs miss about a personal brand. A company can be handed over. An audience has to be built from zero, slowly, by one person. Which is why "I'll start when things calm down" is the most expensive sentence in this job.

I started posting when nobody was asking for it. Since then:

- Podcast invitations that were never pitched
- Stages in Dubai and New York
- Partners who showed up already knowing what we build
- Candidates who applied because of a post instead of a job ad

None of it came from a campaign. It came from showing up while it still felt pointless.

Your work does speak for itself. Only to the people already in the room.

If you started today, who is the one person you would write for?
