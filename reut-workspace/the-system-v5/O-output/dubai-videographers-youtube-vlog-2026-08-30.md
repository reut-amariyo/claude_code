# Dubai videographers + editors for Lior's YouTube vlog channel
Research date: 2026-08-30. Brief: 2-day shoot in Dubai, Lior with influencers, vlog-style channel launch.

## The one thing to know before you hire

Dubai's video market is built on corporate, luxury real estate, hospitality and weddings. Almost every "top videographer in Dubai" list returns people whose entire reel is 30-second brand films. Long-form vlog is a different skill: run-and-gun for 10 hours, clean audio in a moving car and a loud restaurant, and an edit that holds a viewer for 9 minutes.

So split the hire:
- **A-cam / shooter in Dubai** - must be physically there, follows Lior for 2 days, owns audio.
- **YouTube editor** - does not need to be in Dubai, and the best ones are not. Hire the editor on channel-pacing reps, not on location.

Do not let one agency sell you both unless they show a live YouTube channel they built.

## The move that actually finds vlog shooters

SEO lists will not surface them. Reverse-engineer from output instead:

1. **Ask the influencers.** Lior is spending 2 days with them. Ask each one who films their long-form. Highest signal, zero cost, and that person already knows the locations and the permit game.
2. **Credits mining.** Open 6-8 Dubai founder/creator YouTube channels, check descriptions and end cards for "Filmed by / Edited by", then check who they tag on Instagram.
3. **Instagram, but filtered.** #dubaifilmmaker #dubaivideographer #dubaidp, then discard anyone whose grid is only reels. You want the ones linking 8-12 minute YouTube videos.
4. **Poach signal.** Entrepreneurs.com is currently hiring a full-time Dubai filmmaker on Sony FX3 to shoot a founder's daily life and cut monthly vlogs: https://www.welcometothejungle.com/fr/companies/entrepreneurs.com/jobs/filmmaker-videaste-a-dubai_dubai . That job spec is exactly the profile you need, and it tells you this profile gets locked into full-time roles fast. Worth posting the same spec as a 2-day freelance gig.

## Vetting - 4 questions that eliminate 80% of candidates

1. "Send one YouTube video, 8 minutes or longer, that you shot **and** edited. Link to the live channel, not a showreel." Showreels hide pacing. This question alone filters out the corporate crowd.
2. "What is your audio setup for a moving 2-day shoot?" Correct answer involves two wireless lavs plus a backup recorder. If audio is an afterthought, walk away. Bad audio kills a vlog faster than bad picture.
3. "Who pulls the filming permits?" Dubai requires permits for commercial filming in public locations, malls and most venues. The shooter should own this, not you.
4. "Raws handed over at the end of day 2, work-for-hire, no posting our footage before we publish." Get it in writing before the deposit.
