--- POST STARTS ---
I killed the 60-minute meeting at AutoDS.

Then the 30-minute one. Most calls now run 15, and nothing broke.

People warned me decisions would suffer. The opposite happened. Ideas stopped dying in a queue, waiting for a slot on the calendar. The only blocks I never cut were personal development.

That experiment taught me the only statistic worth tracking: time-to-action.

If next quarter becomes next month,
next month becomes next week,
and next week becomes tomorrow,
you will make more money.

Every idea that built this company shipped the same week we thought of it. The ideas we scheduled for later never shipped at all.

Speed compounds. Waiting compounds too, in the wrong direction.

What did you push to next quarter that could start tomorrow?
