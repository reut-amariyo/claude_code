## 1. The big idea in one sentence

**A business that shows up online every day, without you having to.**

AvatarFactory doesn't sell avatars or a video tool. It sells a content business that runs without a camera, built on data from 50M+ videos.

## 2. Target audience: the BYS beginner, one step later

### Why the BYS audience is the right model
The Build Your Store buyer is a complete beginner who wants their first online income and follows creators like Mark Tilbury, AC Hampton and Jordan Welch. That is almost exactly AvatarFactory's primary audience in the brief: make-money-online, ages 18-40, US/UK/Tier 1, mobile-first.

**The link between the two:** BYS solves "I don't have a store". The next problem the same person hits is **"I have a store and nobody visits it"**. AvatarFactory is the answer to that second problem. The AF /start funnel already hands out a ready-made Shopify store after payment, so the two products complete each other.

### Persona: "Jordan, 27"
| | |
|---|---|
| Who | 22-35, US/UK, has a regular job or a gig, studies or recently finished studying. Spends 2+ hours a day on TikTok/Reels |
| Already tried | Watched faceless YouTube / TikTok automation / AI side hustle videos, maybe bought a $27-$297 course. Maybe opened a store, maybe started an account that died after 6 videos |
| What they want | "My first $1K online" → "replace my salary" |
| What stops them | Doesn't want to be on camera: shy, worried about what coworkers and family will think. Doesn't know what to post. Gave up after a few videos with no views. Afraid it's another scam |
| What they believe | "Everyone's making money online except me." "I'm late." "You need to be good on camera to blow up" |
| Language they use | side hustle, faceless, passive income, "go viral", "post daily", niche, digital product, TikTok Shop |
| Where they are | TikTok, IG Reels, YouTube Shorts, MMO creators' YouTube channels, AI side-hustle communities on Discord/Skool, X less so |

### Three segments, in priority order
1. **The hesitant beginner** (core): never started because of the camera. Message: "the avatar goes on camera, not you".
2. **The one who started and gave up**: has a store or an account with no views. Message: "you posted blind, here's what works" + "50M+ videos".
3. **The small online seller**: TikTok Shop / Shopify / digital products, needs a sales channel that isn't ads. Message: "a face for your business, posting every day".

Real creators replacing themselves are organic only, not in paid, per the brief.

## 3. The dream we sell

**The dream:** waking up to an account that published while you slept, views coming in, and a link that sells. A real business with a consistent character, without your face ever being on screen.

The dream ladder, from small to big. Use the rung that fits the stage of the funnel:
1. "My first video is out, and it looks professional" (trial, day 1)
2. "My first 100K views" (approved claim: average within the first 8 videos)
3. "My first sale from the link in bio"
4. "A channel that sells while I sleep" (the brief's line)
5. "A roster of characters, every one of them a business"

**Rule:** sell the dream through rungs 1-3 in ads, where the claims are soft. Rungs 4-5 belong on the site and in organic, with "accounts like" and real examples. Never a dollar promise in paid.

## 4. The emotion we sell

| Before (pain) | After (what we sell) |
|---|---|
| **Exposure**: "what will people think when they see me on camera" | **Relief**: "I can start without anyone knowing it's me" |
| **Helplessness**: "I post and nobody sees it" | **Confidence**: "I know what works, it's built on 50M+ videos" |
| **Being left behind**: "everyone's earning except me, I'm late" | **Being early**: "AI influencers are happening now, and I'm in" |
| **Exhaustion**: filming, editing, five tools | **Control**: "one tool, a video in minutes" |

**The core emotion is relief.** Permission to start without being exposed. Every other emotion depends on it: someone who doesn't feel safe won't start at all.
**The secondary emotion is being early.** It creates urgency without a countdown. The brief bans "your trial ends" style countdowns in email.

What we don't sell: greed, "get rich quick", or Lamborghini fantasy. That's the language of the scams this audience already fears, and it's a red flag for ad platforms.

## 5. The story

### A. The brand story, StoryBrand style
- **Hero:** Jordan, who wants to earn online and doesn't want to go on camera.
- **Villain:** posting blind. Guessing what to post, plus the camera as the thing that stops everything.
- **Guide:** AvatarFactory. We analyzed 50M+ videos and know what works. Brand face: Kate Hollis.
- **Plan:** 1. Build a character: face, voice, story. 2. Pick what's trending in your niche. 3. Get a finished video and post it.
- **Call to action:** "Start your $1 trial." Risk reducer: "$1 three-day trial · First reel in minutes · Cancel anytime".
- **Failure avoided:** another year of "I'll start next month".
- **Success:** one character, one niche, one offer. A channel that sells while you sleep.

### B. The founder story, only if Lior decides to disclose (decision G1)
This is the strongest story available, because it's real and it repeats his life pattern:
- **At 14** he sold on eBay. Nobody saw his face, only the listings. The internet always paid people who knew what sells, not people who knew how to stand in front of a camera.
- **At 21** eBay blocked him, and he built software for himself. That became AutoDS, sold to Fiverr.
- **2023:** Shopify shared that almost a third of new merchants never finish setting up a store, so he built a store in minutes. That's BYS.
- **Today:** the next barrier is the camera. So he built himself an AI influencer, and then the tool that builds one for anyone.
- **The line:** "I always build the tool I need myself first."

If Lior doesn't disclose ownership, only the first half can be used: "I built myself an AI influencer, here's what I learned". That's allowed per the 2026-10-21 rule. AvatarFactory can't be named next to his story in that case.

### C. Customer stories, the engine of proof
"Accounts like" stories from the brief: the faith-niche account at ~$200K a month from a $47 ebook, the wellness account, the small account at $1K-$10K a month. Plus AI influencer pages with live data. Always say "real AI influencers making money", never "personas" or "examples".

## 6. Messaging

**The one-liner (from the brief, use as is):** "We analyzed 50M+ short-form videos, so your AI influencer posts what actually works. You never film, you never guess."

**The fixed order of every piece of content:** go viral → everything in one tool → no face needed.

### Objections and answers
| Objection | Answer |
|---|---|
| "What's the catch with $1?" | It's a three-day trial with 15 credits: one character + one video. Cancel anytime. Never call it "free" |
| "Won't people realize it's AI?" | Consistency is the proof: same face, same voice, every video. 1,389 avatars built on AF have crossed 1M followers |
| "Is it legit/allowed?" | Original characters only. No deepfakes, no real people |
| "I'm not technical" | Under 60 seconds of your work per video. Rendering takes 10-20 minutes in the background |
| "Will I make money?" | Show the math with assumptions labeled; never promise. Five income paths, from your own ebook to TikTok Shop |
| "Another course/tool that won't work for me" | We don't sell knowledge, we sell what's working now: Viral Reels, sorted by views per hour |

## 7. Content and channels

### Content pillars: the same 5 on every channel
1. **Proof:** a breakdown of a real AI influencer account. What it posts, what it sells, the math. *Saves and shares.*
2. **Build in public:** a character from zero, week after week, with real numbers including failures. *Follows, because people want to see the next episode.*
3. **Demo:** Copy Reel, a link in → your character recreates the reel. 20-40 seconds of screen recording. *Direct trials.*
4. **Show the math:** "one $17 ebook × 2% conversion × 100K views = …" with assumptions labeled. *Saves, organic only.*
5. **Myths:** "you have to be on camera", "AI gets blocked", "it's too late". *Comments and reach.*

### Example hooks for the agency to build from, not finished drafts
- "This AI influencer doesn't exist. Her ebook does."
- "3 faceless accounts I'd copy in 2026"
- "I posted 7 videos without showing my face"
- "50M videos analyzed. 1 pattern keeps winning."
- "Paste a viral reel. Get yours in minutes."

### Channels and the role of each
| Channel | Role | What | Main metric |
|---|---|---|---|
| **AF's own TikTok / IG / YouTube Shorts** | The engine | Kate Hollis + the AF characters, 1-3 a day, demos + proof | $1 trials from bio link |
| **Creators / affiliates** | Reach to the exact audience | The same creator type as BYS: Mark Tilbury, AC Hampton, Jordan Welch. 50% lifetime affiliate plan instead of flat fees | Trials per affiliate link |
| **AI influencer pages + SEO** | Evergreen demand | Profiles with live data + a Copy Reel button that works without signing up | Organic signups |
| **Lead magnets** | Collecting people not ready for $1 | Free Trends MCP, income calculator, comment-to-get on IG | Email signups → trial |
| **Email** | Activation | Goal: first video during the trial. Talk about progress, never about time running out | Trial → paid |
| **Paid** (later) | Scaling what works | Only what has already proven itself in organic. Lead with go-viral, never with dollars | CAC vs LTV |
| **Lior on X** | Credibility + founder story | Chapter below | Trials from UTMs |

### X chapter, summary of the separate file
- X isn't the main channel for this audience; TikTok/IG are. X's role is **credibility and founder story**, and reaching the more mature segment of small online sellers.
- About 3 AF posts a week inside Lior's normal mix. Native video demos. The link only in reply 1, with a UTM. Permanent bio + pinned links.
- Everything measured end to end: post → click → register → $1 → paid, with a UTM sheet, branded short links, and UTMs saved on the user record and in billing.

## 8. Funnel and metrics

```
Content (TikTok/IG/X/creators) → bio/reply/affiliate link with UTM
  → AI influencer page or /start → register → $1 trial
  → first video during the trial (activation) → paid plan → renewal
```

| Metric | Why |
|---|---|
| $1 trials per week, by channel | Leading indicator |
| % of trials that render a video | The strongest predictor of conversion. Email's job is to push this |
| Trial → paid, by channel | Separates channels that bring buyers from channels that bring curious people |
| Plan mix, Starter/Creator/Influencer | Creator at $99 is "most popular". Is the channel bringing serious users? |
| Revenue per 1,000 impressions, per channel | Decides where to invest |
| CAC per creator vs LTV | Decides whether to switch to affiliate-only |

Targets get set only after 2 weeks of baseline, not before.
