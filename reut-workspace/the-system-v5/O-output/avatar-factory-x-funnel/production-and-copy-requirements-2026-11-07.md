Built 2026-11-07. Goes with `avatarfactory-marketing-plan-2026-11-07.md` and `x-to-1-dollar-trial-plan-2026-11-07.md`.
Teams: production (graphics, video editing, screen recording) and the copy agency (AvatarFactory posts + Lior's X).

## Part A: production team

### A1. The 7 core screen recordings. Build these first; they feed every channel

| # | Name | What to record | Opening shot (first 2 seconds) | Length |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Copy Reel** (hero demo) | Paste an IG/TikTok link → pick an avatar → AF rebuilds the reel | The original and your avatar's version side by side | 20-35s |
| 2 | **Avatar from zero** | Avatar wizard: identity → 3 face options → voice → "See Your Avatar Say It" | The finished avatar talking | 30-45s |
| 3 | **Viral Reels** | Sorting by views per hour, Outperformers badges 5x/10x/25x, filter by niche | A 25x badge on a reel | 15-25s |
| 4 | **Script → video** | Content expert brainstorms a hook → co-writer → Hear It → render | The finished vertical video, captioned | 30-45s |
| 5 | **Fix one shot** | Re-render a single shot without redoing the whole video | Before / after of that one shot | 15-20s |
| 6 | **Your avatar in your world** | Upload a photo of a room / product → the avatar appears inside it | The avatar inside the uploaded scene | 15-25s |
| 7 | **Trends in a chat** | The free Trends MCP answers "what's trending in my niche" | The answer with formats and hooks | 15-25s |

### A2. Rules for every screen recording
1. **Start with the result, not the process.** The first 2 seconds show the finished video. The process comes after.
2. **A clean demo account.** American example data: names like Sarah Johnson, gmail addresses. No real user data, no personal emails, no browser bookmarks or other tabs on screen.
3. **Only live features on screen.** Never show "Coming soon" buttons, Instagram/TikTok connections, scheduling, Campaign Studio or automation. If one is visible, crop or cover it.
4. **Cut the waits.** Rendering takes 10-20 minutes. Cut it and add a small "render sped up" label. That's honest, and the brief asks for honest limits.
5. **Zoom and a visible cursor** on every click. The viewer is on a phone.
6. **Burned-in captions.** Most people watch muted.
7. **No competitor names**, and no faces of real people or celebrities inside the avatar. Original characters only.
8. **No "free" text** anywhere on screen. Only "$1 three-day trial".

### A3. Formats and delivery
| Use | Aspect | Resolution | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| TikTok / Reels / Shorts | 9:16 | 1080x1920 | Captions inside the safe area, away from the bottom and right UI |
| X feed + LinkedIn | 4:5 | 1080x1350 | A crop of the 9:16 master, not a separate shoot |
| X Article cover | 5:2 | 1500x600 | One image, one number or one artifact |
| Static images for X | 4:5 or 1:1 | 1080x1350 / 1080x1080 | A single number or a single real artifact, never a diagram |

**Every video gets delivered in 2 versions:** a 9:16 master + a 4:5 crop. Plus one version without burned captions.

**The file name = the UTM.** Format: `<MMDD>_<slug>_<version>_<aspect>.mp4`, for example `1110_copy-reel-demo_v1_9x16.mp4`. The slug is identical to the post's `utm_content`, so every file connects to a post and to a sale.

### A4. Ongoing series

**Build in public: a character from zero**
- The team builds one AI influencer with AF, posts daily on its own IG/TikTok, and logs the real numbers every week.
- Weekly deliverable: a 30-45s recap video + a screenshot of the account stats. Real numbers only, including weak weeks.

**Proof: account breakdowns**
- A screen recording of an AI influencer page on the AF site, showing the live data: 30-day views, follower growth, top reels.
- Text on screen: "real AI influencer" + the numbers. Never "persona" or "example".

**Lior, only if ownership is disclosed**
- Lior next to his own avatar in split screen: "which one is real?" 15-30s, filmed on a phone, natural light.
- Lior filmed talking 15-30s per idea, for the founder story.

**Kate Hollis, AF's brand face**
- Every image or video that represents AF itself is built only from Kate's master image. Never a new face.

### A5. Weekly output

## Part B: copy agency (AvatarFactory posts + Lior's X)

### B1. Hard rules
1. **The link never goes in the post body.** The tagged link goes in reply 1 only, taken from the UTM sheet, never typed by hand.
2. **The order is always:** go viral → everything in one tool → no face needed. Never open with "all-in-one".
3. **Only approved claims** from the brief. Any new number needs a founder's sign-off.
4. **The offer:** "Start your $1 trial". Never "free", "start free" or "no card required".
5. **Never promise** auto-posting, scheduling, Campaign Studio or automation.
6. **No dollar promises** in anything that might become an ad. Organic can show "accounts like" with real numbers.
7. **Lior's ownership:** according to Lior's decision, G1. Until he decides, AF is not named next to his personal story.
8. **Lior's voice:** hook under 10 words with a concrete anchor, no parentheses, no em dashes, no "It's not X, it's Y", odd numbers in lists, at most 1 in 3 Lior posts carries the offer.

### B2. Every post goes in with a brief that has these fields
| Field | Example |
|---|---|
| Lane | Demo / Proof / Build in public / Money math / Myth |
| Format | Native video / thread / Article / static |
| Hook | Under 10 words |
| Asset | `1110_copy-reel-demo_v1_4x5.mp4` |
| utm_content | `1110_copy-reel-demo` |
| Reply 1 | One line + the short link |
| Who answers replies | Name + 60-minute window after publishing |

### B3. Reply 1 template
One sentence connected to the post + the short link. Examples of the structure:
- "Here's the tool from the video, $1 three-day trial: [link]"
- "Want to try it on your own niche? [link]"

### B4. After publishing
- 60 minutes of answering every reply. This is the strongest signal on X.
- Within 24 hours, fill the dashboard row: impressions, replies, profile clicks, link clicks.
- Every Sunday: Reut reviews which utm_content brought $1 trials and paid conversions, and the brief for next week changes accordingly.

### B5. Who does what
| Step | Owner |
|---|---|
| Weekly brief: lanes, topics, number of posts | Reut |
| Copy + hooks + reply 1 | Agency |
| Screen recordings, editing, graphics | Production team |
| UTM link + short link per post | Reut / agency, from the sheet |
| Approval before publishing | Reut. Lior on anything about him |
| Publishing + 60 minutes of replies | Agency / Lior, manually |
| Measurement + weekly review | Reut |
