$92M exit at 28.
The timeline starts with a punishment:

Age 10: Dad locked my computer. Hacked it.
Age 12: Fixed neighbors' computers to buy a skateboard.
Age 14: First $40 online, reselling gadgets from China.
Age 21: eBay blocked my 7-year account. Income gone.
Same year: opened 100 new accounts instead.
Built software to manage them. It became AutoDS.
Bootstrapped. No website for the first 2 years.
Then a VAT bill I couldn't pay. Took a loan.
Feared the market leader for years. Acquired them.
Walked away from a PE deal a day before signing.
Age 28: sold to Fiverr in 23 days.
Today: 250 employees. Still CEO. Same game.

From the outside this looks like a straight line.
Half of these lines were disasters in real time.
The blocked account hurt more than the exit felt good.
It also built the exit.

What does line one of your timeline say?
