# Lior LinkedIn — Impressions Recovery Action Plan (30 days)
_Created 2026-06-25 · grounded in lior-performance-log + Top-12 10K+ Playbook_

## Diagnosis — why impressions dropped ~93% from the May peak

| Period | Median impressions | What was posted |
|---|---|---|
| Late May (24–31) | ~18K | Google/Base44 killer (146K), Birthday (18K), "Tough days for Israeli tech" (14.8K) |
| June (1–14) | ~2K | wellness, tactical AI tips, YouTube channel, Claude repricing |

Three controllable causes:
1. **Lane drift.** May winners = 3 proven archetypes (Industry Release Decode, Milestone Reflection, Market-Empathy). June fell into wellness-without-bridge, tactical tips, and channel-marketing — the buckets that always die for Lior (518 / 546 / 1,175 imp).
2. **No personal stake / no friction.** The dead posts are generic takes nobody can disagree with. No debate = no comments = no algorithm.
3. **Low cadence + post-spike cooldown.** ~2–3 posts/month, and the algo cools you after a 146K mega-post. The way out is consistency, not one big swing.

## The plan — 4 tracks

### 1. Content — back to the winning lane (3 posts/week)
Iron rule: every post maps to a proven archetype + a real Lior number/story + an articulable "vs." that invites disagreement. If not, don't publish.

Weekly mix:
- **1× Industry Release Decode (Archetype 4)** — the bread-and-butter (did 146K & 93K). Newsjack a giant-AI release within 24–48h, with operator defense + a number.
- **1× Personal experiment / milestone (Archetype 6 or 8)** — real AI experiment that broke, or a milestone reflection with a real number.
- **1× Empathy / operations (Archetype 9, 5, or 1)** — market-empathy newsjack with generosity, or "He said:" management critique, or AutoDS platform-shock crisis response.

8 concrete starter ideas:
1. **Claude Tag redo** — the failed agents post, but with a personal take: a VC-funded startup that died building "an agent" vs. AutoDS's distribution moat. (Archetype 4, fast)
2. Any new Anthropic/OpenAI/Google release this week → decode "the founder threat + the one defense"
3. **Crisis Response** — any real AutoDS platform shock (algo/policy/dispute) — strongest archetype (620K)
4. A real AI experiment Lior ran that failed at one specific point (Archetype 6)
5. Layoff wave / painful market news → empathy + "DMs open" (Archetype 9, best for engagement)
6. **"He said:"** — a wrong management practice Lior heard at a conference/dinner (Archetype 5)
7. Credible-authority ranking on Israel / SaaS founders (Archetype 7)
8. Upcoming real milestone → 10-lesson list (Archetype 8)

### 2. Comments — the most under-used reach engine
5–10 quality comments/day on big SaaS/AI-founder posts in Lior's lane (200+ reactions). **Every comment carries a real Lior micro-story** (number/client/experience), 2–4 lines, never a mini-post. This is the LinkedIn version of the scout-replies engine — run it manually.

### 3. Network — build the right audience
20–30 targeted connection requests/day: SaaS founders, e-commerce builders, AI builders, Israeli tech. Source from commenters on his + competitors' big posts, conference attendees. Optional short personal note lifts accept rate.

### 4. Mechanics (already in our rules — just enforce)
- First-hour window: Lior replies to every comment in the first 60 min → doubles reach
- Self-repost 6–8h after
- Pinned comment with expansion/link
- 750–1,200 chars (except Archetypes 2 & 8)
- ALT text always

## Measure weekly
Median impressions/post, comments/post (top engagement signal), reposts, follower growth.
30-day target: back to 5K+ median with at least one 10K+ post every two weeks.
