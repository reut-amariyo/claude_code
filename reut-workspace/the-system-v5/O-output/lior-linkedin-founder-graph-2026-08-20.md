Line chart titled Action Pattern on black. The line starts at Just Start, idea, build,
sell, then drops to a point labelled The No. It climbs to Break Through, drops to a
second The No, then rises through Aggressive Scale to What's Next, new game, next peak.

--- POST STARTS ---

Every founder goes through the same stations.

(I struggled for years.)

1. START.
You ship before you feel ready, because ready never comes.

2. GET NO.
The market pushes back.
A launch nobody notices, a trial that never converts, a bad review from a user.

3. BREAK THROUGH.
You build your way around the no. Real products come from this phase.

4. GET NO. AGAIN. BIGGER.
The next rejection scales with you. Bigger company, bigger stakes, same feeling.

5. SCALE AGGRESSIVELY.
You hire faster than you can learn names.
When something finally works, you bet the company on it.

6. NEW GAME, NEXT PEAK.
The prize for winning a level is a harder level.

Trust your grind (:

--- POST ENDS ---

## How the hook was built (iteration log)

Line 1 candidates rejected: "Looking back, the founder journey fits one graph."
  ("Looking back," is on the playbook's banned line-1 list, fails gate check 1)
  "If you want to build a business, these are the stations" (teacher posture, no anchor)
Line 1 final: "Every founder goes through the same stations." — 7 words, pattern opener,
  sanctioned by anti-AI Rule 8. Soft flag: no hard anchor, the aside carries it.

Line-2 parenthetical - the ONE sanctioned parentheses exception: hook position only,
one sentence under 6 words, must flip line 1, max once per post.
  Final:    (I struggled for years.) — 4 words, past tense, plain admission
  Earlier:  (Wish I'd known that sooner.)
  Also OK:  (I thought it was me.) / (I've been there too often.) /
            (More laps than I'd like.) / (I pretended I was fine.) /
            (Would've handled the drops better.) / (The drops would've hurt less.)
  Rejected: "emotional rollercoaster" phrasing (dead cliche, trips anti-AI Rule 2);
            "I thought it was just me" (6 words, burns the one "just" allowance);
            "(Some of them broke me.)" (drama register, off-archetype).

Guardrails held throughout: no fear confession, no imposter/disbelief line,
