# LinkedIn Paid Test Run-Book — $400 Thought Leader Ad
Built 2026-09-01. Owner: Reut. Asset: the exit-deck giveaway post.

## What this test is actually for

$400 does not buy followers. At LinkedIn CPMs for a tight founder audience, roughly $40-70 per 1,000 impressions, $400 buys about **6,000-9,000 impressions**. The same post did 66,682 organically. So paid adds roughly 10% on top of what the post already achieved on its own.

Read that as the whole point: this test does not grow the account. It buys a clean **cost-per-outcome number** that lets you argue for or kill a real budget line. Anyone who promises a follower jump from $400 is selling something.

**Success is a rate, not a total.** Do not judge this on "how many followers did we get".

## Reality check before you spend anything

Organic benchmark from the exit-deck post:
- 66,682 impressions
- 317 reactions = 4.8 reactions per 1,000 impressions
- ~80 connection requests = 1.2 requests per 1,000 impressions

If paid converts at even half the organic rate, 8,000 paid impressions produce about 5 connection requests. That number is inside the noise of a normal week. Which is why the measurement plan below tracks cost per engagement and cost per profile view, not follower delta.

**The strategic conclusion you can already see from the math:** if a follower ends up costing $15-25, then 100K followers via paid costs seven figures. Paid is a supplement to the signature series, never the path to 100K. That matches what the growth-plan data already said, LinkedIn followers need a conversion lever, not more purchased reach.

## Pre-flight, do these before building the campaign

1. **Format eligibility.** Open Campaign Manager and confirm the exit-deck post is selectable as a Thought Leader Ad. It is a document carousel, and format eligibility for TLA has changed repeatedly. If the document post is not selectable, stop and use plan B below. Do not redesign the post to fit the ad format, that destroys the thing you are testing.

2. **Lior's permission.** The Page admin sends the sponsorship request from Campaign Manager, Lior approves it from his notifications. He has to do this himself, you cannot do it for him. Ask him before you build, not after, or the campaign sits unlaunched.

3. **Billing.** AutoDS Campaign Manager account, payment method on file, and confirm the ad account is not sharing a budget cap with a live AutoDS campaign that would starve this one.

4. **Freeze the creative.** Once sponsored, AutoDS cannot edit the copy. Any change has to come from Lior editing the original post, and editing a live post can reset its distribution. Lock it now.

**Plan B if documents are not eligible:** sponsor the highest-performing single-image or text post that also produced connection requests. Accept that you are then testing paid amplification generally, not the exit-deck format specifically, and say so in the write-up.

## Campaign build

| Setting | Value | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Objective | Engagement | The only objective that optimizes for interactions with the post, which is what drives profile clicks. Brand awareness optimizes for impressions and will flatter your CPM while producing nothing. |
| Format | Thought Leader Ad on the existing post | Never a new ad creative. The point is that paid engagement lands on the same public post as organic. |
| Budget | $400 total, $28/day | |
| Schedule | 14 days | Long enough for frequency to build, short enough to read this month. |
| Bidding | Maximum delivery | Check actual CPM on day 3. If it is above $90, switch to manual bidding and cap it. |
| Audience size | 30,000-80,000 | Critical. A 2M audience spreads $400 into one impression per person and teaches you nothing. A small pool gets each person to frequency 2-3, which is where paid actually works. |

**Audience spec**
- Locations: United States, United Kingdom, Canada, Australia. English-speaking ICP geos only.
- Job titles: Founder, Co-Founder, CEO, Owner, Managing Director.
- Company size: 1-200 employees.
- Plus interests or groups: entrepreneurship, e-commerce, SaaS.
- Exclude: students, interns, entry level seniority.
- **Turn OFF Audience Expansion and the LinkedIn Audience Network.** Both are on by default and both will spend your $400 outside the audience you chose.

**The vanity trap, refuse it:** CPMs in cheap geos are a fraction of US rates and will produce 3-4x the impressions for the same $400, plus more followers. Those followers do not match the ICP, they dilute the interest-graph signal that determines Lior's organic reach, and they make every future engagement rate look worse. Do not run this in low-CPM geos to make the report look better.

## Measurement plan

**Baseline, capture before launch.** Screenshot and record:
- Lior's exact follower count, with the date.
- Weekly follower delta for the 2 weeks prior.
- Impressions on each of his last 6 organic posts.
- Weekly profile views from creator analytics.

**During the 14 days:**
- Keep organic posting cadence and post types identical. Same frequency, same mix. If you change organic behavior mid-test the comparison is worthless.
- Log daily: spend, impressions, engagements, CPM, cost per engagement.
- Log weekly: follower delta, profile views, connection requests received.

**The organic suppression question.** This test answers it for your account. Track the organic impressions of Lior's non-sponsored posts during the 14 days against the 2-week baseline. If they hold, the "paid kills organic" claim is dead for this account and you never have to argue about it again. If they genuinely drop by more than 30%, you found a real constraint for $400 instead of hearing it secondhand.

## Decision thresholds, set now so you cannot rationalize later

| Result | Threshold | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Green | Paid engagement rate at 50%+ of the organic rate, and cost per profile view under $2 | Build the case for $1,500-2,000/month on the signature series |
| Amber | Engagement rate at 25-50% of organic, or cost per profile view $2-5 | One more $400 test on a different audience before any scaling decision |
| Red | Under 25% of organic rate, or CPM above $100, or organic posts drop 30%+ | Paid does not fit this asset. Kill it and put the budget into the conversion lever instead |
