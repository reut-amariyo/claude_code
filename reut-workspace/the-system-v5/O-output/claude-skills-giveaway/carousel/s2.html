/grill-me I want to launch a paid newsletter for founders next month
Loaded skill

Here’s round one.

❓Q1 - Who exactly is the reader? “Founders” covers everyone from someone with an idea to a CEO with 200 employees. Each stage has different problems, different budgets, and reads different things.

➡️ Pick a narrow slice, defined by stage and one other filter. A tight niche is easier to charge for in month one than a general founder letter.

❓Q2 - What is the one thing they pay for? Insight, a hard-to-get resource, time saved, or access?

➡️ Paying founders want something they can use the same day.
