## MAIN PROMPT

Ultra-photorealistic RAW smartphone selfie still, captured mid-sentence, using the provided reference photo as absolute visual truth for the subject's identity.

IDENTITY LOCK IS CRITICAL. The man in the frame is my subject, Lior, preserving his identity exactly from the reference photo: same face, facial structure, eyes, eyebrows, nose, jawline, skin tone, hairline and short dark textured haircut, light stubble, ear shape, neck and shoulder build, age. No blending, no face morphing, no approximation, no beautification, no slimming, no de-aging.

POSE AND ACTION: he is standing indoors and filming himself on a phone held in his right hand at arm's length, so the right arm exits the bottom right of the frame and the phone itself is not visible. Head and shoulders fill the frame. His face is tilted slightly up toward the lens, eyes locked on the lens, mouth closed in a relaxed neutral expression about to speak. His left hand is raised to shoulder height in a loose open-palm explaining gesture, fingers relaxed, hand clearly separated from the face and jaw with visible air between them.

WARDROBE: plain white cotton crew-neck t-shirt, slightly loose fit, soft natural wrinkles, no print, no logo, no pocket. No hat, no glasses, no jewelry except a thin black fabric-strap watch on the left wrist.

CAMERA AND FRAMING: front-facing smartphone camera look, 24mm equivalent wide lens with mild edge distortion, high angle about 20 degrees above eye level, tight framing from mid-chest up, deep focus with the background fully readable, slight handheld tilt of 2 to 3 degrees. Vertical 9:16.

ENVIRONMENT: bright modern apartment interior on an overcast day. A wide dark-stained wooden pillar or ceiling beam runs vertically along the right third of the frame, partly behind his left shoulder. Behind him a plain white wall, a white-framed window with daylight coming through, and a pale ceiling visible in the top left corner. Domestic, unstaged, real-life feel, nothing styled, no plants, no decor, no furniture in frame.

LIGHTING: soft flat overcast daylight from a large window camera-left, even exposure across the face, faint natural shadow under the chin and nose, phone-camera dynamic range with the window slightly overexposed. No studio lighting, no rim light, no color gels, no golden hour.

TEXTURE AND REALISM: real skin pores, slight natural shine on the forehead and nose, individual stubble hairs, flyaway hairs on top, visible cotton weave on the t-shirt, subtle phone-sensor noise in the shadows. No smoothing, no glow, no plastic skin, no AI artifacts.

STYLE CONSTRAINTS: must look like a real frame pulled from an Instagram reel filmed on a phone, casual and imperfect, no cinematic grading, no vignette, no text, no captions, no logos, no watermark, no stylization.

COMPOSITION FOR VERTICAL: face centered in the upper-middle of a 9:16 frame, clean space in the lower third for word captions, the wooden beam and window edge providing depth on the right and left.
