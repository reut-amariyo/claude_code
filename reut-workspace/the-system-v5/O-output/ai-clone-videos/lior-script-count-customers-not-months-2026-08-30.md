# AI Clone Video Script — "Count Customers, Not Months"

Created 2026-08-30. Format: 1:1 LIVE STAGE (woman asks from the audience, Lior answers).
Replaces the "terrified to quit my job" script, which was re-rendered on 2026-08-30

## Why the last one didn't land (script diagnosis)

Transcribed the 28s render. It is the identical v1 dialogue, only the images changed.

1. Hook has no anchor. "I have an idea for a business, but I'm terrified" is a stock
   startup line. No number, no name, no confession. Nothing to stop a thumb.
2. The advice is commodity. "Don't quit your job, get one customer" is said by every
   founder account on the platform. Nothing in it is only Lior's to say.
3. Zero proof. Not one number in 28 seconds. The clip has no reason to be him.
4. The best line, "your salary is your runway", lands at 0:13. Too deep.
5. Too many turns. 13 micro-beats in 28s means 12 cut points, each one a drop-off.
6. The closer is a slogan, not a quotable. Nobody stitches "start now, fix later".

## THE SCRIPT (v1, for approval)

Runtime target: 22 seconds. ~62 words.

--- SCRIPT STARTS ---

WOMAN: I've been at this eleven months and made almost nothing.

LIOR: How many people paid you?

WOMAN: Three.

LIOR: Three strangers gave you money and you call that nothing.

LIOR: One week I gave it everything and the deposit said seven dollars. Same work, months later, seventy thousand.

LIOR: Count customers, not months.

--- SCRIPT ENDS ---

First cut if the render runs long: "Same work, months later, seventy thousand."

### Why this one works

- Line 1 is a confession with a number, 9 words, and it is HER admitting failure.
  That is the scroll-stop the old open never had.
- "Three" as a one-word clip is a pattern break and a free cut point.
- The seven-dollar deposit is Lior's own line from reel DZdBg_Dov6K, so it is his real
  voice and his real proof, not a borrowed stat.
- One idea: you are measuring the wrong unit.
- "Count customers, not months" is four words, quotable, and stitchable.
- AutoDS is never named. This is founder journey, not product.
