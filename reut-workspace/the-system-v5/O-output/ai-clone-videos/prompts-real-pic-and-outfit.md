## PROMPT — CHANGE OUTFIT (two-image: person + outfit)

A photorealistic full-body or mid-shot portrait of the person from the first image, seamlessly wearing and accessorized with the entire outfit visible in the second image — including all clothing, shoes, jewelry, eyewear, or accessories. If the outfit image includes a specific hairstyle, hair texture, or grooming style, the subject's hair must adapt to match it naturally while maintaining the color, facial structure, and identity features from image one. The outfit from image two must fit the subject's natural proportions with perfect fabric drape, posture coherence, and lighting consistency. Captured in RAW on an 85mm f/1.4 lens with soft diffused daylight or studio lighting, shallow depth of field, and realistic shadow behavior. Facial features, skin texture, and identity markers from the first image remain fully consistent — natural pores, subtle asymmetry, and human realism. Place the subject in a luxury masculine-style environment that conveys power and authority — such as a premium modern loft, dark wood executive office, marble textures, soft ambient rim lighting, and high-end interior design cues. The final image must look like a high-fashion editorial photo with ultra-sharp detail on face, fabrics, materials, and accessories, with a clean, modern, authoritative luxury background that matches the outfit's aesthetic.

## Pattern notes (what makes these prompts work)

- Identity lock: "same man from the reference / preserving his exact face, hair, skin texture".
- Realism anchors: "RAW", "documentary realism", "85mm f/1.4", "natural pores and
  subtle imperfections", "no stylization or cinematic exaggeration".
- One named mood per prompt (intimate podcast / old money / late-night discipline).
- Light is always described in 3 layers: key at 45°, fill, rim/ambient.
- Microphone positioned "visible but never competing with the subject".
