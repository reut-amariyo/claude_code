# IG Reel Cut — "Best Investment" (sharpened 2026-08-30)

Source: lior-script-best-investment-podcast-qa.md (v2, 20s approved direction).
Sharpened for Instagram Reels: cold open on the answer, rehook at ~9s, ends on the
quotable with no CTA inside the video, per the Dan Martell reference specs.
Runtime target: 21-23s. 4 VEO clips.

## What changed from v2

1. Cut "People think entrepreneurship is freedom, and it is, eventually." Slow ramp,
   dies in the first 3 seconds of a reel. Reels need the payoff line first.
2. Added the rehook "Nothing about your effort explains the difference." His real words
   from the source reel. Lands at ~9s, exactly where IG retention drops.
3. Moved the $7 line before the trophy line so the number hits early. Numbers hold.
4. Killed "Think about that for a second." It softens the ending. The trophy line is
   the quotable, it should be the last thing heard.
5. Title bar pinned the full runtime, ellipsis for the curiosity gap.

## FINAL SCRIPT

--- SCRIPT STARTS ---

HOST, female voice, off camera: What's your best investment?

LIOR: The years that paid me nothing.

VOICE 2, male, off camera: Say it again please.

LIOR, small smile, leans in: You pour everything you have into a week, and the deposit says 7 dollars.

Nothing about your effort explains the difference.

The game never hands you the trophy. It turns you into the person who can take it.

--- SCRIPT ENDS ---

52 words. ~22 seconds at his natural pace.

## Clip split (4 VEO clips, fixed camera, 6-8s each)

| Clip | Shot | Lines | Tonality prompt |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Host, off-camera audio over Lior listening | "What's your best investment?" + "The years that paid me nothing." | Host: light, curious. Lior: flat, certain, no smile |
| 2 | Lior, medium close | "Say it again please." beat of silence | Lior holds eye contact, half beat, faint smile starting |
| 3 | Lior, same framing | "You pour everything... says 7 dollars." + "Nothing about your effort explains the difference." | Leaning in, quieter, almost confessional |
| 4 | Lior, slight punch-in | "The game never hands you the trophy. It turns you into the person who can take it." | Grounded, slower, lands the last word and stops |

Pad each clip's last line with a throwaway word so VEO speeds up delivery, editor cuts it.

## Edit specs

- 9:16, 1080x1920. Podcast setting, mic in frame, fixed camera, zero movement.
- Title bar pinned top center the ENTIRE video, white rounded pill, black text:
  **His best investment wasn't money...**
- Karaoke captions bottom third, 3-5 words per line, one word highlighted per line.
  Highlight: **nothing** / **7 dollars** / **trophy** / **person**.
- Hard cut between clips. No music bed over speech. Optional low pad under clip 4 only.
- First frame must show the title bar within 1 second.
- No CTA inside the video. The trophy line is the ending.
