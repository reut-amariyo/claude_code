## STEP 2 — Script, split + tonality (the big v1 fix)

Same approved dialogue, restructured from ~13 micro-clips to 6 clips of 6-8s.
Merging works because the editor cuts inside a clip. Punctuation added per Nicola:
commas = pauses, exclamation = confident delivery. Filler tails in [brackets] get cut
in editing, they exist only to force faster delivery.

🟠 HER clips (tone: nervous, sincere, a little shaky → warming up):
1. "I have an idea for a business, but I'm terrified! Of quitting my job. Everyone
   says you need to go all in." — tone: anxious, vulnerable, honest
2. "Okay, so how do I start? [Like, what would be the very first step?]" — tone:
   curious, hopeful, leaning in
3. "Just one customer? [That's it? Are you serious right now?]" — tone: surprised,
   half-laughing, disbelieving

🔵 LIOR clips (tone: calm, warm operator → energetic and decisive):
1. "Of what? ... Who said you need to quit? Your salary is your runway." — tone:
   calm, curious, then quietly confident. Editor splits this clip after "Of what?"
2. "Just start! Get one paying customer this month." — tone: energetic, decisive,
   friendly push
3. "One real customer teaches you more than a year of planning. Start now, fix
   later! [That's the whole game, honestly.]" — tone: excited and powerful, closing
   line energy

## STEP 3 — Video (Google Flow, Veo 3.1 — NOT Gemini, kills the watermark)

Per clip, 7-part plain-text prompt. Example, Lior clip 1:

The subject in the photo speaks and communicates in a natural and realistic way.
Tone: calm and quietly confident, like a founder answering a question he has heard
a hundred times.
Gestures: aligned with the meaning of the sentence, always centered and at chest
height, one small open-palm gesture.
Language: American English.
Camera: no zoom, 100% stable for the entire video. The subject remains at the same
distance from the camera for the entire video.
No on-screen text. No music. No additional voices. Only the subject speaks.
Phrase:
"Of what? ... Who said you need to quit? Your salary is your runway."

Example, her clip 1:

The subject in the photo speaks and communicates in a natural and realistic way.
Tone: nervous but sincere, voice slightly shaky, genuinely worried.
Gestures: minimal, one hand holds the microphone, the free hand makes a small
uncertain gesture, movements centered.
Language: American English.
Camera: no zoom, 100% stable for the entire video. The subject remains at the same
distance from the camera for the entire video.
No on-screen text. No music. No additional voices. Only the subject speaks.
Phrase:
"I have an idea for a business, but I'm terrified! Of quitting my job. Everyone
says you need to go all in."

After the first working clip per character, use the batch trick:
"From now on, I will type only the phrase I want the subject to say. Each time,
create a new prompt using the exact same structure and format as before, with the
same photo, the same subject, and the same overall attitude. Only adapt the gestures
so they match the meaning and energy of the new phrase."

Generation: FAST mode first (free) to validate prompt + phrase length → QUALITY mode
regenerate → pick best. Fixed camera always.
