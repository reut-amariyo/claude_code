1. "HOW DID AUTODS START?"
Keep it under 90 seconds, he wants to get to the deal.
- 21, eBay suspends the account that was my main income
- Open 100 stores, stock and prices turn into chaos
- Build software for myself
- Show it at a conference, sellers ask for it, first beta users

2. "YOU NEVER RAISED MONEY. WHY?"
- Profitable from day one
- I didn't know raising was an option. No background, no startup network
- Later it was a choice. No time for decks
- Why it matters for a sale: no investors, no preference stack. Nobody else decides when or to whom you sell
Never say investors turned you down. They never did.

3. "WHAT MADE AUTODS SELLABLE?"
This is his favorite question. He scores companies on recurring revenue, owner dependence and customer concentration.
- Subscription software, recurring revenue
- 1.8M+ users, no single customer matters
- 8 sales channel integrations. The eBay ban at 21 taught me to never depend on one platform
- A real management team: CTO co-founder since 2018, COO, CMO, GM. I run it with OKRs, the team brings the ideas, the company does not depend on me day to day
- We bought the competitors we used to fear, DSM Tool and others. Market leader going into the sale

4. "WAS FIVERR THE FIRST OFFER?"
The PE story. Expect him to dig here.
- Two years before Fiverr: 6 months with a private equity fund, full due diligence, about $30M on the table
- Walked away one day before closing
- Why: their plan was to cut staff and run AutoDS as a cash cow. People who joined in the early years on below-market salaries would have been the first to go
- Frame it as protecting the team. Never "I wanted a bigger number"
- Line: "Know exactly what you're not willing to sell."

5. "HOW DID THE FIVERR DEAL COME TOGETHER?"
- Chose Fiverr for vision and synergy, not only price
- The B-list trick: pitch companies you would never sell to first. Practice on them, watch which slide loses the room, fix it while nothing is at stake. By the time your dream buyer sits down, the deck has survived every hard question
- 47-slide deck, which I later gave away free
- Closed in 23 days, after 6 months with the PE fund
- FILL IN: who reached out first, and did you use a banker

6. "CAN YOU SHARE THE PRICE, THE STRUCTURE, THE MULTIPLE?"
Decide this answer tonight. He asks it in almost every episode.
- Fiverr is public, so the deal terms are in its annual report. He or his team can look them up
- Public number: a reported $92M
- Filing: $55.7M at closing value, which includes an earn-out of up to $36M over 3 years
- Public ARR is $20M+, so he may do the math on air
Suggested answer: "It was reported at $92 million. Part of it is an earn-out, and since Fiverr is public, I'll leave the exact terms to their filings."
- Don't discuss earn-out targets, it is still running
- Don't say "4x the PE offer". $92M vs $30M is about 3x. Say "a multiple of the PE offer"
- Worth a quick check with Fiverr IR or legal on what you can say

7. "YOU'RE STILL CEO. WHAT'S LIFE LIKE AFTER THE SALE?"
- AutoDS runs independently, I'm still CEO
- Since the deal: BuildYourStore.ai, CreateUGC, ShopShark, more acquisitions like Yaballe
- "Business is a game with no finish line. Now I have it, and I still want to play the same game."
- Parents asked when I'd finish my degree right up to the exit. Now they ask me to stop posting about it
Nothing negative about Fiverr.

8. "HOW DID YOU TELL THE TEAM?"
- FILL IN, we have nothing on record
- Don't imply anyone got equity or got rich from the deal. Talk about jobs and the people who built it

9. "ANY SELLER'S REMORSE? WHAT WOULD YOU DO DIFFERENTLY?"
- FILL IN your real answer
- Options from your own canon: hire HR earlier, delegate earlier, bring advisors in sooner

10. "ADVICE FOR AN OWNER WHO WANTS TO SELL IN 2-3 YEARS?"
- Fix the base first: if LTV over CAC doesn't work, great product and great marketing still collapse
- Practice on buyers you'd never sell to
- Decide what you won't sell before anyone makes an offer
- Advisors are the fastest shortcut. You can pay some of them in coffee
