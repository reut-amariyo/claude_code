## FINAL

**What brands never tell you - Behind the scenes of a $10M/year influencer budget, from the CEO who runs it**

Why do two creators with the same audience size get offers that differ by 10x? The answer sits on the other side of the table, and brands almost never share it.

Lior Pozin, the founder and CEO of AutoDS, manages an influencer budget of over $10 million a year across 200+ creators.

In this talk, he'll open his playbook: how creators get chosen, what closes a deal, what belongs in the agreement, and what turns one campaign into a years-long partnership.

The difference comes down to a few decisions:

- How to find the right win-win with every brand, and how to earn beyond a fixed fee with revenue share
- How to turn one-off deals into long-term contracts, instead of hopping between brands and burning your name
- How to build loyalty with your audience and your brands at once, so promoting never feels like selling.

And that's just the tip of the iceberg. The creators who win the next few years will be the ones who understand how brands think.

Creators face the same two conflicts: earning from brands without losing their audience's trust, and building stable income in a deal-by-deal world. Lior Pozin, who sold his company to Fiverr for $92M and invests over $10M a year in 200+ influencers, reveals how brands choose creators, what to put in your agreement, and how to negotiate long-term deals.
