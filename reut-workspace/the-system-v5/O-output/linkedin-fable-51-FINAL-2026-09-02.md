--- POST STARTS ---

Fable 5.1 just killed AI website slop.

(If you are building a site, this one is for you.)

AI design has always failed the same way.

Generic.

Same layout, same gradient, same spacing.

You could spot it in two seconds.

Now the new @Anthropic model gets you past that. Here is the process:

1. Start with brand guidelines so the colors, type, and visual language stay consistent.

2. Define the pain, the person, and the promise so the design fits the business and the people it is for.

3. Give the visitor feedback while they scroll, even if that is only text fading in or elements moving at different speeds.

4. Pull specific inspiration from competitors' sites.

5. Reuse existing components instead of asking the model to invent every button, card, and animation from scratch.

6. Check the mobile version. Most people will look you up on their phone.

7. Run screenshot verification across the full scroll so the agent catches broken layers, awkward cuts, and elements sitting outside the frame.

Skip seven and the build ships with a hard cut in the animation that nobody notices until a visitor does.

Seven is the step everyone skips.

It is also the one doing the work.

Have you built anything with it yet? Tell me what you thought.

#aiagents #webdesign
