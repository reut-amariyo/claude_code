## Donor research, what I actually found on LinkedIn (2026-09-07)

Reut asked about Playtika. Searched their company page and LinkedIn content search:

- I did not find individual senior-hire welcome posts from the Playtika page. Their senior hires go out as press releases, for example the March 2023 CHRO and Global Head of Communications announcement on BusinessWire and their investor site, not as a page post.
- Kissterra never posted a welcome post for Ben when he joined them. Nothing exists in content search.

What company-page welcome posts actually do, live numbers from search:

- ONMARK, new Sales Rep, 2 weeks old: 63 reactions, 2 comments
- Playdigious, new Senior Brand Manager, 1 month old: 44 reactions, 3 comments
- Aforro, new intern, 6 days old: 10 reactions, 1 comment

Ben's own post when he joined Kissterra, from a 1,471-follower personal account: 148 reactions, 42 comments.

### The conclusion that matters

Company-page welcome posts do not go viral. The format tops out around 40-60 reactions no matter how good the copy is. The distribution lever is the person, not the page.

So the play is:
1. Page posts the version above.
2. Ben posts his own "I joined AutoDS" from his profile the same day. That is the post that will actually travel, his last one did 148 reactions and 42 comments.
3. Lior reposts the page post from his personal profile within the hour.
4. Team comments with 15+ words.

If you want reach from this, Ben's personal post is the deliverable to invest in, not the page copy.
