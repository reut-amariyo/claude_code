## Draft

--- POST STARTS ---
The 1st founder asked why a CEO bothers posting.
The 2nd asked who writes the posts.
The 3rd asked where the hours come from.

All three answers go back to age 14.
That's when school kicked me out.
Sitting down to write never worked for that kid.

It still doesn't. So there is no writing.
There is talking.

Every meeting gets recorded.
Every thought that lands goes out as a voice note to one WhatsApp group.
Coaching sessions. Psychologist sessions. All of it.

A year later, that pile became a brain.
It holds the thinking, the building, the whole background.
The AI writes from it off a short brief. Posts, videos, scripts.

The result, while running a company of 200 people:
- [X] impressions a month
- [X] new followers a month
- [X] hours a week, total

Why bother at all?
AI now writes most of what you scroll past.
The more it does, the more people look for a real person.
A personal brand is the cheapest channel left, and people buy from people they know.

The fair pushback I get: you have a team, what about someone just starting?
Same answer. The leverage is the brain, and anyone can start one today.

- Record every meeting.
- Voice-note the thought the moment it lands.
- Feed all of it to the AI, and keep feeding it.

You will forget most of what you said this year.
Record it anyway.

Because an AI can only write like you after it has heard you.

P.S. How many of your best ideas this week died in a meeting nobody recorded?
