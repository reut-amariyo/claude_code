insider intel + operator confession + strategic close. Drop 🚨 openers and free-tool angles.

has 1M followers and 6 reactions per 100K because **his followers came from a newsletter, not
from LinkedIn.** Filter on engine efficiency and mechanism legality, never on follower count.
