## V1 (superseded) — stat-first opener

--- POST STARTS (V1) ---
40% of B2B deals end in no decision.

Not lost to a competitor. The buying group never felt safe enough to say yes. LinkedIn's B2B Institute is building its next two years of research on that number, and the mechanics explain why: buying groups now reach 22 people, and half the decision power sits with people sales never meets. Legal, finance, procurement. If they have never heard of you, the deal dies quietly.

22 people trying to agree hunt for the safest yes. And the safest yes is the name they already know.

That is why founder brands stopped being ego projects and became pipeline. A visible CEO vs. an anonymous logo is a risk decision, and risk kills deals before anyone compares features.

I feel it as a buyer too. Before AutoDS signs a new vendor, I check who the founder is. A visible founder makes the yes easier.

Where this is heading: AI is flooding every feed with perfect content. When anyone can publish anything, trust is the one thing that stays scarce. Trust builds from zero, compounds daily, and no competitor can copy it.

So the real question for the next decade: is the CEO part of the funnel, or hiding behind the logo?
