How to read this

Each platform plays a different role in the funnel: Facebook generates mass awareness (US 47%, broad audience), Instagram converts it into a young global business audience with New York as its #1 city, and LinkedIn holds the audience that matters most — 28% founders and CEOs, three-quarters decision-makers, spread across Tel Aviv, New York, Miami and London. The brand is building width and depth at the same time.

Q3 focus

  • AI-generated videos — new lane launching in Q3: script → AI avatar/voice → publish on IG and TikTok, with a quality bar of 8K+ average views per AI reel
  • Scale the collab motion: the "Money Updates" (עדכוני הכסף) collabs drove significant traffic — find international equivalents and run more of them
  • More content, more Trial Reels: increase overall publishing volume and use IG Trial Reels to test with non-followers before full release
  • Nasdaq billboard (Jul 29) — a cross-platform content campaign around the moment
  • 2-year exit anniversary — anchor post using the proven format (200K+ last time)
  • YouTube — establish a long-form cadence off the launch video
  • Brand Book — complete and roll out

Course corrections

  • Facebook: appeal the restriction + reduce automation pace until it clears
  • X: scale the agency model June validated — quality over volume
  • LinkedIn: 1–2 weekly AI-newsjack slots + personal stories for conversion
  • Podcasts: target Tier-1 international (English) shows — one recorded appearance in Q3; keep declining off-focus Hebrew inbound
