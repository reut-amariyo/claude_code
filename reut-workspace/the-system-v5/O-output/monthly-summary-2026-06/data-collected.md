### Why June impressions dropped (final read, verified against daily data)
1. **May was a two-event outlier:** "Google Base44-killer" (147K) + "Anthropic workflow" (93K) = 240K of May's 293K. Newsjack virality is hit-driven — you can't schedule it monthly.
2. **June's newsjack didn't catch the wave** (Anthropic repricing = 6.5K). Without a breakout, the month reverts to baseline (~50K, same as April).
3. **The controllables all improved in June:** ER 3.4x vs May, +1,009 followers, profile views +24%. Reach is volatile; conversion is trending up.
4. Documented LinkedIn-wide creator reach decline in June (see linkedin-impressions-action-plan-2026-06.md) — contributing headwind.
- **Lesson for Q3:** the reach engine on LinkedIn = fast newsjacks on major AI/tech news (2 of 2 viral hits). Keep 1-2/week newsjack slots + personal-story posts for conversion depth (exit-birthday post = top-3 engagement at 19K reach).
