# LinkedIn advisor shortlist — for Lior

Prepared by Reut, 2026-08-20. Decision-ready. Every name below was checked against what they
actually sell, not against how big their audience is.

## Why we are hiring outside help

LinkedIn is one of the two under-target platforms for the Q3 goal of 7M+ views/month and
190K+ followers. Our own data says the problem is not reach: impressions are up ~6x this year
while follower growth stayed flat at roughly 1,000/month. The 100K LinkedIn target therefore
needs a conversion lever, not more impressions. That is the exact brief for any advisor we hire.

## The three to consider

### 1. Amelia Sordell — recommended first call
https://www.linkedin.com/in/ameliasordell
Founder of Klowt, a UK personal-branding agency, ranked #1 Top Voice on personal branding.
Works with founders of £1M+ businesses on founder-led marketing. Around 370K followers,
attributes ~$5M of inbound revenue to her own brand, author of The Personal Branding Playbook.

Why her: her client profile is the closest to ours, a founder whose personal brand has to serve
the company. She sells done-with-you strategy, which is the shape we asked for.
What to buy: a paid strategy engagement or an initial audit.

### 2. Jasmin Alić (Hey Jay) — the craft layer
https://www.linkedin.com/in/alicjasmin
Ranked #1 LinkedIn expert by Favikon, Taplio and Aware. 340K+ followers. Coaches many of the
other top creators on the platform and runs corporate LinkedIn training.

Why him: strongest on hooks, copy and voice, which is where our posts win or die. He also
trains teams, so an engagement could cover Lior plus the marketing team in one contract.
What to buy: 1-on-1 coaching, or a corporate workshop.

### 3. Richard van der Blom — the data layer, already in contact
https://www.linkedin.com/in/richardvanderblom
Publishes the annual LinkedIn Algorithm Insights report, built on analysis of 1.8M+ posts.
Clients include Salesforce, Philips, Nestlé, Teva. Already identified and spoken with.

Why him: the only one of the three whose advice is grounded in platform-wide measured data
rather than personal experience. Directly relevant to the interest-graph shift we are seeing.
What to buy: an audit against his algorithm data, or an enterprise program.

## Also viable

- Matt Barker — https://www.linkedin.com/in/mattjbarker1 — ghostwrites for CEOs and founders,
  claims $7.5M in client revenue and 100M+ impressions generated. Buy if we decide we want
  done-for-you writing rather than advisory.
- Anna Vatuone — https://www.linkedin.com/in/annavatuone — executive positioning and storytelling,
  smaller and more hands-on.
- Andre Santos — https://www.linkedin.com/in/andresantostreinamentos/ — Favikon #1 in Brazil,
  #2 worldwide. 120+ CEOs mentored, 20K professionals trained across 15 countries, ~440K followers.
  Caveat: Brazil-based and publishes in Portuguese, so it is unproven whether his craft transfers
  to an English-language founder brand.

## Ruled out, with reasons

- Chris Donnelly — approached, declined. His words: "I don't offer that unfortunately."
  He runs The Creator Accelerator and Searchable day to day. Still worth pursuing as a
  guest slot on his Wake Up podcast, which reaches founders and operators.
- The Creator Accelerator — his $6,500 10-week cohort. Four live calls a week plus homework,
  and half the curriculum is newsletters, funnels and monetization, which are not our problems.
  Wrong use of Lior's calendar.
- Justin Welsh — https://www.linkedin.com/in/justinwelsh — the largest relevant audience at
  ~800K, but sells courses only and takes no clients.
- Lara Acosta — https://www.linkedin.com/in/laraacostar — strong operator, audience skews far
  younger than our ICP.

## Recommendation

Open two conversations, not six. Amelia Sordell for strategy, Jasmin Alić for craft, and keep
van der Blom as the data check we already have. Brief all of them on the conversion problem
specifically, so we are buying a diagnosis rather than generic growth advice.

## Caveat

Follower counts and revenue figures above are self-reported by the advisors or taken from
third-party blogs. None are independently audited. Treat them as signal, not proof.
