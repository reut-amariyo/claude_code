# LinkedIn advisor — technologist + founder marketing

Prepared 2026-09-07 for Reut. Brief: a LinkedIn consultant, meaning this is their actual
profession, who can write Lior's posts, who is genuinely technical, and who is world-class at
founder-led marketing.

## What the search actually found

At the top tier of LinkedIn consultants nobody is a technologist. Van der Blom, Sordell, Alic,
Barker, Acosta all came from marketing, recruitment or copywriting. The technical people with
huge LinkedIn audiences, Hassid and Alex Banks among them, are creators and product founders
who do not sell advisory. So the choice is between two routes:

- Route A, hire technical fluency and accept a smaller name.
- Route B, hire the best writer on the market and supply the technical depth from our side.

Route A names below are ranked. Route B is the existing 2026-08-20 shortlist.

## 1. Ron Fybish, Foundera — closest to the brief

https://www.linkedin.com/in/ronfybish/ · https://www.foundera.co/

A LinkedIn thought-leadership studio for deep-tech founders, based in Tel Aviv. Their whole
differentiator is technical fluency: cloud, DevOps, data, AI, LLMs, security. They work with
technical CEOs, CPOs and CROs and translate what those people already think into posts.
Client list is real and checkable: Qodo, Kubiya, Cycode, Piiano, FalkorDB, Apono, DagsHub,
Blink, Pynt, lakeFS. Full service, strategy through writing, video, design and analytics,
on about 2 hours of the founder's time per month.

Why him: this is the only outfit found whose selling point is exactly the gap you named, and
he is in Tel Aviv, so this can start as a meeting rather than a Zoom.
The honest mismatch: his clients sell to engineers and CISOs, so the studio is built for B2B
pipeline. Lior is B2C with a follower target. Ask him directly how he converts reach into
follows for a consumer-facing founder before signing anything.

## 2. Pete Codes — the purest technologist who takes clients

https://www.linkedin.com/in/petecodes/ · https://www.petecodes.io/linkedin-ghost-writer-for-hire/

Self-taught developer, founder of No CS Degree and the High Signal newsletter for founders.
LinkedIn and X ghostwriting is his actual business. Has written for Y Combinator founders,
AI entrepreneurs and CEOs of $100M+ companies. States his job as building authority rather
than producing posts. Market tier for this level is roughly $1,500-3,000/month for 8-12 posts.

Why him: real engineering background, so AI and tooling posts will not read like a marketer
guessing. Buy him for craft and credibility, never for his own reach, which is small.

## 3. Understory — Alex Fine and Ali Yildirim

https://www.linkedin.com/company/understory-agency

Founder and executive ghostwriting for post-product-market-fit B2B companies, built as a
go-to-market agency rather than a content shop. Strong operationally, less technical than
Foundera. Worth a call only if we want the founder brand wired into company pipeline.

## 4. Richard van der Blom — the data technologist, already in contact

https://www.linkedin.com/in/richardvanderblom

The one person in the LinkedIn consulting world whose work is genuinely quantitative: the
Algorithm Insights report is built on 1.8M+ posts. He does not write posts. Use him to audit,
not to produce.

## Ruled out for this brief

- Ruben Hassid. Builder and #1 AI voice, 800K followers, Tel Aviv, but he sells a product and
  a community, not LinkedIn advisory. Wrong shape for a writing engagement.
- Amelia Sordell, Jasmin Alic, Matt Barker, Andre Santos. Top of the market on craft and
  founder-brand strategy, zero technology depth. These are the Route B options.
- Luke Matthews. Works with AI startups and runs an AI-assisted writing process, but his
  background is construction and blogging. AI-adjacent, not technical.
- Justin Welsh, Chris Donnelly. Neither sells advisory.

## The brief we hand whoever we hire

Not "get us more impressions". Impressions are up roughly 6x this year while follower growth
stayed flat at about 1,000/month, against a 100K target. The job is converting existing reach
into follows. Anyone who answers that with a reach plan is the wrong hire.
