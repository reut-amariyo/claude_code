## 🛑 HARD RULE FROM REUT (2026-07-09): PRODUCT, NEVER PEOPLE
The campaign must be perceived as an attack on the PRODUCT, never on people. Fair game: the category, the era, the slogan, the platform choice. Protected: every Wix employee and every Wix merchant — they get matcha, respect, and help. This is now a dedicated slide in the Lior deck ("The one rule"). ## LESSONS FROM SHOPIFY'S OWN GUERRILLA PLAYBOOK
Source: shopify.com/blog — "5 Low-Cost Guerrilla Marketing Tactics" (added 2026-07-09)

The meta-win first: the playbook we'd execute FOR Shopify is published ON Shopify's blog. That's a ready-made wink for the press kit and the LinkedIn post: "We didn't invent this. We read it on Shopify's blog."

1. **Density beats size.** Jolie ran 15 laundry trucks with "dirty truck" ads; Graza papered NYC with lost-pet-style "Missing" flyers. Many small repeated touchpoints read as a bigger brand presence than one big installation. → Add a DENSITY LAYER around the hero stunt: the port area and nearby streets covered in a small repeatable format, so the stunt feels like it's everywhere, not on one plot.
2. **Surprise inside a familiar format is the cheapest virality engine.** Graza's flyers worked because everyone recognizes a lost-pet poster and nobody expects olive oil on one. → New drawer idea, "The Missing Poster": lost-pet-style flyers around Tel Aviv Port — "נעדרת: הצמיחה של החנות שלך. נראתה לאחרונה עוברת ל-Shopify." + QR to the migration offer. Near-zero cost, extremely photographable, extends the stunt's footprint for days.
3. **Pair the stunt with lower-funnel capture or it's just theater.** The article is explicit: guerrilla awareness must feed email/retargeting to convert. → Every QR (cups, flyers, kit cards) lands on ONE page with the migration offer + pixel, and scanners enter a retargeting pool. The stunt fills the funnel, ads close it.
4. **Ambush marketing works on calendars, not just addresses.** Attaching to events without sponsorship (Supergoop at the NYC Marathon). → If Wix holds a developer/partner event in Israel, the Salesforce-Siebel doorstep play transfers to it 1:1. Watch their events calendar.
5. Reinforces existing rules: permission from city/property owner is non-negotiable (backs the window-cleaner adaptation), and measurement is social listening + mentions, so set up tracking BEFORE day 1.

## OUR OWN PROVEN DATA POINT vs WIX (added per Reut, 2026-07-09)

**The May 28 2026 layoffs post.** Lior on LinkedIn, riding the Wix/Meta/Oracle/Cisco layoffs: "Tough days for Israeli tech... you didn't fail, the market shifted under you... we're hiring, my DMs are open." Results: 15.3K impressions, #2 engagement post of May (319 interactions), 102K account-wide peak day. Became Archetype 9, the market-empathy newsjack.

What it proves for THIS campaign, with our audience and not someone else's:
1. **AutoDS contrasting itself against Wix's bad news is already market-tested.** The Israeli tech audience rewarded it, nobody pushed back. The lane is open.
2. **The winning tone was empathy + opportunity, not mockery.** "You didn't fail" + "we're hiring" is what made it explode. This is Rule 3 confirmed with our own data: kindness IS the attack vector.
3. **Wix's soft spot right now is the employer front, not the product front.** They cut people in May 2026. Their employees walking past our stunt just survived a layoff round.
4. **The spicy option this unlocks, flagged with its risk:** the stunt can carry a hiring layer. Green coffee cart outside Wix HQ, cups reading "הקפה עלינו. וגם, אנחנו מגייסים." Free coffee plus we're hiring is poaching theater, the sharpest legal knife in this genre. Done with the May 28 empathy tone it's the "helpful troll" at its peak. Done cold it reads as kicking people after layoffs and loses the moral high ground. If used, the empathy frame is mandatory, joy never superiority.
5. **Mechanics note:** the post tagged @Wix's official handle directly. Tagging them in the stunt content invites the reply we want, per the Duel Invitation pattern.

## Guerrilla precedents this is built on

1. **Salesforce vs Siebel, 2000** — Benioff hired actors to picket Siebel's own conference with "No Software" signs. Lesson: stage the stunt on the enemy's doorstep, the location IS the message.
2. **BMW vs Audi billboard war, Santa Monica 2009** — Audi: "Your move, BMW." BMW answered with a bigger board: "Checkmate." Lesson: design the stunt so the target's best option is to reply. Every reply is a free news cycle.
3. **Slack's open letter to Microsoft, 2016** — full-page "Dear Microsoft, welcome" ad. Lesson: condescending kindness beats aggression. The wink disarms.
4. **Wix vs WordPress, 2016–2021** — Wix sent Bose headphones to Matt Mullenweg, ran Super Bowl ads, then the 2021 campaign that backfired into a PR mess. Lesson: Wix wrote this playbook AND got burned by it. They know exactly where the line is. We stay one step on the charming side of it, and we can wink at this history.

## LEAD CONCEPT: "The Greener Side" (הצד הירוק)

**The one-sentence press story:** "AutoDS rolled out a real grass lawn in Shopify green in front of Wix HQ and offered every employee free coffee and every Wix merchant a free migration."

### The idea
Shopify's brand color is green. The oldest idiom in the book is "the grass is greener on the other side." We make it literal: a lush, real-grass lawn installation with deck chairs and a coffee cart, directly facing the Wix HQ entrance at Tel Aviv Port. Everything in Shopify green. AutoDS logo on the cart and the signage.

The viral photo takes itself: Wix employees, badges visible, drinking free coffee on the green lawn. Nobody resists free espresso and a deck chair in July.

### Copy (plain text, Hebrew primary for street, English for LinkedIn/press)

Main lawn sign:
הדשא באמת ירוק יותר בצד של Shopify.
The grass really is greener on the Shopify side.

Coffee cart:
קפה חינם. גם ההגירה.
Free coffee. Free migration too.

Cup sleeves (rotate 3 lines):
1. החיים קצרים מדי בשביל הפלטפורמה הלא נכונה.
2. שתו. תחשבו על זה.
3. הצד הירוק מחכה.

QR on every cup → landing page: greenerside.autods.com
Landing offer: first 100 Wix stores get white-glove migration to Shopify, free, done by AutoDS.

### The 3-act structure (multi-day = multiple press cycles)

**Act 1 — Day 1 morning, the lawn appears.**
Install overnight, live by 07:30 when employees arrive. Street team in green aprons, professional photographer + video crew from minute one. Drone shot of the green lawn against the Wix building is the hero asset.

**Act 2 — Day 1 noon, the amplification.**
AutoDS LinkedIn + X post with the aerial photo. Press kit already in the inboxes of Geektime, Calcalist tech, TheMarker, CTech at 08:00 with embargo till 12:00. Shopify partner team gets the assets first so they can repost.

**Act 3 — Day 2, the invitation.**
A billboard or mobile LED truck near the port goes live:
Wix, הקפה הראשון עלינו. גם ההגירה.
Wix, first coffee is on us. The migration too.
This is the "your move" board. It exists to make Wix answer.

### The response playbook (pre-written, do not improvise on the day)

- **Wix plays along with humor** → best case. Answer once, escalate cutely once, then stop while ahead. Suggested closer: deliver a potted grass planter and a double espresso to Avishai Abrahami's office with a card: "For whenever you want to visit the green side. No headphones this time." The headphones line is the wink at their WordPress history that every Israeli tech journalist will get.
- **Wix responds sharply** → do NOT match the tone. Hold the wink. The reserve card, only if needed: a single image, the 5-year SHOP vs WIX stock chart, caption "No hard feelings." This is the sharp data jab we deliberately did not open with. Verify all figures with legal on the day, never before.
- **Wix ignores it** → fine. Day 3 post: "Wix didn't come down for coffee. The offer stands." Then close.

### Legal / ops checklist
- Tel Aviv municipality permit for the installation + cart (port area is private management, Atarim/port company approval too)
- Do not block the Wix entrance, do not enter their property, no Wix logo on any material, name only in the invitation line
- Israeli comparative advertising: puffery like "greener" is fine, any factual/stock claim must be verifiable on print day
- Food handling license for the coffee cart
- Shopify partner team briefed BEFORE day 1 — quiet heads-up, not asking permission, but no blindside
- Weather check, July heat: shade sails in green, iced coffee not just espresso

### KPIs
- 3+ earned press pieces in Israeli tech media
- 500K+ combined LinkedIn impressions on stunt content
- Shopify partner/exec engagement (repost, comment, or internal mention)
- 100 migration-offer signups from the landing page

### Budget shape (rough, ₪)
- Lawn install + removal, 2 days: 60–90K
- Coffee cart + staff + product, 2 days: 25–35K
- LED truck / billboard, 3 days: 40–80K
- Video/photo crew + drone + editing: 30–40K
- Landing page + assets + press kit: 20K
- Contingency + permits: 30K
- Total: ~200–300K ₪, within "go big"

## Risks, honestly
1. **Shopify blindside** — mitigated by the pre-brief. Highest-stakes risk because the stunt is FOR them.
2. **Wix out-trolls us** — they are the best in the country at this. Mitigation: the response playbook, never improvise, never match aggression, stop after one escalation.
3. **Reads as punching at employees** — mitigation: employees get free coffee and a lawn, the jab targets the platform choice, never the people. Street team briefed: warm, zero snark in person.
4. **News-cycle collision** — check Wix earnings date (early August window) and avoid running adjacent to layoffs/war news days.
