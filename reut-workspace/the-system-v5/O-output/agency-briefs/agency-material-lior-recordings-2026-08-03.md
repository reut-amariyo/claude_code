## Message to agency (copy below)

Hi guys,

Sending you fresh raw material straight from Lior. Four voice recordings attached, in Hebrew. I translated the key parts for you below so you can work with them right away. Also attaching a screenshot of a Leila Hormozi post that Lior strongly connected to. Use everything however it fits best, LinkedIn or X.

1. Lior's daily energy check-in system. This is the strongest material in the batch. His assistant runs daily check-ins with him: rate your mood 1 to 10, did you eat, did you work out. It is based on a Tony Robbins method. The important part is that it is active, not passive. If he doesn't answer, he gets a reminder. If he answers "2", the follow-up question is not "what happened", it is "why". He says he could have tracked all this in a spreadsheet, but the follow-up is what makes it work. After collecting the data over time he discovered his low point is the evenings, which is exactly the time he keeps for personal development, so it mattered to him. So he acted on the data: he started with 4 check-ins a day, cut to 3, then down to 1, and now manages only the evening, for example with meditation. In his words: "Once I saw the problem was the evening, I started managing just the evening."

2. The screenshot attached connects directly to idea 1. Leila Hormozi wrote: treat your energy like you treat your money, track where it goes and cut what doesn't give you a return. Lior really connected to this line, and the point is he literally already lives it. He tracks his energy daily with real data and cuts what drags it down. This can be a full post on its own: her principle as the hook, his check-in system as the proof.

3. Every founder's first hire should be a personal assistant. From the recording: a PA is the biggest leverage for growth. Not a late-stage luxury, the first hire, before anything else. The moment you feel your time is worth more than the cost, you hire. A PA helps you filter what reaches you, and filtering is the whole game for a founder. The person he was talking to reacted "that's really good" in the recording, so this framing landed live too.

4. Ambient AI at home. Lior set up Alexa and AI devices at home so AI is simply always around, always listening, always aware. His words: the goal is that it's an integral part of life, not something you go to and press. A second brain that is with you, not a tool you open. The shift he describes: AI becomes active instead of passive, it is around us instead of us turning to it. Good angle for how an operator actually lives with AI, not tips.

5. Comment, not a full idea yet: we went fully remote in 2016, ten years ago, years before COVID made it normal. We were among the first.

6. Comment, connects nicely to 5: in the early days there was no budget for computers, so every new hire brought their own laptop from home. Could be folded into the remote-work story as one "how AutoDS really started" post about scrappiness, or kept for another time.

Ideas 5 and 6 are not fully built, use them as you see fit.

Let me know what you pick up and if you want more detail on any of them, I can get Lior to expand.
