Story 1: Staying in shape on business trips

Lior has been training for ten years. The moment that frames the story: he came back from a three-week trip and his numbers in the gym had dropped hard. In his words, you train and train for years, build muscle, feel at your peak, then you fly for a few weeks and come back four months behind. So he built a routine that travels with him. Water first, drinking a lot of it is a habit that stayed with him for years. Nutrition stays relatively clean on the road, he gives up the daily treats but holds the line on protein. And his hotel ritual: every time he lands at a hotel, the first thing he does is order delivery to the room, protein, fruit, a pile of healthy food, and then simply continues the same routine as at home. His summary of what survives a business trip: workouts, water, nutrition. Good angle for the discipline and founder-routine lane, an operator protecting his baseline while running a company from airports.

Story 2: How Build Your Store was born

In 2023 the AI wave started and the team asked how to integrate AI across everything AutoDS does. That year Lior was at Shopify's conference, where Shopify themselves shared that a very high share of their new customers never finish setting up their store, the design, the first products. Around 30 percent. Same picture inside AutoDS, many signups never get a store live. So they combined the two: use AI to remove the hardest barrier in e-commerce, getting a store live. That became Build Your Store, launched on purpose as a separate brand aimed at complete beginners, not only dropshipping, one of the first steps beyond dropshipping. The product: pick a niche and you get a fully designed store with at least 10 trending products, chosen from AutoDS data on over 400 million products, ten years of knowing what actually sells. It catches users at the very start of their journey and brings Shopify new customers, and a year later AutoDS became Shopify's biggest partner, recognized by Shopify for global expansion in 2024 and 2025. Over a million people have created stores with it. It is free for the user, Shopify pays AutoDS for the customers it brings, and every store comes with a beginner e-commerce course. Lior's framing of the value: everything a beginner needs to start a real e-commerce business in one place, a store designed to sell, the most trending products, and the knowledge to succeed.

Lior sees two separate posts here. One is the founder story, why we built it, spotting the opportunity in Shopify's own numbers. The other is a lead-magnet post, comment to get a store, with an auto-DM flow behind it. The free part is what everyone reacts to, so both posts should carry it.

One important wording rule on this story: in public copy we always say Shopify "partner", never "affiliate". The commission mechanics are background for you, not for the posts. And the unfinished-stores stat should stay framed as an opportunity Shopify shared openly, never as criticism of Shopify.
