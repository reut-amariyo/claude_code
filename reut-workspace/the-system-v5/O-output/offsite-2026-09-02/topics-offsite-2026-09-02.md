## 5. Facebook — the collapse, and what actually caused it

**What we know.** August: 107,426 views, down 94%. Interactions 1,521, down 98.6%. Net follows
minus 290. 84K followers against a Q3 target of 96K. July was 3,181,303 views and +14,400 followers.
Facebook alone is 93% of the whole brand's view drop between July and August.

**Three candidate causes, and what the data says about each.**

1. *The profile restriction.* It was already active on 3 July, and July still delivered 3.18M views.
   So the restriction on its own does not explain August. It may have tightened, which we cannot see.
2. *The Instagram cross-posting connection breaking.* Reut's hypothesis. Partly supported: in July
   96.9% of Facebook traffic came from Reels, and in August reels are only 63.3% of views while
   photo posts jumped to 34.4%. That is exactly what a broken reel pipe looks like. But the
   publishing chart shows Facebook posting heavily in the first half of August, up to five to nine
   pieces a day between the 5th and the 12th, and those days produced five to eight thousand views
   each against a July daily average near 103,000. Content was there and it did not travel.
   Publishing did fall to near zero from the 21st to the 29th, so supply dropped too, just later.
3. *No viral hit.* Facebook was never a steady three-million-a-month channel. May was carried by
   two reels at 9.8M and 4.6M, July had one at 860,319. August's biggest piece did 13,936.

**The read.** Distribution is the primary cause and supply is a secondary one. Posts existed in
early August and got roughly 93% less reach each. A broken cross-post would have removed posts,
not made the posts that ran underperform.

**The test that settles it, and it is one number:** Facebook's post count for August against July,
and views per post for each. If views per post fell ~95%, it is distribution. If the post count
fell ~95%, it is the connection. Worth pulling before anyone commits budget to fixing the wrong thing.

**Decision:** who owns closing the restriction, whether the Instagram connection is actually live,
and what the Q4 plan assumes about Facebook until both are answered.

## 6. TikTok — did we do something different?

**Yes, two things, and only one of them is volume.**

Output went from 25 videos in July to 41 in August, up 64%, and views went from 858,333 to
1,391,919, up 62%. So far that reads as pure volume, and average views per video barely moved,
34,333 to 33,949.

**But the median video more than doubled, from 1,877 views to 4,318.** And the top three videos
fell from 82% of the month's views to 66%. The ceiling did not rise. The floor did. August was a
month where the typical video worked, not a month carried by two outliers.

That is the more valuable kind of improvement and it is worth understanding, because it is the
thing Instagram lost in the same month. TikTok is also at four times its September target while
receiving the least attention of any channel we run.

**Ask the room, not just Lior:** what changed in the TikTok process in August? Different hooks,
different editor, different posting times, native captions, trending audio? Somebody made the
median video better and nobody has written down how.

**Ask Lior:**
- אם טיקטוק מביא פי שניים צפיות לסרטון עם רבע מהתפוקה, למה הוא מקבל את תשומת הלב הכי קטנה?

## 7. The AI videos

**What we know.** The target is 6 AI reels a month at 8K+ views. Nicola rejected v1 in July on
six execution problems. The 30 August re-render underperformed again, and the Gemini watermark
that was problem six in July was still in it. That is a process failure, not a creative one.

Against that, the follower data says AI and graphics content wins reach while real and personal
Lior wins follows. We have been spending the AI lane on a synthetic Lior giving generic advice,
which competes with the real one and loses.

**Three options:**
1. Kill the lane and move the hours to real footage.
2. Pivot it to what only AI can do: generate what cannot be filmed. Fourteen years old selling
   on eBay, the first office, the night of the VAT crisis. A camera can never get that footage.
   This also aims the lane at the personal artifact, which is the thing proven to convert.
3. Fix execution under a hard gate: three attempts, watermark fixed, one venue, two images.
   8K views or the lane closes.

Option 2 is the recommendation.
