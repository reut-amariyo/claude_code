## Tweet 1
What people think bootstrapping to a $92M exit feels like:

- Pitch meetings with investors
- A board pushing you to grow faster
- Celebrating every milestone

What actually happens:

## Tweet 2
- You run the company from your parents' apartment and don't pay yourself for 5 years
- eBay bans the account you spent 7 years building, and your income is gone overnight
- You open 100 new accounts by hand because the next step is the only one you can see
- A private equity firm is one day from buying your company, and you walk away
- You ask "what's the worst case, and then what?" until the fear has a size

## Tweet 3
