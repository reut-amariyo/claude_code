# Lior LinkedIn: remote since 2016, COVID was day zero (2026-11-18)

Donor: "We're fully remote with 70 people" (sent by Reut). Facts from story-bible: remote since 2016, laptops from home, 2020 head of HR + doubled, 200 people, <1% monthly turnover, Lior's line "If I need to check what you are doing, we will not work together."

| Donor beat | Lior version |
|---|---|
| Setup: fully remote + headcount | Fully remote since 2016 |
| 2 policy lines | No office budget, laptops from home / weekly outcome per person |
| "But I've noticed something:" | "Then March 2020 came, and I noticed something:" |
| Contrast with tiny real examples | Others: Zoom licenses, monitors, emergency meetings / us: Monday = Friday |
| "It's real." | "Day one of lockdown was year four of remote for us." |
| Reframe + 3 one-liners | Trust / weekly outcomes / managers who lead people they never met |
| "None of that requires a permission slip." | "None of that can be bought in a crisis week." |
| Adult line | Head of HR + doubled, 200 people, Lior's check line |
| 2-line command close | "Trust first. / Location second." |

START
```
We've been fully remote since 2016.

There was no budget for an office. Our first hires brought their own laptops from home.

Nobody tracked hours. Every person owned a weekly outcome.


Then March 2020 came, and I noticed something:

Companies around us panicked. They rushed to buy Zoom licenses, shipped monitors to living rooms and held emergency meetings on how to manage people they couldn't see.

Our Monday looked exactly like our Friday.

Day one of lockdown was year four of remote for us.


The tools were never the hard part.

Trust takes years to build.

Weekly outcomes take months to get right.

Managers who lead people they've never met take time to grow.

None of that can be bought in a crisis week.


So while competitors cut teams, we hired a head of HR and doubled the company.

Today we're 200 people, still remote, with under 1% monthly turnover.

If I need to check what you're doing, we will not work together.

Trust first.

Location second.▮
```
END
