## 3. Company Name
AutoDS, acquired by Fiverr

## 4. Explain what your company does in 1 sentence
```
AutoDS is an all-in-one e-commerce automation platform that helps over 1.8 million online sellers find products, list them, and fulfill orders on autopilot.
```

## 5. Tell us the story of how you started your company
```
- Started selling online at 14. Listed a phone on eBay and made my first sale within 3 hours.
- Spent 7 years running online stores. By 21 I was running 100 eBay stores.
- Built software for myself to automate them. A black screen that found trending products and listed them for me.
- Showed that black screen at a conference in Tel Aviv while everyone else showed strategies. People lined up to ask for it.
- Launched a beta 2 months later. No website, no billing system. Users got an invoice at the end of the month and paid for what they used.
- Bootstrapped from day one. Never raised a dollar from investors.
```

## 6. Why did you start your company?
```
- I had the problem myself. Running 100 stores by hand doesn't scale.
- Other sellers had the exact same problem and were ready to pay for the fix before it even had a website.
- That turned into the mission: help anyone build a real income online.
```

## 7. Tell us the story of selling your company
```
- Two years before Fiverr, a private equity firm approached us. 6 months of due diligence. The offer went from $20M to about $38M.
- I walked away one day before signing. Every question was about who to fire and what to cut. Their plan was to optimize it and milk it.
- The final straw: I asked to be paid more if we beat their revenue targets. They said no. That told me they didn't want growth.
- Our first banker process ran 24 back-to-back meetings in Boston. On bid day, the whole banking team moved to another bank. The momentum was gone.
- We ran a second process with a banker we already trusted. 50 to 60 presentations and multiple offers, some higher than Fiverr.
- We chose Fiverr on trust and vision. The real decision happened over whiskey at their office at 9pm.
- Sold for $92M. The deal closed in 23 days. I'm still the CEO.
```

## 8. Why did you sell your company?
```
- Insurance. I knew I would probably make more by keeping it, and I sold anyway.
- The right partner. Fiverr wanted to grow AutoDS, keep the team, and keep me running it.
- Seeing every term sheet on the table is what let me decide I wanted to sell at all.
- The earn-out freed me to focus only on growth and the team.
```

## 9. What is your best piece of advice for business owners who want to sell?
```
Build it as if you'll never sell it.

- Run every decision as if you'll own the business forever. Never dress up the numbers for a buyer.
- Build systems so the business runs without you.
- Pick the buyer you trust. People buy founders before they buy companies.
```

## 10. Why would that advice help owners looking to sell?
```
- A profitable business that runs on systems is worth more to every serious buyer.
- You negotiate from strength. I could walk away from $38M because the business didn't need the deal.
- The right buyer recognizes what you built. The wrong one tells you who they want to fire. You only see the difference when you can afford to say no.
```
