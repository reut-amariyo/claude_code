# 3 viral AI donor posts — verified numbers + beat maps
Built 2026-09-01. Donors for the next Lior LinkedIn post per [[feedback-build-on-proven-structures]].
Companion file: linkedin-save-driven-creators-2026-09-01.md

**Method note:** LinkedIn does not expose save counts publicly, to anyone. So these were picked on
the two things that ARE observable: verified reaction/comment counts pulled from the live posts,
and save SHAPE — the formats that get filed rather than scrolled (numbered reference list, dated
prediction set, one concrete artifact). Numbers below were read off the posts on 2026-09-01.

---

## 1. Chris Donnelly — "I've been pitched 100s of AI tools. These 12..."
https://www.linkedin.com/posts/donnellychris_ive-been-pitched-100s-of-ai-tools-these-activity-7363901548252282881-p4Pe
**1,742 reactions / 562 comments.** Text post, 4 labelled categories, 12 items.

Why it gets saved: it is a filter, not a list. The reader saves it because someone else already
did the elimination work. Authority comes from volume tested, not from expertise claimed.

| Beat | Donor |
|------|-------|
| Hook | Volume-tested authority, under 10 words |
| Counter-hook | Most of them are noise |
| Body | 4 categories x 3 items, each item one line |
| Proof | 3 checkmark outcomes, one with a number |
| Close | Clarity in the chaos |

⚠️ **Topic guardrail:** the tool-list topic itself fails Lior's no-productivity-tips rule. Port the
STRUCTURE only. Lior-safe version: the shortlist of what actually survived in production at a
200-person company, framed as an operator's kill list, not a tutorial.

## 2. Greg Isenberg — "2026 AI predictions: 1. SaaS and agents merge..."
https://www.linkedin.com/posts/gisenberg_2026-ai-predictions-1-saas-and-agents-merge-activity-7399142389979979777-Jcfp
**1,466 reactions / 360 comments.** Text post, 22 numbered predictions.

Why it gets saved: dated and falsifiable. A reader files it to check it later, and the comment
section fills with people arguing item by item, which is the cheapest comment engine on LinkedIn.

| Beat | Donor |
|------|-------|
| Hook | Year + category, no throat-clearing |
| Body | 22 numbered one-liners, no explanation |
| Range | Mixes safe calls with 2-3 uncomfortable ones |
| Close | No CTA, the list is the CTA |

**Lior angle:** e-commerce and AI predictions for 2027, from the founder who sold the company.
Ship it in the last week of December for the annual re-share. Odd count, 21 or 23.

## 3. Greg Isenberg — AI agency: specialized agents structured like a company
https://www.linkedin.com/posts/gisenberg_i-found-a-github-repo-that-lets-you-spin-activity-7436773518337269760-SABV
**1,003 reactions / 203 comments.** Text post, one artifact, nine roles listed.

Why it gets saved: one concrete artifact plus one reframe. Same mechanism as the exit-deck
giveaway, the reader leaves with an object.

| Beat | Donor |
|------|-------|
| Hook | I found + the artifact |
| Credibility | 10k stars in under 7 days |
| Body | 9 roles as a flat list |
| Reframe | Structure it like a company, not one big agent |
| Close | Implication for how you build |

**Lior angle:** the strongest of the three for him. He already runs Hermes and Codex across a
200-person company, so the org-design frame is first-party, not a repo he found. Post the actual
role split AutoDS runs, one screenshot, one principle.
