## What the date actually is

30.10.26 is the EARLY ENTRY deadline for the 31st Webby Awards, not an event. Judging is done by IADAS members online:

## How it works

- Judges = members of the International Academy of Digital Arts & Sciences (IADAS), 2,000+ people. Membership is invite-only.
- Two tiers: Associate (Round 1, industry professionals + past Webby winners/nominees, commitment ~30 entries a year, all online) and Executive (final vote, CMO-level names). Lior enters as Associate. Executive comes later or via a strong referral.
- Two doors in:
  1. Referral by a current member. This is the strong door. Yoav's name on the email changes the reply rate.
  2. "Request a membership invitation" form on iadas.net/contact-us/?form. Cold, low signal. Fill it anyway, same day, so the name is in their system.
- Lior's natural judging categories: Apps & Software (Shopping & E-commerce, AI), Websites & Mobile Sites (Business), and the AI categories.
