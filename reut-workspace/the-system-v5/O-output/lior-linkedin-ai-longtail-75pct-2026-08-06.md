--- POST STARTS ---

75% of AI purchases aren't bestsellers.

That number was buried in Shopify's earnings yesterday, and it matters more than the 21% stock jump.

3 out of 4 purchases arriving from AI channels happened outside the top 100 categories. Niche products. Small sellers.

For 20 years online retail had one rule: the best-known product beats the best product. Shelf space, Google rank, ad budgets. If you sold something specific, you were invisible.

Here's what changed. A person searches "stroller" and gets whatever ranks. An AI agent gets asked about the trunk size, the twins, the budget. Then it digs through catalogs until something actually fits.

Popularity was always a shortcut for buyers with no time to research. Agents have unlimited time. They research everything.

I've watched small sellers lose the visibility fight for 10 years. This is the first shift that runs in their favor.

The giants spent fortunes optimizing for the algorithm. The algorithm just started reading the fine print.

What would an agent find in your category that Google never showed you?
