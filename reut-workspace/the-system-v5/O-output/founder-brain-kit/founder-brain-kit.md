## 1. The loop

1. **Record.** Every meeting. Every thought that hits, as a voice note.
2. **Store.** Transcripts go into one brain that Claude can read.
3. **Brief.** One line: the idea, the platform, the story it comes from.
4. **Draft.** Claude drafts from the brain, in your words, with your real numbers.
5. **Approve.** You read it, fix what's off, say yes or no.
6. **Learn.** What worked goes back into the brain. So does what flopped.

## 2. What gets recorded, 7 sources

1. Every meeting, on a recorder that runs all day
2. Voice notes to a WhatsApp group whenever a thought hits
3. Coaching and mentor sessions
4. Podcasts and interviews you gave
5. Your best-performing posts, with their numbers
6. Big decisions, and why you made them
7. Personal sessions, only what you're comfortable storing

## 3. What the brain holds, 6 folders

1. **Fixed facts.** Bio, dates, numbers that can never be wrong.
2. **Story bank.** Every story you've told, with the real number attached.
3. **Voice.** How you actually talk: phrases you use, words you never use.
4. **Beliefs.** What you'd argue for on a stage.
5. **Rules.** What never gets posted: topics, words, partners you never criticize.
6. **Results.** Every post and what it did.

## 5. The prompts

Prompt 1: build your voice from your own recordings.

```
Here are transcripts of me talking in meetings, podcasts and voice notes.
Build a voice profile of me:
1. Phrases and sentence shapes I repeat
2. Words I never use
3. How I open a story and how I land it
4. The 7 stories I tell most, each with the number attached
5. Beliefs I keep coming back to
Quote my exact words as evidence for every point. Do not invent anything.
```

Prompt 2: turn a voice note into a post.

```
Using my voice profile and story bank, turn this voice note into a LinkedIn post.
Rules:
- One idea only
- Use only facts and numbers that appear in the brain
- Hook under 10 words, with a number or a name in it
- Write it the way I would say it on a podcast
- If a fact is missing, leave [X] and tell me what to check
Voice note: [paste transcript]
```

## 6. The one rule

Record yourself and never stop.
At some point the brain knows you better than you remember yourself.
