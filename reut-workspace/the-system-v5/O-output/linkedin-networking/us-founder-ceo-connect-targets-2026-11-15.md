## Why not 100

1. Search pool: 76 keyword searches. About 1,080 US 2nd-degree profiles came back, plus last week's saved pool. 281 had 50+ mutuals.
2. Cleaning: 48 were already in batches 1-4. About 140 more were not founders or C-level: VPs, engineers, students, diplomats, nonprofit staff.
3. Activity: about half the remaining founders and CEOs had no original post in the last 30 days, so they were dropped.
4. **LinkedIn paused search** on Lior's account after ~180 search pages in ~3 minutes, with the message "unusual search traffic". The search stopped right away, and only the activity check ran after that. Leave it alone for a few days. The next run should be slower, about 100 pages a day with one page every 5+ seconds.
