# Build Your Store — sales script V2 (2026-08-27)

Brief: sell Build Your Store, get people to open an e-commerce store.
Pain to hit: they bought courses, never made an income, and they dream of high pay for low effort.

## What changed from V1 and why

1. **Hook now starts in their pain, not in the offer.** V1 opened with "You don't need another course. You don't need to spend thousands." Two problems: it is the "not X, you need Y" contrast template that the AI-slop report flags, and it argues with the viewer before they feel seen. V2 opens on the exact moment of failure they recognize: courses bought, store never live.
2. **"Affiliate" removed.** V1 said "We became Shopify's #1 affiliate." Public copy always says **partner**. Commission is still explained, because the catch question needs a real answer, but through the partner relationship.
3. **The low-effort dream is answered, not ignored.** They want high pay for low effort. V1 only said "no get rich quick", which reads as taking something away. V2 splits it: the boring setup work is genuinely removed for free, the selling is still theirs. Honest and still sells.
4. **Proof upgraded to verified numbers.** $40 at 14 and 1.8M+ users instead of the vague "helped millions".
5. **Formatting rules applied.** No parentheses, no em dashes, no "it's not X it's Y", zero "just", first-person kept to 4, Hebrew word replaced.

## SCRIPT (approx 75 seconds spoken)

You bought the course. Your store still is not live.

That is where most people stop. The videos end, the tabs close, and the only thing that actually got built was a folder of PDFs.

At 14 I made my first 40 dollars online, reselling from a Chinese wholesale site onto eBay. No course. No mentor. One product page and a reason to keep going.

Everything after that started the same way. Get something live, fix it later.

Since then 1.8 million people have run their e-commerce business on the software we built. And the same thing stops beginners before day one. Setting up the store.

So we built BuildYourStore. You pick a niche, and it builds you a full Shopify store, with trending products already inside, for free.

Now the question everyone asks. What is the catch?

There is none. We are one of Shopify's biggest partners. When a store we build turns into a Shopify customer, Shopify pays us. You pay nothing.

I would rather remove the cost of starting than sell you one more thing to watch.

And to be straight about the dream you were sold. Nobody hands you 10 thousand dollars next month. What we hand you is the boring part, finished. The store, the design, the products, the first steps.

You still have to sell. That part was always yours.

If starting has been on your list for a year, click below. We build your store today, free.

## Delivery notes
- Beat 1 to 2 is the pain, say it flat and slow, no energy lift.
- Lift energy at "So we built BuildYourStore".
- "What is the catch" gets a pause before the answer, that pause is the whole trust moment.
- Last line is the only hard CTA, do not smile through it, say it like an instruction.
