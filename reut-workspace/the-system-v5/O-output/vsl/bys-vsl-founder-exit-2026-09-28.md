## Delivery notes for Lior

- Selfie style, phone at arm's length or on a mini tripod at eye level. It should feel like he hit record himself, not like a studio.
- Beat 1 calm and flat. The number does the work; no smile on "92 million".
- Beat 2 at story pace, a little faster. These are facts, not a pitch.
- A real pause before "The catch" answer, about 1.5 seconds, eyes on the lens. That pause is the trust moment.
- The 1 to 5 stack gets a rhythm: fingers counting on camera.
