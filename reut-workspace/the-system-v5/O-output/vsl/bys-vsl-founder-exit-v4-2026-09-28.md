2. "making more than $1 billion in revenue" → "Together, they've made more than 1 billion dollars." The public AutoDS stat is $1B+ earned BY users; the original wording reads as AutoDS's own revenue.
3. Mission line: typos fixed, spoken rhythm.
4. 🔴 "it cost 1 dollar a month" → "$1 a month for your first 3 months". Shopify pricing, checked 2026-09-28: 3 days free, then $1/month for 3 months, then Basic at $29/mo billed yearly or $39/mo billed monthly. "1 dollar a month" alone is false after month 3, and viewers will feel tricked.
