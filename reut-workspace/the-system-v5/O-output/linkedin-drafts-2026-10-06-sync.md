## 1. M&A buy rules

--- POST STARTS ---
4 acquisitions at AutoDS. One number decided every deal.

12 months.

If a company can pay back what we paid within a year, after we optimize it, I buy.
If it can't, no pitch changes the math.

Before we even run that number, we walk away from:
A team we don't trust
A business we can't improve, on profit or on product
A product that fills no gap in our ecosystem
A market we don't understand

Most of what we bought, we planned to build anyway.
Buying saved us the time and the focus.

The other side of the table matters just as much.
Every deal we did runs on targets.
So the founder has to believe we'll hit them together, and make more with us than alone.
Sometimes the honest version is harder to say: we're coming into your market either way.

Now the part I'd change.

My worst deals are the ones we never signed.
Same playbook. We knew exactly what to do.
We were too careful to take a loan, or too busy to do it right.

A deal you skip never shows up as a loss on any report.
