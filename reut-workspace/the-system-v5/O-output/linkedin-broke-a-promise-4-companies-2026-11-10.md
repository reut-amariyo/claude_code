## Draft

START
```
I broke a promise to myself after the exit 🫣

I might hold the record for starting things I swore I'd stop. 4 companies. And I'm 31 💀

When AutoDS sold to Fiverr, I promised myself: one company at a time. It's literally tip number 2 on my own list for founders. Kill the side projects.

And well... today I'm also co-founder and CEO of BuildYourStore.ai, CreateUGC.AI and ShopShark. Oops.

Company 4. Done.

But it was for a good reason, I promise.

The last book I listened to was The Science of Scaling. Its rule: pick an unreasonable goal, then an unreasonable deadline. Mine is simple. Every company has to solve one painful problem that ecommerce sellers hit after AutoDS. Building the store. Making the ads. Finding what actually sells on TikTok Shop.

Founders rarely talk about what this looks like on a Tuesday. 4 sets of OKRs. 4 weekly status calls. 4 teams who don't care that your calendar is full. Nothing pauses because you started something new.

And it reminded me why I still love this. Business is a game with no finish line. Selling the company didn't end the game. It gave me more boards to play on.

So here's the lesson. Focus doesn't mean one company. Focus means one customer pain per company, one team that owns it, and numbers you check every week.

Here's to company 4... and hopefully no company 5 for a while. Not making any promises this time 😂▮
```
END
