# Lior Pozin for The Diary of a CEO

**Who he is**
Lior Pozin, 31. Built AutoDS at 21 from his parents' apartment in Beer Sheva, no investors, ever. Bootstrapped it to 200 employees and $20M+ ARR, sold it to Fiverr at 28 in a deal that closed in 23 days. Still CEO, still runs it independently.

**5 proof points**
1. Acquired by Fiverr, a public company, for a reported $92M. Profitable from day one, never raised a dollar.
2. Walked away from a $30M private equity buyout one day before closing, after 6 months of due diligence. Fiverr came two years later.
3. 1.8M+ e-commerce sellers on AutoDS, $1B+ earned through the platform.
4. Bought 4 companies as CEO, including the market leader he was afraid of when he started.
5. Shopify's #1 partner two years in a row, 2024 and 2025. Forbes 30 Under 30 nominee.

**The story**
Parents immigrated from Ukraine. Mom ran a grocery, dad fixed air conditioners. At 10 his dad locked the family computer as punishment and Lior taught himself to hack it. At 14, first $30 on eBay. Expelled from high school in 10th grade. At 21 eBay banned the account that was his entire income, so he opened 100 accounts, wrote software to run them, and other sellers asked for it. That software became AutoDS. He did not know fundraising existed. At 28 he sold it. His parents were still asking when he would finish his degree.

**5 things he can talk about for an hour**
1. The $1 bug. At 3 AM a test repriced every product in every customer store to $1. Hundreds sold. He went live to all customers the next day and took the blame. Nobody left, a competitor's 20% poach offer moved nobody, and he hired the customer whose business he broke. She now runs a 100-person team.
2. Saying no to $30M a day before signing, because the buyer planned to cut the people who built it.
3. Bootstrapped by ignorance, then by choice. Why software no longer needs capital in the AI era.
4. Dyslexic CEO. Cannot sit and read a book. Everything he learned came through audio.
5. "I live on 200." Sold the company and did not slow down: 10 hours a week on personal development, 2 coaches and a therapist. Dream on record: billionaire by 45, and not for the money.

**3 lines in his words**
"I really believe in no. It's our choice whether I take the no or make it a yes."
"Nobody is born a CEO."
"Start now. Fix later."

**Say it right**
- "Reported $92M", never the official price.

- Investors never rejected him, he never asked. Leave equity out.
- Shopify "partner", never "affiliate".
