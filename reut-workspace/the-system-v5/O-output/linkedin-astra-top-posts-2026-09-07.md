## 🔴 What this says, and it is a warning for our draft

The three winners have nothing in common with each other except this: not one of them is an
opinion about which model is better.

- #1 is a joke that punctures the hype. No verdict.
- #2 is a thing that was made. No verdict.
- #3 is a picture you can see the answer in. The verdict is the image, not the text.

Every post that argued a position lost. Dan Shipper actually tested both models across coding,
writing and knowledge work, wrote the most substantive take of the week, and got 127 reactions
against a three-line doctors joke at 968. Abel Baruwa ran 5 head-to-head tests and got 16.

Our 2026-09-07 cross-check draft is an argument. The thesis is right and it is the best angle we
have, but this week the feed is not paying for arguments in prose.

**The fix is not a new angle, it is the carrier.** The draft's own visual plan already has it:
the two model reviews side by side, the invented findings circled in red. Lead with that image
and let the text be short underneath it. Same thesis, delivered the way the winners deliver.

Second option worth considering, if we want the #1 shape: Lior's version of the deadpan reversal.
Three lines, a real number, no verdict, let the comments fight. His account converts on judgment
posts though, and a joke post buys reach, not followers. That makes it a 10% reach-budget play,
not the main event.
