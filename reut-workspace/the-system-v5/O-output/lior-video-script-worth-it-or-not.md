# Lior — "Worth It or Not Worth It?" (Founder Edition)

AI video script for ElevenLabs voice + avatar. Built 2026-06-16 from the @tommoneymays Reel format via Sandcastles, 1.06M views, 110x outlier. Rhythm: question → verdict → one sharp reason. 8 items, alternating Worth/Not, ~45s total, ~5.5s per item.

## Voice script (paste into ElevenLabs)

```
Worth it or not worth it? A great recruiter. A hundred percent worth it. One A-player beats ten good ones — those are the real unicorns.

A fancy headquarters? Not worth it. I live on 200 — that's intensity, not interior design.

First class? Worth it. I want to land sharp and ready — not recover for two days.

A rebrand before product-market fit? Not worth it. You're polishing a logo nobody asked about.

Answering support tickets yourself early on? Worth it. I started with forty dollars — talk to your customers.

A second product before the first one wins? Not worth it. Focus. One thing, all the way.

An assistant to buy back your time? Worth it. Every hour goes straight back into the company.

Chasing a stage just for the photo? Not worth it. I'll speak when I have something real to say.
```

## Production notes

- Hook frame: 1s full-screen text "Worth it or not worth it?" before speech starts
- Verdict stamps per item: green WORTH IT / red NOT WORTH IT
- Pattern break on the $40 support item: punch-zoom or B-roll cutaway to reset attention
- Pace fast, no dead air; pin overlay "Building a company vs. looking like a founder"
- End card / quotable closer: "Most founders spend to look successful. The ones who make it spend to get sharper."
