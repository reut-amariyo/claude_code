--- POST STARTS ---

Tested Fable 5.1 on three websites this week.
Seven conclusions worth keeping.

First, the reason it beats everything that came before it. It can look at what it built. It screenshots its own work, inspects the image, and fixes what it finds before it reports back. Every model before this one shipped a description of a website and called it finished.

Here is how you turn that into a page you would put your name on:

1. Feed it brand guidelines before anything else. Colors, type, logo. Consistency is most of what reads as expensive.

2. Give it the pain, the person and the promise. A page that does not know who it is for looks generic every time.

3. Point it at a specific site and name the exact element you want from it. "Make it look premium" is the instruction that produces slop.

4. Ask for layering. Three or four planes moving at different speeds is the strongest premium tell there is.

5. Hand it components that already exist instead of asking it to invent every button. 21st.dev, Godly, Awwwards and Motion Sites.

6. Make it check the phone. Desktop animation does not survive 390px on its own.

7. Make it screenshot the full scroll and look at every frame. This is the step almost everyone skips, and it is the one that catches the broken layer sitting outside the frame.

All seven come down to the same thing. How specific you are willing to be before you press enter.

Keep this for your next build.

Which one is missing from your process?
