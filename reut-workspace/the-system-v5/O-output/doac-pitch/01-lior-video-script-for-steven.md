# The 90-second film Lior records for Steven

Built 2026-09-23. Shoot on a phone, horizontal, one take is fine. Better slightly rough than
polished: the whole point is that it was made for one person.

## Setup

- **Framing:** horizontal, chest up, he holds the phone himself or it sits on a desk. Office or
  home, not a studio. No lower third, no logo, no music.
- **Length:** 85 to 95 seconds.
- **Delivery:** the way he talks on a podcast, not the way he talks on stage. Short sentences.
  Pauses where they fall. If he stumbles on a word, leave it in.
- **Do not** read it off a teleprompter. Give him the beats, let him say it his way. The wording
  below is his voice already, so it should not fight him.
- **File name when sending:** `lior-pozin-for-steven.mp4`

## The script

--- SCRIPT STARTS ---

Steven, I made this to talk you out of it.

You have never heard of me. That is fair. But 1.8 million people use the software I built, and
most of them have never heard of me either. That is kind of the point.

I have never finished a book. I am dyslexic. So I do not have a framework for you. I only have
what happened.

And I am not coming on your show to warn anyone about AI. I run a company with 200 people. AI
already changed what we ship and who we hire, and I can tell you exactly what it did, with
numbers.

There are two things I have never said out loud.

One is about a week when I was a kid, when I was not allowed to leave my house.

The other one I want to say on your show first.

And I know how you end every episode. Somebody writes a question in the diary for the next
person. I am not a guest, so I am going to break the rule and leave mine early.

What was the last no you accepted, without checking whether it was true?

That is the whole show, for me.

Talk soon.

--- SCRIPT ENDS ---

## Direction, beat by beat

| Beat | What it is doing | Note for Lior |
|---|---|---|
| "talk you out of it" | Buys the next 80 seconds | Say it lightly. It is a joke that is also true |
| 1.8M | The only number he needs here | Flat, undecorated. No pride in the voice |
| dyslexic | Disarms the "what is his thesis" question | Say it and move on. It is not a confession |
| AI | Positions him against both sides of their current AI lane | This is the line that earns the booking |
| two things | The scoops, unspent | Do not name either one |
| the diary | The reason Steven remembers this video | Slow down here. Let the question land, then stop |

Nothing after the question. No sign-off card, no logo, no "looking forward to hearing from you."
He says "talk soon" and the video ends.
