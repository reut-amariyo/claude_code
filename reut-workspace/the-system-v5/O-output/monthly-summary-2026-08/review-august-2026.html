The pace, read honestly

On pace for the quarter. Off pace for the goal.

Those are two different questions and August answers them differently. The Q3 numbers land. The two follower targets do not, on any reading of the current rate.

First, the average flatters us. Instagram's last three months are +4,740, +24,354, +8,976. That is a 5x swing, so the trailing-3 average of 12,690 is one exceptional month carried by one post, sitting on top of two ordinary ones. The median month, 8,976, is the number we actually own. Both are shown below and the truth sits between them.

Instagram: the ceiling is fine, the variance is the problem

A 24-month landing needs 16,472 a month. July did 148% of that. August did 54%. So the target does not need a bigger month, it needs every second month to look like July instead of one in three. Nothing about the pace says 500K is out of reach; it says the growth is event-driven and nobody has put the events on a calendar.

LinkedIn: the pace is a fifth of what the goal needs

A 24-month landing needs 3,626 a month. The best month this account has ever had produced 1,373. Five months of data sit between 821 and 1,373, and four of the five predate the only mechanism that moved the line. So the pace does not say "push harder", it says the current machine tops out at roughly a third of the requirement.

Four months of drift at the current rate adds 9% to the Instagram requirement and 14% to LinkedIn's, which is one more artifact post every month for the rest of the run. The arrears compound, so the cheapest month to start is this one.

The conclusion in one line: Instagram is on a pace that reaches 500K if the good month stops being rare, and LinkedIn is on a pace that reaches 100K in 2033 unless the artifact series becomes weekly. Both fixes are the same fix, and it is a supply of documents rather than a supply of posts.

September

Three things this data asks for

  • 01Name ten documents and ship one a week. The LinkedIn 100K math turns on five artifact posts a month, and each one has to be a different asset. Two artifacts have carried two months and one of them is finished.
  • 02Stop paying for Instagram volume. 161 pieces bought 42% less reach per piece than 114 did, and profile visits stayed flat. The follower line came from five posts. Move the hours from output to artifacts.
  • 03Settle Facebook with one number. Post count for August against July, and views per post for each. If views per post fell 95% it is distribution. If the post count fell 95% it is the Instagram connection. Nobody should commit budget before that number exists.
