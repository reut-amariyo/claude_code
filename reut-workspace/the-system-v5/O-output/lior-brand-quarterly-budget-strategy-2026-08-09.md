# Lior Pozin — Quarterly Personal Brand Budget Strategy

Prepared by Reut Amariyo, August 2026. For Lior's review and budget approval.
Market focus: US / global English. Planning horizon: next full quarter, with moves that need lead time flagged.

---

## The thesis in one paragraph

The organic engine already runs: an agency produces daily content, IG is compounding toward 95K followers, and the system does 6M+ views a month. What money buys now is the layer organic can't reach: third-party credibility. Being quoted by journalists, sitting on Tier-1 podcasts, appearing next to creators with 10x our audience, and showing up in the physical world. Every dollar in this plan is ranked by one question: does it make a stranger who has never heard of Lior Pozin conclude on their own that he is a top-tier SaaS founder?

## How this doc is organized

Every move has a price tag and an impact rating. Three tiers:

- **Tier 1 — Do first.** Highest confidence, compounding returns, start within 30 days.
- **Tier 2 — Scale plays.** Strong expected return, need Tier 1 running or need lead time.
- **Tier 3 — Big swings.** Expensive, high-variance, high-ceiling. Pick 1-2 per quarter, not all.

# TIER 1 — Do first

## 1. The Data PR Engine (Lior's idea, built out) — flagship move

**The idea:** AutoDS sits on data nobody else has: 1.8M+ dropshippers, $1B+ earned by sellers, 800M+ products tracked. Journalists don't quote founders for opinions. They quote founders for numbers nobody else can give them. We package proprietary data into quarterly reports and Lior becomes the named source every e-commerce and creator-economy story has to cite.

**How it works per quarter:**
1. Pull one headline dataset, e.g. "The State of Solo E-commerce 2026": how many one-person stores crossed $100K this year, which product categories exploded, average time from store launch to first sale.
2. Package it as a designed report + press release + 5 pre-cut story angles for journalists.
3. PR firm pitches it to TechCrunch, Business Insider, CNBC, Modern Retail, Marketing Brew. Every citation reads "according to data from AutoDS, whose CEO Lior Pozin says..."
4. Lior posts the insider commentary on LinkedIn and X the same week. Press citation + owned commentary = the authority loop.

**Guardrail:** the data is AutoDS's, the voice is Lior's. Reports carry his byline and his operator read on the numbers. This never becomes a product pitch.

**Cost:** $20K-40K per report (analyst time, design, distribution) on top of the PR retainer below.
**Impact:** the single highest-leverage move in this doc. Press citations are permanent, searchable, and feed every other pillar (podcast bookers, event organizers, and TV producers all Google him first).

## 2. Tier-1 tech PR firm on retainer

A US firm that specializes in founder profiles, not product PR. They pitch the data reports, place opinion columns, land award lists, and field inbound. Examples to vet: BAM, Method Communications, Codeword, or a boutique founder-brand shop. We interview 3, take the one with real reporter relationships in SaaS and e-commerce.

**Concrete asks for the first quarter:** 4+ press citations from report #1, 1 profile piece pitched (Forbes, Inc., Business Insider founder profile), 2 contributed columns placed (Fast Company, Entrepreneur), Lior submitted for 2 award lists (EY Entrepreneur of the Year, Goldman Sachs Builders, Inc. 5000 adjacent founder lists).

**Cost:** $25K-45K/month retainer, $75K-135K per quarter.
**Impact:** high. This is the infrastructure every other earned-media move runs through.

## 3. US podcast tour, professionally booked

We already have a Q3 KR of 1 international English podcast recorded. Budget turns that from 1 to a circuit. A booking agency pitches him weekly with the exit story, the data reports, and the bootstrapped-to-$92M angle.

**Target ladder:**
- **Entry tier, book now:** SaaS and e-commerce operator pods, Nathan Latka, Indie Hackers-adjacent shows, DTC/ecom pods. Purpose: reps and clips.
- **Mid tier, quarter goal:** My First Million guest, 20VC, SaaStr podcast, This Week in Startups. The $92M no-investor exit in 23 days is exactly their kind of story.
- **Top tier, 2-quarter goal:** Diary of a CEO, Lenny's Podcast. These need the mid-tier appearances as proof.

Every appearance gets cut into 10+ clips for IG/TikTok by the existing pipeline, which feeds the under-target platforms for free.

**Cost:** booking agency $6K-15K/month or $3K-8K per placement; media training refresh $10K-20K one-time; travel budget $15K-30K per quarter for in-studio recordings (in-person beats remote for top shows).
**Impact:** high. Podcasts are the highest-trust format for the Builders ICP and the raw material for a quarter of short-form content.

## 4. LinkedIn Thought Leader Ads

LinkedIn is an under-target platform and the March 2026 interest-graph shift rewards exactly this: paid distribution of organic-style posts from a personal profile. We take the 3-5 posts per month that already proved themselves organically and put real spend behind them, targeted at US SaaS founders, operators, and investors.

**Cost:** $30K-60K per quarter.
**Impact:** medium-high, and directly attacks the 70K impressions/month KR. This is the cheapest way in the whole doc to buy reach on a platform where we're behind.

# TIER 2 — Scale plays

## 5. Creator collaborations, brand-side

Lior already runs $10M+/year in influencer collabs at AutoDS, so this is a lane where he's genuinely the insider. For the personal brand the move is appearing IN big creators' content, not sponsoring it:

- Guest appearances in business YouTubers' videos: "I sold my company for $92M with zero investors, ask me anything" formats with creators in the 500K-2M sub range.
- Paid collab videos with founder-content creators on IG, structured as co-created content where he's the story, following the עדכוני הכסף model that already worked in Hebrew. We find 3 international equivalents, which is literally a written Q3 lever.
- One "creator swap" per month: Lior's team produces a reel for their channel, they appear in his.

**Cost:** $10K-50K per collaboration depending on creator size; budget $60K-150K per quarter for 4-6 collabs.
**Impact:** high for follower growth, the fastest borrowed-audience mechanism available. Directly serves the IG 95K and total 202K follower targets.

## 6. Speaking circuit, paid where needed

Conference stages are ranked last in the organic platform priority, but with budget the calculus changes: stage photos and talk recordings are authority assets everything else reuses. Target: SaaStr Annual, Web Summit, Shoptalk, SubSummit, and the 1 Billion Followers Summit pitch already in motion for Dubai in January 2027.

**Cost:** speaker-support agency or bureau $5K-10K/month; some stages have pay-to-speak packages, $15K-50K each, worth it only for Tier-1 stages with full session video rights.
**Impact:** medium on its own, high as an asset generator. One SaaStr talk becomes 30 clips plus permanent credibility line.

## 7. Production level-up: dedicated video team

The gap between where the brand is and Diary-of-a-CEO-guest level is partly production polish. A retained US-quality editing and shooting setup: quarterly 2-day shoot days in a studio, batch-recording 30+ pieces of talking-head content, plus a senior editor for podcast-clip turnaround within 24 hours of any appearance.

**Cost:** $20K-40K per quarter for shoot days, $8K-15K/month senior editor.
**Impact:** medium direct, high multiplier. Every other pillar produces better raw material.

## 8. Awards and lists program

Founder credibility shorthand: Forbes Next 1000-type lists, EY Entrepreneur of the Year, Inc. and Fast Company founder features, industry awards in SaaS and e-commerce. Applications are real work: essays, references, financials. The PR firm drives it but it needs a dedicated budget line.

**Cost:** $10K-25K per quarter for submission support and entry fees.
**Impact:** medium, slow-burn. Each list is a permanent bio line and a post.

# TIER 3 — Big swings, pick 1-2

## 9. The founder documentary

A 20-30 minute cinematic YouTube documentary on the AutoDS story: 100 eBay accounts at 21, the VAT crisis, walking away from PE a day before signing, the 23-day Fiverr deal. The format that made Diary of a CEO guests into brands of their own. Produced by a top doc-style team, released on his YouTube, cut into 40+ shorts.

**Cost:** $80K-200K.
**Impact:** high-variance. If it lands, it's the definitive Lior asset for years and the answer to "who is this guy" for every journalist, booker, and creator we pitch. This is my recommended big swing for the quarter after next, with pre-production starting now.

## 10. Out-of-home, done as content

We already know the play from the Nasdaq billboard in July: the billboard is not the media buy, the CONTENT ABOUT the billboard is. A Times Square digital spot or an SF-101 billboard with a [ life is beta ] brand message, shot professionally, becomes a week of posts, a LinkedIn moment, and a press hook.

**Cost:** $25K-60K for a digital share-of-voice buy plus shoot.
**Impact:** medium and short-lived on its own, but cheap as big swings go, and proven in our own playbook. Worth one per quarter as a content stunt, not as a media strategy.

## 11. TV and business media on-air

CNBC, Bloomberg Technology, Yahoo Finance segments as an e-commerce and creator-economy commentator. This is earned, not bought: the PR firm pitches him as the data guy whenever e-commerce is in the news cycle. The data reports are the entry ticket.

**Cost:** covered by the PR retainer plus media training above; sizzle reel production $5K-10K.
**Impact:** medium for the ICP, high for gravitas. TV clips are disproportionately powerful as social proof in every other channel.

## 12. The book

"Life Is Beta" as a real published book, done with a top ghostwriting house through interviews, which fits how Lior actually works. A book is the strongest single authority asset a founder can own and the master key to top-tier podcasts and stages.

**Cost:** $100K-250K over 3-4 quarters including ghostwriting, publishing, and launch campaign.
**Impact:** highest ceiling in the doc, longest timeline. Decision needed this quarter only if we want it to exist by end of 2027.

## 13. Own event: the Builders Dinner series

Instead of sponsoring other people's events: a quarterly invite-only dinner for 20-30 US SaaS founders and operators, hosted by Lior. No stage, no pitch. The network compounds, the guests become collaborators and podcast invitations, and one recap post per dinner writes itself.

**Cost:** $20K-40K per dinner including a producer.
**Impact:** medium-high and unusually durable. Relationships are the one asset the agency can't produce.

# First 90 days

- **Weeks 1-2:** shortlist and interview 3 PR firms and 2 podcast agencies. Define data report #1 topic with AutoDS data team.
- **Weeks 3-4:** sign PR firm and booking agency. Media training booked. Thought Leader Ads live on 3 proven posts.

# What we measure

- Press citations naming Lior (target: 4+ in first full quarter after report #1)
- Tier-1 podcast appearances recorded (target: 3)
- Follower and impression movement on under-target platforms, X and LinkedIn, versus the existing OKR baselines
- Inbound quality: speaking invitations, journalist requests, creator collab inbound
- Kill rule: any retainer that produces zero placements in 60 days gets replaced, not extended

# Hard guardrails, non-negotiable

1. Everything is the founder journey, never AutoDS product marketing. AutoDS appears as credential and data source only.
2. Archetype stays Architect of Growth. No motivational-speaker positioning, no hype-guy bookings.
3. Public numbers only: $92M exit, 250 employees, 1.8M+ dropshippers, $20M+ ARR. Nothing unverified.
4. Reut remains the final gate on every placement, appearance, and creative before it goes out.
