---

## Final Script (ElevenLabs)

The guy who built Twitter just handed every founder a million-dollar lesson with one decision! And he came this close to losing everything.

In 2005, Twitter started as a podcasting startup called Odeo.

Then Apple walked on stage. With one update, they baked podcasts straight into iTunes, for free! Most teams would have folded right there and blamed the competition.

But they refused to give up on the company. They understood one thing. When you can't win the game you're playing, you change the game. You pivot!

So they looked hard at what they had already built, and they noticed something. There was one tiny feature that their own users couldn't stop using. A simple tool for posting short status updates.

So they bet everything on it! That tiny tool became Twitter, the company Elon Musk would later buy for forty four billion dollars!

Here's the lesson for us. Your customers are always showing you where your real product is. The feature you almost ignored could be the company you're meant to build!

---

## Fact notes
- Apple added podcasts to iTunes in 2005, undercutting Odeo's podcasting product.
- Odeo's team pivoted the side status-update project into Twitter.
- Elon Musk acquired Twitter (public company) for ~$44B, closing October 2022. The founders did not sell to Musk directly; phrased as "the company Musk would later buy."
