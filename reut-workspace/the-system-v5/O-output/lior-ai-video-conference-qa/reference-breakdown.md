## Format specs to replicate

- 9:16 vertical, exactly 2 camera setups: audience shot + speaker shot. No b-roll, no music bed over speech.
- Static title bar pinned top center for the ENTIRE video, white rounded pill, black text, ends with ellipsis to open a curiosity gap: "Some people don't want help..."
- Karaoke captions bottom third: white words, key word highlighted yellow, 3-5 words per line.
- Question takes ~24% of runtime, answer ~73%, close ~3%.
- The answer formula: personal principle → visual analogy → quotable reframe. No step-by-step advice.
- NO follow CTA inside the video. The quotable line is the ending. Caption text is one short line restating the principle.
