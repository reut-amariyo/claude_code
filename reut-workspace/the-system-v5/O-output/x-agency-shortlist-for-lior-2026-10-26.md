# X agency and expert shortlist for Lior

Prepared 2026-10-26. Every name below was checked against its own site or public profile.
No independent ranking of X agencies exists. Most "top 10" lists online are written by agencies
ranking themselves, so they were used only to find names, never as proof.

## The brief

KPI is followers, not impressions. On X, growth comes mainly from replies and the "I'm Lior"
origin-thread format. Any agency has to show founder follower growth with numbers, and has to
run replies, not only scheduled posts.

## Top picks

### 1. FounderBrands, Collin Rutherford: done-for-you, best value
- Site: https://www.founderbrands.io
- X: @collin_ruth89
- Book a call: https://api.leadconnectorhq.com/widget/booking/ha5ujZOj9XTbz6SPhi2f
- Price: $3,999/month done-for-you. Also $499 for a 2-hour intensive and $3,999 for 12-week coaching.
- Proof: 70+ B2B founders served, 5.0 on Clutch, his own X post hit 62M views. Named clients
  report sales calls and revenue from X leads.
- Fit: X and LinkedIn, with daily content in your voice plus manual outreach and replies.
- Caveat: his clients are smaller B2B operators, not founders with exits. Ask for a client
  at Lior's level.

### 2. Pete Codes: X ghostwriter for SaaS and AI founders
- Site: https://www.petecodes.io/twitter-ghost-writer-for-hire/
- Email: pete@highsignal.io
- X: @petecodes
- Price: $3,300/month for 16 posts, 40 replies and 100 niche follows. $5,500/month for X plus LinkedIn.
- Proof: grew a Y Combinator founder from 250 to 2,500 followers in 4 months. One client post
  reached 1.3M impressions.
- Fit: SaaS and AI niche, and replies are built into the package.
- Caveat: a one-person shop with modest results so far.

### 3. Matt Gray, Founder OS: strategy session, not done-for-you
- Book: https://intro.co/MattGray, $1,500 per 1:1 video session
- X: @matt_gray_
- Site: https://www.mattgray.com
- Proof: around 900K followers, mostly on X. He sold Bitmaker and now runs Founder OS, a
  personal-brand system for founders.
- Fit: the closest X equivalent to Amelia Sordell. Worth one paid session for a strategic audit
  of Lior's X, before or alongside any agency.

## Update 2026-10-26: Reut wants an X-only writer

FounderBrands is X plus LinkedIn plus lead-gen outreach, so it drops. The writers whose main
product is X: ContentGhost, run by Coinbound, which has crypto roots, and Pete Codes, whose
offer is X-first with LinkedIn as an add-on. No credible writer found sells X alone with
proof at Lior's level.

## Questions for every call

1. Show us a founder you took from roughly Lior's follower count to 2-3x, with dates.
2. What is your follower growth per month, not your impressions?
3. Who writes replies, how many a day, and on which accounts?
4. Here is our voice export and one brief. Send 5 sample posts.
