# The 100 — follow list for the research profile

Built 2026-09-06. Split 60 AI niche / 40 general high-engagement LinkedIn writers.

**How to use:** LinkedIn search box, type the name, open the profile, click **Follow**.
Never paste URLs in bulk. Waves match the warm-up schedule in `00-setup-guide.md`.

**Verify as you go.** Names are researched but LinkedIn has duplicates and impostor
profiles. The right profile is the one with the follower count roughly matching the
"why" column and recent posting activity. If the top search result has 200 followers
and no posts, it is the wrong person, scroll down.

Column key:
- **Cadence** — how often they post. Daily creators are the engine of this project.
- **Donor value** — how usable their structures are for Lior.
  🟢 high · 🟡 structure only, topic is off-pillar · 🔴 tracked for data, barred as donor
