### Algorithm context — the interest-graph shift (added per Reut 2026-08-04; source: Devin Reed article, verified against LinkedIn Engineering blog Mar 2026; full analysis in M-memory/linkedin-interest-graph-shift-analysis-2026-08-04.md)
- **Verified fact:** LinkedIn rebuilt the feed from a social graph (who follows you) to an **interest graph** (who cares about this topic). LLM embeddings classify every post semantically; ranking runs on dwell time, saves-type signals, and each reader's interaction history. Rolled out through 2025-early 2026, publicly explained only March 2026.
- **How this reframes July:** low reach on a post is now first a TOPIC-MATCH signal, not proof of bad writing. Agency July topics were scattered (World Cup, Forbes, Fable 5, Shopify, shutdown, gym) — under an interest graph, every off-pillar post also teaches the classifier the wrong thing about Lior, so the cost of unfocused volume compounds. This is the mechanical explanation behind "lots of content, no breakouts".
- **What it prescribes for the take-back plan (Q3 actions):**
  1. One-time profile audit — headline/About must carry the pillar keywords (SaaS founder, e-commerce automation, bootstrapped, 0→1, exit); the graph reads the profile before distributing posts.
  2. 90-day pillar lock — every post maps to one of the three pillars while classification settles.
  3. Commenting as distribution — 5-10 substantive manual comments/day on target SaaS/founder accounts.
  4. Draft for dwell/saves — knowledge-density posts over applause-bait.
- Trust tier: the graph mechanics are platform ground truth; Reed's tactical rules stay hypothesis-tier below Lior's own data (his length data beats Reed's "length doesn't matter").
