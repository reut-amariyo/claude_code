What July Proved — and What We Replicate

Data-based conclusions

The month's biggest finding: one format won everywhere at once.

The "At [age]:" personal-timeline confession, anchored in a real artifact, took Instagram's top spot (933K views, 14K follows from one childhood photo) and 3 of X's top 5 posts in the same month. This is now a proven, repeatable series — not a lucky post.

Replicate on Instagram

  • 2–3 personal-artifact posts per month mined from Lior's real archive: childhood, army, first office, early AutoDS days. Photo posts, not reels — that's where the follows come from.
  • Brief every reel against "would someone save this" — the 822K reel carried 15K saves.
  • Best windows per IG's own recap: Thu 9am–3pm, Fri 9am–12pm.

Why the totals can hold through September

  • Reel volume doubled and views still grew 56% — the current cadence isn't saturating the algorithm.
  • 88–96% of views on every big platform come from non-followers — the discovery engines are open.
  • Follower churn is negligible everywhere (IG unfollows ~50–107/day against 300–800 daily follows).
  • Conversion is concentrated: a handful of artifact assets do the follower work, volume does the reach work. Both levers are independent.

LinkedIn: The Algorithm Changed — Here's the Plan

The fact

LinkedIn rebuilt its feed from a social graph (who follows you) to an interest graph (who cares about this topic).
Pillar (90-day lock)RoleProof it worksProfile keywords
1 · The bootstrapped founder journey — eBay ban to $92M exit Follower conversion AGE-14 post: 14K follows on IG · At-age format: 3 of X's top 5 bootstrapped, founder journey, exit, 0→1
2 · Operating a 250-person SaaS company in real time Authority with the ICP July's #1 engagement post · audience already 30% founders/CEOs SaaS, B2C, scaling, leadership
3 · AI in practice for e-commerce/SaaS + AI newsjacks Reach May's 147K + 93K breakouts · feeds the Shopify affiliate push AI, e-commerce automation, agents

The action list

  • One-time profile audit — headline/About carry the pillar keywords; the graph reads the profile before it distributes a single post.
  • 90-day pillar lock — every post maps to one of the three pillars, measured weekly. What fell outside the pillars (World Cup, gym, Levi's) is exactly what diluted July.
  • Write for dwell and saves, not applause — every post briefed against "would a founder save this".
  • 3–5 substantive comments/day on target SaaS/founder accounts — comments are now distribution, posted manually.
  • Consultant engaged: Richard van der Blom — one of the best-known LinkedIn algorithm researchers; first conversation already held.
