## DRAFT

At 14, school kicked me out.

That same year I started selling on eBay from a bedroom at my parents' place.

At 21, eBay banned the account. Seven years of work, erased overnight.

That same week I opened 100 new accounts and built a tool to run them automatically.

That tool became AutoDS.

At 28, we sold it to Fiverr. Bootstrapped, zero investors.

At 31, four competitors we used to race against run under our roof.

-> Today: 31 years old, 17 years in e-commerce.

Nobody plans the ban.
Nobody schedules the expulsion.
The only thing you actually choose is what tomorrow morning looks like.

But if I could change one thing?

I would have stopped building on land I did not own, years earlier.

So here is what I would tell you if you are standing in the middle of your own wipeout:

1. Never build your whole business on someone else's platform
2. Rebuild the same week, not next quarter
3. Boring markets have the least competition and the best margins
4. Bootstrap as long as you can, it keeps every decision yours
5. Count customers, not months
6. Hire people who have already failed at something real
7. The ban, the rejection, the shutdown: that is the syllabus
8. Buy the competitors you respect instead of arguing with them
9. Protect the one asset nobody can ban, your own audience

A platform can hand you a business.

It can also take it back on a Tuesday morning.

If everything you have is sitting on someone else's account, you are renting.

Start owning something this week. Even something small.

Every wipeout is a respawn point.
