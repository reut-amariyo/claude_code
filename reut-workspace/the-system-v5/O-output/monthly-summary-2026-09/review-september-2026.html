The weak step is the first one. ManyChat joined 8,769 people, and 31 started a trial. Most automations send a list or a guide, like HUSTLE (1,804 runs) and CEO (404 runs), and never ask for the trial. Once someone starts a trial, the organic audience converts to paid at 13%, close to the 20% that paid traffic reaches.

The Meta campaigns are paused for now. There is a data issue in the tracking, and the campaigns stay off until it is fixed.

Four months of data

Six patterns that held from June to September

  • →Followers come from his own story or his own artifact, on every platform. On X, the origin thread. On Instagram, the lists and age 14. Age 14, the school expulsion and the eBay ban are the most reusable asset he has.
  • →One post makes the month. Facebook: the Sep 3 repost of "You're about to become the reason your whole family never worries about money again" was 78% of the month. X: the three "I'm Lior" threads, the eBay ban on Sep 4, "founder of AutoDS, here's who I am" on Sep 17 and "my parents' apartment in Beer Sheva" on Sep 28, were 94%. TikTok: the flight-home theory, the Elon Musk founder question and 10 side hustles were 82%. Volume does not produce hits.
  • →Reposting a winner works on Facebook and decays everywhere else. The FB repost did 3.9M. The third X thread did one eighth of the first two. The 10-startups list fell 94% by its fourth run.
  • →A creator share multiplies a strong post. Nick's share brought about 6,000 X followers in days. It is the cheapest follower lever we have tested.
  • →Borrowed hooks do not repeat. "Reddit just LEAKED" did 219K once on X, then 1,196 and 2,176. A real news leak still works, like the Anthropic post on LinkedIn.
  • →Audience quality is diverging from reach. Facebook followers are viral-reel viewers. Instagram is 78% men with India at 15%. From now on we report quality next to quantity.
