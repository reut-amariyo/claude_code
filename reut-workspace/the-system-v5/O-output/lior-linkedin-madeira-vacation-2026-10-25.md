If you've ever opened your laptop at a wedding, checked Slack on vacation, or believed nothing moves without you... this is for you:

For years, my laptop came everywhere.
Weddings. Vacations. Every trip, one eye on Slack.

I was sure AutoDS would stop the second I closed it.
Every decision went through me, and I called that leadership.

Then it hit me.
Everything in the company was waiting for one person.

So I started letting go.

The 70% rule: if someone can do it 70% as well as you, it's theirs.
Real decisions for new hires from their first week.
A manager layer strong enough to make the call without asking.

This year Galit officially became a dentist, and we flew to Madeira to celebrate.

The team closed one of our best deals of the year.
I didn't worry once.

Being the person everything waits for feels important.
It's the most expensive job in the company.

If you can't go 3 days offline without something breaking, the fix starts with you.
That's good news. You can start changing it this week.

Once you let go, you get to:
- Leave the laptop home for a wedding
- Be fully there for the people who earned it
- Land and hear about a deal you never touched

This time Galit got a husband without a laptop.▮
