## Final draft (primary)

--- POST STARTS ---
You know every number in your business.

But your salary is a guess.

Every founder has the same dilemma.

Take too much, it looks wrong for the business.
Take too little, and you are working this hard for free.

I didn't take a salary for 5 years.
(AutoDS was profitable from day one.)

That was a mistake.

Here is the math I would run today.

Cash in the bank ÷ 18 months, minus what the team costs.

That is your ceiling.

$600K ÷ 18 = $33K a month.
The team takes $28K.
You get $5K.

Under the ceiling, take what your life actually costs. Not your market rate.

If your life costs more than the ceiling, go fix revenue first.

The one line I never cut was people. First hire, strongest person available, paid what it took.

Be cheap on yourself for as long as you need to. Never on the people building it with you.

What number are you taking, and who decided it?

#foundersalary #startupfinance
--- POST ENDS ---

## Final polish, 2026-08-29 (v5, FINAL)

Reut's v4 structure is kept exactly. The dilemma stays above the confession, the confession
stays above the formula. Five line-level fixes on top of it:

1. "You know the numbers" became "You know every number in your business." Her four-word
   version had no punctuation and no specificity. Seven words buys back the concreteness
   without losing the cadence, and it makes the guess in line 2 land harder.
2. The parenthetical is now one clean line of 6 words, "(AutoDS was profitable from day one.)"
   In v4 it floated as a stray fragment after a blank line. Six words also puts it inside
   the sanctioned parenthetical exception instead of over it.
3. "Here is the formula I would use now" became "Here is the math I would run today."
   Shorter, and "run" is a verb a founder does to a number.
4. Cut "Not what a CEO is supposed to earn." Two consecutive "Not" sentences read as a
   template, which is exactly the shape LinkedIn's AI-slop classifier reportedly flags.
   One "Not" line survives and does the whole job.
5. "Under that ceiling" became "Under the ceiling." One less possessive in a paragraph
   already thick with "your".

Nothing else moved. Hook, dilemma, formula, example, principle, question close all stand
as Reut set them.

Open flag, unchanged and hers to call: line 1 still carries no number, named entity, or
confession, so this is outside the anchored hook shape all 13 of the 10K+ wins share.
The anchor arrives on the 5 years, five lines down. If first-hour reach is low, test line 1
before touching the formula. Anchored swap ready: "You know your burn, your margin, your churn."

Superseded hooks, kept for the next founder-comp post:
- Who's the worst-paid person at your company? / You.
- My company was profitable for 5 years. I wasn't.
- I sold my company to Fiverr. My salary was $0.

Fact-check notes:

  filing says $55.658M plus up to $36M earn-out.
- Do not couple the $20M+ ARR figure to the no-salary years. The ARR is current, those
  years are early. Together they imply a timeline we have not verified.
- The 18 months and the $600K example still need Lior's sign-off. If he manages by a
  different number of months, swap it in and the example numbers move with it.
