# Q3 2026 at AutoDS

A note from Lior: what happened this quarter, and who made it happen.

## 📈 The business

We're at more than $50M in annual revenue, and growing fast.

And Q4 started even stronger. We're on pace for 40K+ paid trials this quarter.

## 📣 Marketing

**Build Your Store on Meta, from zero to scale. Led by Julia.**
Julia launched BYS on Meta ads this quarter and scaled it fast, while beating the cost goal per paid trial.

**The niche selector test: +15% paid trials. Led by Yarden.**
Visitors on the BYS homepage now pick a niche and see a store being built for it. Free trials went from 53.4% to 58.3%. End to end, paid trials rose about 15%.
🖼️ Free trials CVR + Paid trials CVR charts

**Site speed: 10% → 94%. Led by Alae.**
The share of AutoDS desktop pages passing Google's speed test went from about 10% to 94%. Slow pages were holding back our search ranking, and 94% of website trials start on the homepage and landing pages.
🖼️ Search Console desktop graph

## 🛠️ Product

**AutoDS is inside Claude.**
The AutoDS MCP is live in Claude's connectors directory. Search "autods" and we're there.
🖼️ Claude Directory screenshot

**New mobile funnels for AutoDS and BYS.**
Both funnels were rebuilt from scratch. In the AutoDS test, the new funnel brought 40% more revenue. More improvements are coming in Q4.
🖼️ BYS "Choose your niche" + "Pick 2 designs" screens

**ShopShark is ready to launch.**
We closed the gap with FastMoss and Kalodata on the features that matter, and added Ads, Shops and Category Analysis this quarter. Today's Data is something only ShopShark has.
🖼️ Comparison table

**CreateUGC, redesigned.**
A new home page and funnel, and the top video models in the industry, Kling and Seedance among them.
🖼️ CreateUGC plans screen

## 💬 CX

CX started bringing revenue in this quarter, while our AI took on more of the work.

**The AI widget keeps customers.**
It reaches customers right after a failed payment or a broken store connection. In a real A/B test, 42.6% of them stayed, vs 17.9% without it.

**Orin solves more on its own.**
Our AI support agent went from resolving 41% of conversations in Q2 to 53% by the end of September. Customer satisfaction with Orin: 89.5%.

**Our ratings went up.**
AutoDS on the Shopify app store: 4.3 → 4.6. ZipStore: 3.4 → 3.9.

**Every conversation is now quality-checked.**
AI scores every support conversation, not a sample, with 96% accuracy.

## 🤝 Partnerships

**100 creators signed for our 2027 creator campaign.**
About 90 videos go live this quarter. We expect to see the impact on AutoDS and BYS in Q4 and Q1.
