# Lior's Slack updates to employees: system + drafts

Built 2026-10-04 from the meeting with Shomron. Notes: `B-brain/meetings/2026-10-04-internal-comms-shomron.md`.
Goal from the company survey: employees feel closer to management and know what's going on.

## The system, 3 layers

| Layer | When | Who feeds it | Format |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Lior's update | Quarterly, all company, per Shomron's outline. First one end of Oct or Nov | Managers, up to 4 bullets each | Slack vs email vs landing page still open. Recommended: one Slack message, pinned |
| 2. Real-time win | When it happens. Aim for ~2 a month so there's a pulse | Managers drop wins in the managers channel any time | 3-5 lines |
| 3. Recognition | Formal spotlight at the all-hands. Optional short shout-out in Slack | HR, incl. bonus decisions, + managers | 4-5 lines, tags the person |

The managers channel solves the open "who feeds Lior?" thread: it stays open after the first
round, managers drop wins there, Reut drafts in Lior's voice within 24 hours, Lior sends.

Rules for every message: reads like Lior typed it on his phone. English, short lines, no design,
no deck. No financial numbers until cleared. Never imply employee equity. "200 employees" if size
comes up. "Partner" for Shopify, never "affiliate".

## Draft 3. Lior's update, the recurring template, English

Lior / 2. travels and Lior's brand, photos + summary / 3. high-focus projects status and
releases / 4. recognition, Shomron checks frequency.
Fill the brackets from the managers' bullets. Keep 2 wins per team at most, cut teams with nothing real.

[One line on the focus or the challenge ahead.]

✈️ Where I've been
[2-3 lines: trips, events, what happened on the brand side and how it came back to AutoDS. Attach 2-3 photos to the message.]

🚀 What you shipped
[Project / release]: [status or result, one line]
[Project / release]: [status or result, one line]
[Project / release]: [status or result, one line]
