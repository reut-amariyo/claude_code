--- POST STARTS ---
This is how I scaled my business to $20M ARR.

I had no background in business. Nobody in my family ever built a company. No neighbor, no uncle, no friend of a friend I could call and ask how it's done.

So I found my teachers online.

When I started, this is all I had.

I followed the people who were already doing what I wanted to do, and I studied every post.

- It gave me the mentors I didn't have at home.
- It showed me the mistakes before I paid for them.
- It gave me a network of people who had already solved my problems.

The result of committing to this simple habit?

1. I made decisions faster, because someone had already made them.
2. I skipped years of trial and error.
3. AutoDS grew to 1.8M users.

Today, one of my most asked questions is:

"Who do you follow? I don't know where to start."

So I spent the last week compiling my list, from 10,000 to millions of followers.

Divided into 5 niches:

1. Mindset, for the days you want to quit
2. Leadership, for when the team gets bigger than you
3. AI, for what actually works inside a company
4. Business, for the numbers behind the growth
5. Founders and investors, for the people in the arena right now

Follow them if you want to be a better business owner.
Follow them if you need inspiration.
Follow them if you want to learn from people who did it.

This is the list I wish someone had handed me at the beginning.

P.S. Save it. Next time you open LinkedIn with nothing to learn from, start here.
