## Part 1 · Why I buy

I buy what I would have built anyway. A company I buy should save my team time and focus, close a gap
my customers already feel, and pay itself back within a year after I fix what I know how to fix.

The deals I regret are the ones I skipped. Same playbook, I knew what I was doing, and I was too
careful. Sometimes I should have taken the loan.

## Part 2 · The 5 rules

1. **People I trust.** It starts with a connection to people I trust.
2. **Value we can add.** We improve profitability or we improve the product.
3. **A gap in our ecosystem.** A complementary product that fills the holes between our features.
4. **Payback in 12 months.** "If I can get the investment back within a year, I'm buying." After the
   optimization, the deal has to return the investment in a year. "That's what we did with every
   acquisition so far."
5. **A product we know.** We don't buy something we don't know. Usually it's something we planned to
   build anyway.

## Part 6 · How I buy the next one

1. **Find it.** [LIOR] where targets come from: inbound founders, rivals, the roadmap.
2. **Run the 5 rules.** People first. If rules 1 to 3 fail, stop.
3. **Run the payback math.** Asking price divided by monthly profit after the fixes. 12 or less, buy.
4. **Talk to the seller about targets.** Show how you hit them together and why it pays them more
   than running alone. Be honest that you are entering the space anyway, if you are.
5. **Plan the first 90 days before you sign.** [LIOR confirm] that the integration plan exists
   before signing.
6. **Don't let cash be the reason you say no.** If a deal passes all 5 rules, finance it.
