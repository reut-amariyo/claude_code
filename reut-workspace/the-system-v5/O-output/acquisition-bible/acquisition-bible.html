5 questions before any money moves

Every company AutoDS looks at goes through the same 5 rules, in the same order. People first. Payback math only once the first 3 hold up.

Use it when
  • A founder asks if you would buy their company
  • A product shows up that you planned to build anyway
  • You feel the deal before you have the numbers
What you need on the table
  • The asking price
  • Monthly profit today
  • An honest list of what you would fix on day one

01 · People
[ 1.0 ]

People I trust

"It starts with a connection to people I trust."
Answer before the first call
  • Who on our side knows the founders personally?
  • Would we hire these people if the company did not exist?
  • Will they keep working hard once the money is in the bank?
Fill in
Who we know there [Name, role]
How we know them [Years, context]
Who vouches for them [Name]
Trust score [1 to 5]

04 · Payback
[ 4.0 ]

Payback in 12 months

"If I can get the investment back within a year, I'm buying."
The payback math
A · Asking price[$_____]
B · Monthly profit after our fixes[$_____]
A ÷ B = months to payback[____]
12 OR LESS · BUY
OVER 12 · PASS
Why after the fixes
  • Use the profit after rule 02, once the fixes are in
  • That is the number you are paying for
  • If you cannot name the fixes, use today's profit

05 · Know it

A product we know

"We don't buy something we don't know. Usually it's something we planned to build anyway, and it saves us time and focus."
Answer before the first call
  • Is this on our roadmap already?
  • How long would building it take our team?
  • What would that team build instead?

Time to build it ourselves [Months]

06 · The other side of the table
[ 6.0 ]

What the seller needs from you

"Every deal we do runs on targets. They need to trust that together, we hit them."
Your job in the room
  • Show the targets and how you hit them together
  • Show why it pays them more than running alone
  • Be honest about what happens if they say no
The honest part

Sometimes the seller already knows you are entering their space anyway. Say it plainly. A seller who sees the whole picture decides faster.
