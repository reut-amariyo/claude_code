## Two things worth saying out loud
1. Almost every name above monetizes with a newsletter or a tool. Their content is a funnel to an
   owned asset. Lior has no newsletter, so copy the FORMAT, never the CTA.
2. The biggest AI numbers on LinkedIn belong to lab CEOs, and those are identity plays. The
   copyable craft sits in the 100K to 1.5M band, which is where this list lives.

## The one anomaly worth studying
Mike Leber is the only person in this top 10 who is not famous outside LinkedIn. 255K followers,
5,437 median likes, 246 posts in a year. He out-engages Satya Nadella with 2% of the audience,
purely on cadence plus a fixed visual format. That is the only replicable model in the table.

## Engagement-rate footnote
Reese Witherspoon converts at 3.23% with under 100K followers, the highest in the top 20.
Bill Gates sits at 0.01% with 40M. Follower count and engagement rate move in opposite directions,
which is the same finding as Lior's own data: reach does not equal followers, and size does not
equal conversion.
