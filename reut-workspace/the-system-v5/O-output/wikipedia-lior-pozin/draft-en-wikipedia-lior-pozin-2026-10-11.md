'''Lior Pozin''' (born {{circa}} 1995) is a co-founder and the chief executive officer of [[AutoDS]], an e-commerce automation software company that was acquired by [[Fiverr]] in 2024.<ref name="ctech2024">{{cite news |title=Fiverr publishes positive earnings and announces acquisition of Israeli dropshipping startup |url=https://www.calcalistech.com/ctechnews/article/rjnypqdy0 |work=[[Calcalist|Ctech by Calcalist]] |date=31 July 2024}}</ref>

== Early life ==
Pozin was born and raised in [[Beersheba]]. His mother worked at a supermarket and his father was an air-conditioning technician.<ref name="calcalist2025">{{cite news |last=Manela |first=Maayan |title=מחנות אונליין בתיכון - לאקזיט של סטארט-אפ עם 200 עובדים |trans-title=From an online store in high school to the exit of a startup with 200 employees |url=https://www.calcalist.co.il/calcalistech/article/bkjkt16rle |work=[[Calcalist]] |date=11 July 2025 |language=he}}</ref> At 12 he began repairing computers and building websites and forums for clients in his neighborhood.<ref name="calcalist2025" />

At 14 he opened a store on [[eBay]] after noticing price gaps between Chinese marketplaces and eBay, which introduced him to [[dropshipping]].<ref name="forbes30">{{cite web |title=ליאור פוזין |trans-title=Lior Pozin |url=https://forbes.co.il/rankings/2025under30/lior-pozin/ |work=[[Forbes Israel]] 30 Under 30 2025 |date=30 March 2025 |language=he}}</ref>

== Career ==
While running his own eBay stores, Pozin wrote software to automate them for his own use and later turned it into a commercial product. He co-founded AutoDS with Michael Royf, the company's chief technology officer, and Ofir Bokobza, its chief marketing officer.<ref name="geektime">{{cite news |last=Elkaslassi |first=Oshri |title=אקזיט כחול-לבן: Fiverr רוכשת את AutoDS הישראלית |trans-title=Israeli exit: Fiverr acquires Israel's AutoDS |url=https://www.geektime.co.il/fiverr-acquires-autods/ |work=[[Geektime]] |date=31 July 2024 |language=he}}</ref> Some sources date the start of development to 2016<ref name="entrepreneur">{{cite news |last=Shibu |first=Sherin |title=He Started an eBay Side Hustle at 14 – Then Grew It Into a $92 Million Business |url=https://www.entrepreneur.com/starting-a-business/this-founders-ebay-side-hustle-led-to-a-92m-acquisition/499534 |work=[[Entrepreneur (magazine)|Entrepreneur]] |date=13 November 2025}}</ref> and others give 2018 as the company's founding year.<ref name="geektime" />

The platform sells subscriptions to online merchants for product sourcing, inventory management, automated pricing and order fulfillment, and integrates with marketplaces including eBay, [[Shopify]], [[Amazon (company)|Amazon]] and [[Facebook]].<ref name="globes">{{cite news |last=Sapir |first=Shira |title=המערכת שמלמדת סוחרי אינטרנט מה כדאי למכור ואיך |trans-title=The system that teaches online merchants what to sell and how |url=https://www.globes.co.il/news/article.aspx?did=1001448244 |work=[[Globes]] |date=1 June 2023 |language=he}}</ref> In 2023 the company employed about 100 people in 13 countries.<ref name="globes" /> By 2025 it employed about 200 people and reported more than 100,000 paying customers.<ref name="calcalist2025" /><ref name="forbes2025">{{cite news |title=נעלמו לי ביום אחד 30% מהמנהלים - והפכנו את המשבר לשנתיים הכי טובות שלנו אי פעם |trans-title=In one day 30% of my managers were gone, and we turned the crisis into our two best years ever |url=https://forbes.co.il/next-billion-dollar-startups-2025-israel-lior-pozin/ |work=[[Forbes Israel]] |date=27 October 2025 |language=he}}</ref> AutoDS grew without outside investment<ref name="geektime" /> and acquired competitors including DSMTool.<ref name="globes" />

=== Acquisition by Fiverr ===
In July 2024 Fiverr announced that it had acquired AutoDS as part of an expansion into e-commerce services, without disclosing the price.<ref name="ctech2024" /><ref name="fiverrpr">{{cite press release |title=Fiverr Deepens eCommerce Solutions with Acquisition of Leading Dropshipping Automation Tool AutoDS |publisher=Fiverr International |date=31 July 2024}}</ref> In its annual report on [[Form 20-F]] for 2024, Fiverr reported total consideration of about US$55.7 million, of which US$39.3 million was paid in cash, plus an [[earnout]] of up to US$36 million in cash and shares contingent on financial targets over the three years after the acquisition.<ref name="20f">{{cite web |title=Form 20-F for the fiscal year ended December 31, 2024 |url=https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001762301/000117891325000559/zk2532679.htm |publisher=Fiverr International Ltd. via [[U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission]] |at=Note 3}}</ref> Media reports described the deal as worth US$92 million.<ref name="entrepreneur" />

After the acquisition AutoDS continued to operate as a separate business unit within Fiverr, with Pozin remaining its CEO.<ref name="ctech2024" /><ref name="forbes30" />

=== Other activities ===
Pozin co-founded BuildYourStore, an AI-based online store builder launched in 2024.<ref name="forbes30" />
