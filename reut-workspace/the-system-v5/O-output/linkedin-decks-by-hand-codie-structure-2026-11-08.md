## Draft

Unpopular truth: I still build my monthly decks by hand.

Am I the last founder who doesn't tell AI "just do it"?

In 2004, Jeff Bezos banned PowerPoint from Amazon's senior meetings.
Every meeting starts with a 6-page memo, read in silence.
His reason: full sentences force you to think. Bullet points let you hide.

The memo was there to make the writer think.
20 years later, the same rule applies to AI.

A monthly deck has 3 jobs:

→ Show where we stand. AI is great at this. Numbers, charts, done in minutes.
→ Stop and look back. What did we do this month? What worked? What didn't?
→ Look forward. Where are we going next? What would we try if we started over?

Job 1 is for AI.
Jobs 2 and 3 are the whole reason the deck exists. Nobody can do them for you.

Same with emails and messages.

You can tell in two lines when someone pasted the question into Claude and sent back whatever came out.
Perfect grammar. Zero personality.

An email is one of the few places people still get to see who you are.
How you think. What you care about. How you joke.

Why hand that to a machine?

Let AI pull the numbers. Write the thinking yourself.

Do you still write your own decks and emails, or has AI taken over?▮
