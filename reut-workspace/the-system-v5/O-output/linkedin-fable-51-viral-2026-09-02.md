### The hook problem, in detail

"Anthropic just killed every AI website builder tool" is the one line that cannot ship as
written. Three reasons:

1. It is factually an overclaim. Fable 5.1 is a model, and the source's own testing showed
   near-identical visual output to the previous model.
2. Lovable is an AI website and app builder. There is a hard rule never to criticise Lovable.
   A line that kills "every AI website builder tool" criticises it by category, which is
   worse than naming it, because it also sweeps in every partner in that space.
3. The threat frame is still the right instinct for reach. It just has to point at an
   outcome rather than at a category of companies. "The AI website look is dead" gets the
   same energy with nobody in the crosshairs.

Also removed: the 15-word parenthetical on line 2. The one parenthetical exception is a
counter-hook under 6 words, so this one does not qualify.

## Line-2 counter-hook, round 2 (Reut 2026-09-02)

Her draft was "(it looks like a professional designer job)". Right instinct, wrong length and
wrong subject. The payoff belongs to the reader, and the bracket exception caps it at 6 words.

about their own site, and it flips line 1 from a bad thing ending to a good thing starting.

"הבעיה הגדולה בעיצוב ב-AI היא שזה יוצא גנרי, אבל עכשיו זה נראה הרבה יותר אישי וטוב."

Two changes:
- The problem block moved from technical defects to GENERIC. Broken layers are a builder's

  the one that makes the seven steps feel necessary rather than optional.
- The save instruction is now a plain sentence in the reader's own future tense, "next time

  above the list, which is where a save decision gets made.

The technical defect language survives where it earns its place, in item 7 and in the
first-pass confession, because there it is proof rather than framing.
