# Donor teardown: Amjad Masad (Replit CEO), "I was 12, addicted to Counter-Strike" (2026-10-06)

Reut flagged this as a structure that worked for another CEO. Archetype: childhood origin scene
that turns out to be today's company problem. Single narrative, ~1,250 chars, no list, no CTA.

## Donor text (reference only, never reuse wording)
I was 12, addicted to Counter-Strike, and I'd just sold a LAN cafe owner software that didn't exist yet.
... (cafe in Jordan, ran on paper, "I told him I could - only half true", pitched, "Then I disappeared
for two years", "nobody teaches you the part that comes after the sale", 3 concrete lines, sold it,
took his whole class to the new McDonald's in Amman "like I was a baller",
"The hard part was never the code. It was everything around it.",
"25 years later, that's still the problem I'm working on at Replit.")

## The 9 beats
1. Hook: exact age + identity detail + bold act done BEFORE being able to deliver. All in line 1.
2. Place with one physical texture line (computers everywhere, business on paper).
3. What the kid noticed that the adult missed, shown through a concrete scene (staff tapping your shoulder).
4. The naive ask/promise + a self-deflating admission ("only half true"). Humility beat.
5. The yes, then a short twist line alone ("Then I disappeared for two years").
6. "Nobody teaches you the part after X" + 3 short fragment lines naming the unseen work.
7. Kid-scale payoff + a funny, specific spend (McDonald's flex). The likeable beat.
8. One quotable lesson line.
9. Bridge: "N years later, that's still the problem I'm working on at [company]." Last line reveals the
   whole post was about the company without a single product claim.

## Why it works
- Line 1 carries age, quirk and stakes, so the scroll stops without a list or a number gimmick.
- The company is named only in the final line; the post reads as a story, never as marketing.
- Confession of overpromising makes him likeable; the McDonald's line makes him human.
- The ending reframes everything above it, which drives re-reads and comments.

## Strip before reusing
- Beat 8 in the donor is the banned "never X, it was Y" contrast. Rewrite every time.
- Hook rule for Lior: under 10 words, so compress beat 1.
