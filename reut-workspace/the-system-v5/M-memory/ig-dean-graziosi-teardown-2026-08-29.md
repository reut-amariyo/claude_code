## What the account actually is

1. **Two posts a day, forever.** Every single day: one Reel + one white text card. Aug 25, 26, 27, 28 are all identical in shape. This is a machine, not a mood.
2. **The text card is not a filler post.** Two of his three best non-pinned posts this week are text cards. Format: plain background, 2-3 lines of black type, `@deangraziosi` watermark bottom-right. Zero design cost, and it out-performs half his reels.
3. **The caption IS the content.** LinkedIn-style writing pasted into IG: one line per paragraph, heavy white space, 100-250 words, no hashtags, no link, no CTA. The image is just the thing that makes it postable.
4. **The grid is brand. The profile is the funnel.** Nothing in 12 posts sells anything. The selling is all in the top bar: bio ("Entrepreneur | Investor | Educator / Co-Founder: Mastermind.com + AI Advantage / NYT Bestselling Author"), one link (joinaiaclub.com), and 6 highlights used as product shelves - FAMILY, Fit Over 50, AI Advantage, Mastermind, OYF Podcast.
5. **One spine, repeated to death.** 8 of the 12 posts say the same thing: stop waiting, you are the bottleneck, hunger beats talent, stay in the game. He says it out loud in one caption: "I talk about success a lot here. And yes, I repeat myself." That post was his 2nd best of the week.
6. **Inverse caption law.** His 360K reel had an 8-word caption. His 100+ word essays sit at 600-1,800 likes. When the message is inside the video with burned-in text, it travels. When the message needs reading, it converts the people already following him and stops there.
7. **The question CTA killed the post.** The only post that ends with "What's something you believe every kid should learn at home?" is his worst of the week: 477 likes, 5 comments. Engagement bait did not produce engagement.

## Conclusions for Lior

**Steal these five:**

1. **The daily pair.** One video + one text card, every day, same time. Lior's IG is our #1 platform by priority and 500K is a run-rate problem. Dean proves the base layer is a rhythm, not an event. The text card half costs nothing to produce - we already have `render_photo_card.py` and the `[ life is beta ]` lockup, which is a better-looking version of what he's doing.
2. **Grid = brand, profile = funnel.** Audit Lior's IG bio + highlights against this. Dean gets 1.4M people past a bio that stacks three credentials and one link. Lior's equivalent stack is stronger and more specific: founder, exit to Fiverr, 250 employees, an actual product people can try. Highlights should become shelves: AutoDS / Build Your Store / podcast appearances / behind the scenes / family.
3. **Repeat the spine, change the door.** He rides one idea for years. Our three pillars ("I live on 200", loves 0 to 1, focus) are that spine. The fix for "the drafts start the same" is not new topics, it's new entry points into the same three ideas.
4. **Growth reels are a different product from daily reels.** 360K vs 1,100 is not variance, it's two different formats. The winners are 10-20 seconds, one sentence, message burned into the frame, caption under 10 words. Everything else is retention content for people who already follow. If the 500K IG target is real, we need a named format for the outlier lane and we should be shipping it several times a week, not hoping a daily post breaks out.
5. **No CTA in the post.** His best posts ask for nothing. His only question-CTA post flopped. This backs the existing rule about killing newsletter-style CTAs and using open endings instead.

**Do not steal these three:**

1. **Content-free motivation.** Dean can post "Build it anyway." because 20 years of books and stages already carry the proof. Lior's whole edge is receipts: the exit, the ARR, the VAT crisis, the dollar bug, 250 people. A Lior text card should carry an operator fact or a number, not a mantra. Same format, opposite fill.
2. **His engagement rate as a benchmark.** 1.4M followers producing 1,000 likes is a 0.08% floor. Do not benchmark Lior against his follower count. Benchmark against his outliers, which is where the growth actually came from.
3. **Volume as the strategy.** Two a day only works because he has a decade of catalogue behind it. For Lior the same production budget is better spent on fewer, higher-ceiling assets - the same logic that made the exit-deck giveaway the LinkedIn follower engine.

## The one-line takeaway

Dean's daily posting maintains an audience he already has, and his growth comes from a handful of 10-second reels with 8-word captions. If we copy him, copy the rhythm and the profile architecture, and put Lior's proof where Dean puts affirmation.
