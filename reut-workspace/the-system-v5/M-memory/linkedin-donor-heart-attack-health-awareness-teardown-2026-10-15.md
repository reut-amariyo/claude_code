## Why it works
- Real body, real date, real minutes. Specificity makes it impossible to scroll past.
- Proof the message already changed behavior, so the repeat feels earned.
- The writer is still imperfect today; no preaching from the mountain.
- Share CTA fits health content: people forward it to family groups, which LinkedIn can't see but the
  post still spreads.

## Do NOT copy
- "Not weakness, it's maintenance for your heart" and "not for the diet, for life" = banned contrast templates.
- Parentheses, "the least sexy word" aphorism, 4-item list (go 3 or 5).
