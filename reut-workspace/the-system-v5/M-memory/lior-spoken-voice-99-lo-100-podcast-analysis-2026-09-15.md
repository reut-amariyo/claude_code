## Part 2 — NEW signatures only the Hebrew reveals

### 1. "זאת אומרת" is his engine (≈30 uses in 38 minutes)
He states a thing, then says "זאת אומרת" (meaning / that is) and restates it in
simpler, more concrete words. Claim → restatement → example. This is why his
English posts feel right when a line is followed by a plainer twin of itself.
Draft mechanic: after any abstract sentence, add one sentence that says the same
thing with a number or an object in it. Never leave an abstraction alone.

### 2. Every story carries one line of reported speech, opened with "שומע/שומעת/תקשיב"
"שומע, התעוררתי ממלא צ'ה-צ'ינגים" / "תקשיב, אף אחד לא נולד מנכ"ל" / "תגיד, הוא
עדיין יכול למצוא עבודה בתור מתכנת?" / "אתם שומעים, אני לא עושה שום דבר עד ש..."
He does not narrate what people said, he plays them. Draft mechanic: one quoted
line per story, opened with "Listen," in English. Not two. One.

### 3. "בסוף" (in the end) is the Hebrew "I believe" (≈15 uses)
"בסוף זה הערך שמוביל אותי" / "בסוף, אם הם יכולים..." / "בסוף אתה יכול לקבל את
זה, אתה יכול לא". It marks the sentence he wants remembered. In English drafts
this is "In the end," and it should appear once per post, on the quotable line.

### 4. "אגב" (by the way) carries the biggest claim
"החלום שלי זה להיות מיליארדר. עד גיל 45, אגב." The boldest number in the whole
episode is dropped as an aside. Draft mechanic: put the largest number in a
throwaway position (line 2 parenthetical counter-hook, or a trailing "by the
way"). He never leads with the flex.

### 5. He pre-empts the objection himself, mid-sentence
"לא בהכרח הספר הכי טוב, אבל הוא כן האחרון." / "שכר שהיום לא הייתי מציע לאף אחד,
כי זה נמוך." / "המון דברים כאלה שהיום אגב אני יותר מבין." He audits his own
claim before the listener can. This is the honesty texture that AI drafts lack.
Draft mechanic: one self-correction per post, in his voice, not an apology.

### 6. Dry ethnic self-mockery as warmth
"כמו סובייטי טוב" / "כמו רוסי טוב" / "כמובן יזמות, סתם." Never about others,
always about himself. This is the only humor register he uses unprompted.
Tom Orbach check: the humor is self-directed and cultural, never a joke about
the audience. Keep it that way.

### 7. Numbers are the plot, not the decoration
In order: 1990, 3 languages, 12, 100 shekels, 14, $30, 18, 21, 100 stores,
tens of thousands of products, $40-50, $1, 3 AM, 30-something sales, $200,
$400, 100 employees, 20%, 10 hours a week, 45, 100 million lives, 5 pages,
5 minutes. Every beat of every story has a number attached. A story of his
without a number is a paraphrase, not a story.

### 8. Filler to strip when converting to text
"כאילו", "אה", "נגיד", "וזה", "כזה". These are breath, not voice. Strip all.
Keep "זאת אומרת" (as a restatement), "בסוף", "אגב", "שומע".

## Part 5 — What this changes in how I draft him

1. After every abstract line, a concrete restatement (the "זאת אומרת" rule).
2. One quoted line of dialogue per story, opened with "Listen,".
3. "In the end," once per post, on the quotable.
4. The biggest number goes in the aside, never the hook.
5. One self-correction per post, stated flat, not apologised.
6. Humor only self-directed, cultural, dry.
7. A story beat without a number is not finished.
8. Vulnerability = named skill gap + plan. Never fear, never disbelief.
