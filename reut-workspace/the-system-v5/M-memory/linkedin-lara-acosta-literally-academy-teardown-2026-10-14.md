## 1. The SLAY framework (her core IP, from the 25-min video)

Story → Lesson → Actionable advice → You. Built so that a person with no credentials can still
stand out, because ChatGPT can write the advice but cannot write your story.

| Beat | Her rule | Note for Lior |
|---|---|---|
| **S, Story** | 2 lines max, "no longer and no less." The hook is the story. If more context matters, bullet the crucial moments instead of narrating them, to cut friction. | Lior's receipts compress well. Bullet the beats of a long story, never a paragraph. |
| **L, Lesson** | One line. Not exaggerated. Think A → B: how fast can the reader get from the story to the point? Package it so it "reads like a song." | Same as our one quotable principle. |
| **A, Actionable advice** | Succinct. **Go deep on ONE sub-topic, never the whole list.** Her example: a post only on finding a cofounder beats a list of 8 stages of building a drinks brand. Reader goes from "I knew that" to "I can apply that." | Reinforces one-idea-per-post with a new reason: depth earns saves. |
| **Y, You** | Close by speaking to the one reader, with a line that makes them feel good and echoes the hook. Then a P.S. with a simple question they can answer in a comment. | P.S. question = optional, see conflicts below. |

Extra rules from the same video:
- **Hook anatomy, 3 keywords:** a teaching signal ("The #1 reason"), the ICP called out by name
  ("LinkedIn creators"), and a tension word ("fail"). Each word does a job.
- **Line 2 = the rehook.** Either contradict, which triggers curiosity and negativity bias, or state the
  overly obvious, which triggers agreement and confirmation bias. Same as Rule F in
  [[lior-top-8-10k-playbook]], now with the two flavors named.
- **Add one trade secret** a non-insider would not know, in one sentence. That line carries the authority.
- **Story first, then the topic.** Ask two questions: what do I want to teach, and what recent story of
  mine proves I can teach it? Her worked example is a founder: "built my first business with no
  investors, today it's worth X" → lesson "you don't need VC, you need distribution" → playbook.
  That is Lior's lane almost exactly.
- **Halo and authority bias:** a recent win is the best story slot because it lends credibility to the advice.
- **The image is the first hook.** People stop on the photo, then read. SLAY text posts underperform without
  one. Her working formats are a relevant photo of the moment, an infographic, or a tweet screenshot. She
  avoids video.

## 2. Her annotated breakdowns: the beat labels she uses

From her 8 best posts. These are the labels she drew on her own posts:

- **A Simpler Way:** implies there's a simpler way → who it's for → things to avoid → do this instead →
  manageable time frame → next step. 1,407 reactions / 576 comments.
- **My Proven System:** big promise as insider info → relatable starting point → 3-step system → personal
  example → proof it works → what to reflect on → key takeaway.
- **Alternative Approach:** relatable struggles → new approach → actionable advice → unlock this benefit →
  key takeaway.
- **Hitting Goals:** bold statement → impressive achievement → reflects on success → past struggles →
  corrective actions → key takeaway → fosters engagement with a P.S. question.
- **Transformational Tips:** tip as a reframe → tips each tied to a benefit with an arrow → adopt this
  mindset → example to illustrate → unlock this benefit.
- **A Quick Masterclass:** common struggle → big promise → tip as a formula → example templates →
  explanation and benefits → bonus tip.
- **True Impact:** challenges a common perception → personal anecdote → lesson → motivational advice as a
  rule of three.
- **The (Re)Introduction:** impressive achievement → personal facts → what to expect from my posts.

Pattern across all 8: hook → credibility or struggle → a structured middle with bullets or arrows → one
takeaway line → optional P.S. Every middle is a **list with a benefit attached to each item**.

## 3. Line-level writing rules (from her "Transformational Tips" post and the prompts)

- Treat every line as a hook. Write each sentence like it's the last one.
- Put a quick win in every line.
- Delete "very."
- Don't over-explain. Give solutions only.
- Everything should be usable "now." Less inspiration, more immediate application. This is why
  infographics and how-to carousels work.
- Line 1 makes a statement; line 2 adds context.
- Hook formula: **curiosity + storytelling + transformation**. Shapes: "How I did [outcome] in [time]",
  "How I did [hard thing] using [x]", "I was at [low point]."
- Her prompt spec: line 1 is a bold statement of 8 words or fewer. Line 2 opens a numbered list. Sentences
  are 8 words or fewer, formatting changes as you go, and there are no hashtags.
- The arrow sub-bullet "↳" explains a list item without making it a new paragraph.

## 4. Driving story framework (from Content Systems)

1. Name a key challenge. Her own examples include **being dyslexic**, failed businesses, debt and hiring
   the wrong people.
2. Link it to your expertise or success.
3. Pull the lesson the audience can use.
4. Frame it as growth: "I failed at X, today it led to Y."
5. No exaggeration. A personal brand is not a diary. Show the human side, not a bid for sympathy.

Plus **powerful elements**: recurring signatures only you have, such as the images, the small references
to people you worked with or past wins, and the specific personal details. Together they become a
running story readers can follow, and that is what sets you apart in a crowded niche.

## 5. Operator system and distribution

- **10x10x10 engagement list:** 10 authority leaders your ICP admires, 10 industry peers who could talk
  you up later, 10 active potential clients. Quality over quantity.
- **Funnel split:** TOFU = journey, stories, beliefs. MOFU = market trends and tools. BOFU = how-tos
  and testimonials. **Growth needs more TOFU; conversion needs more BOFU.**
- **"Golden retriever" effect:** cheerful, fun replies on your own posts and on other people's pages. It's
  a slow burn, but it builds community and makes you shareable.
- **Selective networking:** 3 meaningful comments a day on key people. Share 1 useful resource a week.
  Check in monthly. Congratulate milestones.
- **Authority checklist:** a relevant hook, a body that keeps the hook's promise, one key point, no
  filler, one CTA, open questions, and replies to comments.
- **Her swipe method:** a 30-minute scroll block saving posts that stopped her → find patterns in hooks,
  format and images → a 60-minute sprint writing 2-3 posts in that "vibe" → post one and test. This is
  the same loop as our swipe engine and [[feedback-build-on-proven-structures]].
- **30-day challenge:** post daily and log one 1% improvement per post.

## 7. Conflicts with our rules, and how they resolve

- **P.S. engagement question.** She uses one on most posts. Our [[feedback-linkedin-engagement-gap]] calls a
  closed ending a note, not a blocker. Allowed when the question is easy to answer and on topic; never
  "Agree?". No "Repost ♻️" line.
- **Contrast lines.** Her quotables often take a "not X, but Y" shape. Rewrite them as one declarative
  sentence, per [[feedback-anti-ai-writing-rules]] and [[linkedin-ai-slop-penalty]].
- **Line-2 parenthetical.** Her "(the secret trick I used to get to 150k fast)" is our allowed exception,
  but it must stay under 6 words.
- **Arrows "→" and "↳".** Fine for Lior; they are not dashes. Cap them at one list per post.
- **Images.** Her "image is the first hook" agrees with our rule of one artifact, one number or one candid
  face. Her infographics suggestion does NOT override [[feedback-linkedin-visual-one-artifact-not-diagram]].
- **Hashtags.** She uses none either. Aligned.
- **Dyslexia as a driving story.** Our dyslexia rule already says the angle is fair game when the post is
  really about it. SLAY gives it a ready-made spine. Reut decides before it ships.
