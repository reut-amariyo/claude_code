## Diagnosis, ranked by confidence

1. **Same failure as the Astra post, 5 days later, with the rule already written.** The memory
   feedback-linkedin-visual-one-artifact-not-diagram (written 2026-09-13) says: trims removed the
   confession, permission, trigger and cost beats, only the steps and the close survived, and without
   the Lior beats a how-to is a tool tip. That is this post, beat for beat. The Codex winner and both
   flops share the identical 3-step skeleton. The only difference is the Lior beats around it.
   Two flops on the same pattern: this is a law now, not a hypothesis.

2. **No trigger.** The Codex post rode Astra's launch week. Grok Bot launched in August; by 09-14 the
   X wave (Sabrina 313K, ridark 601K) was 2-3 weeks old. A how-to with no "why now" gets no
   out-of-network push on an interest-graph feed. 54% out-of-network of 1.2K is the algorithm
   sampling and stopping.

3. **Zero reason to follow.** No first-party artifact survived: the WHOOP receipt was cut, the roster
   of his 13 real bots was never in it, no confession, no operator number. The gate row "why would a
   stranger follow" was answered "a procedure plus a thesis". Wrong answer: a procedure for a
   third-party tool converts nobody. It converts only when it carries the operator's own artifact.

4. **Visual said nothing the copy did not.** The meme is the post redrawn as a joke. Same rule as the
   Astra flowchart: one real artifact or one big number. Hypothesis only: a cartoon bodybuilder in
   briefs may also trip LinkedIn's quality classifier. Unproven.

5. **Process.** The gate ran once, on v24. Reut's later edits were treated as untouchable per the
   never-revert rule, and I never named the structural cost of the cuts. The rule allows exactly one
   sentence naming a real problem. That sentence was owed on v25 and not said.
