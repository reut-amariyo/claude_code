# Donor teardown: Israeli VC "seed-strap" thesis post + personal coda (2026-10-15)

Source: pasted by Reut 2026-10-15 as "an unusually successful LinkedIn post". Hebrew, author Alon Houri (אלון חורי),
Israeli investor. Investor writing his investment thesis, ~2,500 chars, then a personal coda: his mother
died, he went silent 3 weeks, today he turns 50.

## Why it worked, 3 engines stacked
1. **Data that breaks a consensus.** "Everyone says there's no money" -> $400B invested, 88% went to
   $100M+ rounds, strip 5 mega-deals and the market shrank 73%. Readers save and reshare numbers that
   let them win an argument.
2. **A thesis with a single metric and a reason.** Operating profit in 6-18 months. "Not growth, not
   multiples, not retention" + why: it can't be dressed up in a deck. Conviction + why = authority.
3. **The human coda.** Business value first, then a short personal section that explains the absence.
   It turns readers into commenters: condolences + birthday wishes = comment flood = distribution.
   The coda is not a tactic, it only exists because it was true.

## What to take for Lior
- **His topic, his receipt.** Beat 8 IS Lior: AutoDS profitable from day one, never needed the next round,
  turned down PE a day before closing, "I believe in no". The investor has a thesis; Lior has the operator
  proof. Topic gate passes: only an exited, profitable-from-day-one founder can write the operator side.
  See lior-story-pe-offer-refused-day-before, lior-podcast-99-lo-100-canonical-stories ("I believe in no").
  NEVER write "investors said no to me" (feedback-lior-investors-never-said-no).
- **Two-scene contrast opener**: a real conversation this week vs a headline this week. Needs a REAL
  meeting, mine Plaud recordings, never invent the founder.
- **One metric + why you chose it** is a strong conviction beat for Lior posts.
- **Self-skepticism paragraph** before the close: names who this won't work for. Kills the guru smell.
- **Coda mechanic**: when something real happens in Lior's life, a short human P.S. after a value post
  beats a separate personal post. Only with a real event Reut confirms. Never manufacture.

## Do NOT copy
- "I'm not selling magic. I'm saying..." = the banned contrast template. Rewrite.
- "Not growth, not multiples, not retention" is fine as a negative list, but never follow it with "it's X".
- Coining a branded thesis name ("seed-strap") collides with the no-concept-naming rule. Only OK if Lior
  already uses the term himself and will repeat it as a series. Ask Reut first.
- Market stats in the donor are the donor's. Any number Lior uses needs a source we can link.
- Length ~2,500 chars works for an investor essay; for Lior keep the thesis part tight, ~1,200-1,500 + coda.
