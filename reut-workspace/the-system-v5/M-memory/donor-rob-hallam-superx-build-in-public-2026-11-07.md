## The structural lessons
1. **The person beats the demo.** Launch post with a screen recording: 32K views, 26 signups. A selfie at his laptop the next day: 46K views, more signups, +$1K MRR in 24h.
2. **Vulnerability went viral.** A post listing all his failures got 150K views and his first $3K client.
3. **Profile foundation:** stable real face, one-sentence bio, pinned post = the public goal ("left job… build my dream SaaS to 10K/month").
4. **One public goal, documented daily** = consistency + storyline + trust.
5. **The content loop:** entertaining (attention) → educational or convincing (teach or sell from goodwill) → inspirational (screenshot of the result + what it means) → repeat.
6. **Systemize virality:** analyze top posts in the niche, copy the CONCEPT, not the words. Use the product to grow the product.
7. **Double down, kill what doesn't work,** even a viral feature nobody uses.
8. **Real connections:** reply to the same niche people daily with real value. AI-spammed generic replies build nothing.
9. **Go where the platform pushes:** video got 10x the reach of text. Demos made in Screen Studio.
