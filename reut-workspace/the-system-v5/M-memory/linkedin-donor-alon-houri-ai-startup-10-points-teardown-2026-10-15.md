# Donor teardown: Alon Houri, "The founder's guide: 10 points on building an AI startup in 2026" (2026-10-15)

Third Alon Houri winner Reut sent on 2026-10-15. Hebrew, numbered playbook, ~2,800 chars. Same thesis world as
"seed-strap": small team, AI-run company, profitability early, founder keeps a double-digit stake.

## Why it worked
1. **Hook calls the reader's plan outdated.** "If you're planning to hire 20 developers to build for a year, you're
   building a company of the past." A concrete plan the reader actually has, with a number, declared dead.
2. **Every point has a NAMED rule.** "The rule of 4", "the triple AI filter", "agent-first methodology", "A-strap",
   "the new moat". Named rules get saved, quoted and screenshotted. Same coined-term strategy as seed-strap.
3. **Points are frameworks, not tool tips.** Each one is a decision filter a founder can apply tomorrow:
   replace / 10x / superpower, the 4 core seats, the 3-question agent-first order.
4. **Money payoff for the founder**: single-digit stake after endless dilution vs double-digit stake at exit.
5. **Soft close**: "which point did you connect with most?" Easy comment, no ask.

## Alon Houri pattern across all 3 posts
- 📌 on the hook, year "2026" in the hook, numbered or thesis structure, ~2,500-3,000 chars.
- Coins a term in almost every post: seed-strap, A-strap, רדיוס יזמי, rule of 4. Deliberate, it's his tip 10.
- Same worldview repeated: lean, AI-run, profitable early, founder keeps control. One thesis, many posts.
- Closes with a question to the reader.

## What to take for Lior
- **This topic is Lior's lane.** Exited founder, profitable from day one, now building with AI agents. An
  operator version of this guide passes the topic gate ONLY if each point carries a Lior receipt: what AutoDS
  did, what he'd do differently now, what he builds with Claude today. Without receipts it's the how-to that
  flopped twice (feedback-howto-beats-are-structural-two-flops). The Claude->Codex post shows the receipt version works.
- **Hook shape**: name the reader's real plan with a number, then call it a company of the past.
- **Named rule per item**: makes the list saveable. Keep names plain, never "the art of X".
- **Decision filter items** beat advice items: give a test the reader can run, like the 3-question agent-first order.
- **One thesis, many posts**: Lior should own one repeated worldview the way Alon owns "lean + profitable".
  Natural candidate: "I believe in no" / profitable from day one / never depend on the next round.
