Pasted by Reut 2026-10-15 right after the Alon Houri set. Author not named; voice, "Growth First", "companies I
found with founders" and the coined term match Alon Houri. ~3,000 chars Hebrew.

## Beat map
| # | Beat |
|---|------|

## Why it works
- Death hook + a contrarian claim the reader can argue with = comments.
- The list is a map of business ideas, highly save-shaped for founders.
- One repeated sentence shape per item ("AI does X, the human signs Y") makes 15 items skimmable.
- Close question asks readers to extend the list, so comments add value instead of "great post".

## Lior angle and risks
- Same Alon worldview again: Growth First, small team, the license/human as moat.
- "SaaS is dead" is risky for Lior: his audience includes SaaS CEOs, and partners like Shopify and Lovable are
  software. If borrowed, aim the death claim at a practice, never at a partner or a category his audience runs.
- Lior has the receipt for "boring industry + growth first": e-commerce sellers, profitable from day one.
  Keep it founder journey, never Build Your Store marketing.

## Do NOT copy
- "The revolution is not building an app, but replacing..." and "Not 'build it and they will come', the opposite"
  = banned contrast templates.
- 15 items is fine as odd, but Lior's version should be 5 or 7 with a receipt each.
- Parentheses, ‼️, unsourced "99%".
