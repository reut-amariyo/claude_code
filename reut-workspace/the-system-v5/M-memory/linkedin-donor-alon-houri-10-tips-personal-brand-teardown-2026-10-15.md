# Donor teardown: Alon Houri, "10 tips for founders building a personal brand on LinkedIn in 2026" (2026-10-15)

Second Alon Houri post Reut flagged as a winner, same day as the seed-strap thesis post
(linkedin-donor-seedstrap-vc-thesis-coda-teardown-2026-10-15.md). Hebrew, numbered list, ~3,000 chars.

## Why it worked
1. **The post has a reason to exist.** "This week 3 founders told me over coffee that my post got them writing
   and it changed their lives." The list is a follow-up to proven impact, not a random how-to.
2. **Operator receipts inside the tips.** "I built a skill that scans Reddit", "in my posts the comments are as
   interesting as the post", "I don't do carousels enough, it's on my todo". Each tip carries his own proof or
   his own weakness.
3. **Built-in save CTA mid-list** with a wink: tip 3 is "track saves", then "you know what to do with this post".
4. **Reciprocity CTA = comment + follower engine.** "Post your first post this week, tag me, I'll read, comment
   and cheer you on. Pay me in karma." Turns readers into tagged posts that point back to his profile.
5. **Coined terms are his strategy, not an accident.** Tip 10: coin your own term, it brings people through
   Google and AI search ("רדיוס יזמי"). That is why the thesis post was named "seed-strap".

## Alon Houri's signature, both posts
- Opens with a real coffee meeting THIS WEEK, then the insight it triggered.
- 📌 at the end of the hook line.
- Data or receipt in every section, then a human close: grief + 50 in one, "the first step is psychological,
  there's fear" in the other.
- Ends by giving the reader an action plus a question: "who picks up the gauntlet?"

## What to take for Lior
- **"Why this post exists" opener**: real people told Lior something this week. Needs a real Plaud moment.
- **Receipt or confession inside every list item**, never a bare tip. This is exactly what the two how-to flops
  lacked (feedback-howto-beats-are-structural-two-flops).
- **Reciprocity CTA**: "do X this week, tag me, I'll comment on every one". Strong for the followers KPI,
  but only if Lior or Foundera will actually answer every tag. Confirm capacity with Reut before using it.
- **Coin a term Lior will repeat** to feed AI-search citations (linkedin-ai-search-citations-2026). Clashes with
  the no-concept-naming rule; Reut decides per term. Candidate he already owns: "I believe in no".

## Do NOT copy for Lior
- The topic. Generic "how to grow on LinkedIn" tips fail Lior's topic gate and the no-tool-tips rule.
  Alon is an investor-creator, his audience wants this; Lior's lane is operator + exit + AI building.
- "A personal brand isn't ego, it's a magnet" / "be the ones who give, not the ones who market" = banned
  contrast template.
- 10 items = even; Lior lists stay odd unless data forces even.
- Parentheses everywhere in the donor. Lior gets one line-2 parenthetical max.
- Algorithm claims (3s/15s dwell, first 15 minutes, carousels 3-5x) are Alon's, unsourced. Don't repeat as fact.
