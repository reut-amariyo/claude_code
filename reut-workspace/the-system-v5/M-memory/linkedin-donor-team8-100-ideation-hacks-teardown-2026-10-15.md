# Donor teardown: "100 Ideation hacks" build-in-public guide teaser (2026-10-15)

Pasted by Reut 2026-10-15. 570+ likes AND Lior liked it. Author not named, works at Team8.

## The mechanic: a guide in progress, sampled in public
Announce a big artifact you're writing (100 hacks), give 8 samples now, say you're 20% done,
ask for a like as a demand vote, ask which tip landed, ask readers to submit their own hack
"maybe it gets into the guide". Likes become votes, comments become contributions, and every
commenter now wants the finished guide = a reason to follow.

## Beat map
| # | Beat |
|---|------|
| 1 | Hook: "A list of 100 X that will help you Y" + 🔴 |
| 2 | "I took on a slightly ambitious project lately" |
| 3 | Source of authority: our experience + the methods we run at Team8 + founders' daily questions |
| 4 | Goal: "the complete guide", 100 hacks, no philosophy |
| 5 | "Still being written, here's a first taste of 8" |
| 6 | 8 items: command title + why + concrete how; several carry a confession ("I still fall into this") or an in-house receipt ("at Team8 we present monthly to an outside partner") |
| 7 | Progress: "I'm at 20%" |
| 8 | Like = vote for demand |
| 9 | Which tip hit you? what topics should it cover? |
| 10 | Share your own hack, it might get in |

  into co-authors.
