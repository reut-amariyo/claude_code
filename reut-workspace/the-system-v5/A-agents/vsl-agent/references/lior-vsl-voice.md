# Lior's voice on camera, for VSL scripts

Source: `claude-memory/memory/lior-spoken-voice-dna-e97.md` (English podcast) and `lior-spoken-voice-dna-hebrew-99-lo-100.md`. These are the only evidence of how he really talks.

## How he sounds

1. Short sentences, subject-verb-object, present tense. Connectors: "So", "OK, so", "And then", "Because". Never "However" or "Moreover".
2. Threes come natural to him: "So many people said... So many people didn't believe... So many people said..."
3. Self-interrogation: "What's the worst case? OK. And then what?" Good for the catch beat and the fear beat.
4. The flat dismissal is the flex: "So we opened a new one." Calm, never hype.
5. Wins as undecorated facts: "8 years, over 200 employees, acquired by Fiverr." Never "incredible", never "insane".
6. Boldness is always calculated: "I do things from logic." Never reckless, never "I bet everything".
7. Conviction marker: "I believe that..." Agreement: "100 percent."
8. He's an introvert. He talks like a quiet operator, not a hype host. No shouting, no "guys!!!".

## Vault writing rules, apply to every line

- No parentheses. No em dashes, en dashes, or double dashes.
- No "It's not X, it's Y" in any wording, including "You don't need X. You need Y."
- Never open with "Honestly", "Frankly", or "To be honest".
- No aphoristic concept names like "the art of X" or "setup paralysis".
- Red list: delve, crucial, pivotal, tapestry, robust, seamless, realm, "Here's the thing", "Hope this helps", "Most people..." as a hook, adverb-propped verbs.
- "just" max once in the whole script.
- Odd numbers in lists: 3 steps, 5 things you get, 7 at most.
- English only, even when Reut briefs in Hebrew.
- No imposter syndrome: his wins are earned, never "I still can't believe".
- Vulnerability = past failures only, never current fears.
- Never arrogant about money. The $92M is a fact, said once, then moved past.

## Teleprompter formatting

- One sentence per line.
- Numbers written the way he says them: "1.8 million", "92 million dollars".
- Contractions on: "you're", "it's", "don't". Written-out "do not" sounds read, not spoken.
- Mark emphasis sparingly with CAPS on one word per beat, max.
