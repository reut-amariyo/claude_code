# The free-offer VSL spine for Build Your Store

Classic VSLs sell a price. BYS is free, so the friction isn't money. There are 3 other kinds of friction, and the script has to take each one down:
1. **Belief.** "I tried before, it didn't work. I'm not technical."
2. **Trust.** "Free from a millionaire? What's the catch?"
3. **Inertia.** "I'll do it later."

## Length and pace

- Lior's pace: about 150 spoken words per minute.
- 60s short: about 150 words. Beats 1, 2, 5, 6, 8, 11 only.
- 2-3 min standard: 300-450 words. All 11 beats. Default.
- 5-7 min long-form: 750-1,050 words. All 11 beats; expand story, demo and objections. Only for cold traffic.
- Visual change every 6-10 seconds: cut to B-roll, a screen, on-screen text, or a new framing.

## The 11 beats, 2-3 min version

| # | Beat | Job | Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hook | Stop the scroll or the bounce. A concrete moment the viewer recognizes, under 10 words. | 0:00-0:05 |
| 2 | Call-out | Name who this is for, and let everyone else leave. "If you've wanted an online store for a year and it still isn't live, this is for you." | 0:05-0:15 |
| 3 | The real problem | Relocate the blame. They didn't fail; setup killed them. Almost a third of new stores never go live. | 0:15-0:35 |
| 4 | Credibility | Lior's receipt, 2-3 undecorated facts. The story beat, where "I" is allowed. | 0:35-0:55 |
| 5 | The reveal | "So we built Build Your Store." One sentence of what it does. Energy lifts here. | 0:55-1:05 |
| 6 | How it works | The 3 steps, on screen. Pick a niche, review your store, start selling. Show, don't describe. | 1:05-1:30 |
| 7 | What you get | Fast stack: 10 products, theme, product pages, .store domain, support. Rhythm, not a list read. | 1:30-1:45 |
| 8 | The catch | "So what's the catch?" [PAUSE] Partnership answer. Mention the Shopify plan if the script talks cost. | 1:45-2:05 |
| 9 | Dream honesty | The setup is removed; the selling is still theirs. Reverse the excuse: the thing that stopped them is gone. | 2:05-2:20 |
| 10 | Mid-CTA recap | Only in versions over 3 min. Say the button text once more after the demo. | optional |
| 11 | Close + CTA | One instruction, button text word for word, the reason to act today with no fake deadline. | 2:20-2:40 |

## Hook bank: shapes that fit Lior, not guru shapes

- Recognition moment: "You bought the course. Your store still isn't live."
- Number + gap: "Almost a third of new stores never open."
- Origin receipt: "At 14, my first store ran out of my parents' apartment."
- Start-over: "If I lost everything tomorrow, this is the store I'd build first."
- Direct offer from an unlikely source: "I sold my company for $92 million. Now I'll build your store for free."
- Confession: "I made the mistake of thinking setup was the easy part."

Avoid: "Most people...", "What if I told you", "Stop scrolling", any "It's not X, it's Y".

## Objection bank: answer the top 3 inside the script, leave the rest to the page FAQ

| Objection | Answer inside the script |
|---|---|
| What's the catch? | Partnership frame, beat 8. |
| I'm not technical. | Pick a niche; the AI builds the rest. Show the screen. |
| I don't know what to sell. | 10 winning products already inside, picked from data on 800M+ products. |
| I tried before and failed. | Relocate the blame to setup, beat 3. |
| Will I make money? | Honest: the store is ready; selling is your part. Never promise income. |
| Is it really free forever? | Yes, no card. Shopify's own plan is separate, if you continue. |
| Does it take long? | Minutes. The page says "Live in Minutes". |

## Close shapes without fake urgency

BYS is free forever, so there's no deadline. Urgency comes from the viewer's own cost of waiting:
- "Every month you wait is a month the store isn't selling."
- "You've already lost a year thinking about it. The store takes minutes."
- "Click Build my FREE store. Pick a niche. Tonight it's live."

## Retention tricks that suit a founder talking head

- Open loop in beat 1 or 2, closed in beat 8: "and I'll tell you why it's free."
- Pattern break at the reveal: Lior turns the laptop to camera.
- Numbers on screen as text when he says them.
- End-screen button animation synced to the spoken CTA.
