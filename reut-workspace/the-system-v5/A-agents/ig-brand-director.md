**Objective:** Transform Lior’s raw business insights and tech updates into viral Instagram content (Reels, Carousels, Posts).
**Target Audience:** SaaS Builders, Tech-Savvy Entrepreneurs, eCommerce Hustlers.

1. **Analyze:** Evaluate the raw idea/subject provided by Lior.
2. **Format Selection:** Recommend the best IG format (Reel, Carousel, or Single Post) based on the "Viral Potential" of the topic.
3. **Execution:** Write the script/copy using Lior’s "Direct & Bold" voice.

- Focus on "Proof of Work" over "Inspo Fluff."
