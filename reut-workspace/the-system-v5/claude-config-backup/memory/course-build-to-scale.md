# Build to Scale - Course by Lior Pozin

## Course Structure

### Module 1: Business Foundations
- **L1 - Intro:** Lior's background. Started dropshipping at 14, no financial help/connections/entrepreneurs in family. Built AutoDS for himself, grew to 230+ employees from 30+ countries. Managed 4 companies (dropshipping, fulfillment, AI stores builder, AutoDS). Sold AutoDS to Fiverr.
- **L2 - Course Overview:** Three pillars: processes, strategy, people. Not just e-commerce - business in general.
- **L3 - What is Business:** Core principle: everything starts and ends with bringing value. Business isn't about reinventing the wheel - it's about solving problems in a new or better way. Build the experience, not just the product.
- **L4 - Standing Out:** Read competitor reviews (Trustpilot, websites). When starting AutoDS, Lior read what competitors did well and showed AutoDS does the same. More importantly - read what they DON'T do well. "We get tens of thousands of people per month that just go and read our reviews." Branding is what helped stand out.
- **L7 - Unique Value Proposition:** Be very specific with the problem you solve. Word of Mouth is the best marketing channel - free, easy, stays forever. "All what you need to do is to be different and be straight to the point."

### Module 2: Goals & OKRs
- **L1 - Long-term Goals:** Set goals for at least 1 year forward. 2-3 years feels too inaccurate. Take one day off where all management sits down and works just on long-term planning. "Thinking about the long term is more important than any other step in the short term."
- **L2 - Key Business Metrics:** Three top metrics: AOV (Average Order Value), LTV (Customer Lifetime Value), CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost). "Today, running a campaign shouldn't be directly ROI positive."
- **L3 - OKRs:** 2-3 objectives per quarter, 2-3 KPIs per objective. KPIs must be measurable with X and Y. Example: "Get AOV from $70 to $100" not "Get higher AOV." Don't measure team based on KPIs - KPIs show the road, not define success/failure.
- **L4 - OKR Process:** Plan in December for Q1. Track bi-weekly. Mid-quarter check. Set KPIs 20% more ambitious than expected. Retrospective each quarter. "The OKRs strategy is what really changed my business."

### Module 3: Partners & Advisors
- **L1 - Business Partners:** Stats: companies with 2+ co-founders grow 3x faster. 30%+ more likely to raise capital. Lior started alone, did everything (finance, legal, product, marketing, sales). "At some point, I got to a place where I feel really lonely." Getting a partner was "the best decision I've ever done." "1% of a billion-dollar company is worth much more than 100% from something that will not succeed."
- **L2 - Choosing Partners:** Rejected offer from well-known entrepreneur. Rejected PE firm offer. Why? "The business partner is just another relationship, a long-term one." Like finding a significant other - dating, spending time together. His partners are very different from him. "Lior, this is the agreement. You just keep running forward and I will organize everything." Give shares over time (vesting over 3 years). Responsibilities must be crystal clear.
- **L3 - Advisors:** "The shortcut to success." Used 30+ consultants. "People are really happy to help." Start with hourly rate, then retainer. Replace advisors as challenges change. Find them on LinkedIn, communities, and network. "It's not a shame to say you're struggling."

### Module 4: Team Building
- **L1 - Why Team Matters:** "You can't scale alone. Hiring is about freeing your time, not adding complexity." First 5 years - spent everything back into the business for team growth. Got feedback: "Lior, why don't you get money to your home?" "That's what made the difference."
- **L2 - When to Hire (The 70% Rule):** "If someone can do the task at least 70% as well as I would, it's time to hire." Did 100+ YouTube videos himself, then hired. STORY: Hired head of product (10 years exp) - left after 7 months. Hired VP product (15 years exp) - left after 3 months. Why? Wrong expectations. They wanted to lead vision; Lior wanted an executor. Hired product owner (3 years exp) - still there 3+ years later. "You don't need the best person, you need the person that is right exactly for what you are doing."
- **L3 - How to Hire:** 95% find talent on LinkedIn. Process: define tasks + skills → job description → publish → CVs → task (30 min max) → HR screening → interview with hiring manager. "Motivation beats skills 100% of the cases." Onboarding: don't expect value in first days/weeks. Pair with same-level buddy, not manager.

### Module 5: HR Department
- **L1 - HR as Growth Engine:** When COVID started 2020, competitors fired teams. Lior hired head of HR and asked to double the company. "That was the best decision I've ever done." HR maintains DNA, handles events, overlooks employee status.
- **L2 - HR Framework:** Two key processes alternating quarterly:
  1. **Retention calls** (employee → manager): Employee shares feedback about manager and company. Manager does NOT give feedback. Send prep message beforehand. "Give me something I'm doing well, and something I'm not doing well."
  2. **Feedback calls** (manager → employee): Manager shares direct professional feedback. Don't connect to salary discussion.
  Result: Less than 1% monthly employee turnover out of 200+ employees.

### Module 6: Management
- **L1 - Healthy Organization:** Share everything - bad and good. "People smell that" if you only share good things. "Your employees are the #1 most important thing - more than KPIs, tasks, goals, even more than the product." "Let them fly."
- **L2 - Macro vs Micro Management:** "If I need to check what you are doing, we will not work together." Micro kills creativity, slows decisions, burns managers, disengages employees. "When someone does a mistake, we encourage that."
- **L3 - Transition to Macro:** Set expectations from interview. CMO story: "I actually don't have tasks for you. I only know that someone needs to take the marketing." PA story: "I don't know how to work with a personal assistant, we need to figure it out together." "I don't track hours. I don't care." Focus on outcome-based, not task-based. Don't wait for feedback calls - be direct on weekly calls.
- **L5 - Action Plan:** Find right people → identify micromanaged tasks → convert to KPIs → delegate → weekly check-ins → track OKRs bi-weekly → trust the team.

### Module 7: Metrics & Systems
- **L1 - Key Metrics:** Track max 5 overall KPIs for the company lifetime. Top 3: AOV, CAC, LTV. Department-level: NPS, employee retention rate. "We saw more than 20% improvement just from one pricing optimization in AutoDS." Best businesses do price optimization at least once a year.
- **L2 - Data-Driven Decisions:** "Without data, you are just another person with an opinion." Early AutoDS had fights based on feelings. Spent millions implementing data systems. Use Google Analytics, Shopify analytics.
- **L3 - Processes Summary:** Weekly calls, bi-weekly OKR check-ins, quarterly reports with OKR status + ideas for future + what worked and didn't.
- **L4 - Sprints:** Use Asana (or Monday, ClickUp, Trello). Backlog → To Discuss → In Progress → Done. Plan 2 weeks ahead. Story points for complexity. Retrospective every 2 weeks. Quarterly review for repeatable tasks.

### Module 9: Closing
- **L2 - Summary:** "Implement systems one by one. Don't jump and do everything together." "Advisors are the best shortcut to success." "Remember the vision of your business why you started and focus on the long term."
