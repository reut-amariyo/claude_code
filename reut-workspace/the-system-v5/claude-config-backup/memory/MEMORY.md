## Social Media Writing
- **THE REAL PERSON PRINCIPLE (ALL PLATFORMS):** People don't follow accounts for opinions. They follow real people on a real path, just like them. Every post must show Lior as a real human - stories beat opinions, progress beats perfection, journey content compounds, forced content fails.
- **PERSONAL BRAND ≠ AUTODS MARKETING:** See [feedback-lior-personal-brand-not-autods.md](feedback-lior-personal-brand-not-autods.md). All Lior content is about HIS founder journey/lessons, never product marketing for AutoDS.
- See [social-media-style.md](social-media-style.md) for post writing guidelines and reference examples
- See [lior-inspiration-by-platform.md](lior-inspiration-by-platform.md) for Lior's inspiration creators organized by platform (LinkedIn, X, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube)
- **CRITICAL REPLY RULE:** Every comment/reply on X, LinkedIn, and Bluesky MUST include a real story from Lior's life - a specific number he tracked, a client story, a concrete experience. Never generic opinions.
- **Reply Post Selection Rules:** Only reply to posts with 200+ likes or 10K+ views. Never pick hardware/robotics/crypto posts. Stick to topics Lior lives daily: SaaS, AI tools, startup scaling, pricing, founder journey. When the insight already landed, stop. Don't add a reframe line on top.
- **Lior's Team Facts:** Always say "250 employees" in public content/replies. Never use "100 engineers" — it undersells the team size.
- See [feedback-stop-overusing-250-employees.md](feedback-stop-overusing-250-employees.md) — don't drop "250 employees" into every post; use only when team size is central to the point
- See [feedback-posts-always-english.md](feedback-posts-always-english.md) — all social post drafts for Lior are written in English, even when Reut briefs in Hebrew
