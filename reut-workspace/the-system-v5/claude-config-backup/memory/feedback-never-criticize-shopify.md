description: Shopify is a key partner - never write content that could be read as criticism of Shopify
type: feedback

Never criticize Shopify in any content. Not even subtly.
**Why:** Shopify is an important business partner for AutoDS. Any public content that implies Shopify was doing something wrong (e.g., "long overdue", "finally doing X") could damage the relationship.
**How to apply:** When writing about Shopify announcements or changes, frame them positively - celebrate the direction, don't imply they were late or wrong before. Review every draft for implicit criticism.
