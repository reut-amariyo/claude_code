When a LinkedIn post gets high impressions but low likes and zero comments, the problem is usually the ending, not the content.

**Pattern identified:** The "AI agents Paperclip" post got 10K impressions but only 12 likes and 0 comments. The content and hook were strong (people stopped to read), but nothing triggered engagement.

**Root causes:**
1. **Closed ending** — statements like "Humans aren't going anywhere" or "greatest time to be building" are agreeable but don't invite response. People nod and scroll.
2. **Post gives the full answer** — when the reader gets story + problem + conclusion, there's nothing left to add. No reason to comment.
3. **Too balanced/respectful tone** — not criticizing the tool, not making a bold claim. Balanced = no friction = no debate.

**How to apply:**
- End viral-topic posts with a genuine open question ("What's the one thing you'd never trust an agent to do?") or a polarizing take that invites pushback
- Leave one gap in the conclusion — let the reader feel they have something to contribute
- For engagement: "article" format (full story + wrapped conclusion) = impressions. "Conversation starter" format (story + open question) = comments + likes
- When the goal is engagement, sacrifice a bit of polish for provocation
