---
name: Stop overusing social proof numbers
description: Don't default to "250 employees" or "9 years" social proof in every LinkedIn post - use sparingly
type: feedback
---

Stop defaulting to social proof numbers (250 employees, 9 years, 300+ hires, etc.) in every post. It's fine occasionally, but not as a default in every single piece of content.

**Why:** Reut flagged that it's becoming repetitive - the same numbers show up in nearly every post and it feels formulaic rather than authentic.

**How to apply:** Only use social proof numbers when they're directly relevant to the story being told (e.g., a post about scaling teams can mention team size). For most posts, let the story and insight carry the authority instead of leaning on credentials. If the post works without the number, leave it out.
