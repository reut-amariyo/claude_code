**Why:** Lior has a senior SaaS founder audience that behaves differently than generic LinkedIn. External patterns (Ruben Hassid, Justin Welsh, etc.) are hypotheses. Lior's own data is ground truth. Don't override Lior data with external patterns.

**How to apply:** Before drafting any Lior LinkedIn post, scan the learning-log and the 3-5 most recent analyst-reports in O-output. The rules below summarize what's been confirmed.

---

## Iron-Clad Failure Patterns (confirmed 3+ times each)

1. **Zero personal stake = failure.** Every Lior post below 1,000 impressions has zero personal stake. No exceptions in 8 data points. Every post MUST include a specific Lior moment, number, or lived experience — not a generic take.

2. **Arrow-listicle (→) = template content.** Killed Posts 17, 19, 20 (800 impressions each). "→ Anthropic built... → Cursor scaled... → Notion hit..." Senior audience recognizes template structure and scrolls past. Do NOT use arrow-list formatting on Lior's LinkedIn even when the topic begs for it.

3. **LinkedIn template phrases kill voice.** "Read that again." / "Not X. Not Y. Z." / "One person... One team..." — overused LinkedIn-bro patterns that contradict Lior's authentic voice.

4. **Graph/chart images hurt performance.** Post 17 died partly because of a graph. Use personal photos or scene context instead.

5. **Zero friction = zero comments = algorithm death.** If no one can disagree, no one engages.

6. **Celebrity commentary is the weakest archetype.** Summarizing Jensen/Elon/Sam Altman = zero differentiation. Avoid unless Lior has a direct personal counter-experience.

7. **Off-ICP topics hit a ceiling (~300-2K).** Niche AI tools (OpenClaw) fail. ICP cares about SaaS scaling, pricing, revenue, founder journey.

---

## What Works (4K+ impressions — the winners)

- **Post 15 (company party / 240+ employees):** Scene opener + origin story + concrete Lior numbers
- **Post 16 (Israel #1 AI usage):** Credible external source + surprising stat + personal reframe
- **Post 18 (personal/emotional):** High personal stake + emotional arc

**The winning formula:** Scene → Personal proof with real Lior numbers → Friction/contrarian angle → Open gap ending (not a generic question).
