# Lior's Content Channels (as of 2026-04-18)

- **Website** ([lior-website-base44.md](lior-website-base44.md))

## What Lior DOES NOT have
- ❌ **Newsletter** — no email list, no Substack, no beehiiv
- ❌ **Podcast of his own** — only guest appearances
- ❌ **YouTube channel** (TBD — verify if needed)

## Why this matters for content adaptation

**Why:** Many top creators (Justin Welsh, Matt Gray, Dan Koe, Tyler Denk) run their LinkedIn as a funnel to their newsletter. Their post structures often end with a newsletter CTA. We can study and steal their HOOKS and STRUCTURES, but we cannot copy their CTA ending verbatim — we'd be pointing to a newsletter that doesn't exist.

**How to apply:**
- When adapting a Justin Welsh / Matt Gray / Dan Koe / Tyler Denk structure for Lior: keep the hook, body, and tension. Replace the newsletter CTA with one of:
  - An open question back to the reader ("What's your version of this?")
  - A reflection close (no CTA — let the post breathe)
  - A conversation invitation ("Curious how others see this")
  - Link to a specific website page if relevant (articles, podcasts pages)
- **Do not invent a newsletter CTA.** Do not promise "more on this soon" if we can't deliver.
- If a strategic decision is later made to launch a newsletter, update this file and unlock the full Justin Welsh / Matt Gray playbook.
