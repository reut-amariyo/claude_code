### Key learnings from this post
- Written from Lior Fozin's (CEO) first person perspective
- Hook: personal emotional moment at company party, ties back to origin story
- Used "Israeli reference style" - relatable culture description then impressive stats casually
- Bullet points for stats (240+, Fiverr exit, impact)
- Team DNA woven naturally: FIFA, dogs, music
- Closing punch: "Startup energy. Corporate backing. Nothing holds you back." - three short hits
- No fluff, no filler words like "something special" or "this is how we roll"
- Link in first comment (not in post body)
- Roles listed without emojis in final version
