## Key Recommendations

1. **Instagram is the biggest investment** — make sure the US agency has proven case studies with personal brands (not just product brands)
2. **Conferences = content machines** — every conference should produce 20+ pieces of social content (clips, photos, quotes, behind-the-scenes)
3. **PR amplifies everything** — a Forbes feature or TechCrunch article gives credibility that boosts all platforms
4. **Podcast guest appearances are the highest-ROI item** — each appearance puts Lior in front of an established audience for relatively low cost
5. **X/Twitter is the long game** — starting from 255 followers means 3 months won't make it huge, but it plants the seed for SaaS thought leadership where the conversation happens
6. **Measure weekly** — set up a dashboard tracking followers, engagement rate, and reach across all platforms. Review every Friday and shift budget to what's working

- **Podcast launch timeline**: Realistic to launch by mid-May if we start production immediately
