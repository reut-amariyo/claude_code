# The KPI: net new followers per month

Changed 2026-08-23 by Reut. Impressions are a diagnostic input, not a win condition.

## Why

- April to July 2026 impressions moved 6x while net new followers never left a 922-1,100 band.
  May's two viral posts bought 240,000 extra impressions and roughly 91 extra followers.
- The two best-converting months were the two LOWEST-reach months. April 18.7 and June 19.5
  followers per 1K impressions; May, the record month, 3.8.
- LinkedIn is about 1% of the 7M/month company reach OKR. It was never the place to buy reach.

## The steerable model

```
followers = impressions × (profile visits / impressions) × (follows / profile visits)
               reach            curiosity rate                profile conversion
```

Monthly profile visits have never been pulled and are blocking.

## Baselines and targets

Baseline 963 net new followers/month. Total 11,607 at end of July 2026.
Q3 KR unchanged: 13.5K by 30 September, on track.
Q4 ladder proposed to Lior: 1,400 / 1,800 / 2,200.
Organic 100K lands around 2029. Earlier requires paid, or the Instagram port.

## Per-post win condition

**150+ net followers in the 48 hours after publishing**, replacing "10,000+ impressions".
Provisional until one month of daily follower-delta attribution recalibrates it.

## The mechanism behind the mega-accounts

Measured live 2026-08-23: Zain Kahn has 1,001,483 followers and his last five posts drew
38 to 121 reactions, about 6 per 100K, the weakest in the sample. His posts are newsletter
advertising case studies, not audience content. His headline is a follow CTA and his banner is
the Superhuman newsletter brand; Hamna Aslam Kahn runs the same headline formula at 280,636
followers, pointing at the same 1M-reader list.

**The newsletter built the LinkedIn following, not the posts.** Allie K. Miller looks
identical at 2M followers and 17 reactions per 100K.

Consequence for us: 100,000+ Instagram followers against 11,607 on LinkedIn. The Instagram
port plus a follow-CTA headline is the observed mechanism, not a hypothesis. And you cannot
copy that growth by copying those posts, because the growth did not happen in the posts.
