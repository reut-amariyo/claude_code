# Voice and anti-AI rules — apply to EVERY draft

Senior audiences detect AI instantly. These are the confirmed tells.

- No parentheses. Delete if non-essential, fold into the sentence if important.
- No em dashes, en dashes, or double dashes. Regular hyphen only, or restructure.
- No "It's not X, it's Y" contrast templates, in any wording. This includes compressed
  forms like "Measure in times, not years" and "You're not stuck, you're zoomed in".
- Never open a sentence with "Honestly", "Frankly", or "To be honest".
- No aphoristic two-word concept-naming or renamed phenomena, no "the art of X".
- Odd numbers beat even in hooks and lists, 3 / 5 / 7, unless real data is even.
- Hooks under 10 words with a concrete anchor in line 1: named entity, number, year, or
  provocative confession. Never open with "I" unless the verb is a confession: fired,
  killed, gave up, made the mistake of.
- Keep "I / me / my" to about 3 per short post. The default voice speaks TO the reader.
- Maximum one "just" per post.
- One idea per post. One quotable principle. Simple language.
- Read it out loud. If Lior would not say it on a podcast, cut it.
- English always, even when the brief is in Hebrew.
- Present drafts as plain copy-paste text, never blockquotes.

## Formatting facts

- Unicode "bold" glyphs are invisible to LinkedIn search and to screen readers. Never bold
  the keyword line. One bolded line per post maximum.
- Markdown does not render on LinkedIn. Asterisks show literally.
- Arrow lists killed three posts at roughly 800 impressions. Do not use them.
- No "Read that again" template phrases.
