## How IG Reels hooks actually work (2026 mechanics)

These are non-negotiable. Score every hook against them.

1. **The hook is the first 1.5 seconds.** Not the first 5. The scroll decision happens almost instantly.
2. **85% of Reels are watched with no sound.** The on-screen text overlay IS the hook. It must land with zero audio.
3. **First 3 words carry the punch.** If the first 3 words don't stop the thumb, the rest doesn't matter.
4. **A text overlay in second 1 buys 2-3 more seconds of watch time** before audio even kicks in. Always pair a spoken hook with a bold overlay.
5. **The first-frame text is 10x more important than any other text.** Spend the most effort here.
6. **Readable on mobile, one breath.** Bold sans-serif, short, no second clause to parse.
7. **Curiosity gap or visible payoff.** Either open a loop the viewer needs closed, or show the end result in frame one.

## ✅ Validated by REAL data (Sandcastles, founder niche, 2026-06)

Pulled and deep-analyzed the top reels of Hormozi, Dan Martell, Steven Bartlett, GaryVee. These are the patterns that actually scored outlier views in Lior's niche. Use these madlibs first — they beat the generic formulas below.

**The deep engine behind every winner: flip a common belief.** Every top reel takes a thing the audience assumes and inverts it ("Luxury watches are for flexing" → "a watch is a clock for the value of your time"). Before drafting, name the common belief, then state the contrarian reality.

**Highest-performing madlibs (with real outlier scores):**
1. **Question + luxury-object reveal** — `How much is [high-status object]? Take a guess. [shocking number].` Then turn the object into a lesson. (Dan Martell, $500K watch, **x23 outlier, 3.9M views** — the single best performer.)
2. **Question + subversive answer** — `What was your best [investment/achievement]? [unexpected human answer].` Expect business, deliver human. (Martell, "Marrying you," **x21.7, 3.6M**.)
3. **Contrarian binary** — `[Action] from [Group A], [opposite action] from [Group B]. Not the other way around.` (Hormozi, **5.9M, x14.3**.)
4. **Time-compression list** — `I'm gonna compress [X years] of [niche] into [short time].` (Hormozi "13 years in 60 seconds.")
5. **High-stakes setup + reassurance overlay** — spoken: `I'm [age], just [high-stakes action].` / overlay: a blunt contrarian reassurance ("YOU'RE NOT GOING TO DIE"). (Hormozi, x1.7.)
6. **Authority/proof open** — `I'm a [profession]. I did [impressive number].` Number first, earns the watch-time. (Hormozi hot-seat clips.)
7. **Reframe aphorism** — `[X] isn't [what you think], it's [reframe].` ("Success and failure are on the same road. Failure is just an earlier exit.")

**The luxury-setting template (CRITICAL for supercar/rally footage):** Martell's #1 reel uses a luxury object (watch) + a question hook + a value-of-time lesson, filmed against a contrasting backdrop. This maps 1:1 onto Lior in a supercar. Open on the car with a question, subvert to a founder lesson, never flex. This is both the top-performing format AND on-brand (anti-arrogance).

## The 8 hook formulas (generate across ALL of these)

For any topic, the engine writes candidates across every formula below, then scores and keeps the best. Volume first, filter second.

### 1. Transformation / Number Arc  *(highest-converting archetype in 2026)*
> "I went from [bad number] to [good number] in [timeframe]."
- Lior fuel: "I went from selling phones at school to 1M+ customers."
- Lior fuel: "We went from 2 founders to 250 people in 9 years."
- Why it works: concrete before/after = built-in curiosity + proof.

### 2. Contrarian / Unpopular Opinion
> "Everything you believe about [topic] is wrong."
- Lior fuel: "I love my competitors. Here's why."
- Lior fuel: "Speed beats perfection. Until it doesn't."
- Why it works: polarizing stance forces a side. 35-45% higher 3-sec retention.

### 3. Direct Callout (the "if you" filter)
> "If you [trait/situation], stop scrolling."
- Lior fuel: "If you think you need funding to start, watch this."
- Lior fuel: "If you're a control freak, this 70% rule will hurt."
- Why it works: self-selects the exact viewer, who then can't look away.

### 4. Open Question
> "Why is nobody talking about [thing]?"
- Lior fuel: "Why did I walk away from millions one day before signing?"
- Lior fuel: "Who are you actually racing against?"
- Why it works: a question the brain auto-tries to answer.

### 5. Numbered List (algorithm-favored for watch time)
> "3 things I wish I knew before [decision]."
- Lior fuel: "3 mistakes that almost killed my company."
- Lior fuel: "The 70% rule that scaled me to 250 people."
- Rule of 3: always 3 items, never 5 or 7. Each item on screen 2-3 sec.

### 6. Confession / Vulnerability
> "I made [reckless decision] and here's what happened."
- Lior fuel: "The mistake that almost ended everything." (VAT crisis)
- Lior fuel: "From a warehouse in my parents' apartment to this."
- Why it works: a real near-death moment beats any polished win.

### 7. Visible Payoff (result-in-frame-one)
> Show the end state first, explain after.
- Lior fuel: "This is what 9 years of 'no finish line' looks like." (over the Ferrari B-roll)
- Lior fuel: "First dropshipping software ever bought by a public company."
- Why it works: the payoff stops the scroll before the viewer even commits.

### 8. Open Loop / Cliffhanger
> "The decision that changed everything wasn't the one you'd expect."
- Lior fuel: "Everyone told me to sign. I didn't. Here's why."
- Lior fuel: "One question killed a 6-month deal."
- Why it works: an unresolved gap the viewer must close by watching.

## Scoring Rubric (the filter)

Score each candidate 1-5 on all five axes. Max 25. **Only show hooks scoring 18+.** Show the score next to each.

| Axis | 1 (weak) | 5 (strong) |
|------|----------|------------|
| **Scroll-stop** | first 3 words are setup/throat-clearing | first 3 words punch or shock |
| **Curiosity gap** | tells the whole thing, no reason to watch on | opens a loop the viewer must close |
| **Specificity** | generic, no number/name/stake | real number, name, or concrete stake |
| **Lior-voice fit** | fluffy, corporate, or arrogant | direct, eye-level, real, no fluff |
| **3-sec comprehension** | needs a second read or audio to land | reads instantly as silent overlay |

**Tie-breaker:** when two score equal, the one with the more specific real number wins.
