## Phase 1: Format Recommendation
Based on the input, choose the best vehicle:
- **REELS:** Best for: Stories (Hacking the PC), "Day in the Life," Controversial Takes, or "How-To" AI demos. (Vibe: Raw walk-and-talk or High-prod B-roll).
- **CAROUSELS:** Best for: Deep-dives, "Proof of Work" metrics, AI Tool Stacks, or step-by-step business frameworks (OKRs/Management).
- **HYBRID CAROUSELS (Photo + Video):** Best for: **Deep "Proof of Work."** Use a high-quality photo to stop the scroll, followed by short video clips (15-30s) explaining the tech/strategy behind the photo. Perfect for AI demos or behind-the-scenes of BuildYourStore.ai.
- **STATIC POSTS:** Best for: Single, hard-hitting screenshots of metrics, "One-Liner" quotes, or major announcements.

## Phase 2: Copywriting Frameworks

### The "Lior" Reel Script:
- **Hook (0-3s):** One-liner that stops the scroll: hard truth, controversial statement, or big number.
- **Body (3-20s):** Fast-paced logic. "I did X to get Y."
- **Visual Cue:** [Describe the B-roll or Camera angle].
- **CTA:** Directed at the caption or a keyword.

### The "Proof of Work" Carousel:
- **Slide 1:** The "Big Result" Hook.
- **Slides 2-5:** The "How-To" or the "Why." Use specific data/tactics.
- **Slide 6:** The "Tech Leader" Insight (The AI angle).
- **Slide 7:** The CTA (Promote the Ebook/Tool).

### The Static Post:
- **Header:** Bold statement.
- **Body:** Personal story + Business lesson.
- **Close:** One punchy sentence.

**Structure for Hybrid Carousels:**

1. **Slide 1 (Hero Photo):** High-quality, professional shot (Lior at the office, gym, or with the McLaren).

    - **Overlay Text:** A bold, "impossible" result (e.g., "How we hit 1M+ users with 0 funding").

2. **Slide 2 (The Proof Video):** A raw, 30-second screen recording or "phone-in-hand" video.

    - **Content:** Show the dashboard, the AI workflow, or Lior explaining the "Why" behind Slide 1.

3. **Slide 3-4 (The Framework):** Static graphics or text-heavy slides breaking down the 3-step process.

4. **Slide 5 (The Call to Action):** Static image of the Ebook or a "Comment below" graphic.

## Phase 3: Instagram Stories Framework

### Stories Purpose
Stories = trust building, documentation, raw/authentic content. NOT polished feed content. Stories are where followers peel the curtain back and get to know Lior on a personal level.

**When to maximize views:** Only when there's a launch, sale, promotion, hiring push, or a message Lior really wants people to hear. The rest of the time (75%+), stories should be casual documentation - photos, quick thoughts, behind-the-scenes moments.

### 5 Things That KILL Story Views (Avoid on max-view days)
1. **Sharing feed posts to stories** - people go to stories for stories, not to re-watch reels
2. **Re-sharing someone else's story** - even tagged stories hurt your views
3. **Link stickers** - people who don't click = skip = negative engagement. Use DM automation instead
4. **Long talking-head videos** - no rewind on stories, people tap through. Keep video clips SHORT (under 15s)
5. **Overposting** - sweet spot is 1-3 stories. Each additional story = diminishing returns. 5+ stories and people see the dots at top and skip entirely

### The STD Strategy (Max Views Formula)
Use this when Lior has something important to push (hiring, launch, announcement):

**S = Single story.** Post ONE story (max 3). One clip = highest ceiling for views.

**T = Text-heavy.**
- Photo story, NOT video
- Lior's selfie in bottom-right corner (face visible = more views)
- Text fills the rest of the screen
- 3+ sentences minimum (forces people to hold-to-pause = increased watch time)
- Break text into 1-3 sentence chunks with spacing between them
- Highlight key words in a different color for skimmability
- Photo stories display for 3-5 seconds; text makes people pause longer

**D = DM reply CTA.**
- End with a specific call to action: "Reply [KEYWORD] to get [VALUE]"
- The value must be genuinely appealing (freebie, link, discount, exclusive info)
- Use ManyChat or similar DM automation to auto-respond
- Replies are THE #1 engagement signal for story algorithm
- Do NOT use link stickers. DM automation replaces them

**After posting:** Don't post anything else for 24 hours. Let the single story breathe. No reshares, no video stories, nothing.

### What Makes Stories Shareable (SHARE Acronym)
- **S = Simple** - easy to understand, no complex language
- **H = Hook** - first line must grab attention instantly
- **A = Authentic** - in-the-moment, not scripted or AI-generated feeling
- **R = Relatable** - specificity creates relatability (concrete details > vague feelings)
- **E = Entertaining** - stories over tips, narrative over bullet points

### 5 Categories of Shareable Stories
1. **Inspirational** - powerful quotes, stories that made Lior stop and think (not cliches)
2. **Community-oriented** - promoting causes, raising awareness, celebrating team/users
3. **Relatable humor** - memes, real moments, self-aware jokes
4. **Controversial / Hot takes** - unpopular opinions, myth-busting, bold stances on industry topics
5. **Timely / Breaking news** - NAP (Niche Adjacent Post): take what everyone's talking about, relate it back to Lior's niche (SaaS, eCommerce, AI, founder life)

### 3 Ways to Drive Profile Visits from Stories (2nd best engagement signal)
1. **Screenshot of profile** - circle/highlight a specific post, create FOMO to make people go watch it
2. **Hidden keyword in bio** - gamification: edit bio temporarily with a secret code/keyword
3. **Link in bio CTA** - least preferred, but still drives profile visits

### Stories Copywriting Template (Text-Heavy Format)

```
[HOOK LINE - bold, scroll-stopping, 1 sentence]

[STORY/CONTEXT - 2-3 sentences, broken into short chunks]

[PUNCHLINE or INSIGHT - 1 short punchy line]

[CTA - "Reply [KEYWORD] and I'll send you [VALUE]"]
```

**Visual layout:**
- Background: selfie of Lior (casual, real, in-the-moment) OR solid dark background
- Text: large, readable, broken into spaced paragraphs
- Key words highlighted in accent color
- CTA at the bottom, clearly visible
