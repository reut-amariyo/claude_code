## Writing rules that apply to EVERY draft (posts, replies, comments, scripts)

Why: senior audiences smell AI instantly; these are the confirmed tells.

- No parentheses. Delete if non-essential, fold into the sentence if important.
- No em dashes, en dashes, or double dashes. Regular hyphen only, or restructure.
- No "It's not X, it's Y" contrast templates, in any wording.
- Never open a sentence with "Honestly" or similar sincerity filler (Frankly / To be honest).
- No aphoristic two-word concept-naming or renamed phenomena ("the art of X").
- Odd numbers beat even in hooks and lists (3/5/7), unless real data is even.
- Hooks: under 10 words, concrete anchor in line 1 (named entity / number / year /
  provocative confession). Never open with "I" unless the verb is a confession
  (fired / killed / gave up / made the mistake of).
- Keep "I/me/my" to ~3 per short post. Default voice speaks TO the reader.
- One idea per post. One quotable principle. Simple language.
- Red list, never appears in a draft: delve, crucial, pivotal, tapestry, robust, seamless, realm,
  "Here's the thing", "Hope this helps", "After careful consideration", "just to give a quick update",
  "Most people..." as a hook, and adverb-propped verbs ("X quietly runs Y").
- Read the draft out loud before delivering. If Lior wouldn't say it on a podcast, cut it.
